Kako se uči ovdeHow learning works here

Čitaj, eksperimentiši, proveri seRead, experiment, check yourself

  1. 01

    ČitajRead

    Svaka lekcija ide korak po korak: ciljevi, objašnjenje, formule sa legendom, rešeni primeri i česte greške.

    Every lesson goes step by step: goals, explanation, formulas with a legend, worked examples and common mistakes.

  2. 02

    EksperimentišiExperiment

    Okreći 3D modele, pomeraj klizače i gledaj animacije: motor koji se vrti, struja u petlji, talasi usmerivača, ekran osciloskopa.

    Rotate 3D models, drag the sliders and watch animations: a spinning motor, current in a loop, rectifier waves, an oscilloscope screen.

  3. 03

    Proveri seCheck yourself

    Mali test sa vrlo lakim pitanjima radi se u kviz sobi. Uz odgovor možeš da napišeš i formulu koju si koristio.

    A small test of very easy questions runs in a quiz room. Next to your answer you can write the formula you used.

Kviz sobeQuiz rooms

Napravi svoj kviz i gledaj odgovore uživoMake your own quiz and watch the answers come in

Autor postavlja pitanja, bira vrstu odgovora i da li se traži formula ili postupak, pa dobija kratak kod sobe. Učenici ukucaju kod i korisničko ime, a autor vidi ko je šta odgovorio.

The author writes the questions, picks the answer type and whether a formula or working is required, and gets a short room code. Students enter the code and a username, and the author sees who answered what.

Pravljenje kviza traži lozinku autora.Creating a quiz needs the author password.
