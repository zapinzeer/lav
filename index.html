Otvorena biblioteka za fiziku i elektronikuAn open library for physics and electronics

lav

Lekcije iz fizike i elektronike, objašnjene korak po korak. Uz formule i rešene primere tu su 3D modeli i animacije koje možeš da pomeraš, a na kraju svake lekcije je mali test da vidiš šta ti je ostalo u glavi.

Physics and electronics lessons, explained one step at a time. Besides the formulas and worked examples there are 3D models and animations you can move around, and a small test at the end of every lesson to see what stuck.

Kako izgleda lekcijaWhat a lesson looks like

Svaka lekcija ima tri delaEvery lesson has three parts

  1. 01

    PročitajRead

    Lekcija kreće od osnovne ideje i polako stiže do rešenih primera. Uz svaku formulu piše šta znači svaka oznaka, a na kraju su greške koje se najčešće prave.

    A lesson starts from the basic idea and builds up to worked examples. Every formula comes with a note on what each symbol means, and the last part covers the mistakes people make most often.

  2. 02

    IsprobajTry it

    Okreni 3D model, pomeri klizač i vidi šta se menja: motor koji se vrti, struja u petlji, talasi na usmerivaču, ekran osciloskopa.

    Turn a 3D model, drag a slider and see what changes: a spinning motor, a current in a loop, the waves on a rectifier, an oscilloscope screen.

  3. 03

    ProveriCheck

    Test na kraju ima samo laka pitanja i radi se u kviz sobi. Uz odgovor možeš da upišeš i formulu kojom si došao do njega.

    The test at the end has only easy questions and runs in a quiz room. Along with your answer you can write down the formula that got you there.

Kviz sobeQuiz rooms

Napravi kviz i vidi ko je šta odgovorioMake a quiz and see who answered what

Napišeš pitanja, izabereš kako se odgovara i da li treba upisati formulu ili postupak, i dobiješ kratak kod sobe. Ostali ukucaju kod i korisničko ime, a ti vidiš odgovore čim stignu.

You write the questions, choose how they are answered and whether a formula or working is needed, and you get a short room code. Everyone else types in the code and a username, and you see the answers as they arrive.

Za pravljenje kviza treba lozinka autora.Making a quiz needs the author password.
