Magnetna indukcijaMagnetic induction

Oko svakog magneta i oko svakog provodnika kroz koji teče struja postoji magnetno polje. Koliko je to polje jako i u kom smeru deluje, opisuje vektor magnetne indukcije B.

Every magnet, and every wire carrying a current, is surrounded by a magnetic field. How strong that field is, and which way it acts, is described by the magnetic induction vector B (also called magnetic flux density).

Polje prikazujemo linijama polja. Izvan magneta one izlaze iz severnog pola (N) i ulaze u južni pol (S), i uvek su zatvorene. Vektor B je tangenta na liniju polja, a gde su linije gušće, polje je jače.

We draw the field with field lines. Outside a magnet they leave the north pole (N) and enter the south pole (S), and they always form closed loops. B is tangent to the field line, and where the lines are packed closer, the field is stronger.

Kako merimo B?How do we measure B?

Magnetno polje deluje silom na provodnik sa strujom. Ta sila se zove Amperova sila i zavisi od ugla između provodnika i linija polja:

A magnetic field pushes on a wire that carries a current. This push is called the Ampère force, and it depends on the angle between the wire and the field lines:

\[ F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha \]

Sila je najveća kada je provodnik normalan na polje (α = 90°, sin 90° = 1), a jednaka nuli kada je paralelan sa poljem (sin 0° = 0). Iz najveće sile definišemo magnetnu indukciju:

The force is largest when the wire is perpendicular to the field (α = 90°, sin 90° = 1) and zero when the wire is parallel to it (sin 0° = 0). From the largest force we define magnetic induction:

\[ B = \frac{F}{I\,l} \qquad 1\,\mathrm{T} = 1\,\frac{\mathrm{N}}{\mathrm{A\cdot m}} \]

Polje ima indukciju od 1 tesle ako na provodnik dužine 1 m, kroz koji teče struja od 1 A, deluje silom od 1 N. Tesla je velika jedinica, pa se često koriste mT (mili) i μT (mikro).

A field has an induction of 1 tesla if it pushes on a 1 m wire carrying 1 A with a force of 1 N. The tesla is a large unit, so mT (milli) and μT (micro) are common.

Pravilo leve rukeLeft-hand rule

Postavi levu šaku tako da linije polja ulaze u dlan, a ispruženi prsti pokazuju smer struje. Odvojeni palac tada pokazuje smer sile.

Hold your left hand so the field lines enter your palm and your outstretched fingers point along the current. Your thumb, held out to the side, then shows the direction of the force.

Magnetni fluksMagnetic flux

Magnetni fluks Φ govori koliko magnetnog polja prolazi kroz neku površinu, na primer kroz žičanu petlju. Najlakše ga je zamisliti kao broj linija polja koje prolaze kroz tu površinu.

Magnetic flux Φ tells us how much magnetic field passes through a surface, for example through a wire loop. The easiest way to picture it is as the number of field lines that pass through that surface.

Fluks se menja kada se promeni jačina polja B, površina S ili ugao α.

The flux changes when the field strength B, the area S or the angle α changes.

Zašto je fluks važan?Why does flux matter?

Kada se fluks kroz kalem menja, u kalemu nastaje (indukuje se) napon. Na toj pojavi, elektromagnetnoj indukciji, rade generatori u elektranama i transformatori.

When the flux through a coil changes, a voltage appears (is induced) in the coil. Generators in power plants and transformers work on this effect, called electromagnetic induction.

Tema 3Topic 3 · Erstedov ogled · pravilo desne rukeØrsted's experiment · right-hand rule

Magnetno polje strujnog provodnikaMagnetic field of a current-carrying wire

Godine 1820. danski fizičar Hans Kristijan Ersted primetio je da se igla kompasa pomeri kada kroz obližnju žicu pusti struju. Tako je otkriveno da električna struja stvara magnetno polje.

In 1820 the Danish physicist Hans Christian Ørsted noticed that a compass needle moved when he switched on a current in a nearby wire. This showed that an electric current creates a magnetic field.

Oko pravog provodnika linije polja su koncentrični krugovi, a provodnik je u njihovom centru. Što smo dalje od provodnika, polje je slabije.

Around a straight wire the field lines are concentric circles with the wire at their centre. The farther we are from the wire, the weaker the field.

Pravilo desne rukeRight-hand rule

Obuhvati provodnik desnom šakom tako da ispruženi palac pokazuje smer struje. Savijeni prsti tada pokazuju smer linija polja.

Grip the wire with your right hand so your outstretched thumb points along the current. Your curled fingers then show the direction of the field lines.

Kružni zavojakA circular loop

Ako žicu savijemo u krug poluprečnika R, polja svih delova žice se u centru sabiraju:

If we bend the wire into a circle of radius R, the fields from every part of the wire add up at the centre:

\[ B = \frac{\mu_0\, I}{2R} \]

Mnogo takvih zavojaka jedan do drugog čine kalem, a to nas vodi do elektromagneta.

Many such loops side by side make a coil, and that leads us to the electromagnet.

Tema 4Topic 4 · kalem + gvozdeno jezgrocoil + iron core

ElektromagnetElectromagnet

Kada žicu namotamo u mnogo zavojaka, dobijamo kalem (solenoid). Polja svih zavojaka se sabiraju, pa je unutar kalema polje jako i skoro homogeno. Spolja, kalem sa strujom ponaša se kao šipkasti magnet sa severnim i južnim polom.

Wind a wire into many turns and you get a coil (solenoid). The fields of all the turns add up, so inside the coil the field is strong and almost uniform. From the outside, a coil with a current behaves like a bar magnet with a north and a south pole.

Gvozdeno jezgroThe iron core

Ako u kalem stavimo gvozdeno jezgro, gvožđe se namagnetiše i polje postaje μr puta jače. Kalem sa gvozdenim jezgrom zovemo elektromagnet.

If we put an iron core into the coil, the iron becomes magnetised and the field gets μr times stronger. A coil with an iron core is called an electromagnet.

Gde je severni pol?Where is the north pole?

Obuhvati kalem desnom šakom tako da savijeni prsti prate smer struje kroz zavojke. Ispruženi palac pokazuje severni pol N.

Grip the coil with your right hand so your curled fingers follow the current around the turns. Your outstretched thumb points to the north pole N.

Zašto elektromagnet?Why an electromagnet?

  • Može da se uključi i isključi.
  • Jačina se podešava strujom i brojem zavojaka.
  • Kada se smer struje obrne, polovi zamene mesta.
  • It can be switched on and off.
  • Its strength is set by the current and the number of turns.
  • When the current is reversed, the poles swap places.

Elektromagneti se nalaze u dizalicama za staro gvožđe, električnim zvoncima, relejima, zvučnicima, MRI aparatima i vozovima na magnetnoj levitaciji.

Electromagnets are found in scrapyard cranes, electric bells, relays, loudspeakers, MRI scanners and magnetic levitation trains.
