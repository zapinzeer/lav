Deo 1Part 1 · uvodintroduction

Šta je naizmenična struja?What is alternating current?

Jednosmerna struja (DC) uvek teče istim smerom, na primer iz baterije. Naizmenična struja (AC) menja smer, i to periodično: ista slika se ponavlja iznova. Najvažniji oblik je sinusni, jer se tako menja napon kojeg stvara generator, o čemu smo učili kod elektromagnetne indukcije: petlja koja se obrće u magnetnom polju daje napon \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \).

Direct current (DC) always flows in the same direction, for example from a battery. Alternating current (AC) changes direction, and does so periodically: the same picture repeats again and again. The most important shape is the sine, because that is how the voltage from a generator changes, as we learned in electromagnetic induction: a loop turning in a magnetic field gives a voltage \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \).

Mreža u Srbiji i većem delu sveta ima 230 V i 50 Hz. Pogledajmo šta sve opisuje takvu veličinu. Ti parametri se zovu elementi naizmenične struje.

The mains in Serbia and most of the world is 230 V and 50 Hz. Let us look at everything that describes such a quantity. These parameters are called the elements of alternating current.

Deo 2Part 2 · T · f · ωT · f · ω

Perioda, učestanost i kružna učestanostPeriod, frequency and angular frequency

Perioda T je vreme za koje se veličina vrati na isto stanje i krene isto kao pre. Učestanost f kaže koliko se perioda desi u jednoj sekundi, pa je to recipročna vrednost periode. Kružna učestanost ω je ugaona brzina fazora: jedna perioda je pun krug, 2π radijana.

The period T is the time after which the quantity returns to the same state and sets off the same way as before. The frequency f tells how many periods happen in one second, so it is the reciprocal of the period. The angular frequency ω is the angular speed of the phasor: one period is a full circle, 2π radians.

\[ f = \frac{1}{T} \qquad \omega = 2\pi f = \frac{2\pi}{T} \]

Za mrežu je f = 50 Hz, pa je T = 1/50 s = 20 ms i ω = 2π · 50 = 314 rad/s. Struja u mreži dakle 100 puta u sekundi menja smer i 100 puta prođe kroz nulu.

For the mains f = 50 Hz, so T = 1/50 s = 20 ms and ω = 2π · 50 = 314 rad/s. The mains current therefore reverses 100 times a second and passes through zero 100 times.

Pomisli prvo: lampa na mreži svetli i treperi 100 puta u sekundi. Zašto toga ne vidimo?Think first: a lamp on the mains flickers 100 times a second. Why do we not see it?

Oko ne razlikuje promene brže od oko 50–60 puta u sekundi, a žarna nit se usput ne stigne ni ohladiti do kraja. Zato vidimo stalno svetlo.

The eye cannot tell changes faster than about 50–60 per second, and a filament does not manage to cool down completely in between. So we see a steady light.

Dve jedinice za ugaoTwo units for an angle

Jedan krug je 360° ili 2π rad. Zato faza može da se piše u stepenima, kao \( \sin(\omega t + 30^\circ) \), ili u radijanima, kao \( \sin(\omega t + \pi/6) \). Pazi da kalkulator bude u pravom režimu.

One full circle is 360° or 2π rad. So a phase can be written in degrees, as \( \sin(\omega t + 30^\circ) \), or in radians, as \( \sin(\omega t + \pi/6) \). Make sure your calculator is in the right mode.

Deo 3Part 3 · U_m · U_pp · φU_m · U_pp · φ

Trenutna vrednost, amplituda i fazaInstantaneous value, amplitude and phase

Vrednost u bilo kom trenutku daje jednačina sinusne veličine:

The value at any moment is given by the equation of the sine quantity:

\[ u(t) = U_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi_u) \qquad i(t) = I_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi_i) \]

Amplituda Um je najveća vrednost (vrh). Razlika između najvišeg i najnižeg vrha je vrh-vrh vrednost \( U_{pp} = 2U_m \), a to je ono što na ekranu osciloskopa merimo između dva vrha. Početna faza φ kaže gde je kriva u trenutku t = 0. Pozitivno φ pomera krivu ulevo (kriva „kreće ranije”), negativno udesno.

The amplitude Um is the largest value (the peak). The difference between the highest and the lowest peak is the peak-to-peak value \( U_{pp} = 2U_m \), which is what we measure between two peaks on an oscilloscope screen. The initial phase φ tells where the curve is at the moment t = 0. A positive φ shifts the curve to the left (the curve "starts earlier"), a negative one to the right.

Kada su napon i struja u istom kolu, njihove početne faze se najčešće razlikuju. Ta razlika, \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i \), zove se fazna razlika i najvažniji je podatak o kolu naizmenične struje. Uključi struju u modelu iznad da je vidiš.

When the voltage and the current are in the same circuit, their initial phases usually differ. That difference, \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i \), is called the phase difference and is the most important piece of information about an AC circuit. Switch on the current in the model above to see it.

Deo 4Part 4 · U_ef · U_srU_rms · U_avg

Srednja i efektivna vrednostAverage and RMS value

Prosek sinusa preko cele periode je nula, jer je pozitivna poluperioda jednaka negativnoj. Zato se za srednju vrednost uzima prosek preko jedne poluperiode (ono što bi pokazao instrument sa jednosmernim usmeravanjem):

The average of a sine over a whole period is zero, because the positive half-period equals the negative one. That is why the average value is taken over one half-period (what an instrument with rectification would show):

\[ U_{sr} = \frac{2}{\pi}\,U_m \approx 0{,}637\,U_m \]

Mnogo je važnija efektivna vrednost. To je jednosmerni napon koji bi na istom otporniku proizveo istu toplotu (istu snagu) kao naizmenični. Računa se iz srednje vrednosti kvadrata, a za sinus je rezultat:

Much more important is the RMS (effective) value. It is the direct voltage that would produce the same heat (the same power) in the same resistor as the alternating one. It is calculated from the mean of the squares, and for a sine the result is:

\[ U = \frac{U_m}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0{,}707\,U_m \qquad I = \frac{I_m}{\sqrt{2}} \]

Voltmetri i ampermetri za naizmeničnu struju pokazuju efektivnu vrednost. Kada kažemo „mreža je 230 V”, mislimo na efektivnu vrednost, a amplituda je \( 230\sqrt{2} = 325 \) V. Odnos efektivne i srednje vrednosti zove se faktor oblika: \( k_f = U/U_{sr} = 1{,}11 \).

Voltmeters and ammeters for AC show the RMS value. When we say "the mains is 230 V", we mean the RMS value, and the amplitude is \( 230\sqrt{2} = 325 \) V. The ratio of the RMS to the average value is called the form factor: \( k_f = U/U_{sr} = 1.11 \).

Zašto baš √2?Why exactly √2?

Snaga na otporniku je \( p = u^2/R \) i menja se između nule i \( U_m^2/R \). Njena srednja vrednost je tačno pola maksimuma, \( U_m^2/(2R) \). Izjednačimo to sa \( U^2/R \) i dobijamo \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \).

The power in a resistor is \( p = u^2/R \) and varies between zero and \( U_m^2/R \). Its mean is exactly half of the maximum, \( U_m^2/(2R) \). Equating that with \( U^2/R \) gives \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \).

Primer 2 · prvi prolazak kroz nuluExample 2 · the first zero crossing

Dato: \( u = 100\sin(\omega t - 30^\circ) \) V pri 50 Hz. Kada prvi put posle \( t = 0 \) napon postaje nula?

Given: \( u = 100\sin(\omega t - 30^\circ) \) V at 50 Hz. When does the voltage first become zero after \( t = 0 \)?

  1. Sinus je nula kada je argument 0: \( \omega t - 30^\circ = 0 \Rightarrow \omega t = 30^\circ \).
  2. Ugao od 30° je \( 30/360 \) periode: \( t = \dfrac{30}{360}\cdot 20\ \mathrm{ms} = 1{,}67\ \mathrm{ms} \).
  3. Provera: u trenutku \( t = 0 \) je \( u = 100\sin(-30^\circ) = -50 \) V, napon raste ka nuli.
  1. A sine is zero when its argument is 0: \( \omega t - 30^\circ = 0 \Rightarrow \omega t = 30^\circ \).
  2. An angle of 30° is \( 30/360 \) of a period: \( t = \dfrac{30}{360}\cdot 20\ \mathrm{ms} = 1.67\ \mathrm{ms} \).
  3. Check: at \( t = 0 \) we have \( u = 100\sin(-30^\circ) = -50 \) V, and the voltage rises towards zero.

Primer 3 · ista toplotaExample 3 · the same heat

Dato: na otpornik od 100 Ω priključen je naizmenični napon amplitude 141 V. Kolika je srednja snaga i koji jednosmerni napon daje istu snagu?

Given: an alternating voltage of amplitude 141 V is connected to a 100 Ω resistor. What is the average power, and which direct voltage gives the same power?

  1. Efektivni napon: \( U = 141/\sqrt{2} = 100\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Srednja snaga: \( P = U^2/R = 100^2/100 = 100\ \mathrm{W} \).
  3. Isto daje jednosmerni napon od 100 V: \( P = 100^2/100 = 100\ \mathrm{W} \). To je smisao efektivne vrednosti.
  1. RMS voltage: \( U = 141/\sqrt{2} = 100\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Average power: \( P = U^2/R = 100^2/100 = 100\ \mathrm{W} \).
  3. A direct voltage of 100 V gives the same: \( P = 100^2/100 = 100\ \mathrm{W} \). That is the meaning of the RMS value.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Amplituda umesto efektivne vrednosti. Voltmetar i podaci na tablicama uređaja daju efektivnu vrednost. Za snagu i toplotu koristi efektivne vrednosti, a za dimenzionisanje izolacije i oscilograme amplitudu.
  • Srednja vrednost cele periode. Ona je nula. Srednja vrednost se uzima za poluperiodu, ili posle usmeravanja.
  • Mešanje f i ω. \( \omega = 2\pi f \). Za 50 Hz je ω = 314 rad/s, ne 50. U jednačini \( \sin(\omega t) \) uvek stoji ω.
  • Stepeni i radijani. \( \sin(314\cdot 0{,}0025) \) računaj u radijanima. Ako ti je kalkulator u stepenima, dobićeš pogrešan rezultat.
  • Amplitude instead of RMS. A voltmeter and the data on device labels give the RMS value. Use RMS values for power and heat, and the amplitude for insulation ratings and oscilloscope traces.
  • The average over a whole period. It is zero. The average value is taken over a half-period, or after rectification.
  • Mixing up f and ω. \( \omega = 2\pi f \). For 50 Hz we have ω = 314 rad/s, not 50. The equation \( \sin(\omega t) \) always contains ω.
  • Degrees and radians. Calculate \( \sin(314\cdot 0.0025) \) in radians. If your calculator is in degrees, you will get a wrong result.

Deo 7Part 7

SažetakSummary

  • Naizmenična veličina se periodično ponavlja. Perioda i učestanost su \( f = 1/T \), a kružna učestanost \( \omega = 2\pi f \).
  • Sinusna veličina je opisana amplitudom, kružnom učestanošću i početnom fazom: \( u = U_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi) \).
  • Srednja vrednost (za poluperiodu) je \( 2U_m/\pi = 0{,}637U_m \), efektivna je \( U_m/\sqrt{2} = 0{,}707U_m \).
  • Efektivna vrednost je jednosmerna vrednost koja daje istu snagu, a to pokazuju voltmetar i ampermetar.
  • Fazna razlika \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i \) govori ko prednjači: ona je ključ za otpornik, kalem i kondenzator.
  • An alternating quantity repeats periodically. The period and frequency are related by \( f = 1/T \), and the angular frequency is \( \omega = 2\pi f \).
  • A sine quantity is described by its amplitude, angular frequency and initial phase: \( u = U_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi) \).
  • The average value (over a half-period) is \( 2U_m/\pi = 0.637U_m \), the RMS value is \( U_m/\sqrt{2} = 0.707U_m \).
  • The RMS value is the direct value that gives the same power, and it is what a voltmeter and an ammeter show.
  • The phase difference \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i \) tells who leads: it is the key to the resistor, the coil and the capacitor.
