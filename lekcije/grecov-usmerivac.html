Deo 1Part 1 · iskoristi obe poluperiodeuse both half-cycles

Grecov (mosni) usmerivačThe Graetz (bridge) rectifier

Poluperiodni usmerivač baca polovinu svake periode. Prirodno pitanje je: može li se i negativna poluperioda „okrenuti” i iskoristiti? Može, pomoću četiri diode vezane u obliku romba. To je Grecov ili mosni usmerivač (po nemačkom fizičaru Leu Grecu, engl. Graetz).

A half-wave rectifier throws away half of every period. A natural question is: can the negative half-cycle be "turned over" and used too? It can, with four diodes connected in a diamond. This is the Graetz or bridge rectifier (after the German physicist Leo Graetz).

Most ima dva naizmenična priključka (ka izvoru) i dva jednosmerna (ka opterećenju). Diode su vezane tako da se kraj izvora koji je trenutno pozitivan uvek spaja sa plusom opterećenja, a negativan sa minusom:

The bridge has two alternating terminals (to the source) and two direct ones (to the load). The diodes are connected so that the end of the source that is currently positive is always joined to the plus of the load, and the negative one to the minus:

  • Gornji kraj izvora je +: struja ide kroz D1, preko opterećenja odozgo naniže, pa nazad kroz D4.
  • Donji kraj izvora je +: struja ide kroz D2, preko opterećenja opet odozgo naniže, pa nazad kroz D3.
  • The upper end of the source is +: the current goes through D1, across the load from top to bottom, and back through D4.
  • The lower end of the source is +: the current goes through D2, across the load again from top to bottom, and back through D3.

U obe poluperiode struja kroz opterećenje ima isti smer, pa je izlazni napon jednosmeran, a pulsevi dolaze dvaput češće: 100 puta u sekundi. U svakom trenutku kroz izvor i opterećenje su na red dve diode, pa se gubi pad od dvaput 0,7 V.

In both half-cycles the current through the load has the same direction, so the output voltage is unidirectional, and the pulses come twice as often: 100 times per second. At every moment two diodes are in series with the source and the load, so a drop of twice 0.7 V is lost.

Pomisli prvo: u negativnoj poluperiodi donji kraj izvora je +. Koje diode vode i u kom smeru teče struja kroz opterećenje?Think first: in the negative half-cycle the lower end of the source is +. Which diodes conduct and in which direction does the current flow through the load?

Vode D2 i D3. Struja izlazi iz donjeg kraja izvora, ide kroz D2 do plusa opterećenja, kroz opterećenje odozgo naniže (isti smer kao malopre), pa kroz D3 nazad do gornjeg kraja izvora.

D2 and D3 conduct. The current leaves the lower end of the source, goes through D2 to the plus of the load, through the load from top to bottom (the same direction as before), and through D3 back to the upper end of the source.

Kako zapamtiti putanjuHow to remember the path

Struja izlazi iz pozitivnog kraja izvora, prolazi kroz onu od dve „gornje” diode (D1 ili D2) koja je povezana sa tim krajem, kroz opterećenje, pa kroz onu od dve „donje” diode (D3 ili D4) koja vodi ka negativnom kraju izvora. Dve gornje diode imaju zajedničke katode (to je plus izlaza), a dve donje zajedničke anode (minus izlaza).

The current leaves the positive end of the source, passes through that one of the two "upper" diodes (D1 or D2) that is connected to this end, through the load, and then through that one of the two "lower" diodes (D3 or D4) that leads to the negative end of the source. The two upper diodes have common cathodes (this is the output plus), and the two lower ones common anodes (the output minus).

Pomisli prvo: izvor daje 12 V (efektivno). Koliki je srednji napon na izlazu idealnog mosta? Koliko je to puta više nego kod poluperiodnog?Think first: the source gives 12 V (RMS). What is the average output voltage of an ideal bridge? How many times more is that than for a half-wave rectifier?

\( U_m = 12\sqrt{2} = 17\ \mathrm{V} \), pa je \( U_{dc} = 2\cdot 17/\pi = 10{,}8\ \mathrm{V} \). Poluperiodni daje \( 17/\pi = 5{,}4\ \mathrm{V} \), dakle tačno dvaput manje.

\( U_m = 12\sqrt{2} = 17\ \mathrm{V} \), so \( U_{dc} = 2\cdot 17/\pi = 10.8\ \mathrm{V} \). A half-wave rectifier gives \( 17/\pi = 5.4\ \mathrm{V} \), so exactly half as much.

Deo 2Part 2 · tri načina da se ispravi naponthree ways to rectify a voltage

Poluperiodni, srednji izvod ili mostHalf-wave, centre tap or bridge

Pored mosta, obe poluperiode koristi i usmerivač sa srednjim izvodom: sekundar transformatora ima izvod na sredini, svaka polovina napaja po jednu diodu. D1 vodi kada je gornji kraj pozitivan prema sredini, a D2 kada je donji. Katode su spojene i čine plus izlaza, a srednji izvod je minus.

Besides the bridge, a centre-tap rectifier also uses both half-cycles: the transformer secondary has a tap in the middle and each half feeds one diode. D1 conducts when the upper end is positive relative to the middle, and D2 when the lower one is. The cathodes are joined and form the output plus, and the centre tap is the minus.

Sve što smo naučili za izlaz važi za oba punotalasna usmerivača:

Everything we learnt about the output holds for both full-wave rectifiers:

\[ U_{dc} = \frac{2U_m}{\pi} \qquad U_{ef} = \frac{U_m}{\sqrt{2}} \]
\[ \gamma = \sqrt{\left(\frac{U_{ef}}{U_{dc}}\right)^2 - 1} = 0{,}48 \qquad \eta = \frac{P_{dc}}{P_{ac}} = \frac{8}{\pi^2} = 81{,}1\ \% \]

Faktor talasanja 0,48 je skoro tri puta manji od 1,21 kod poluperiodnog, a stepen iskorišćenja je dvostruko veći. Sa kondenzatorom je prednost još veća: isti kondenzator se dopunjava dvaput češće, pa je talasanje upola manje.

A ripple factor of 0.48 is almost three times smaller than 1.21 for a half-wave rectifier, and the efficiency is twice as large. With a capacitor the advantage is even larger: the same capacitor is topped up twice as often, so the ripple is half as large.

Za radoznale: odakle 2/π i 0,48?For the curious: where do 2/π and 0.48 come from?

Izlaz je \( |U_m\sin\omega t| \) sa periodom \( T/2 \). Srednja vrednost je površina jednog „brega” podeljena njegovim trajanjem:

The output is \( |U_m\sin\omega t| \) with period \( T/2 \). The average is the area of one "hump" divided by its duration:

\[ U_{dc} = \frac{1}{T/2}\int_0^{T/2} U_m\sin\omega t\,dt = \frac{2}{T}\cdot U_m\cdot\frac{T}{\pi} = \frac{2U_m}{\pi} \]

Kvadrat sinusa ima istu srednju vrednost \( U_m^2/2 \) bez obzira na znak, pa je \( U_{ef} = U_m/\sqrt{2} \). Zato je \( \gamma = \sqrt{\dfrac{\pi^2}{8} - 1} = 0{,}48 \), a \( \eta = U_{dc}^2/U_{ef}^2 = \dfrac{8}{\pi^2} \).

The square of a sine has the same mean \( U_m^2/2 \) regardless of the sign, so \( U_{rms} = U_m/\sqrt{2} \). That is why \( \gamma = \sqrt{\dfrac{\pi^2}{8} - 1} = 0.48 \), and \( \eta = U_{dc}^2/U_{rms}^2 = \dfrac{8}{\pi^2} \).

U koloni „srednji izvod” \( U_m \) je vrh napona jedne polovine sekundara. Zato je inverzni napon \( 2U_m \): zakočena dioda vidi napon cele sekundarne namotaje. Most nema tu manu, ali ima dva pada napona umesto jednog, što je važno kada su naponi mali.

In the "centre tap" column \( U_m \) is the peak voltage of one half of the secondary. That is why the reverse voltage is \( 2U_m \): a blocked diode sees the voltage of the whole secondary winding. The bridge does not have this flaw, but it has two voltage drops instead of one, which matters when the voltages are small.

Deo 3Part 3 · filter i stabilizatorfilter and regulator

Most, kondenzator i stabilizatorBridge, capacitor and regulator

Kompletno napajanje je lanac: transformator → most → kondenzator → stabilizator. Račun za kondenzator je isti kao kod poluperiodnog, samo je učestanost talasanja 2f = 100 Hz:

A complete power supply is a chain: transformer → bridge → capacitor → regulator. The calculation for the capacitor is the same as for a half-wave rectifier, only the ripple frequency is 2f = 100 Hz:

\[ \Delta u \approx \frac{I_{dc}}{2f\,C} \qquad C \ge \frac{I_{dc}}{2f\,\Delta u} \qquad U_{dc} \approx U_m - 2U_\gamma - \frac{\Delta u}{2} \]
\[ U_{in,min} = U_m - 2U_\gamma - \Delta u \qquad P_{reg} = (U_{dc} - U_{out})\,I_{dc} \]

Izbor elemenataChoosing the parts

  1. Vrh: \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \), pa najveći napon posle mosta \( U_m - 2U_\gamma \).
  2. Kondenzator: \( C \ge I_{dc}/(2f\,\Delta u) \), zaokruži nagore na standardnu vrednost. Radni napon bar \( 1{,}5\,U_m \).
  3. Diode: inverzni napon \( U_{RRM} \ge U_m \) (uzmi bar \( 1{,}5U_m \) za rezervu), a struja \( I_{F(AV)} \) bar \( I_{dc} \): svaka dioda nosi samo \( I_{dc}/2 \), ali u kratkim jakim impulsima.
  4. Stabilizator: ulaz mora ostati iznad \( U_{out} \) plus pad na stabilizatoru. Za 7805 je to oko 7 V, pa je uslov \( U_{in,min} \ge 7\ \mathrm{V} \). Ulaz ne sme da pređe 35 V.
  1. Peak: \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \), so the largest voltage after the bridge is \( U_m - 2U_\gamma \).
  2. Capacitor: \( C \ge I_{dc}/(2f\,\Delta u) \), round up to a standard value. A voltage rating of at least \( 1.5\,U_m \).
  3. Diodes: reverse voltage \( U_{RRM} \ge U_m \) (take at least \( 1.5U_m \) as a margin), and the current \( I_{F(AV)} \) at least \( I_{dc} \): every diode carries only \( I_{dc}/2 \), but in short strong pulses.
  4. Regulator: the input must stay above \( U_{out} \) plus the drop across the regulator. For a 7805 it is about 7 V, so the condition is \( U_{in,min} \ge 7\ \mathrm{V} \). The input must not exceed 35 V.

Stabilizator se grejeThe regulator gets hot

Linearni stabilizator „skida” višak napona kao toplotu: \( P = (U_{dc} - U_{out})\,I \). Što je ulaz viši od potrebnog, to se više greje. Preko oko 1 W treba mu hladnjak. Zato se ulaz bira tek malo iznad praga, a ne „što veće”.

A linear regulator "burns off" the surplus voltage as heat: \( P = (U_{dc} - U_{out})\,I \). The higher the input is above what is needed, the hotter it gets. Above about 1 W it needs a heatsink. That is why the input is chosen only a little above the threshold, not "as high as possible".

Pomisli prvo: kako se promeni talasanje ako isti kondenzator pređe sa poluperiodnog usmerivača na most?Think first: how does the ripple change if the same capacitor moves from a half-wave rectifier to a bridge?

Prepolovi se. Kondenzator se dopunjava na svakih 10 ms umesto na svakih 20 ms, pa ima upola manje vremena da se isprazni: \( \Delta u = I/(2fC) \) umesto \( I/(fC) \).

It halves. The capacitor is topped up every 10 ms instead of every 20 ms, so it has half the time to discharge: \( \Delta u = I/(2fC) \) instead of \( I/(fC) \).

Priključci mosnog modulaThe terminals of a bridge module

Modul ima četiri nožice: dve označene sa ~ (naizmenični ulaz, bez polariteta) i dve označene sa + i − (jednosmerni izlaz). Ako se izvor priključi na + i −, nastaje kratak spoj preko dioda i modul se uništava. Isto radi i most od četiri odvojene diode (npr. 1N4007).

The module has four pins: two marked ~ (the alternating input, without polarity) and two marked + and − (the direct output). If the source is connected to + and −, a short circuit forms across the diodes and the module is destroyed. A bridge of four separate diodes (e.g. 1N4007) does the same job.

Stabilizator sa fiksnim izlazom (7805, 7812) ima tri nožice: ulaz, masa i izlaz. Uz njega se obično stavljaju mali kondenzatori (0,1 μF do 0,33 μF) bliže nožicama, koji sprečavaju oscilovanje.

A fixed-output regulator (7805, 7812) has three pins: input, ground and output. Small capacitors (0.1 μF to 0.33 μF) are usually placed near its pins to prevent oscillation.

Primer 5 · transformator je premaliExample 5 · the transformer is too small

Dato: isto napajanje kao u primeru 4, ali sa \( U_2 = 6\ \mathrm{V} \). Šta se dešava i može li se popraviti većim kondenzatorom?

Given: the same supply as in example 4, but with \( U_2 = 6\ \mathrm{V} \). What happens, and can it be fixed with a larger capacitor?

  1. \( U_m = 6\sqrt{2} = 8{,}49\ \mathrm{V} \), vrh posle mosta \( 8{,}49 - 1{,}4 = 7{,}09\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Isto talasanje od 1,36 V daje \( U_{in,min} = 7{,}09 - 1{,}36 = 5{,}7\ \mathrm{V} < 7\ \mathrm{V} \). Na dnu testere stabilizator ispada iz regulacije i izlaz pada na oko \( 5{,}7 - 2 = 3{,}7\ \mathrm{V} \).
  3. Da bi dno bilo na 7 V, talasanje bi smelo da bude samo \( 7{,}09 - 7 = 0{,}09\ \mathrm{V} \), to jest \( C \ge \dfrac{0{,}3}{100\cdot 0{,}09} \approx 33\,000\ \mu\mathrm{F} \). To nije razumno.
  1. \( U_m = 6\sqrt{2} = 8.49\ \mathrm{V} \), the peak after the bridge \( 8.49 - 1.4 = 7.09\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. The same ripple of 1.36 V gives \( U_{in,min} = 7.09 - 1.36 = 5.7\ \mathrm{V} < 7\ \mathrm{V} \). At the bottom of the sawtooth the regulator drops out of regulation and the output falls to about \( 5.7 - 2 = 3.7\ \mathrm{V} \).
  3. For the bottom to be at 7 V, the ripple could only be \( 7.09 - 7 = 0.09\ \mathrm{V} \), that is \( C \ge \dfrac{0.3}{100\cdot 0.09} \approx 33\,000\ \mu\mathrm{F} \). That is not reasonable.

Primer 6 · merenje osciloskopom na izlazu mostaExample 6 · measuring the bridge output with an oscilloscope

Dato: izlaz filtriranog mosta sa \( C = 2200\ \mu\mathrm{F} \). DC sprega, 5 V/div: vrh je 3,1 podeoka iznad nule. AC sprega, 0,2 V/div, 5 ms/div: talasanje je 4 podeoka vrh–vrh, a jedna perioda 2 podeoka. Traže se \( \Delta u \), f, približno \( U_{dc} \) i struja opterećenja.

  1. Vrh: \( 3{,}1\cdot 5 = 15{,}5\ \mathrm{V} \). Talasanje: \( \Delta u = 4\cdot 0{,}2 = 0{,}8\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. \( T = 2\cdot 5 = 10\ \mathrm{ms} \), pa je \( f = 100\ \mathrm{Hz} \): to je punotalasni usmerivač (most ili srednji izvod).
  3. \( U_{dc} \approx 15{,}5 - \dfrac{0{,}8}{2} = 15{,}1\ \mathrm{V} \).
  4. Iz \( \Delta u = I/(2fC) \) sledi \( I = \Delta u\cdot 2fC = 0{,}8\cdot 100\cdot 2{,}2\cdot 10^{-3} = 0{,}176\ \mathrm{A} \).

  4. From \( \Delta u = I/(2fC) \) it follows \( I = \Delta u\cdot 2fC = 0.8\cdot 100\cdot 2.2\cdot 10^{-3} = 0.176\ \mathrm{A} \).

Primer 7 · kvar: talasanje od 50 HzExample 7 · a fault: a ripple of 50 Hz

Dato: isto napajanje kao u primeru 6 posle nekog vremena pokazuje talasanje od 1,6 V sa periodom 20 ms, a srednji napon je pao na 14,7 V. Šta se desilo?

Given: the same supply as in example 6 shows after some time a ripple of 1.6 V with a period of 20 ms, and the average voltage has fallen to 14.7 V. What happened?

  1. Period 20 ms znači \( f = 50\ \mathrm{Hz} \), a ne 100 Hz. Obe poluperiode se više ne koriste.
  2. Talasanje se udvostručilo, \( 0{,}8\ \mathrm{V} \to 1{,}6\ \mathrm{V} \), kao što i treba kada kondenzator dobija dopunu upola ređe.
  3. Zaključak: u mostu je jedna dioda prekinuta (otvorena). Most radi kao poluperiodni usmerivač.
  1. A period of 20 ms means \( f = 50\ \mathrm{Hz} \), not 100 Hz. Both half-cycles are no longer used.
  2. The ripple has doubled, \( 0.8\ \mathrm{V} \to 1.6\ \mathrm{V} \), exactly as expected when the capacitor is topped up half as often.
  3. Conclusion: one diode in the bridge is open. The bridge works as a half-wave rectifier.

Deo 5Part 5 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Izvor na + i − mosta. Naizmenični ulaz ide na priključke ~, a opterećenje na + i −. Obrnuto je kratak spoj kroz diode.
  • Zaboravljena dva pada napona. U mostu su uvek dve diode u seriji, pa je vrh izlaza \( U_m - 1{,}4\ \mathrm{V} \), a ne \( U_m - 0{,}7\ \mathrm{V} \).
  • PIV jednak \( 2U_m \) kod mosta. Kod mosta je \( \mathrm{PIV} = U_m \). Dvostruko veći je kod usmerivača sa srednjim izvodom.
  • Učestanost 50 Hz u formuli. Kod punotalasnog usmerivača je talasanje na 100 Hz: \( \Delta u = I/(2fC) \). Ako uzmeš f = 50 Hz, kondenzator ispadne dvaput veći nego što treba.
  • Srednja struja diode. Svaka dioda mosta nosi \( I_{dc}/2 \) u proseku, ali u kratkim jakim impulsima sa filtrom. Izaberi diodu sa velikom rezervom.
  • Obrnut elektrolitski kondenzator. Ima plus i minus. Pogrešan polaritet ga naduva i može da ga raznese.
  • Efektivna vrednost umesto vrha. Sve se dimenzioniše prema \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \).
  • Preniski ulaz stabilizatora. Računaj dno testere: \( U_m - 2U_\gamma - \Delta u \) mora ostati iznad 7 V (za 7805).
  • Previsok ulaz stabilizatora. Višak napona se pretvara u toplotu: \( P = (U_{dc} - U_{out})\,I \). Dodaj hladnjak ili smanji napon sekundara.
  • Masa osciloskopa na izlazu mosta. Masa sonde je spojena sa uzemljenjem. Ako je kolo već uzemljeno na drugom mestu (npr. minus izlaza), masa na naizmeničnom priključku mosta kratko spaja jednu diodu. Masu sonde stavi na minus izlaza, a ulaz mosta ne meri običnom sondom.
  • Mreža bez transformatora. Most priključen direktno na 230 V nije odvojen od mreže i opasan je po život. Eksperimentiši samo na malom naponu iza transformatora.
  • The source on + and − of the bridge. The alternating input goes to the ~ terminals and the load to + and −. The other way round is a short circuit through the diodes.
  • Forgetting two voltage drops. In a bridge there are always two diodes in series, so the output peak is \( U_m - 1.4\ \mathrm{V} \), not \( U_m - 0.7\ \mathrm{V} \).
  • PIV equal to \( 2U_m \) for a bridge. For a bridge \( \mathrm{PIV} = U_m \). The double value belongs to the centre-tap rectifier.
  • 50 Hz in the formula. In a full-wave rectifier the ripple is at 100 Hz: \( \Delta u = I/(2fC) \). If you take f = 50 Hz, the capacitor comes out twice as large as needed.
  • The mean current of a diode. Each bridge diode carries \( I_{dc}/2 \) on average, but in short strong pulses with a filter. Choose a diode with a large margin.
  • A reversed electrolytic capacitor. It has a plus and a minus. The wrong polarity bloats it and it can burst.
  • RMS instead of the peak. Everything is dimensioned from \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \).
  • Too low a regulator input. Calculate the bottom of the sawtooth: \( U_m - 2U_\gamma - \Delta u \) must stay above 7 V (for a 7805).
  • Too high a regulator input. The surplus voltage turns into heat: \( P = (U_{dc} - U_{out})\,I \). Add a heatsink or reduce the secondary voltage.
  • The oscilloscope ground on the bridge output. The probe ground is connected to the earth. If the circuit is already earthed somewhere else (e.g. the minus of the output), a ground clip on an alternating terminal of the bridge shorts one diode. Put the probe ground on the minus of the output, and do not measure the bridge input with an ordinary probe.
  • Mains without a transformer. A bridge connected directly to 230 V is not isolated from the mains and is dangerous to life. Experiment only at low voltage behind a transformer.

Deo 6Part 6

SažetakSummary

  • Grecov (mosni) usmerivač ima četiri diode. Kada je gornji kraj izvora +, vode D1 i D4, a kada je donji +, vode D2 i D3. Struja kroz opterećenje uvek ima isti smer.
  • Izlaz: \( U_{dc} = 2U_m/\pi \approx 0{,}637\,U_m \), \( U_{ef} = U_m/\sqrt{2} \), učestanost talasanja \( 2f = 100\ \mathrm{Hz} \), \( \gamma = 0{,}48 \), \( \eta = 81{,}1\ \% \), \( \mathrm{PIV} = U_m \).
  • U svakom trenutku vode dve diode u seriji, pa je pad \( 2U_\gamma \approx 1{,}4\ \mathrm{V} \). Svaka dioda vodi pola vremena, srednja struja je \( I_{dc}/2 \).
  • Usmerivač sa srednjim izvodom ima 2 diode i jedan pad, ali traži transformator sa izvodom i \( \mathrm{PIV} = 2U_m \).
  • Sa kondenzatorom: \( \Delta u \approx I_{dc}/(2fC) \), upola manje nego kod poluperiodnog. \( U_{dc} \approx U_m - 2U_\gamma - \Delta u/2 \).
  • Napajanje je lanac transformator → most → kondenzator → stabilizator. Za 7805 mora biti \( U_{in,min} \ge 7\ \mathrm{V} \), a stabilizator troši \( P = (U_{dc} - 5)\,I \).
  • Talasanje od 50 Hz umesto 100 Hz znači da je jedna dioda u mostu prekinuta.
  • The Graetz (bridge) rectifier has four diodes. When the upper end of the source is +, D1 and D4 conduct, and when the lower one is +, D2 and D3 conduct. The current through the load always has the same direction.
  • Output: \( U_{dc} = 2U_m/\pi \approx 0.637\,U_m \), \( U_{rms} = U_m/\sqrt{2} \), ripple frequency \( 2f = 100\ \mathrm{Hz} \), \( \gamma = 0.48 \), \( \eta = 81.1\ \% \), \( \mathrm{PIV} = U_m \).
  • At every moment two diodes conduct in series, so the drop is \( 2U_\gamma \approx 1.4\ \mathrm{V} \). Each diode conducts half of the time, its mean current is \( I_{dc}/2 \).
  • A centre-tap rectifier has 2 diodes and one drop, but needs a tapped transformer and \( \mathrm{PIV} = 2U_m \).
  • With a capacitor: \( \Delta u \approx I_{dc}/(2fC) \), half of that for a half-wave rectifier. \( U_{dc} \approx U_m - 2U_\gamma - \Delta u/2 \).
  • A power supply is the chain transformer → bridge → capacitor → regulator. For a 7805 it must hold \( U_{in,min} \ge 7\ \mathrm{V} \), and the regulator dissipates \( P = (U_{dc} - 5)\,I \).
  • A ripple of 50 Hz instead of 100 Hz means that one diode in the bridge is open.
