Deo 1Part 1 · Erstedov ogled · pravilo desne rukeØrsted's experiment · right-hand rule

Struja stvara magnetno poljeA current creates a magnetic field

Godine 1820. danski fizičar Hans Kristijan Ersted primetio je da se igla kompasa pomeri kada kroz obližnju žicu pusti struju. Tako je otkriveno da električna struja stvara magnetno polje. Do tada su se elektricitet i magnetizam smatrali potpuno odvojenim pojavama.

In 1820 the Danish physicist Hans Christian Ørsted noticed that a compass needle moved when he switched on a current in a nearby wire. This showed that an electric current creates a magnetic field. Until then electricity and magnetism had been thought to be completely separate phenomena.

Oko pravog provodnika linije polja su koncentrični krugovi, a provodnik je u njihovom centru. Linije su zatvorene: nemaju početak ni kraj. Što smo dalje od provodnika, polje je slabije.

Around a straight wire the field lines are concentric circles with the wire at their centre. The lines are closed: they have no beginning and no end. The farther we are from the wire, the weaker the field.

Pravilo desne rukeRight-hand rule

Obuhvati provodnik desnom šakom tako da ispruženi palac pokazuje smer struje. Savijeni prsti tada pokazuju smer linija polja.

Grip the wire with your right hand so your outstretched thumb points along the current. Your curled fingers then show the direction of the field lines.

Na crtežima provodnik često gledamo „sa kraja”. Tada struju označavamo tačkom ako izlazi iz crteža prema nama (vrh strele), a krstićem ako ulazi u crtež (perje strele):

In drawings we often look at the wire "end-on". Then a dot means the current comes out of the page towards us (the tip of an arrow), and a cross means it goes into the page (the feathers of an arrow):

Pomisli prvo: kompas se nalazi ispod provodnika kroz koji struja teče s leva na desno. Na koju stranu skreće severni kraj igle?Think first: a compass sits under a wire carrying a current from left to right. Which way does the north end of the needle turn?

Palac desne ruke ide udesno. Gledano odozgo, savijeni prsti ispod provodnika idu od tebe, a iznad provodnika ka tebi. Ispod provodnika polje, a time i severni kraj igle, pokazuje u smeru pogleda, od posmatrača.

Your right thumb points to the right. Seen from above, the curled fingers go away from you below the wire and towards you above it. Below the wire the field, and so the north end of the needle, points along your line of sight, away from the viewer.

Deo 2Part 2 · od čega zavisi Bwhat B depends on

Polje opada sa rastojanjemThe field falls with distance

U formuli B = μ0I / (2πr) rastojanje r stoji u imeniocu. To znači dve stvari:

In the formula B = μ0I / (2πr) the distance r is in the denominator. That means two things:

  • Dvostruko veća struja daje dvostruko jače polje (B je srazmerno I).
  • Dvostruko veće rastojanje daje dvostruko slabije polje (B je obrnuto srazmerno r).
  • Twice the current gives twice the field (B is proportional to I).
  • Twice the distance gives half the field (B is inversely proportional to r).

Na grafiku se vidi kriva koja naglo raste kada se približiš provodniku i lagano se spljošti daleko od njega. Tačkasta vodoravna linija je magnetno polje Zemlje, oko 50 μT: već na desetak centimetara od provodnika sa 10 A polje provodnika jedva da je jače od njega.

The graph shows a curve that rises steeply close to the wire and flattens out far away. The dotted horizontal line is Earth's magnetic field, about 50 μT: already about ten centimetres from a wire carrying 10 A, the wire's field is barely stronger than it.

Pazi na jediniceWatch the units

Rastojanje uvek pretvori u metre pre računanja: 2 cm = 0,02 m. Tada je rezultat u teslama. Za male vrednosti piši mT ili μT.

Always convert the distance to metres before calculating: 2 cm = 0.02 m. The result is then in teslas. For small values write mT or μT.

Deo 3Part 3 · princip superpozicijethe superposition principle

Dva provodnika: polja se sabirajuTwo wires: the fields add up

Kada je blizu više provodnika, svaki od njih stvara svoje polje, kao da onih drugih nema. Ukupno polje u nekoj tački je vektorski zbir pojedinačnih polja:

When several wires are nearby, each of them creates its own field as if the others were not there. The total field at a point is the vector sum of the individual fields:

\[ \vec B = \vec B_1 + \vec B_2 \]

Zbir je vektorski, pa smer mora da se uzme u obzir. Ako dva polja u tački imaju isti smer, jačine se sabiraju. Ako imaju suprotan smer, oduzimaju se. Ako su pod pravim uglom, ukupna jačina je \( B=\sqrt{B_1^2+B_2^2} \).

The sum is a vector sum, so direction matters. If the two fields point the same way at a point, their magnitudes add. If they point in opposite ways, they subtract. If they are at a right angle, the total magnitude is \( B=\sqrt{B_1^2+B_2^2} \).

U modelu su provodnici na rastojanju 10 cm. Probaj tri slučaja i povuci tačku P između provodnika i van njih.

In the model the wires are 10 cm apart. Try three cases and drag the point P between the wires and outside them.

Isti smer struja

Same direction

Između provodnika polja se poništavaju (na sredini je B = 0). Van njih se sabiraju.

Between the wires the fields cancel (at the midpoint B = 0). Outside them they add.

Suprotan smer struja

Opposite directions

Između provodnika polja se sabiraju i polje je jako. Van njih se oduzimaju.

Between the wires the fields add and the field is strong. Outside they subtract.

Only one wire

Deo 4Part 4 · prvi korak ka kalemuthe first step towards a coil

Kružni zavojakA circular loop

Ako žicu savijemo u krug poluprečnika R, polja svih delova žice se u centru zavojka sabiraju i usmerena su na istu stranu:

If we bend the wire into a circle of radius R, the fields from every part of the wire add up at the centre of the loop and all point the same way:

\[ B = \frac{\mu_0\, I}{2R} \]

Ako imamo kalem od N tesno namotanih zavojaka istog poluprečnika, svaki doprinosi isto, pa je polje N puta jače:

If we have a coil of N tightly wound turns of the same radius, each one contributes equally, so the field is N times stronger:

\[ B = \frac{\mu_0\, N\, I}{2R} \]

Smer polja kroz zavojakDirection of the field through a loop

Obuhvati zavojak desnom šakom tako da savijeni prsti prate smer struje. Ispruženi palac pokazuje smer polja kroz zavojak.

Wrap your right hand around the loop so your curled fingers follow the current. Your outstretched thumb points along the field through the loop.

Zašto je polje u unutrašnjosti jako? Zamisli zavojak presečen po sredini. Presek izgleda kao dva provodnika sa suprotnim strujama (⊙ i ⊗). Iz Dela 3 znamo da se između provodnika sa suprotnim strujama polja sabiraju.

Why is the field inside strong? Imagine the loop cut through its middle. The cut looks like two wires with opposite currents (⊙ and ⊗). From Part 3 we know that between wires with opposite currents the fields add.

Deo 5Part 5 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · polje pravog provodnikaExample 1 · field of a straight wire

Dato: kroz dugačak prav provodnik teče struja I = 15 A. Traži se B na rastojanju r = 5 cm.

Given: a current I = 15 A flows through a long straight wire. Find B at a distance r = 5 cm.

  1. Rastojanje u metrima: r = 0,05 m.
  2. \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} = \dfrac{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 15}{2\pi\cdot 0{,}05} \)
  3. Skratimo π: \( B = \dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 15}{0{,}05} = 6\cdot 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{T} \)
  1. Distance in metres: r = 0.05 m.
  2. \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} = \dfrac{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 15}{2\pi\cdot 0.05} \)
  3. Cancel π: \( B = \dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 15}{0.05} = 6\cdot 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{T} \)

B = 60 μT, što je malo više od magnetnog polja Zemlje.B = 60 μT, which is a little more than Earth's magnetic field.

Primer 2 · koja struja je potrebnaExample 2 · which current is needed

Dato: B = 1 mT na rastojanju r = 4 cm od provodnika. Traži se I.

Given: B = 1 mT at a distance r = 4 cm from the wire. Find I.

  1. Iz formule izrazimo struju: \( I = \dfrac{2\pi r B}{\mu_0} \).
  2. Jedinice: r = 0,04 m, B = 10−3 T.
  3. \( I = \dfrac{2\pi\cdot 0{,}04\cdot 10^{-3}}{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}} = \dfrac{0{,}04\cdot 10^{-3}}{2\cdot 10^{-7}} = 200\ \mathrm{A} \)
  1. Solve the formula for the current: \( I = \dfrac{2\pi r B}{\mu_0} \).
  2. Units: r = 0.04 m, B = 10−3 T.
  3. \( I = \dfrac{2\pi\cdot 0.04\cdot 10^{-3}}{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}} = \dfrac{0.04\cdot 10^{-3}}{2\cdot 10^{-7}} = 200\ \mathrm{A} \)

I = 200 A. Za jako polje je potrebna velika struja.I = 200 A. A strong field needs a large current.

Primer 3 · dva provodnikaExample 3 · two wires

Dato: dva paralelna provodnika na rastojanju 10 cm, u svakom teče 10 A. Traži se ukupno B na sredini između njih ako su struje (a) istog smera, (b) suprotnog smera.

Given: two parallel wires 10 cm apart, each carrying 10 A. Find the total B at the midpoint between them if the currents are (a) in the same direction, (b) in opposite directions.

  1. Svaki provodnik je na rastojanju r = 0,05 m od sredine.
  2. \( B_1 = B_2 = \dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 10}{0{,}05} = 4\cdot 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{T} = 40\ \mu\mathrm{T} \)
  3. (a) Isti smer: u tački između provodnika polja imaju suprotan smer, pa \( B = B_1 - B_2 = 0 \).
  4. (b) Suprotan smer: polja imaju isti smer, pa \( B = B_1 + B_2 = 80\ \mu\mathrm{T} \).
  1. Each wire is at a distance r = 0.05 m from the midpoint.
  2. \( B_1 = B_2 = \dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 10}{0.05} = 4\cdot 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{T} = 40\ \mu\mathrm{T} \)
  3. (a) Same direction: at a point between the wires the fields point in opposite ways, so \( B = B_1 - B_2 = 0 \).
  4. (b) Opposite directions: the fields point the same way, so \( B = B_1 + B_2 = 80\ \mu\mathrm{T} \).

(a) B = 0, (b) B = 80 μT(a) B = 0, (b) B = 80 μT

Primer 4 · kalem od zavojakaExample 4 · a coil of turns

Dato: kružni kalem poluprečnika R = 5 cm sa N = 100 zavojaka, struja I = 2 A. Traži se B u centru.

Given: a circular coil of radius R = 5 cm with N = 100 turns, current I = 2 A. Find B at the centre.

  1. R = 0,05 m.
  2. \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{2R} = \dfrac{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 100\cdot 2}{2\cdot 0{,}05} \)
  3. \( B = \dfrac{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 200}{0{,}1} = 2{,}51\cdot 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{T} \)
  1. R = 0.05 m.
  2. \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{2R} = \dfrac{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 100\cdot 2}{2\cdot 0.05} \)
  3. \( B = \dfrac{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 200}{0.1} = 2.51\cdot 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{T} \)

B ≈ 2,5 mT, oko 50 puta jače od polja Zemlje.B ≈ 2.5 mT, about 50 times stronger than Earth's field.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Centimetri u formuli. Rastojanje mora u metre. Ako zameniš 5 umesto 0,05, polje ispadne sto puta slabije.
  • Zaboravljeno 2π. U imeniocu je 2πr, a ne samo r. Ako staviš μ0 = 4π·10⁻⁷, jedno π se skrati i ostaje 2·10⁻⁷.
  • 1/r², a ne 1/r. Kod pravog provodnika polje opada kao 1/r, ne kao 1/r². Kvadrat se javlja kod tačkastog naelektrisanja.
  • Smer linija. Linije polja nisu radijalne, nego kružne. Vektor B je uvek tangenta na krug, a ne usmeren ka provodniku ili od njega.
  • Sabiranje intenziteta. Dva polja se sabiraju kao vektori. Prvo odredi smer svakog polja u tački, pa tek onda sabiraj ili oduzimaj jačine.
  • Centimetres in the formula. The distance must be in metres. If you substitute 5 instead of 0.05, the field comes out a hundred times too weak.
  • Forgetting 2π. The denominator is 2πr, not just r. If you put μ0 = 4π·10⁻⁷, one π cancels and 2·10⁻⁷ remains.
  • 1/r², not 1/r. For a straight wire the field falls as 1/r, not 1/r². The square appears for a point charge.
  • Direction of the lines. Field lines are not radial but circular. The vector B is always a tangent to the circle, never pointing towards or away from the wire.
  • Adding magnitudes. Two fields add as vectors. First find the direction of each field at the point, and only then add or subtract the magnitudes.

Deo 7Part 7

SažetakSummary

  • Struja stvara magnetno polje (Erstedov ogled). Linije polja pravog provodnika su koncentrični krugovi, smer daje pravilo desne ruke.
  • Pravi provodnik: \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} \). Polje je srazmerno struji i obrnuto srazmerno rastojanju.
  • Polja više provodnika sabiraju se kao vektori (superpozicija). Isti smer struja: polja se između provodnika poništavaju. Suprotan smer: sabiraju se.
  • Kružni zavojak, centar: \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2R} \), a za N zavojaka \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{2R} \).
  • μ0 = 4π·10⁻⁷ T·m/A. Rastojanja uvek u metrima.
  • A current creates a magnetic field (Ørsted's experiment). The field lines of a straight wire are concentric circles, with the direction from the right-hand rule.
  • Straight wire: \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} \). The field is proportional to the current and inversely proportional to the distance.
  • The fields of several wires add as vectors (superposition). Currents in the same direction: the fields cancel between the wires. Opposite directions: they add.
  • Circular loop, centre: \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2R} \), and for N turns \( B = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{2R} \).
  • μ0 = 4π·10⁻⁷ T·m/A. Distances always in metres.
