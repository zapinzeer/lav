Deo 1Part 1 · ponavljanjea quick recap

Sinusna veličina i njeni parametriA sine quantity and its parameters

Napon gradske mreže menja se po sinusnom zakonu. Trenutna vrednost napona (napon u trenutku t) zapisuje se ovako:

The mains voltage changes according to a sine law. The instantaneous value of the voltage (the voltage at the moment t) is written like this:

\[ u(t) = U_m \sin(\omega t + \varphi) \]

Argument sinusa, ωt + φ, zove se faza, a meri se u radijanima ili stepenima. Faza stalno raste jer vreme teče, pa sinus ide „gore-dole”. Za 50 Hz je ω = 2π · 50 ≈ 314 rad/s.

The argument of the sine, ωt + φ, is called the phase, and it is measured in radians or degrees. The phase keeps growing because time flows, so the sine goes "up and down". For 50 Hz we have ω = 2π · 50 ≈ 314 rad/s.

Kada u kolu ima više napona i struja, svi imaju istu učestanost ω, a razlikuju se samo po amplitudi i početnoj fazi. Sabirati sinuse pomoću trigonometrije je naporno. Zato postoji lakši način: fazor.

When a circuit contains several voltages and currents, they all have the same frequency ω and differ only in amplitude and initial phase. Adding sines with trigonometry is laborious. That is why there is an easier way: the phasor.

Pomisli prvo: ako sve veličine u kolu imaju isto ω, koja dva podatka o svakoj od njih još treba da pamtimo?Think first: if all quantities in a circuit have the same ω, which two pieces of information about each of them do we still need to remember?

Amplitudu Um (ili efektivnu vrednost) i početnu fazu φ. Fazor je baš način da se ta dva broja nacrtaju kao jedna strelica.

The amplitude Um (or the RMS value) and the initial phase φ. A phasor is exactly a way to draw those two numbers as one arrow.

Deo 2Part 2 · obrtni vektora rotating vector

Šta je fazor?What is a phasor?

Zamisli strelicu dužine Um koja se obrće oko početka koordinatnog sistema ugaonom brzinom ω, suprotno kazaljci na satu. Ugao strelice prema vodoravnoj osi u trenutku t je baš faza ωt + φ.

Imagine an arrow of length Um turning around the origin with angular speed ω, counter-clockwise. The angle of the arrow to the horizontal axis at the moment t is exactly the phase ωt + φ.

Njen vertikalni „senka” (projekcija na vertikalnu osu) iznosi Um sin(ωt + φ), a to je tačno trenutni napon u(t). Obrtna strelica je dakle isto što i sinusna funkcija, samo nacrtana drugačije. Takva strelica zove se fazor (obrtni vektor).

Its vertical "shadow" (the projection on the vertical axis) equals Um sin(ωt + φ), and that is exactly the instantaneous voltage u(t). A rotating arrow is therefore the same thing as a sine function, only drawn differently. Such an arrow is called a phasor (a rotating vector).

Pošto se u kolu sve strelice obrću istom brzinom, njihov međusobni položaj se ne menja. Zato fazore crtamo „zamrznute” u trenutku t = 0. Tada svaka strelica ima samo dva podatka:

Since in a circuit all the arrows turn at the same speed, their positions relative to each other do not change. That is why we draw phasors "frozen" at the moment t = 0. Each arrow then carries just two pieces of information:

dužina = Um

Dužina strelice je amplituda. U praksi se često crta efektivna vrednost U = Um/√2, jer se ona meri voltmetrom.

The length of the arrow is the amplitude. In practice the RMS value U = Um/√2 is often drawn instead, because that is what a voltmeter measures.

ugao = φ

Ugao prema vodoravnoj osi je početna faza. Pozitivan ugao meri se suprotno kazaljci na satu, negativan u smeru kazaljke.

The angle to the horizontal axis is the initial phase. A positive angle is measured counter-clockwise, a negative one clockwise.

Dogovor o crtanjuDrawing convention

Vodoravna osa je referentna (0°). Jednom veličinom u kolu se obično izabere da ima fazu 0°, a sve ostale se crtaju u odnosu na nju.

The horizontal axis is the reference (0°). One quantity in the circuit is usually chosen to have phase 0°, and all the others are drawn relative to it.

Deo 3Part 3 · ko prednjačiwho leads

Fazna razlika dva fazoraThe phase difference of two phasors

Napon u i struja i u istom kolu mogu imati različite početne faze. Razlika faza je fazni stav ili fazna razlika:

The voltage u and the current i in the same circuit can have different initial phases. The difference of the phases is the phase shift or phase difference:

\[ \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i \]
φ
fazna razlika napona i strujephase difference between voltage and current
°

φ > 0

Fazor napona je ispred fazora struje (suprotno kazaljci). Kažemo da napon prednjači struji, a struja kasni za naponom.

The voltage phasor is ahead of the current phasor (counter-clockwise). We say the voltage leads the current, and the current lags the voltage.

φ < 0

Fazor struje je ispred fazora napona. Struja prednjači naponu.

The current phasor is ahead of the voltage phasor. The current leads the voltage.

φ = 0

Napon i struja su u fazi: istovremeno dostižu maksimum i istovremeno prolaze kroz nulu.

Voltage and current are in phase: they reach their maximum together and cross zero together.

φ = ±90°

Kažemo da su u kvadraturi. Takav je slučaj kod idealnog kalema i idealnog kondenzatora.

We say they are in quadrature. This is the case for an ideal coil and an ideal capacitor.

Fazna razlika se može pretvoriti u vreme. Pošto jedna perioda T odgovara uglu 360°, vremenski pomak je:

A phase difference can be turned into time. Since one period T corresponds to the angle 360°, the time shift is:

\[ \Delta t = \frac{\varphi}{360^\circ}\,T = \frac{\varphi}{\omega} \]

Deo 4Part 4 · zašto nam trebajuwhy we need them

Sabiranje pomoću fazoraAdding with phasors

Zamisli da dva izvora vezana na red daju napone u1 = Um1 sin(ωt + φ1) i u2 = Um2 sin(ωt + φ2). Ukupan napon je u = u1 + u2.

Imagine two sources in series giving the voltages u1 = Um1 sin(ωt + φ1) and u2 = Um2 sin(ωt + φ2). The total voltage is u = u1 + u2.

Zbir je opet sinus iste učestanosti, a njegov fazor je vektorski zbir fazora u1 i u2. Crta se pravilom paralelograma ili „glava na rep”. Probaj u modelu iznad (odeljak „Šta je fazor?”): uključi drugi napon i menjaj uglove.

The sum is again a sine of the same frequency, and its phasor is the vector sum of the phasors u1 and u2. It is drawn with the parallelogram rule or "head to tail". Try it in the model above (the section "What is a phasor?"): switch on the second voltage and change the angles.

Pomisli prvo: koliki je zbir dva napona iste amplitude Um koji su u kontrafazi (φ razlika 180°)?Think first: what is the sum of two voltages of equal amplitude Um that are in antiphase (a 180° difference)?

Nula. Strelice su iste dužine, a okrenute na suprotne strane, pa se potiru u svakom trenutku.

Zero. The arrows have equal length and point in opposite directions, so they cancel at every moment.

Zbir se računa preko komponenata duž dve ose (tako se ne mora pamtiti nikakva formula za zbir sinusa):

The sum is calculated through components along the two axes (so no formula for the sum of sines needs to be memorised):

\[ U_{m}\cos\varphi = U_{m1}\cos\varphi_1 + U_{m2}\cos\varphi_2 \qquad U_{m}\sin\varphi = U_{m1}\sin\varphi_1 + U_{m2}\sin\varphi_2 \]
\[ U_m = \sqrt{U_{m1}^2 + U_{m2}^2 + 2U_{m1}U_{m2}\cos(\varphi_1-\varphi_2)} \]

Provera zdravim razumom: ako su sabirci u fazi (razlika 0°), amplitude se prosto saberu. Ako su u kontrafazi (180°), oduzimaju se. Ako su u kvadraturi (90°), važi Pitagorina teorema, Um = √(Um1² + Um2²).

Sanity check: if the terms are in phase (a 0° difference), the amplitudes simply add. If they are in antiphase (180°), they subtract. If they are in quadrature (90°), the Pythagorean theorem applies, Um = √(Um1² + Um2²).

Deo 5Part 5 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · kvadraturaExample 1 · quadrature

Dato: u1 = 30 sin ωt V i u2 = 40 sin(ωt + 90°) V. Traži se zbir u = u1 + u2.

Given: u1 = 30 sin ωt V and u2 = 40 sin(ωt + 90°) V. Find the sum u = u1 + u2.

  1. Fazori su pod pravim uglom (razlika 90°), pa je amplituda: \( U_m = \sqrt{30^2 + 40^2} = 50\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Ugao: \( \tan\varphi = \dfrac{40}{30} \Rightarrow \varphi = 53{,}1^\circ \) (fazor u2 je „gore”, pa je φ pozitivno).
  3. Zbir je opet sinus iste učestanosti.
  1. The phasors are at a right angle (a 90° difference), so the amplitude is \( U_m = \sqrt{30^2 + 40^2} = 50\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Angle: \( \tan\varphi = \dfrac{40}{30} \Rightarrow \varphi = 53.1^\circ \) (the phasor u2 points "up", so φ is positive).
  3. The sum is again a sine of the same frequency.

u = 50 sin(ωt + 53,1°) Vu = 50 sin(ωt + 53.1°) V

Primer 2 · komponenteExample 2 · components

Dato: u1 = 10 sin(ωt + 30°) V i u2 = 10 sin(ωt − 90°) V. Traži se zbir.

Given: u1 = 10 sin(ωt + 30°) V and u2 = 10 sin(ωt − 90°) V. Find the sum.

  1. Vodoravne komponente: \( 10\cos 30^\circ + 10\cos(-90^\circ) = 8{,}66 + 0 = 8{,}66\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Vertikalne komponente: \( 10\sin 30^\circ + 10\sin(-90^\circ) = 5 - 10 = -5\ \mathrm{V} \).
  3. Amplituda: \( \sqrt{8{,}66^2 + 5^2} = 10\ \mathrm{V} \). Ugao: \( \varphi = \arctan\dfrac{-5}{8{,}66} = -30^\circ \).
  1. Horizontal components: \( 10\cos 30^\circ + 10\cos(-90^\circ) = 8.66 + 0 = 8.66\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Vertical components: \( 10\sin 30^\circ + 10\sin(-90^\circ) = 5 - 10 = -5\ \mathrm{V} \).
  3. Amplitude: \( \sqrt{8.66^2 + 5^2} = 10\ \mathrm{V} \). Angle: \( \varphi = \arctan\dfrac{-5}{8.66} = -30^\circ \).

u = 10 sin(ωt − 30°) Vu = 10 sin(ωt − 30°) V

Primer 3 · ko prednjači i za koliko vremenaExample 3 · who leads and by how much time

Dato: u = 325 sin(314t + 60°) V i i = 5 sin(314t − 30°) A. Traži se fazna razlika i vremenski pomak.

Given: u = 325 sin(314t + 60°) V and i = 5 sin(314t − 30°) A. Find the phase difference and the time shift.

  1. Fazna razlika: \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i = 60^\circ - (-30^\circ) = 90^\circ \). Pozitivna je, pa napon prednjači struji.
  2. Učestanost: \( f = \omega/2\pi = 314/6{,}28 = 50\ \mathrm{Hz} \), pa je \( T = 20\ \mathrm{ms} \).
  3. Vremenski pomak: \( \Delta t = \dfrac{90^\circ}{360^\circ}\cdot 20\ \mathrm{ms} = 5\ \mathrm{ms} \).
  1. Phase difference: \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i = 60^\circ - (-30^\circ) = 90^\circ \). It is positive, so the voltage leads the current.
  2. Frequency: \( f = \omega/2\pi = 314/6.28 = 50\ \mathrm{Hz} \), so \( T = 20\ \mathrm{ms} \).
  3. Time shift: \( \Delta t = \dfrac{90^\circ}{360^\circ}\cdot 20\ \mathrm{ms} = 5\ \mathrm{ms} \).

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Mešanje amplitude i efektivne vrednosti. Fazor može imati dužinu Um ili U = Um/√2. Izaberi jedno i drži se toga u celom zadatku.
  • Sabiranje amplituda “napamet”. Amplitude se sabiraju samo kada su sabirci u fazi. Inače se sabiraju vektori (fazori), a ne brojevi.
  • Pogrešan smer ugla. Pozitivna faza je ugao suprotno kazaljci na satu. Napon sa φ = −30° kasni, ne prednjači.
  • Različite učestanosti. Fazori se mogu sabirati samo ako imaju isto ω. Strelice različitih brzina menjaju međusobni ugao u vremenu i zbir nije sinusoida.
  • Mixing amplitude and RMS. A phasor can have length Um or U = Um/√2. Choose one and stick to it throughout the problem.
  • Adding amplitudes "by heart". Amplitudes add only when the terms are in phase. Otherwise vectors (phasors) are added, not numbers.
  • The wrong direction of the angle. A positive phase is an angle counter-clockwise. A voltage with φ = −30° lags, it does not lead.
  • Different frequencies. Phasors can be added only if they have the same ω. Arrows with different speeds change their mutual angle with time and the sum is not a sine.

Deo 7Part 7

SažetakSummary

  • Fazor je obrtna strelica dužine Um (ili U) i ugla ωt + φ. Njena projekcija na vertikalnu osu je sinusna veličina.
  • Pošto se svi fazori u kolu obrću istom brzinom, crtamo ih zamrznute u t = 0: potrebna su samo dva podatka, dužina i ugao.
  • Fazna razlika \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i \): pozitivna znači da napon prednjači struji. Vremenski pomak je \( \Delta t = \varphi T/360^\circ \).
  • Zbir sinusa iste učestanosti je fazor koji je vektorski zbir fazora sabiraka. Računa se preko komponenata ili formulom sa kosinusom razlike uglova.
  • A phasor is a rotating arrow of length Um (or U) and angle ωt + φ. Its projection on the vertical axis is the sine quantity.
  • Since all phasors in a circuit turn at the same speed, we draw them frozen at t = 0: only two pieces of information are needed, the length and the angle.
  • Phase difference \( \varphi = \varphi_u - \varphi_i \): positive means the voltage leads the current. The time shift is \( \Delta t = \varphi T/360^\circ \).
  • The sum of sines of the same frequency is a phasor that is the vector sum of the phasors of the terms. It is computed through components or with the cosine of the angle difference.
