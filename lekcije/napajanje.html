Deo 1Part 1 · zašto napajanjewhy a power supply

Čemu služi napajanje?What is a power supply for?

Iz zidne utičnice dolazi struja koju delovi računara ne mogu direktno da koriste. Napajanje (engl. PSU, power supply unit) je kutija u kućištu koja tu struju pretvara u oblik koji odgovara komponentama i razvodi je kablovima do matične ploče, procesora, grafičke kartice i diskova. Bez napajanja računar je samo hrpa delova koja ne radi.

The wall outlet delivers power that the parts of a computer cannot use directly. The power supply (PSU) is a box in the case that converts that power into a form the components can use and distributes it through cables to the motherboard, processor, graphics card and drives. Without it a computer is just a heap of parts that does not work.

Napajanje nema „inteligenciju”: ono samo daje ono što komponente traže. Zato je bitno da bude dovoljno jako, da ima prave konektore i da bude kvalitetno, jer njegov kvar može da povuče i druge delove.

A power supply has no "intelligence": it simply gives what the components ask for. That is why it matters that it is strong enough, has the right connectors and is good quality, because its failure can take other parts with it.

Pomisli prvo: koji delovi računara su vezani direktno na napajanje?Think first: which parts of a computer connect directly to the power supply?

Matična ploča (24-pin), procesor (EPS), grafička kartica (PCIe kabl), diskovi (SATA) i neki ventilatori. Ostali delovi dobijaju struju preko ploče.

The motherboard (24-pin), the processor (EPS), the graphics card (PCIe cable), drives (SATA) and some fans. Other parts get their power through the board.

Deo 2Part 2 · obiđi napajanje u 3Dtour the PSU in 3D

Delovi napajanja u 3DParts of the power supply in 3D

Napajanje u modelu je standardnog ATX oblika. Iz njega izlazi nekoliko kablova, a svaki je namenjen određenom uređaju. Konektori imaju urez i štipaljku, pa ulaze samo u jednom položaju.

The power supply in the model has the standard ATX shape. Several cables come out of it, each meant for a particular device. The connectors have a key and a latch, so they fit only one way.

Modularno ili ne?Modular or not?

Kod modularnih napajanja se kablovi priključuju po potrebi, pa u kućištu nema nepotrebnih kablova i protok vazduha je bolji. Poluodularna imaju stalno prikačen samo 24-pin i EPS kabl. Nemodularna su jeftinija.

Modular power supplies let you attach cables as needed, so there are no unnecessary cables in the case and airflow is better. Semi-modular ones have only the 24-pin and EPS cables permanently attached. Non-modular ones are cheaper.

Deo 3Part 3 · jačina i kvalitetrating and quality

Jačina napajanja i oznake kvalitetaThe rating and the quality marks

Na napajanju piše broj vati, npr. 650 W. To je najveća jačina koju može da da komponentama. Jači procesor i grafička kartica traže jače napajanje. Najbolje je izabrati napajanje sa rezervom, jer takvo radi tiše i hladnije, a ostaje prostora za kasniju nadogradnju.

A power supply is marked with a number of watts, e.g. 650 W. That is the greatest rating it can give to the components. A stronger processor and graphics card need a stronger supply. It is best to choose a supply with some headroom, because it then runs quieter and cooler, and leaves room for a later upgrade.

Oznaka 80 PLUS (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Titanium) govori koliko je napajanje štedljivo i kvalitetno napravljeno: što je viši nivo, manje se greje i troši manje struje iz mreže. Gold je dobar kompromis cene i kvaliteta.

The 80 PLUS mark (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Titanium) tells how economical and well built the supply is: the higher the level, the less it heats up and the less power it takes from the mains. Gold is a good compromise of price and quality.

Deo 4Part 4 · zaštiteprotections

Zaštite napajanjaPower supply protections

Dobro napajanje ima ugrađene zaštite: ako nešto krene po zlu, samo se isključi i tako spreči da ošteti ostale delove. Najčešće se na kutiji nalaze ovi skraćenici:

A good power supply has built-in protections: if something goes wrong, it simply switches itself off and so prevents damage to the other parts. These abbreviations are most often listed on the box:

Cheap supplies without these protections are a common reason for damaged motherboards and drives. That is why you should not save money on the power supply.

Deo 5Part 5 · kako biratihow to choose

Kako se bira napajanjeHow to choose a power supply

  • Jačina: pogledaj preporuku proizvođača grafičke kartice, pa izaberi napajanje sa rezervom.
  • Konektori: dovoljno PCIe kablova za grafičku (jedan ili dva 8-pinska, ili 12-pinski), EPS za procesor, SATA za diskove.
  • Form-faktor: ATX ili manji SFX, prema kućištu.
  • Oznaka kvaliteta: 80 PLUS Bronze je minimum, Gold je dobar izbor.
  • Modularnost: modularno je lakše za uredno ožičenje.
  • Zaštite i garancija: OVP, OCP, SCP i slično, pouzdan proizvođač i 5 do 10 godina garancije.
  • Rating: look at the recommendation of the graphics card maker, then choose a supply with headroom.
  • Connectors: enough PCIe cables for the graphics card (one or two 8-pin, or a 12-pin), EPS for the processor, SATA for drives.
  • Form factor: ATX or the smaller SFX, depending on the case.
  • Quality mark: 80 PLUS Bronze is the minimum, Gold is a good choice.
  • Modularity: modular makes tidy cabling easier.
  • Protections and warranty: OVP, OCP, SCP and so on, a reliable maker and 5 to 10 years of warranty.

Deo 6Part 6 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · koje napajanje?Example 1 · which power supply?

Dato: kupuješ računar za igre sa jakom grafičkom karticom koja traži dva 8-pinska PCIe kabla. Koje napajanje ima smisla?

Given: you are buying a gaming computer with a powerful graphics card that needs two 8-pin PCIe cables. Which power supply makes sense?

  1. Napajanje mora da ima bar dva odvojena PCIe kabla.
  2. Preporučena jačina je oko 750 W ili više, prema preporuci proizvođača kartice.
  3. 80 PLUS Gold i zaštite (OVP, OCP, SCP) su dobar izbor.
  1. The supply must have at least two separate PCIe cables.
  2. The recommended rating is around 750 W or more, according to the card maker.
  3. 80 PLUS Gold and protections (OVP, OCP, SCP) are a good choice.

Modularno 750 W Gold sa dva PCIe kablaA modular 750 W Gold supply with two PCIe cables

Primer 2 · kuda ide kabl?Example 2 · where does the cable go?

Dato: imaš kabl sa oznakom „CPU”, kabl sa oznakom „PCIe” i kabl sa „ATX”. Kuda ide svaki?

Given: you have a cable marked "CPU", one marked "PCIe" and one marked "ATX". Where does each go?

  1. „ATX” (24-pin) ide u veliki konektor na ploči.
  2. „CPU” (EPS) ide u konektor pored procesora.
  3. „PCIe” ide u grafičku karticu, nikad u ploču.
  1. "ATX" (24-pin) goes into the large connector on the board.
  2. "CPU" (EPS) goes into the connector next to the processor.
  3. "PCIe" goes into the graphics card, never into the board.

ATX → ploča, CPU → pored procesora, PCIe → grafičkaATX → board, CPU → next to the processor, PCIe → graphics card

Primer 3 · ne pali seExample 3 · it does not start

Dato: računar se uopšte ne pali, ventilatori ne rade. Šta proveravaš prvo kod napajanja?

Given: a computer does not start at all, the fans do not turn. What do you check first on the power supply?

  1. Da li je mrežni kabl ukopčan i prekidač na napajanju na „I”.
  2. Da li su 24-pin i EPS konektori ukopčani do kraja.
  3. Ako i dalje ništa, napajanje može da bude neispravno i zameni se probnim.
  1. Whether the mains cable is plugged in and the switch on the supply is at "I".
  2. Whether the 24-pin and EPS connectors are fully plugged in.
  3. If still nothing, the supply may be faulty and should be replaced with a test one.

Mrežni kabl, prekidač, 24-pin i EPSMains cable, switch, 24-pin and EPS

Primer 4 · modularno ili neExample 4 · modular or not

Dato: kućište je malo i uredno ožičenje je važno. Koju vrstu napajanja biraš?

Given: the case is small and tidy cabling matters. Which kind of supply do you choose?

  1. Modularno napajanje priključuje samo kablove koji su potrebni.
  2. Manje kablova znači bolji protok vazduha i lakšu ugradnju.
  3. Nemodularno je jeftinije, ali višak kablova se mora negde sakriti.
  1. A modular supply connects only the cables that are needed.
  2. Fewer cables mean better airflow and an easier installation.
  3. Non-modular is cheaper, but the spare cables have to be hidden somewhere.

Deo 7Part 7 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Štednja na napajanju. Jeftino napajanje bez zaštita može da ošteti ploču i diskove kad se pokvari.
  • Jedan kabl sa dva konektora za jaku karticu. Jaka kartica traži dva odvojena PCIe kabla iz napajanja.
  • Mešanje kablova različitih napajanja. Kablovi modularnih napajanja nisu zamenljivi između proizvođača, čak ni između modela.
  • EPS u PCIe konektor. Izgledaju skoro isto, ali nisu isto. EPS ide samo u ploču, PCIe samo u grafičku karticu.
  • Saving on the power supply. A cheap supply without protections can damage the board and drives when it fails.
  • One cable with two connectors for a powerful card. A powerful card needs two separate PCIe cables from the supply.
  • Mixing cables of different supplies. Cables of modular supplies are not interchangeable between makers, and often not even between models.
  • EPS into a PCIe connector. They look almost the same, but they are not. EPS goes only to the board, PCIe only to the graphics card.

SažetakSummary

  • Napajanje pretvara struju iz utičnice u oblik koji komponente mogu da koriste i razvodi je kablovima.
  • Glavni kablovi: 24-pin (ploča), EPS (procesor), PCIe (grafička), SATA i Molex (diskovi i ostalo).
  • Jačina se bira sa rezervom; oznaka 80 PLUS govori o kvalitetu i štednji.
  • Zaštite (OVP, OCP, SCP...) isključuju napajanje kad nešto nije u redu.
  • Modularno napajanje olakšava uredno ožičenje i protok vazduha.
  • A power supply converts the power from the outlet into a form the components can use and distributes it through cables.
  • Main cables: 24-pin (board), EPS (processor), PCIe (graphics card), SATA and Molex (drives and the rest).
  • The rating is chosen with headroom; the 80 PLUS mark tells about quality and economy.
  • Protections (OVP, OCP, SCP...) switch the supply off when something is wrong.
  • A modular supply makes tidy cabling and good airflow easier.
