Deo 1Part 1 · zašto napajanjewhy a power supply

Zašto računaru treba napajanje?Why does a computer need a power supply?

Iz zidne utičnice dolazi naizmenični napon 230 V, a čipovi u računaru rade sa jednosmernim naponima od 12 V, 5 V i 3,3 V, pa i ispod 1 V. Napajanje (engl. PSU, power supply unit) zato radi tri stvari: ispravlja struju (AC → DC), spušta napon i stabilizuje ga.

The wall socket delivers an alternating voltage of 230 V, but the chips in a computer work with direct voltages of 12 V, 5 V and 3.3 V, and even below 1 V. A power supply (PSU) therefore does three things: it rectifies the current (AC → DC), it steps the voltage down and it regulates it.

Moderna napajanja su impulsna (SMPS, switch-mode power supply). Umesto velikog, teškog transformatora na 50 Hz koriste prekidače (tranzistore) koji radi na desetinama kilohertza. Na tim visokim učestanostima transformator može da bude vrlo mali i lagan, a efikasnost visoka. Lanac je ovakav:

Modern power supplies are switch-mode (SMPS). Instead of a large, heavy 50 Hz transformer they use switches (transistors) running at tens of kilohertz. At such high frequencies the transformer can be very small and light, and the efficiency high. The chain looks like this:

Pomisli prvo: zašto je ovaj način bolji od običnog transformatora od 50 Hz?Think first: why is this approach better than an ordinary 50 Hz transformer?

Veličina transformatora opada sa učestanošću (isti fluks se postiže sa manje jezgra). Zato prekidačko napajanje na 100 kHz ima transformator veličine šake, a ne cigle, i manje gubitaka.

The size of a transformer falls as frequency rises (the same flux is reached with a smaller core). So a switching supply at 100 kHz has a transformer the size of a fist, not a brick, and lower losses.

Modularno ili ne?Modular or not?

Kod modularnih napajanja se kablovi priključuju po potrebi, pa u kućištu nema nepotrebnih kablova i protok vazduha je bolji. Poluodularna imaju stalno prikačen samo 24-pin i EPS kabl. Nemodularna su jeftinija.

Modular power supplies let you attach cables as needed, so there are no unnecessary cables in the case and airflow is better. Semi-modular ones have only the 24-pin and EPS cables permanently attached. Non-modular ones are cheaper.

Deo 3Part 3 · vati i efikasnostwatts and efficiency

Snaga i efikasnostPower and efficiency

Snaga napajanja (npr. 650 W) je izlazna snaga, to jest ono što može da da komponentama. Ono samo troši više iz zida jer deo snage pretvara u toplotu. Odnos izlazne i ulazne snage je efikasnost:

The rating of a power supply (e.g. 650 W) is the output power, that is what it can give to the components. It draws more from the wall because part of the power is turned into heat. The ratio of output to input power is the efficiency:

\[ \eta = \frac{P_{out}}{P_{in}} \qquad P_{loss} = P_{in} - P_{out} = P_{out}\left(\frac{1}{\eta} - 1\right) \]

Kao što smo videli u lekcijama o naizmeničnoj struji, ako struja ne prati napon (kao kod kalema ili kondenzatora) faktor snage je manji od 1. Napajanje sa običnim ispravljačem i velikim kondenzatorom povlači struju u kratkim impulsima, pa je faktor snage loš (~0,6). Aktivni PFC (power factor correction) oblikuje struju da prati napon, pa je faktor snage ~0,99.

As we saw in the lessons on alternating current, when the current does not follow the voltage (as with a coil or a capacitor) the power factor is below 1. A power supply with an ordinary rectifier and a large capacitor draws current in short pulses, so the power factor is poor (~0.6). Active PFC (power factor correction) shapes the current to follow the voltage, giving a power factor of ~0.99.

Deo 5Part 5 · kako biratihow to choose

Kako se bira napajanjeHow to choose a power supply

  • Snaga: saberi potrošnju komponenti (TDP procesora i grafičke, plus oko 75 W za ostalo) i dodaj 30 % rezerve.
  • Grana +12 V: mora da daje dovoljno ampera; skoro sve komponente su na njoj.
  • Efikasnost: Bronze je minimum, Gold je dobar kompromis.
  • Konektori: dovoljno PCIe kablova za grafičku (jedan 8-pinski ili 12VHPWR), EPS za procesor, SATA za diskove.
  • Form-faktor: ATX ili SFX, prema kućištu.
  • Kvalitet i garancija: zaštite (OVP, OCP, OPP, SCP), pouzdan proizvođač, 5 do 10 godina garancije.
  • Power: add up the consumption of the components (CPU and graphics TDP, plus about 75 W for the rest) and add 30 % headroom.
  • The +12 V rail: it must give enough amps; almost all components are on it.
  • Efficiency: Bronze is the minimum, Gold is a good compromise.
  • Connectors: enough PCIe cables for the graphics card (one 8-pin or 12VHPWR), EPS for the CPU, SATA for drives.
  • Form factor: ATX or SFX, depending on the case.
  • Quality and warranty: protections (OVP, OCP, OPP, SCP), a reliable manufacturer, 5 to 10 years of warranty.

Primer 3 · struja grane +12 VExample 3 · the +12 V rail current

Dato: grafička troši 285 W i sva se napaja sa +12 V. Kolika je struja i da li je dovoljno jedan 8-pinski PCIe kabl (150 W) uz slot (75 W)?

Given: a graphics card draws 285 W, all from +12 V. What is the current and is one 8-pin PCIe cable (150 W) plus the slot (75 W) enough?

  1. \( I = P/U = 285/12 = 23{,}75\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. Dostupno: \( 150 + 75 = 225\ \mathrm{W} \), što je manje od 285 W.
  3. Treba dva 8-pinska konektora: \( 2\cdot 150 + 75 = 375\ \mathrm{W} \).
  1. \( I = P/U = 285/12 = 23.75\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. Available: \( 150 + 75 = 225\ \mathrm{W} \), which is less than 285 W.
  3. It needs two 8-pin connectors: \( 2\cdot 150 + 75 = 375\ \mathrm{W} \).

Primer 4 · faktor snageExample 4 · the power factor

Dato: napajanje bez PFC uzima iz mreže 3 A pri 230 V, a stvarna snaga je 400 W. Koliki je faktor snage i koliko bi iznosila struja sa PFC (λ = 0,99)?

Given: a power supply without PFC draws 3 A at 230 V from the mains, and the real power is 400 W. What is the power factor, and what would the current be with PFC (λ = 0.99)?

  1. Prividna snaga: \( S = UI = 230\cdot 3 = 690\ \mathrm{VA} \).
  2. \( \lambda = P/S = 400/690 = 0{,}58 \).
  3. Sa PFC: \( I = \dfrac{P}{U\lambda} = \dfrac{400}{230\cdot 0{,}99} = 1{,}76\ \mathrm{A} \).
  1. Apparent power: \( S = UI = 230\cdot 3 = 690\ \mathrm{VA} \).
  2. \( \lambda = P/S = 400/690 = 0.58 \).
  3. With PFC: \( I = \dfrac{P}{U\lambda} = \dfrac{400}{230\cdot 0.99} = 1.76\ \mathrm{A} \).

Deo 7Part 7 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Pogrešan kabl u pogrešan konektor. PCIe i EPS izgledaju isto, ali se ne zamenjuju. Kablovi različitih proizvođača (modularni) nisu međusobno zamenljivi, jer pinovi na strani napajanja nisu standardizovani.
  • Birati samo prema vatima. Dve „650 W” jedinice mogu biti vrlo različite: proveri ampere na +12 V i zaštite.
  • Otvaranje napajanja. Veliki kondenzatori ostaju nabijeni na ~325 V dugo nakon isključenja. Napajanje ne popravljamo.
  • Mešanje W i VA. UPS ima snagu u VA. Stvarna snaga je VA pomnoženo faktorom snage.
  • The wrong cable in the wrong connector. PCIe and EPS look the same but are not interchangeable. Cables from different manufacturers (modular) are not interchangeable, because the pinout on the power supply side is not standardised.
  • Choosing only by watts. Two "650 W" units can be very different: check the amps on +12 V and the protections.
  • Opening a power supply. The large capacitors stay charged to ~325 V long after switching off. We do not repair power supplies.
  • Mixing up W and VA. A UPS is rated in VA. The real power is the VA multiplied by the power factor.

SažetakSummary

  • Napajanje pretvara mrežni AC 230 V u stabilne DC napone 12 V, 5 V i 3,3 V: ispravlja, spušta i stabilizuje.
  • Moderna napajanja su impulsna (SMPS) sa visokom efikasnošću; ( eta = P_{out}/P_{in} ).
  • Glavni kablovi: 24-pin (ploča), 4+4 EPS (procesor), PCIe 6+2 (grafička), SATA i Molex (diskovi).
  • Boje žica: žuta +12 V, crvena +5 V, narandžasta +3,3 V, crna masa.
  • Biraj prema snazi sa 30 % rezerve, struji grane +12 V, efikasnosti (80 PLUS), konektorima i zaštitama; aktivni PFC daje faktor snage ~0,99.
  • A power supply turns the mains AC 230 V into stable DC voltages of 12 V, 5 V and 3.3 V: it rectifies, steps down and regulates.
  • Modern power supplies are switch-mode (SMPS) with high efficiency; ( eta = P_{out}/P_{in} ).
  • Main cables: 24-pin (board), 4+4 EPS (CPU), PCIe 6+2 (graphics), SATA and Molex (drives).
  • Wire colours: yellow +12 V, red +5 V, orange +3.3 V, black ground.
  • Choose by power with 30 % headroom, the +12 V rail current, efficiency (80 PLUS), connectors and protections; active PFC gives a power factor of ~0.99.
