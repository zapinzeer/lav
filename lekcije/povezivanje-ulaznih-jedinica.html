Deo 1Part 1 · pregledoverview

Kako se ulazne jedinice povezujuHow input devices are connected

Ulazna jedinica mora nekako da preda podatke računaru. To radi preko interfejsa: dogovorenog načina povezivanja koji određuje oblik konektora, brzinu i pravila razgovora između uređaja i računara. Veza može biti:

An input device has to hand its data to the computer somehow. It does so through an interface: an agreed way of connecting that defines the shape of the connector, the speed and the rules of the conversation between the device and the computer. The link can be:

  • žična: USB (danas skoro sve), PS/2 (stare tastature i miševi), 3,5 mm audio priključak (mikrofon), a nekada i serijski COM port i „gameport” za džojstike;
  • bežična: Bluetooth, radio-veza na 2,4 GHz preko malog USB prijemnika (dongle) i infracrvena veza (daljinski upravljači).
  • wired: USB (almost everything today), PS/2 (old keyboards and mice), the 3.5 mm audio jack (microphone), and in the past the serial COM port and the "gameport" for joysticks;
  • wireless: Bluetooth, a 2.4 GHz radio link through a small USB receiver (dongle), and infrared (remote controls).

Deo 2Part 2 · univerzalna serijska magistralauniversal serial bus

USBUSB

USB (Universal Serial Bus) je napravljen da zameni mnoštvo različitih priključaka jednim. Njegove najvažnije osobine:

USB (Universal Serial Bus) was made to replace many different ports with one. Its most important features:

  • Plug and Play: sistem sam prepozna uređaj i učita drajver;
  • hot swap: uređaj se priključuje i isključuje dok računar radi;
  • kroz isti kabl uređaj dobija i napajanje, pa tastatura i miš ne trebaju poseban adapter;
  • tastature, miševi i džojstici pripadaju klasi HID (Human Interface Device), za koju operativni sistem već ima drajver;
  • preko USB habova na jedan kontroler može da se poveže do 127 uređaja.
  • Plug and Play: the system recognises the device by itself and loads the driver;
  • hot swap: a device can be connected and disconnected while the computer is running;
  • the device gets power through the same cable, so a keyboard and a mouse need no separate adapter;
  • keyboards, mice and joysticks belong to the HID (Human Interface Device) class, for which the operating system already has a driver;
  • through USB hubs up to 127 devices can be connected to one controller.

Deo 3Part 3 · stariji standardolder standard

PS/2 priključakThe PS/2 port

PS/2 je okrugli priključak sa 6 pinova (mini-DIN). Po dogovoru je ljubičasti za tastaturu, a zeleni za miš, i nisu zamenljivi. Neke ploče imaju jedan dvobojni PS/2 port koji prima bilo koji od njih.

PS/2 is a round 6-pin (mini-DIN) port. By convention purple is for the keyboard and green for the mouse, and they are not interchangeable. Some boards have one two-colour PS/2 port that takes either.

PS/2 ne podržava hot swap: uređaj se priključuje dok je računar ugašen, inače ga sistem možda neće prepoznati. Ipak, neki ga i danas koriste jer tastatura šalje podatke odmah, preko prekida (interrupt), i radi i u najranijoj fazi pokretanja računara.

PS/2 does not support hot swap: the device is plugged in while the computer is off, otherwise the system may not recognise it. Still, some people use it today because the keyboard sends data right away, through an interrupt, and works even in the earliest stage of start-up.

Pomisli prvo: imaš PS/2 tastaturu, a računar ima samo USB. Šta radiš?Think first: you have a PS/2 keyboard and the computer only has USB. What do you do?

Koristiš aktivni adapter PS/2→USB. Pasivni adapter radi samo sa uređajima koji „znaju” oba protokola, a obična PS/2 tastatura zahteva aktivni (sa čipom).

You use an active PS/2→USB adapter. A passive adapter only works with devices that "know" both protocols, while an ordinary PS/2 keyboard needs an active one (with a chip).

Bežični uređaji rade na baterije ili ugrađeni akumulator, pa kada baterija slabi, kursor počinje da „seče”. Signal mogu da ometaju Wi-Fi ruter, metalne površine i USB 3.0 portovi pored prijemnika, pa je dobro da prijemnik bude blizu uređaja (na primer preko produžnog kabla na prednjem USB-u).

Wireless devices run on batteries or a built-in rechargeable battery, so when it gets weak the cursor starts to "stutter". The signal can be disturbed by a Wi-Fi router, metal surfaces and USB 3.0 ports next to the receiver, so it helps to keep the receiver close to the device (for example on an extension cable in a front USB port).

Bluetooth uparivanje ide ovako: uključi režim uparivanja na uređaju (obično dugme koje se drži 3 sekunde), na računaru otvori Podešavanja → Bluetooth i uređaji → Dodaj uređaj, izaberi ga sa spiska i, ako zatraži, potvrdi PIN.

Bluetooth pairing goes like this: switch on pairing mode on the device (usually a button held for 3 seconds), on the computer open Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device, pick it from the list and, if asked, confirm the PIN.

Deo 5Part 5 · koliko čestohow often

Prozivanje i kašnjenjePolling and delay

Računar USB miš ili tastaturu ne čeka pasivno, nego ga proziva (polling) u stalnim razmacima: „imaš li nešto novo?” Broj prozivanja u sekundi zove se učestanost prozivanja (polling rate). Običan miš se proziva 125 puta u sekundi, odnosno na svakih 8 ms, a miš za igre 1000 puta (svake milisekunde) ili više.

The computer does not wait passively for a USB mouse or keyboard, but polls it at regular intervals: "do you have anything new?" The number of polls per second is called the polling rate. An ordinary mouse is polled 125 times a second, that is every 8 ms, and a gaming mouse 1000 times (every millisecond) or more.

Deo 7Part 7 · podešavanjesetup

Drajveri i provera radaDrivers and checking that it works

Drajver je program koji operativnom sistemu objašnjava kako da razgovara sa uređajem. Za tastature, miševe, kamere i USB mikrofone sistem već ima opšte drajvere, pa rade odmah. Uređaji sa posebnim mogućnostima (dodatni tasteri, osvetljenje, grafičke table, skeneri) traže drajver ili program proizvođača.

A driver is a program that tells the operating system how to talk to a device. For keyboards, mice, cameras and USB microphones the system already has generic drivers, so they work at once. Devices with special features (extra buttons, lighting, graphics tablets, scanners) need the maker's driver or program.

Ako uređaj ne radi, otvori Upravljač uređajima (Device Manager: desni klik na Start). Uređaj sa žutim uzvičnikom ima problem sa drajverom; desni klik → Ažuriraj upravljački program ili Deinstaliraj, pa ga isključi i ponovo priključi.

If a device does not work, open Device Manager (right-click Start). A device with a yellow exclamation mark has a driver problem; right-click → Update driver or Uninstall, then unplug it and plug it in again.

Primer 1 · razmak prozivanjaExample 1 · polling interval

Dato: miš se proziva 500 puta u sekundi. Koliki je razmak između dva prozivanja?

Given: a mouse is polled 500 times a second. What is the interval between two polls?

  1. Jedna sekunda ima 1000 ms.
  2. Razmak: 1000 ms / 500 = 2 ms.
  1. One second has 1000 ms.
  2. Interval: 1000 ms / 500 = 2 ms.

Primer 2 · broj portovaExample 2 · number of ports

Dato: računar ima 4 slobodna USB porta. Treba priključiti tastaturu, miš, kameru, štampač, skener i USB fleš. Šta radiš?

Given: a computer has 4 free USB ports. You need to connect a keyboard, a mouse, a camera, a printer, a scanner and a USB flash drive. What do you do?

  1. Uređaja je 6, a portova 4: fale 2.
  2. Priključi USB hab sa 4 porta u jedan slobodan port.
  3. Sada ima 3 + 4 = 7 portova; sporije uređaje (tastatura, miš) stavi na hab, a kameru i fleš direktno na računar.
  1. There are 6 devices and 4 ports: 2 are missing.
  2. Plug a 4-port USB hub into one free port.
  3. Now there are 3 + 4 = 7 ports; put the slow devices (keyboard, mouse) on the hub, and the camera and flash drive straight into the computer.

Given: you plugged in a PS/2 keyboard while the computer was running and it does not respond.

Primer 4 · bežični miš sečeExample 4 · wireless mouse stutters

Dato: bežični miš sa prijemnikom pozadi na kućištu ponekad zastaje. Šta proveravaš?

Given: a wireless mouse with its receiver at the back of the case sometimes freezes. What do you check?

  1. Bateriju: zameni je ili napuni.
  2. Udaljenost i prepreke: metalno kućište između miša i prijemnika slabi signal.
  3. Premesti prijemnik napred ili ga stavi na produžni kabl, dalje od USB 3.0 portova i rutera.
  1. The battery: replace or charge it.
  2. Distance and obstacles: a metal case between the mouse and the receiver weakens the signal.
  3. Move the receiver to the front or put it on an extension cable, away from USB 3.0 ports and the router.

Deo 9Part 9 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Zamenjeni PS/2 portovi. Tastatura u zelenom ili miš u ljubičastom portu ne radi. Gledaj boje.
  • Mikrofon u zelenom priključku. Zeleni je izlaz za zvučnike. Mikrofon ide u roze.
  • Bluetooth uređaj nije u režimu uparivanja. Računar ga ne vidi na spisku dok se ne uključi uparivanje na samom uređaju.
  • Pasivni hab za jake uređaje. Hab bez svog napajanja ne može da napaja više spoljnih diskova ili kamera. Koristi hab sa adapterom.
  • Swapped PS/2 ports. A keyboard in the green or a mouse in the purple port does not work. Watch the colours.
  • A microphone in the green jack. Green is the speaker output. The microphone goes into pink.
  • A Bluetooth device not in pairing mode. The computer does not see it in the list until pairing is switched on on the device itself.
  • An unpowered hub for hungry devices. A hub without its own power cannot feed several external drives or cameras. Use a hub with an adapter.

SažetakSummary

  • Ulazne jedinice se povezuju žično (USB, PS/2, 3,5 mm audio) ili bežično (Bluetooth, 2,4 GHz, IR).
  • USB je Plug and Play i hot swap, napaja uređaj i ima klasu HID za tastature i miševe.
  • PS/2: ljubičasti za tastaturu, zeleni za miš, bez hot swap-a.
  • Bežični uređaji zavise od baterije i ometanja; Bluetooth traži uparivanje.
  • Računar USB uređaj proziva: 125 Hz = 8 ms, 1000 Hz = 1 ms.
  • Mikrofon u roze priključak; problemi sa drajverom se rešavaju u Upravljaču uređajima.
  • Input devices connect by wire (USB, PS/2, 3.5 mm audio) or wirelessly (Bluetooth, 2.4 GHz, IR).
  • USB is Plug and Play and hot swap, powers the device and has the HID class for keyboards and mice.
  • PS/2: purple for the keyboard, green for the mouse, no hot swap.
  • Wireless devices depend on the battery and interference; Bluetooth needs pairing.
  • The computer polls a USB device: 125 Hz = 8 ms, 1000 Hz = 1 ms.
  • The microphone goes into the pink jack; driver problems are fixed in Device Manager.
