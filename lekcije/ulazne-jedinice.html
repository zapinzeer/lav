Deo 1Part 1 · pojam i podelaidea and kinds

Šta su ulazne jediniceWhat input devices are

Računar radi samo sa brojevima, a mi pritiskamo tastere, pomeramo ruku, pričamo i pokazujemo slike. Ulazne jedinice su uređaji koji te radnje i pojave iz stvarnog sveta pretvaraju u digitalne podatke (nule i jedinice) i šalju ih računaru. Podaci kroz njih idu samo u jednom smeru: od korisnika ka računaru.

A computer works only with numbers, while we press keys, move our hand, talk and show pictures. Input devices are devices that turn those actions and real-world things into digital data (zeros and ones) and send them to the computer. Data goes through them in one direction only: from the user to the computer.

Pomisli prvo: da li je ekran na dodir ulazna ili izlazna jedinica?Think first: is a touch screen an input or an output device?

Oba. Kao ekran prikazuje sliku (izlaz), a sloj osetljiv na dodir javlja računaru gde ga dodiruješ (ulaz). Takve uređaje zovemo ulazno-izlaznim.

Both. As a screen it shows a picture (output), and its touch-sensitive layer tells the computer where you touch it (input). We call such devices input/output devices.

Deo 2Part 2 · unos tekstatext entry

TastaturaThe keyboard

Tastatura je osnovna ulazna jedinica za unos teksta i komandi. Standardna ima 104 do 105 tastera podeljenih u grupe:

The keyboard is the basic input device for entering text and commands. A standard one has 104 to 105 keys divided into groups:

  • alfanumerički deo: slova, brojevi, znaci, razmaknica i Enter;
  • funkcijski tasteri F1–F12, čije značenje zavisi od programa;
  • modifikatori: Shift, Ctrl, Alt, AltGr i Windows taster, koji menjaju značenje drugih tastera;
  • navigacija: strelice, Home, End, Page Up/Down, Insert, Delete;
  • numerički deo desno, za brz unos brojeva (uključuje se tasterom Num Lock).
  • the alphanumeric part: letters, digits, symbols, the space bar and Enter;
  • the function keys F1–F12, whose meaning depends on the program;
  • modifiers: Shift, Ctrl, Alt, AltGr and the Windows key, which change the meaning of other keys;
  • navigation: arrows, Home, End, Page Up/Down, Insert, Delete;
  • the numeric pad on the right, for typing numbers quickly (switched on with Num Lock).

Raspored slova zavisi od jezika. Englesku tastaturu zovemo QWERTY (po prvih šest slova), a srpska latinična je QWERTZ, jer su Y i Z zamenili mesta i dodata su slova Š, Đ, Č, Ć i Ž. Raspored se bira u operativnom sistemu; isti fizički taster može da daje različita slova.

The letter layout depends on the language. The English keyboard is called QWERTY (after its first six letters), while the Serbian Latin one is QWERTZ, because Y and Z swapped places and the letters Š, Đ, Č, Ć and Ž were added. The layout is chosen in the operating system; the same physical key can give different letters.

Ispod tastera nalazi se matrica: mreža redova i kolona. Mali kontroler u tastaturi stalno, hiljadama puta u sekundi, „proziva” red po red i proverava u kojoj koloni je kontakt zatvoren. Kada pronađe pritisnut taster, šalje računaru njegov kod (scan code), a operativni sistem prema rasporedu odlučuje koje je to slovo.

Under the keys there is a matrix: a grid of rows and columns. A small controller in the keyboard constantly, thousands of times a second, "calls" row after row and checks in which column the contact is closed. When it finds a pressed key, it sends the computer its code (scan code), and the operating system decides which letter it is according to the layout.

Deo 3Part 3 · pokazivanjepointing

Miš i drugi pokazivački uređajiThe mouse and other pointing devices

Miš pomera pokazivač (kursor) na ekranu. Savremeni optički miš ima malu kameru i LED (ili laser) koji osvetljava podlogu. Kamera snima nekoliko hiljada sitnih slika podloge u sekundi, a čip u mišu poredi svaku sliku sa prethodnom i izračunava za koliko se slika „pomerila”. To pomeranje šalje računaru.

The mouse moves the pointer (cursor) on the screen. A modern optical mouse has a tiny camera and an LED (or a laser) that lights up the surface. The camera takes several thousand tiny pictures of the surface every second, and the chip in the mouse compares each picture with the previous one and works out how far the picture has "moved". It sends that movement to the computer.

Osetljivost miša se izražava u DPI (tačaka po inču, tačnije CPI): koliko koraka miš prijavi kada ga pomeriš za jedan inč (2,54 cm). Miš od 1600 DPI za pomeraj od jednog inča javlja 1600 koraka. Veći DPI znači da kursor pređe veći put za isti pokret ruke.

Mouse sensitivity is given in DPI (dots per inch, more precisely CPI): how many steps the mouse reports when you move it one inch (2.54 cm). A 1600 DPI mouse reports 1600 steps for one inch of movement. A higher DPI means the cursor travels further for the same hand movement.

  • tačped (touchpad): površina na laptopu koja prati pokret prsta; podržava pokrete sa više prstiju;
  • ekran na dodir: kapacitivni (telefoni) reaguje na prst, a otporni (rezistivni) na pritisak, pa radi i sa olovkom ili rukavicom;
  • grafička tabla: olovka i podloga za crtanje, prepoznaje i jačinu pritiska;
  • džojstik i gejmped: za igre i upravljanje mašinama;
  • trekbol: kuglica koja se okreće prstom, a sam uređaj stoji.
  • touchpad: the surface on a laptop that follows a finger; it supports multi-finger gestures;
  • touch screen: a capacitive one (phones) reacts to a finger, a resistive one to pressure, so it also works with a stylus or a glove;
  • graphics tablet: a pen and a pad for drawing; it also senses how hard you press;
  • joystick and gamepad: for games and controlling machines;
  • trackball: a ball you turn with a finger while the device stays put.

Deo 4Part 4 · slika i zvukimage and sound

Skener, kamera i mikrofonScanner, camera and microphone

Skener pretvara papir u digitalnu sliku. Ispod stakla se kreće traka sa svetlom i nizom senzora (CCD ili CIS) koji meri koliko svetla odbija svaka tačka papira. Rezolucija skenera se daje u DPI: za dokumente je dovoljno 300 DPI, a za fotografije 600 i više. Program za OCR (optičko prepoznavanje znakova) može od skeniranog teksta da napravi tekst koji se menja u Wordu.

A scanner turns paper into a digital image. Under the glass a bar with a light and a row of sensors (CCD or CIS) moves and measures how much light each point of the paper reflects. Scanner resolution is given in DPI: 300 DPI is enough for documents, and 600 or more for photos. An OCR (optical character recognition) program can turn scanned text into text you can edit in Word.

Veb kamera i digitalni foto-aparat imaju senzor slike sa milionima ćelija osetljivih na svetlo. Kamera se opisuje rezolucijom (720p, 1080p, 4K) i brojem slika u sekundi (30 ili 60 FPS).

A webcam and a digital camera have an image sensor with millions of light-sensitive cells. A camera is described by its resolution (720p, 1080p, 4K) and the number of frames per second (30 or 60 FPS).

Mikrofon pretvara zvuk (titranje vazduha) u signal, a zvučna kartica ga uzorkuje: meri ga mnogo puta u sekundi i svako merenje zapisuje kao broj. Muzika na CD-u se uzorkuje 44 100 puta u sekundi (44,1 kHz), a razgovor je razumljiv i pri 16 kHz.

A microphone turns sound (vibrating air) into a signal, and the sound card samples it: measures it many times a second and records each measurement as a number. Music on a CD is sampled 44,100 times a second (44.1 kHz), while speech is clear even at 16 kHz.

Deo 5Part 5 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · DPI mišaExample 1 · mouse DPI

Dato: miš ima 800 DPI. Koliko koraka prijavi računaru ako ga pomeriš 5 cm udesno?

Given: a mouse has 800 DPI. How many steps does it report to the computer if you move it 5 cm to the right?

  1. Jedan inč je 2,54 cm, pa je 5 cm = 5 / 2,54 ≈ 1,97 inča.
  2. Broj koraka: 1,97 · 800 ≈ 1575.
  1. One inch is 2.54 cm, so 5 cm = 5 / 2.54 ≈ 1.97 inches.
  2. Number of steps: 1.97 · 800 ≈ 1575.

≈ 1575 koraka≈ 1575 steps

Primer 2 · skeniranjeExample 2 · scanning

Dato: skeniraš papir veličine 8 × 10 inča pri 300 DPI. Koliko piksela ima slika?

Given: you scan a sheet of 8 × 10 inches at 300 DPI. How many pixels does the image have?

  1. Širina: 8 · 300 = 2400 piksela.
  2. Visina: 10 · 300 = 3000 piksela.
  3. Ukupno: 2400 · 3000 = 7 200 000 piksela.
  1. Width: 8 · 300 = 2400 pixels.
  2. Height: 10 · 300 = 3000 pixels.
  3. Total: 2400 · 3000 = 7,200,000 pixels.

7,2 miliona piksela (2400 × 3000)7.2 million pixels (2400 × 3000)

Primer 3 · pogrešno slovoExample 3 · wrong letter

Dato: kucaš „zdravo”, a na ekranu piše „ydravo”. Šta nije u redu?

Given: you type "zdravo", and the screen shows "ydravo". What is wrong?

  1. Slova Y i Z su zamenjena.
  2. To se dešava kada je u sistemu izabran engleski (QWERTY) raspored, a tastatura je srpska (QWERTZ).
  3. Promeni raspored (Win + razmaknica ili Alt + Shift).
  1. The letters Y and Z are swapped.
  2. This happens when the English (QWERTY) layout is chosen in the system, but the keyboard is Serbian (QWERTZ).
  3. Change the layout (Win + Space or Alt + Shift).

Primer 4 · izbor uređajaExample 4 · choosing a device

Dato: biblioteka želi brzo da evidentira knjige pri iznošenju. Koju ulaznu jedinicu da koristi?

Given: a library wants to record books quickly at checkout. Which input device should it use?

  1. Ručno kucanje broja knjige je sporo i dovodi do grešaka.
  2. Na svaku knjigu se zalepi bar kod.
  3. Čitač bar koda ga očita za delić sekunde i pošalje broj kao da je otkucan.
  1. Typing the book number by hand is slow and error-prone.
  2. A barcode is stuck on every book.
  3. A barcode reader reads it in a fraction of a second and sends the number as if it had been typed.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Miš na staklu ili sjajnoj površini. Senzor ne vidi teksturu podloge, pa kursor skače ili stoji. Koristi podlogu za miš.
  • Uključen Caps Lock ili Num Lock. Lozinka „ne radi”, a brojevi na numeričkom delu pomeraju kursor. Proveri lampice na tastaturi.
  • Previsok DPI skeniranja. Dokument skeniran na 1200 DPI zauzima ogroman prostor, a ne izgleda bolje. Za tekst je 300 DPI dovoljno.
  • Mešanje DPI miša i brzine kursora. DPI je osobina senzora, a brzina pokazivača je podešavanje u sistemu. Oba utiču na to koliko kursor pređe.
  • A mouse on glass or a glossy surface. The sensor sees no texture in the surface, so the cursor jumps or stops. Use a mouse pad.
  • Caps Lock or Num Lock on. The password "does not work", and the numeric pad moves the cursor. Check the lights on the keyboard.
  • Scanning DPI too high. A document scanned at 1200 DPI takes huge space and does not look better. 300 DPI is enough for text.
  • Mixing up mouse DPI and pointer speed. DPI is a property of the sensor, while pointer speed is a system setting. Both affect how far the cursor travels.

SažetakSummary

  • Ulazne jedinice pretvaraju radnje i pojave iz sveta u digitalne podatke za računar.
  • Tastatura ima matricu tastera koju kontroler skenira i šalje kod pritisnutog tastera.
  • Srpska latinična tastatura je QWERTZ; raspored se bira u sistemu.
  • Optički miš poredi hiljade slika podloge u sekundi; osetljivost se meri u DPI.
  • Skener radi red po red (300 DPI za tekst), a mikrofon i kamera pretvaraju zvuk i sliku u brojeve.
  • Postoje i posebni ulazni uređaji: čitači bar koda, otiska prsta i kartica, senzori.
  • Input devices turn actions and things in the world into digital data for the computer.
  • A keyboard has a key matrix that the controller scans, sending the code of the pressed key.
  • The Serbian Latin keyboard is QWERTZ; the layout is chosen in the system.
  • An optical mouse compares thousands of pictures of the surface a second; sensitivity is measured in DPI.
  • A scanner works row by row (300 DPI for text), and a microphone and a camera turn sound and pictures into numbers.
  • There are also special input devices: barcode, fingerprint and card readers, and sensors.
