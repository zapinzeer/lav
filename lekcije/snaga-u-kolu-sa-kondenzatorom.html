Deo 1Part 1 · R i C na redR and C in series

Kondenzator i otpornik u koluA capacitor and a resistor in a circuit

Idealan kondenzator, kao i idealan kalem, ne troši snagu: energiju čuva u električnom polju između ploča i vraća je izvoru. U pravom kolu uz kondenzator uvek postoji neka otpornost: otpornik, potrošač ili gubici u dielektriku. Zato posmatramo rednu vezu otpornika R i kondenzatora C.

An ideal capacitor, like an ideal coil, consumes no power: it stores energy in the electric field between its plates and returns it to the source. In a real circuit there is always some resistance next to the capacitor: a resistor, a load, or losses in the dielectric. That is why we look at a series connection of a resistor R and a capacitor C.

Napon na otporniku je u fazi sa strujom, a napon na kondenzatoru kasni za strujom 90°. Kod kondenzatora je dakle obrnuto nego kod kalema: struja prednjači naponu (ICE).

The voltage across the resistor is in phase with the current, and the voltage across the capacitor lags the current by 90°. So for a capacitor it is the opposite of a coil: the current leads the voltage (ICE).

\[ X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C} \qquad Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_C^2} \qquad \operatorname{tg}\varphi = -\frac{X_C}{R} \qquad I = \frac{U}{Z} \]

Deo 2Part 2 · p = u · ip = u · i

Trenutna snaga u RC koluInstantaneous power in an RC circuit

Neka je \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \), a struja prednjači za ugao |φ|: \( i = I_m\sin(\omega t - \varphi) \) uz φ < 0. Ista formula kao kod kalema i dalje važi:

Let \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \), and let the current lead by the angle |φ|: \( i = I_m\sin(\omega t - \varphi) \) with φ < 0. The same formula as for the coil still holds:

\[ p = UI\cos\varphi - UI\cos(2\omega t - \varphi) \qquad P = UI\cos\varphi \]

Pošto je \( \cos(-\varphi) = \cos\varphi \), aktivna snaga ne zavisi od toga da li struja kasni ili prednjači, već samo od veličine ugla. Razlika je u tome kada se energija vraća: kondenzator vraća energiju dok se prazni, u delu periode pre nego što napon prođe kroz nulu.

Since \( \cos(-\varphi) = \cos\varphi \), the active power does not depend on whether the current lags or leads, only on the size of the angle. The difference is when the energy is returned: a capacitor returns energy while it discharges, in the part of the period just before the voltage crosses zero.

Kalem i kondenzator rade u suprotnom ritmuThe coil and the capacitor work in opposite rhythm

Kalem prima energiju dok struja raste, a kondenzator dok napon raste. Pošto su struja i napon pomereni, u kolu sa oba elementa jedan vraća energiju upravo kada je drugi uzima. Energija tada može da „šeta” između njih umesto da ide nazad do izvora.

A coil takes in energy while the current grows, and a capacitor while the voltage grows. Since the current and the voltage are shifted, in a circuit with both elements one returns energy exactly when the other takes it. The energy can then "travel" between them instead of going back to the source.

Deo 3Part 3 · Q < 0Q < 0

Snage u RC koluPowers in an RC circuit

Aktivna i prividna snaga računaju se isto kao kod kalema. Reaktivna snaga kondenzatora po dogovoru ima negativan znak: kondenzator reaktivnu snagu daje mreži, a kalem je uzima.

The active and the apparent power are calculated the same way as for a coil. The reactive power of a capacitor has a negative sign by convention: a capacitor supplies reactive power to the mains, and a coil draws it.

\[ P = UI\cos\varphi = RI^2 \qquad Q_C = UI\sin\varphi = -X_CI^2 = -\frac{U_C^2}{X_C} \qquad S = UI = \sqrt{P^2 + Q_C^2} \]

U zadacima se često piše samo veličina, „kondenzator ima 166 var”, a iz konteksta se zna da je kapacitivna. Ako u kolu postoje i kalem i kondenzator, ukupna reaktivna snaga je njihov algebarski zbir: \( Q = Q_L + Q_C = Q_L - |Q_C| \).

In problems we often write only the size, "the capacitor has 166 var", and the context tells us it is capacitive. If a circuit has both a coil and a capacitor, the total reactive power is their algebraic sum: \( Q = Q_L + Q_C = Q_L - |Q_C| \).

Deo 4Part 4 · popravka cos φimproving cos φ

Kompenzacija reaktivne snageReactive power compensation

Većina potrošača u fabrikama su motori, dakle induktivni: uzimaju reaktivnu snagu QL i imaju mali cos φ. Ako im paralelno vežemo kondenzator, on daje reaktivnu snagu na licu mesta, pa mreža više ne mora da je dovodi. Aktivna snaga motora se ne menja, ali ukupna struja iz mreže opada.

Most loads in factories are motors, so they are inductive: they draw reactive power QL and have a small cos φ. If we connect a capacitor in parallel with them, it supplies the reactive power on the spot, so the mains no longer has to bring it. The active power of the motor does not change, but the total current from the mains falls.

\[ Q_C = P(\operatorname{tg}\varphi_1 - \operatorname{tg}\varphi_2) \qquad C = \frac{Q_C}{\omega U^2} \]

Formula za C dolazi iz \( |Q_C| = U^2/X_C = \omega C U^2 \), jer je kondenzator vezan paralelno i na njemu je ceo napon mreže. U praksi se cos φ popravlja na oko 0,95, a ne na 1: potpuna kompenzacija zahteva veliki kondenzator, a mala promena opterećenja tada bi kolo učinila kapacitivnim.

The formula for C comes from \( |Q_C| = U^2/X_C = \omega C U^2 \), because the capacitor is connected in parallel and has the full mains voltage across it. In practice cos φ is improved to about 0.95, not to 1: full compensation needs a large capacitor, and a small change in the load would then make the circuit capacitive.

Primer 3 · kompenzacija motoraExample 3 · compensating a motor

Dato: motor 2 kW, cos φ1 = 0,6, mreža 230 V, 50 Hz. Koliki kondenzator popravlja cos φ na 0,95?

Given: a 2 kW motor, cos φ1 = 0.6, mains 230 V, 50 Hz. What capacitor improves cos φ to 0.95?

  1. \( \operatorname{tg}\varphi_1 = 1{,}333 \), \( \operatorname{tg}\varphi_2 = 0{,}329 \).
  2. \( Q_C = 2000\,(1{,}333 - 0{,}329) = 2010\ \mathrm{var} \).
  3. \( C = 2010/(314\cdot 230^2) = 121\ \mu\mathrm{F} \).
  4. Struja iz mreže pada sa \( 2000/(230\cdot 0{,}6) = 14{,}5\ \mathrm{A} \) na \( 2000/(230\cdot 0{,}95) = 9{,}2\ \mathrm{A} \).
  1. \( \tan\varphi_1 = 1.333 \), \( \tan\varphi_2 = 0.329 \).
  2. \( Q_C = 2000\,(1.333 - 0.329) = 2010\ \mathrm{var} \).
  3. \( C = 2010/(314\cdot 230^2) = 121\ \mu\mathrm{F} \).
  4. The current from the mains drops from \( 2000/(230\cdot 0.6) = 14.5\ \mathrm{A} \) to \( 2000/(230\cdot 0.95) = 9.2\ \mathrm{A} \).

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Pogrešan smer. Kod kondenzatora struja prednjači (ICE), pa je φ negativan.
  • XC = ωC. Kapacitivna reaktansa je recipročna: \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \). I pretvori μF u F.
  • Kompenzacioni kondenzator na red. Kondenzator se vezuje paralelno; na red bi promenio napon na motoru.
  • Sabiranje QL i |QC|. Reaktivne snage kalema i kondenzatora se oduzimaju, jer imaju suprotne znakove.
  • Kompenzacija menja P. Ne menja. Smanjuju se samo Q, S i struja iz mreže.
  • The wrong direction. In a capacitor the current leads (ICE), so φ is negative.
  • XC = ωC. The capacitive reactance is reciprocal: \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \). And convert μF to F.
  • A compensation capacitor in series. The capacitor is connected in parallel; in series it would change the voltage on the motor.
  • Adding QL and |QC|. The reactive powers of a coil and a capacitor subtract, because they have opposite signs.
  • Compensation changes P. It does not. Only Q, S and the current from the mains get smaller.

SažetakSummary

  • U RC kolu \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_C^2} \), \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \), a struja prednjači naponu za ugao do 90°.
  • \( P = UI\cos\varphi = RI^2 \), \( Q_C = -X_CI^2 \) (kondenzator daje reaktivnu snagu), \( S = UI \).
  • Reaktivne snage kalema i kondenzatora se algebarski sabiraju, pa se mogu poništiti.
  • Kompenzacija: \( Q_C = P(\operatorname{tg}\varphi_1 - \operatorname{tg}\varphi_2) \), \( C = Q_C/(\omega U^2) \), kondenzator paralelno potrošaču.
  • In an RC circuit \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_C^2} \), \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \), and the current leads the voltage by an angle up to 90°.
  • \( P = UI\cos\varphi = RI^2 \), \( Q_C = -X_CI^2 \) (the capacitor supplies reactive power), \( S = UI \).
  • The reactive powers of a coil and a capacitor add algebraically, so they can cancel.
  • Compensation: \( Q_C = P(\tan\varphi_1 - \tan\varphi_2) \), \( C = Q_C/(\omega U^2) \), with the capacitor in parallel with the load.
