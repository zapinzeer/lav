Deo 1Part 1 · pojmovithe terms

Šta su UI i UX?What are UI and UX?

UI (User Interface, korisnički interfejs) je sve ono što korisnik vidi i dodiruje: dugmad, polja za unos, meniji, ikonice, boje, slova i raspored na ekranu. UX (User Experience, korisničko iskustvo) je ono što korisnik doživi dok koristi proizvod: da li je brzo našao šta mu treba, da li se zbunio, da li je uspeo da završi posao i kakav mu je osećaj ostao posle toga.

UI (user interface) is everything the user sees and touches: buttons, input fields, menus, icons, colours, fonts and the layout of the screen. UX (user experience) is what the user goes through while using a product: whether they found what they needed quickly, whether they got confused, whether they managed to finish the job, and how they felt afterwards.

Zgodno poređenje je kašika: oblik, materijal i boja su UI, a to koliko lako jedeš supu je UX. Lepa kašika sa rupom u sredini ima dobar UI i loš UX. UI je deo UX-a, ali UX obuhvata mnogo više: brzinu učitavanja, jasne poruke o greškama, tekst, pa čak i podršku kad nešto ne radi.

A handy comparison is a spoon: its shape, material and colour are the UI, and how easily you eat soup with it is the UX. A pretty spoon with a hole in the middle has a good UI and a bad UX. UI is a part of UX, but UX covers much more: loading speed, clear error messages, the wording, even the help you get when something does not work.

Dobar dizajn ima tri osobine koje se često navode zajedno: korisnost (rešava pravi problem), upotrebljivost (lako se uči i koristi, bez grešaka) i prijatnost (lepo je i ostavlja dobar utisak). Upotrebljivost se proverava jednostavno: daš ljudima zadatak i gledaš gde zapnu.

Good design has three qualities that are often named together: usefulness (it solves a real problem), usability (it is easy to learn and use, without errors) and delight (it is pleasant and leaves a good impression). Usability is checked simply: you give people a task and watch where they get stuck.

Pomisli prvo: seti se aplikacije koja te je iznervirala. Da li je problem bio UI ili UX?Think first: remember an app that annoyed you. Was the problem UI or UX?

Najčešće UX: predugačka registracija, dugme koje se ne vidi, poruka „Greška” bez objašnjenja, sadržaj koji se pomera dok se učitava. Ružna boja retko nekog otera, a nejasan tok skoro uvek.

Most often UX: a sign-up that is too long, a button you cannot find, a message saying just "Error", content that jumps while it loads. An ugly colour rarely drives anyone away, but an unclear flow almost always does.

Deo 2Part 2 · kako se dizajnirahow design is done

Dizajn usmeren na korisnikaUser-centred design

Najvažnije pravilo UX-a glasi: ti nisi korisnik. Ono što je tebi očigledno, jer si napravio aplikaciju, drugima nije. Zato se dizajn ne radi „iz glave”, već u krugu u kome se korisnici pitaju i posmatraju, a rešenje se popravlja dok ne proradi za njih.

The most important rule of UX is: you are not the user. What is obvious to you, because you built the app, is not obvious to others. So design is not done "from your head", but in a cycle in which users are asked and watched, and the solution is improved until it works for them.

  • Istraživanje. Intervju, anketa, posmatranje ljudi dok rade posao. Cilj je da saznaš šta im stvarno treba, a ne šta misliš da im treba.
  • Persona. Izmišljen, ali realan opis tipičnog korisnika („Milica, 16 godina, uči sa telefona u autobusu”). Pomaže timu da se seti za koga radi.
  • Mapa puta korisnika. Koraci kroz koje korisnik prolazi da bi stigao do cilja, sa mestima gde se nervira.
  • Skica (wireframe). Crno-beli raspored bez boja i slika: samo gde šta stoji.
  • Prototip. Skica na koju se može kliknuti (Figma, papir). Dovoljno da se testira.
  • Test upotrebljivosti. Petoro ljudi dobije zadatak („promeni lozinku”) i glasno priča šta misli. Već pet ljudi otkrije većinu velikih problema.
  • Research. Interviews, surveys, watching people do the job. The aim is to find out what they really need, not what you think they need.
  • Persona. A made-up but realistic description of a typical user ("Milica, 16, studies on her phone on the bus"). It helps the team remember who they are building for.
  • User journey map. The steps a user goes through to reach a goal, with the places where they get annoyed.
  • Wireframe. A black-and-white layout with no colours or pictures: just what goes where.
  • Prototype. A sketch you can click on (Figma, paper). Enough to test.
  • Usability test. Five people get a task ("change your password") and think aloud. Just five people uncover most of the big problems.

Deo 3Part 3 · rasporedlayout

Vizuelna hijerarhija i geštalt principiVisual hierarchy and the Gestalt principles

Ljudi ekran ne čitaju, već ga preleću pogledom. Vizuelna hijerarhija govori oku šta je najvažnije, šta sledeće, a šta može da sačeka. Postiže se veličinom, debljinom slova, bojom, kontrastom i praznim prostorom. Na zapadnim ekranima pogled obično ide u obliku slova F ili Z: gore levo, pa preko, pa dole.

People do not read a screen, they scan it. Visual hierarchy tells the eye what matters most, what comes next and what can wait. It is built with size, font weight, colour, contrast and white space. On Western screens the eye usually moves in an F or Z shape: top left, then across, then down.

Geštalt principi opisuju kako mozak sam grupiše ono što vidi. Dizajner ih koristi da raspored „objasni sebe” bez ijedne reči:

The Gestalt principles describe how the brain groups what it sees by itself. A designer uses them so that a layout "explains itself" without a single word:

  • Blizina. Stvari koje su blizu doživljavamo kao grupu. Natpis treba da bude bliži svom polju nego susednom.
  • Sličnost. Stvari istog oblika, boje ili veličine doživljavamo kao srodne. Svi linkovi iste boje, sva dugmad istog oblika.
  • Poravnanje. Elementi na zajedničkoj nevidljivoj liniji deluju uredno i povezano. Nasumično centriranje i ivice deluju haotično.
  • Kontrast. Ono što se razlikuje privlači pažnju. Glavna radnja (npr. „Pošalji”) treba da odskače od sporednih.
  • Zajednička oblast. Okvir ili pozadina oko elemenata ih spaja u celinu (kartica).
  • Proximity. Things that are close together are seen as a group. A label should sit closer to its own field than to the next one.
  • Similarity. Things of the same shape, colour or size are seen as related. All links one colour, all buttons one shape.
  • Alignment. Elements on a shared invisible line look tidy and connected. Random centring and ragged edges look chaotic.
  • Contrast. What is different draws attention. The main action (e.g. "Send") should stand out from the secondary ones.
  • Common region. A frame or background around elements joins them into one unit (a card).

Prazan prostor nije bačen prostorWhite space is not wasted space

Prostor oko elemenata nije „višak” koji treba popuniti. On razdvaja grupe, odmara oko i daje važnim stvarima mesto da se istaknu.

The space around elements is not "spare" room to be filled. It separates groups, rests the eye and gives important things room to stand out.

Boja, kontrast i tipografijaColour, contrast and typography

Tekst mora da se jasno odvaja od pozadine, i to ne samo na tvom dobrom monitoru, već i na jeftinom telefonu na suncu, i za ljude koji slabije vide. Koliko se tekst odvaja meri se odnosom kontrasta između dve boje. Prvo se za svaku boju izračuna relativna osvetljenost L, od 0 (crna) do 1 (bela), pa se uporede:

Text must stand out clearly from its background, not just on your good monitor, but also on a cheap phone in the sun, and for people with weaker sight. How much the text stands out is measured by the contrast ratio between two colours. Boja nikad ne sme da bude jedini nosilac značenja. Oko 8 % muškaraca ne razlikuje dobro crvenu i zelenu. Polje sa greškom zato pored crvene ivice dobija ikonicu i tekst („Unesi ispravnu e-poštu”). Paleta neka bude mala: jedna glavna boja za radnje, neutralne za tekst i pozadine, i boje za stanja (uspeh, upozorenje, greška).

Colour must never be the only carrier of meaning. About 8 % of men cannot tell red and green apart well. So a field with an error gets an icon and text ("Enter a valid e-mail") as well as a red border. Keep the palette small: one main colour for actions, neutrals for text and backgrounds, and colours for states (success, warning, error).

Tipografija: najviše dve porodice slova; osnovni tekst oko 16 px na ekranu; red od 45 do 75 znakova (duži zamara oko); razmak između redova oko 1,5 visine slova; veličine naslova u jasnim koracima (npr. 16, 20, 25, 31 px), a ne 16, 17, 18.

Typography: two font families at most; body text around 16 px on screen; lines of 45 to 75 characters (longer ones tire the eye); line spacing of about 1.5 times the font size; heading sizes in clear steps (e.g. 16, 20, 25, 31 px), not 16, 17, 18.

Deo 5Part 5 · pravila palcarules of thumb

Nilsenovih deset heuristikaNielsen's ten heuristics

Jakob Nilsen je 1994. sažeo iskustvo iz mnogo testova u deset heuristika, opštih pravila po kojima se interfejs može brzo proceniti i bez korisnika. Kad pregledaš aplikaciju, prođi kroz listu i za svako pravilo zapiši gde je prekršeno.

In 1994 Jakob Nielsen summed up the experience from many tests in ten heuristics, general rules by which an interface can be judged quickly, even without users. When you review an app, go through the list and note for each rule where it is broken.

Uz heuristike, dobar interfejs uvek daje povratnu informaciju (feedback): dugme menja izgled kad se pritisne, polje se oboji kad je unos neispravan. Svaki element ima više stanja: običan, pod mišem (hover), fokusiran tastaturom, pritisnut, onemogućen, učitava se. Ako neko stanje nije dizajnirano, korisnik ne zna šta se dešava.

On top of the heuristics, a good interface always gives feedback: a button changes when pressed, a field is coloured when the input is wrong. Every element has several states: default, hover, keyboard focus, pressed, disabled, loading. If a state has not been designed, the user does not know what is going on.

Pomisli prvo: koju heuristiku krši sajt koji posle klika na „Pošalji” ne pokaže ništa?Think first: which heuristic does a site break if nothing happens after you click "Send"?

Prvu, vidljivost stanja sistema. Korisnik ne zna da li je poruka otišla, pa klikne još jednom i pošalje je dva puta. Rešenje: dugme pređe u stanje „Šalje se…”, pa stigne poruka „Poslato”.

The first one, visibility of system status. The user does not know whether the message went, so they click again and send it twice. The fix: the button switches to "Sending…", and then a "Sent" message appears.

Fitsov i Hikov zakonFitts's law and Hick's law

Fitsov zakon kaže da vreme potrebno da se pogodi cilj (dugme) raste sa udaljenošću do njega, a opada sa njegovom veličinom. Teškoća pogađanja meri se indeksom težine u bitima:

Fitts's law says that the time needed to hit a target (a button) grows with the distance to it and shrinks with its size. How hard it is to hit is measured by the index of difficulty in bits:

Posledice za dizajn: važna dugmad neka budu velika i blizu mesta gde je ruka ili pogled. Na telefonu ciljevi treba da imaju bar oko 44 × 44 px (otprilike jagodica prsta). Ivice i uglovi ekrana su „beskonačno široki” za miš, jer kursor tamo ne može da promaši, pa se tu stavljaju meniji.

What it means for design: important buttons should be large and close to where the hand or the eye already is. On a phone targets should be at least about 44 × 44 px (roughly a fingertip). The edges and corners of the screen are "infinitely wide" for a mouse, because the pointer cannot overshoot them, which is why menus go there.

Hikov zakon kaže da vreme odlučivanja raste sa brojem ponuđenih izbora n, ali sporije nego sam broj, po logaritmu:

Hick's law says that decision time grows with the number of choices offered, n, but more slowly than the number itself, by a logarithm:

\[ T = a + b \cdot \log_2(n + 1) \]

Zato meni sa 30 stavki odjednom zbunjuje, a isti meni podeljen u 5 grupa po 6 ide brže. Kad je izbora mnogo, grupiši ih, sakrij retko korišćene iza „Još” i istakni preporučeni izbor.

That is why a menu with 30 items at once is confusing, while the same menu split into 5 groups of 6 is quicker. When there are many choices, group them, hide the rarely used ones behind "More" and highlight the recommended choice.

Milerov brojMiller's number

Kratkoročna memorija čuva oko 7 ± 2 stvari (novija istraživanja kažu i manje, oko 4). Ne teraj korisnika da pamti broj sa prethodnog ekrana: pokaži mu ga ponovo.

Short-term memory holds about 7 ± 2 things (newer research says even fewer, about 4). Do not make the user remember a number from the previous screen: show it to them again.

Deo 7Part 7 · za sve korisnikefor every user

Pristupačnost i odzivni dizajnAccessibility and responsive design

Pristupačnost (accessibility, skraćeno a11y) znači da proizvod mogu da koriste svi: ljudi koji slabije vide ili su slepi i koriste čitač ekrana, koji ne mogu da koriste miš, koji slabije čuju, ali i neko sa slomljenom rukom ili na suncu sa telefonom. Ono što pomaže njima, pomaže i svima ostalima.

Accessibility (a11y for short) means everyone can use the product: people with weak sight or who are blind and use a screen reader, who cannot use a mouse, who are hard of hearing, but also someone with a broken arm or with a phone in bright sun. What helps them helps everybody else too.

  • Tekst za slike. Svaka slika koja nosi značenje ima opis (alt), koji čitač ekrana pročita naglas.
  • Tastatura. Sve što radi mišem mora da radi i tasterima Tab, Enter i razmaknica, a fokus mora da bude vidljiv.
  • Natpisi polja. Svako polje ima pravi natpis, ne samo sivi tekst unutar polja koji nestane čim počneš da kucaš.
  • Kontrast i veličina. Najmanje 4,5 : 1 za tekst; stranica mora da radi i kad korisnik uveća slova na 200 %.
  • Ne samo boja. Greška ima i ikonicu i reči, link je i podvučen.
  • Jasan jezik. Kratke rečenice, poznate reči, dugmad koja kažu šta rade („Sačuvaj izmene”, a ne „OK”).
  • Pokret i zvuk. Video ima titlove, animacija se može zaustaviti i ne treperi više od tri puta u sekundi.
  • Text for images. Every image that carries meaning has a description (alt) which a screen reader reads aloud.
  • Keyboard. Everything that works with a mouse must also work with Tab, Enter and Space, and the focus must be visible.
  • Field labels. Every field has a real label, not just grey text inside the field that disappears as soon as you start typing.
  • Contrast and size. At least 4.5 : 1 for text; the page must still work when the user zooms text to 200 %.
  • Not just colour. An error has an icon and words too, a link is also underlined.
  • Plain language. Short sentences, familiar words, buttons that say what they do ("Save changes", not "OK").
  • Motion and sound. Video has captions, animation can be paused and does not flash more than three times a second.

Odzivni dizajn (responsive) znači da se ista stranica prilagođava širini ekrana: na telefonu kolone idu jedna ispod druge, meni se skuplja u dugme, a dugmad su dovoljno velika za prst. Dobra praksa je prvo mobilni (mobile first): dizajniraš najpre za mali ekran, gde moraš da odlučiš šta je zaista važno, pa onda dodaješ za veći.

Responsive design means the same page adapts to the width of the screen: on a phone columns stack one under another, the menu folds into a button, and buttons are big enough for a finger. A good habit is mobile first: you design for the small screen first, where you have to decide what really matters, and then add for the larger one.

Brza provera za 2 minutaA two-minute check

Prođi kroz stranicu samo tasterom Tab. Uvećaj na 200 %. Pogledaj je u crno-beloj varijanti. Ako u sva tri slučaja i dalje sve razumeš i sve možeš da uradiš, na dobrom si putu.

Go through the page using only the Tab key. Zoom to 200 %. Look at it in black and white. If in all three cases you still understand everything and can do everything, you are on the right track.

Primer 3 · pregled forme heuristikamaExample 3 · reviewing a form with heuristics

Dato: forma za prijavu ima polja bez natpisa (samo sivi tekst u polju), crvenu ivicu kao jedini znak greške, dugme „OK” i posle slanja ništa se ne dešava 5 sekundi. Šta popraviti?

Given: a sign-in form has fields with no labels (only grey text inside), a red border as the only sign of an error, an "OK" button, and after sending nothing happens for 5 seconds. What should be fixed?

  2. Greška sa ikonicom i rečima šta nije u redu i kako da se ispravi (heuristika 9, ne samo boja).

  4. Stanje „Prijavljivanje…” sa indikatorom dok se čeka (heuristika 1).
  1. Labels above the fields: accessibility, and recognition rather than recall.
  2. An error with an icon and words saying what is wrong and how to fix it (heuristic 9, not colour alone).
  3. A button that says what it does: "Sign in" (heuristic 2).
  4. A "Signing in…" state with an indicator while waiting (heuristic 1).

Četiri izmene, a forma postaje jasna, pristupačna i daje povratnu informaciju.Four changes, and the form becomes clear, accessible and gives feedback.

Primer 4 · Hikov zakonExample 4 · Hick's law

Dato: meni ima 15 stavki u jednom spisku. Uz b = 150 ms po bitu, koliko se vreme odlučivanja menja ako se svede na 3 glavne stavke i „Još”?

Given: a menu has 15 items in one list. With b = 150 ms per bit, how much does decision time change if it is cut to 3 main items and "More"?

  1. Pre: log₂(15 + 1) = 4 bita, pa je b · 4 = 600 ms.
  2. Posle: 4 izbora, log₂(4 + 1) ≈ 2,32 bita, pa je b · 2,32 ≈ 348 ms.
  3. Razlika ≈ 250 ms za svaki izbor, a korisnici koji traže retku stavku imaju jedan klik više.
  1. Before: log₂(15 + 1) = 4 bits, so b · 4 = 600 ms.
  2. After: 4 choices, log₂(4 + 1) ≈ 2.32 bits, so b · 2.32 ≈ 348 ms.
  3. Difference ≈ 250 ms for every choice, while users looking for a rare item have one more click.

Oko 250 ms brže za većinu korisnika; zato se retko korišćeno sakriva.About 250 ms faster for most users; that is why rarely used items are tucked away.

Deo 9Part 9 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • „Meni je jasno, biće i drugima.” Ti nisi korisnik. Testiraj sa ljudima koji aplikaciju vide prvi put.
  • UX je samo lep izgled. Lepa aplikacija u kojoj ne možeš da završiš posao je loš UX.
  • Sivi tekst na beloj pozadini. Izgleda elegantno, ali se slabo čita. Proveri kontrast.
  • Boja kao jedini znak. Crveno i zeleno mnogi ne razlikuju; dodaj ikonu ili tekst.
  • Sitna dugmad na telefonu. Ispod oko 44 px prst promašuje.
  • Previše izbora odjednom. Grupiši i istakni glavni izbor.
  • Nedosledan izgled. Ako isto dugme na svakoj strani izgleda drugačije, korisnik mora ponovo da uči.
  • Nema povratne informacije. Svaka radnja treba da pokaže da je primljena i šta se desilo.
  • "It's clear to me, so it will be to others." You are not the user. Test with people who see the app for the first time.
  • UX is just good looks. A pretty app in which you cannot get the job done is bad UX.
  • Grey text on a white background. It looks elegant, but it is hard to read. Check the contrast.
  • Colour as the only sign. Many people cannot tell red from green; add an icon or text.
  • Tiny buttons on a phone. Below about 44 px a finger misses.
  • Too many choices at once. Group them and highlight the main choice.
  • Inconsistent look. If the same button looks different on every page, the user has to learn it again.
  • No feedback. Every action should show that it was received and what happened.

SažetakSummary

  • UI je ono što vidiš i dodiruješ; UX je celo iskustvo korišćenja. UI je deo UX-a.
  • Dizajn usmeren na korisnika: istraži, definiši, smisli, napravi prototip, testiraj, ponovi. Ti nisi korisnik.
  • Vizuelna hijerarhija vodi oko; geštalt principi blizine, sličnosti, poravnanja i kontrasta grupišu elemente bez reči.
  • Kontrast K = (L₁ + 0,05) / (L₂ + 0,05); običan tekst bar 4,5 : 1, krupan bar 3 : 1. Boja nije jedini znak.
  • Nilsenovih 10 heuristika služi za brz pregled interfejsa; najvažnije su vidljivost stanja, doslednost, sprečavanje grešaka i jasne poruke.
  • Fitsov zakon: veći i bliži cilj se brže pogađa; Hikov zakon: više izbora, sporija odluka.
  • Pristupačnost: alt tekst, tastatura, natpisi polja, kontrast, jasan jezik; odzivni dizajn i prvo mobilni.
  • UI is what you see and touch; UX is the whole experience of using it. UI is a part of UX.
  • User-centred design: research, define, ideate, prototype, test, repeat. You are not the user.
  • Visual hierarchy guides the eye; the Gestalt principles of proximity, similarity, alignment and contrast group elements without words.
  • Contrast K = (L₁ + 0.05) / (L₂ + 0.05); normal text at least 4.5 : 1, large text at least 3 : 1. Colour is not the only sign.
  • Nielsen's 10 heuristics are for a quick review of an interface; the key ones are system status, consistency, error prevention and clear messages.
  • Fitts's law: a bigger, nearer target is hit faster; Hick's law: more choices, slower decision.
  • Accessibility: alt text, keyboard, field labels, contrast, plain language; responsive design and mobile first.
