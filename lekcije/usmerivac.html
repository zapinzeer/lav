Deo 1Part 1 · p-n spojthe p-n junction

Dioda: ventil za strujuThe diode: a valve for current

Mikrokontroler, pojačavač i LED traka rade na jednosmernom naponu, a iz utičnice (i iz transformatora) dolazi naizmenični. Pretvaranje naizmeničnog napona u jednosmerni zove se ispravljanje, a uređaj koji to radi je usmerivač. Njegov najvažniji deo je dioda.

A microcontroller, an amplifier and an LED strip run on a direct voltage, while the wall socket (and a transformer) gives an alternating one. Converting an alternating voltage into a direct one is called rectification, and the device that does it is a rectifier. Its most important part is the diode.

Dioda ima dva priključka: anodu (A) i katodu (K). Ponaša se kao ventil: struju propušta samo od anode ka katodi, kada je anoda pozitivnija od katode, a u suprotnom smeru je zaustavlja.

A diode has two terminals: the anode (A) and the cathode (K). It acts as a valve: it passes current only from the anode to the cathode, when the anode is more positive than the cathode, and stops it in the opposite direction.

Zašto dioda to može?Why can a diode do that?

Dioda je od silicijuma sa dve različito dopirane oblasti koje se dodiruju, i to se zove p-n spoj:

A diode is made of silicon with two differently doped regions that touch, and this is called a p-n junction:

  • P-strana ima višak „šupljina”, to jest mesta gde elektron nedostaje. Šupljine se ponašaju kao pokretna pozitivna naelektrisanja.
  • N-strana ima višak slobodnih elektrona, pokretnih negativnih naelektrisanja.
  • The P side has an excess of "holes", places where an electron is missing. Holes behave as mobile positive charges.
  • The N side has an excess of free electrons, mobile negative charges.

Na dodiru elektroni pređu u P-stranu i spoje se sa šupljinama. Ostaju nepokretni naelektrisani joni, pa nastaje osiromašeni sloj bez slobodnih nosilaca. U njemu je jako električno polje koje je barijera za dalji prelaz, visine oko 0,7 V kod silicijuma.

At the contact, electrons cross into the P side and combine with holes. Immobile charged ions are left behind, so a depletion layer without free carriers appears. In it there is a strong electric field that is a barrier to further crossing, about 0.7 V high in silicon.

Propusni smerForward direction

A (+) → K (−)

Spoljašnji napon poništava barijeru, sloj se sužava. Iznad praga od oko 0,7 V struja naglo raste.

The external voltage cancels the barrier, the layer narrows. Above the threshold of about 0.7 V the current rises sharply.

Zaporni smerReverse direction

A (−) → K (+)

Barijera raste, sloj se širi. Teče samo zanemarljiva struja curenja (nA do μA).

The barrier grows, the layer widens. Only a negligible leakage current flows (nA to μA).

Dioda nije otpornikA diode is not a resistor

Kada dioda vodi, njen otpor je mali, pa struju ne ograničava ona nego ostatak kola. Dioda ili LED bez otpornika na red povući će toliku struju da se uništi.

When a diode conducts, its resistance is small, so the current is not limited by it but by the rest of the circuit. A diode or an LED without a series resistor will draw so much current that it is destroyed.

Za radoznale: jednačina diodeFor the curious: the diode equation

Struja stvarne diode opisuje se Šoklijevom jednačinom:

The current of a real diode is described by the Shockley equation:

\[ I = I_S\left(e^{\,U_D/(n\,U_T)} - 1\right) \qquad U_T = \frac{kT}{q} \approx 26\ \mathrm{mV}\ \ (T = 300\ \mathrm{K}) \]

Struja zasićenja \( I_S \) je vrlo mala (pA do nA). Zato za male napone struje skoro nema, a svakih oko 60 mV iznad toga struja poraste 10 puta. To je razlog što napon na provodnoj diodi ostaje skoro stalan oko 0,7 V.

The saturation current \( I_S \) is very small (pA to nA). That is why at small voltages there is almost no current, and for every 60 mV or so above that the current grows 10 times. This is the reason that the voltage across a conducting diode stays almost constant at about 0.7 V.

Deo 2Part 2 · najjednostavniji usmerivačthe simplest rectifier

Poluperiodni usmerivačThe half-wave rectifier

Najjednostavniji usmerivač je jedna dioda vezana na red sa potrošačem R, na naizmenični izvor (obično sekundar transformatora) napona \( u_2 = U_m\sin\omega t \).

The simplest rectifier is a single diode in series with a load R, on an alternating source (usually a transformer secondary) of voltage \( u_2 = U_m\sin\omega t \).

  • Pozitivna poluperioda: anoda je pozitivnija, dioda vodi i kroz R teče struja. Izlazni napon prati ulazni, umanjen za pad na diodi.
  • Negativna poluperioda: dioda je zakočena, struje nema, izlazni napon je nula. Ceo napon izvora pada na diodi.
  • Positive half-cycle: the anode is more positive, the diode conducts and a current flows through R. The output voltage follows the input one, reduced by the drop on the diode.
  • Negative half-cycle: the diode is blocked, there is no current, the output voltage is zero. The whole source voltage falls on the diode.

Struja kroz opterećenje uvek ima isti smer, pa je napon jednosmeran, ali nije stalan: to je niz pulseva sa pauzama, koji se ponavlja 50 puta u sekundi. Takav napon se zove pulsirajući.

The current through the load always has the same direction, so the voltage is unidirectional, but it is not constant: it is a train of pulses with pauses, repeated 50 times per second. Such a voltage is called pulsating.

Faktor talasanja \( \gamma = \sqrt{(U_{ef}/U_{dc})^2 - 1} = 1{,}21 \) pokazuje koliko je naizmenična komponenta velika u odnosu na jednosmernu. Veći od 1 znači da je „nemirna” komponenta veća od korisne, pa je izlaz poluperiodnog usmerivača bez filtra loš izvor napajanja. Stepen iskorišćenja \( \eta = P_{dc}/P_{ac} = 4/\pi^2 \approx 40{,}5\ \% \): manje od polovine snage završi kao koristan jednosmerni napon.

The ripple factor \( \gamma = \sqrt{(U_{rms}/U_{dc})^2 - 1} = 1.21 \) shows how large the alternating component is compared to the direct one. A value above 1 means the "restless" component is larger than the useful one, so the output of a half-wave rectifier without a filter is a poor power supply. The efficiency \( \eta = P_{dc}/P_{ac} = 4/\pi^2 \approx 40.5\ \% \): less than half of the power ends up as useful direct voltage.

Ima i drugi nedostatak: jednosmerna struja kroz sekundar transformatora magnetiše jezgro u jednom smeru i može da ga dovede do zasićenja. Zato se poluperiodni usmerivač koristi samo za male snage.

There is another drawback: the direct current through the transformer secondary magnetises the core in one direction and can drive it into saturation. That is why the half-wave rectifier is used only for small powers.

Šta pokazuju instrumenti (vežbe 8 i 9)What the instruments show (exercises 8 and 9)

Voltmetar na DC pokazuje srednju vrednost \( U_{dc} \). Multimetar na AC sa True RMS meri efektivnu vrednost samo naizmenične komponente. Osciloskop sa DC spregom pokazuje oblik, a sa AC spregom samo talasanje oko nule.

A voltmeter on DC shows the average value \( U_{dc} \). A multimeter on AC with True RMS measures the RMS value of the alternating component only. An oscilloscope with DC coupling shows the shape, and with AC coupling only the ripple around zero.

Za radoznale: odakle π?For the curious: where does π come from?

Srednja vrednost je površina ispod krive podeljena periodom. Dioda vodi samo u prvoj polovini periode:

The average value is the area under the curve divided by the period. The diode conducts only in the first half of the period:

\[ U_{dc} = \frac{1}{T}\int_0^{T/2} U_m\sin\omega t\,dt = \frac{U_m}{T}\cdot\frac{2}{\omega} = \frac{U_m}{T}\cdot\frac{T}{\pi} = \frac{U_m}{\pi} \]

Za efektivnu vrednost, srednja vrednost kvadrata je \( \frac{1}{T}\int_0^{T/2} U_m^2\sin^2\omega t\,dt = \frac{U_m^2}{4} \), pa je \( U_{ef} = U_m/2 \).

For the RMS value, the mean of the square is \( \frac{1}{T}\int_0^{T/2} U_m^2\sin^2\omega t\,dt = \frac{U_m^2}{4} \), so \( U_{rms} = U_m/2 \).

Deo 3Part 3 · od pulsa do skoro stalnog naponafrom pulses to a nearly constant voltage

Kondenzator kao filterThe capacitor as a filter

Pulsirajući napon pada na nulu u svakoj periodi, pa je neupotrebljiv za napajanje elektronike. Rešenje je kondenzator C paralelno sa opterećenjem. On skladišti naelektrisanje pri vrhu sinusoide, a kada dioda ne vodi, daje ga opterećenju.

A pulsating voltage falls to zero in every period, so it is useless for powering electronics. The solution is a capacitor C in parallel with the load. It stores charge near the top of the sine, and when the diode does not conduct, it supplies it to the load.

  1. Punjenje. Kada \( u_2 \) naraste iznad napona kondenzatora (plus pad na diodi), dioda provede i kondenzator se brzo napuni do vrha, \( U_m - U_\gamma \).
  2. Pražnjenje. Kada \( u_2 \) počne da pada, kondenzator je viši od izvora, pa je dioda zakočena. Kondenzator se prazni kroz R i njegov napon lagano opada.
  3. Dopuna. U sledećem vrhu \( u_2 \) ponovo premaši napon kondenzatora, dioda kratko provede i dopuni izgubljeno naelektrisanje.
  1. Charging. When \( u_2 \) rises above the capacitor voltage (plus the drop on the diode), the diode conducts and the capacitor quickly charges to the peak, \( U_m - U_\gamma \).
  2. Discharging. When \( u_2 \) starts to fall, the capacitor is higher than the source, so the diode is blocked. The capacitor discharges through R and its voltage slowly falls.
  3. Topping up. At the next peak \( u_2 \) exceeds the capacitor voltage again, the diode conducts briefly and replaces the lost charge.

Izlazni napon sada ima oblik „testere” koja se zove talasanje (engl. ripple). Izmerimo ga vrednošću Δu od vrha do dna. Dok dioda ne vodi, kondenzator daje struju \( I_{dc} \) skoro celu periodu \( T = 1/f \), pa izgubi naelektrisanje \( \Delta Q \approx I_{dc}\,T \), a napon mu padne za \( \Delta u = \Delta Q/C \):

The output voltage now has a "sawtooth" shape called ripple. We measure it by the value Δu from peak to bottom. While the diode does not conduct, the capacitor supplies the current \( I_{dc} \) for almost the whole period \( T = 1/f \), so it loses the charge \( \Delta Q \approx I_{dc}\,T \), and its voltage falls by \( \Delta u = \Delta Q/C \):

\[ \Delta u \approx \frac{I_{dc}}{f\,C} \qquad U_{dc} \approx U_m - U_\gamma - \frac{\Delta u}{2} \qquad r = \frac{\Delta u}{2\sqrt{3}\,U_{dc}} \]

Veći C, manje talasanjeA larger C, a smaller ripple

Talasanje je srazmerno struji opterećenja, a obrnuto srazmerno kapacitivnosti i učestanosti. Udvostručiš li C, talasanje se prepolovi. Udvostručiš li struju, udvostruči se i talasanje.

The ripple is proportional to the current of the load and inversely proportional to the capacitance and the frequency. Double C and the ripple is halved. Double the current and the ripple doubles too.

Cena filtra: impulsi strujeThe price of the filter: current pulses

Dioda vodi samo kratko, pa joj struja nije stalna nego niz jakih impulsa, mnogo većih od srednje struje. Što je C veći, to je vođenje kraće, a impulsi jači. Pri uključenju prazan kondenzator se ponaša kao kratak spoj, pa dioda mora da izdrži i veliku udarnu struju. Zato se bira dioda sa velikom rezervom. Visinu impulsa ograničava samo unutrašnji otpor transformatora i diode (simulator uzima 0,1 Ω).

The diode conducts only briefly, so its current is not steady but a train of strong pulses, much larger than the mean current. The larger C is, the shorter the conduction and the stronger the pulses. At switch-on an empty capacitor acts as a short circuit, so the diode must also withstand a large surge current. That is why a diode with a large margin is chosen. The height of the pulses is limited only by the internal resistance of the transformer and the diode (the simulator takes 0.1 Ω).

Pomisli prvo: kolika je talasanje ako se struja opterećenja udvostruči, a C ostane isti? A ako se udvostruči i C?Think first: what happens to the ripple if the load current is doubled and C stays the same? And if C is doubled too?

Za dvostruku struju talasanje se udvostruči (\( \Delta u \propto I \)). Ako se uz to udvostruči i C, talasanje se vrati na staru vrednost.

For a doubled current the ripple doubles (\( \Delta u \propto I \)). If C is doubled as well, the ripple returns to its old value.

Stabilizator (npr. tipa 7805 ili Zener dioda) traži da ulazni napon nikada ne padne ispod izlaznog plus nekoliko volti. Zato se talasanje ne gleda samo kao kvar: najniža tačka testere \( U_m - U_\gamma - \Delta u \) mora ostati iznad tog praga.

A regulator (e.g. a 7805 or a Zener diode) requires the input voltage to never fall below the output voltage plus a few volts. So the ripple is not just a defect: the lowest point of the sawtooth \( U_m - U_\gamma - \Delta u \) must stay above that threshold.

Deo 4Part 4 · kako se projektuje napajanjehow a power supply is designed

Izbor diode i kondenzatoraChoosing the diode and the capacitor

Zadatak je obično ovakav: transformator daje \( U_2 \), potrošaču treba struja \( I_{dc} \), a talasanje sme da bude najviše \( \Delta u \). Redosled rada:

The task is usually like this: the transformer gives \( U_2 \), the load needs the current \( I_{dc} \), and the ripple may be at most \( \Delta u \). The order of work:

  1. Vrh napona: \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \). Sve se dimenzioniše prema vrhu, a ne prema efektivnoj vrednosti.
  2. Kondenzator: \( C \ge \dfrac{I_{dc}}{f\,\Delta u} \), pa se zaokruži nagore na standardnu vrednost (red E6: 100, 150, 220, 330, 470, 680 …). Elektrolitski kondenzatori imaju toleranciju ±20 % i starenjem gube kapacitivnost.
  3. Napon kondenzatora: najmanje \( 1{,}5\,U_m \).
  4. Dioda: inverzni napon \( U_{RRM} \ge 2\,U_m \) (sa filtrom!), srednja struja \( I_{F(AV)} \) veća od \( I_{dc} \) (bolje bar dvostruko) i dovoljna udarna struja \( I_{FSM} \).
  5. Provera: \( U_{dc} \approx U_m - U_\gamma - \Delta u/2 \) i \( R = U_{dc}/I_{dc} \).
  1. Peak voltage: \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \). Everything is dimensioned for the peak, not for the RMS value.
  2. Capacitor: \( C \ge \dfrac{I_{dc}}{f\,\Delta u} \), then round up to a standard value (series E6: 100, 150, 220, 330, 470, 680 …). Electrolytic capacitors have a ±20 % tolerance and lose capacitance with age.
  3. Capacitor voltage: at least \( 1.5\,U_m \).
  4. Diode: reverse voltage \( U_{RRM} \ge 2\,U_m \) (with a filter!), mean current \( I_{F(AV)} \) larger than \( I_{dc} \) (better at least twice) and a sufficient surge current \( I_{FSM} \).
  5. Check: \( U_{dc} \approx U_m - U_\gamma - \Delta u/2 \) and \( R = U_{dc}/I_{dc} \).

Zašto je inverzni napon 2·Um?Why is the reverse voltage 2·Um?

Kondenzator drži vrh \( +U_m \) na katodi. U negativnom vrhu izvor daje \( -U_m \) na anodi. Napon na zakočenoj diodi je razlika, dakle \( U_m - (-U_m) = 2U_m \). Bez kondenzatora to je samo \( U_m \).

The capacitor holds the peak \( +U_m \) on the cathode. At the negative peak the source gives \( -U_m \) on the anode. The voltage across the blocked diode is the difference, so \( U_m - (-U_m) = 2U_m \). Without the capacitor it is only \( U_m \).

Primer 2 · silicijumska dioda i otpornikExample 2 · a silicon diode and a resistor

Dato: E = 5 V, R = 330 Ω, silicijumska dioda u propusnom smeru. Koliki su struja i snaga na otporniku i na diodi?

Given: E = 5 V, R = 330 Ω, a silicon diode in the forward direction. What are the current and the power on the resistor and on the diode?

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Dioda okrenuta naopako. Izlaz je nula (ili negativan). Traka na kućištu je katoda i ide ka plusu potrošača.
  • Zaboravljen pad od 0,7 V. Pri malim naponima (oko 5 V i manje) to je velik deo napona.
  • Dioda ili LED bez otpornika. Struju ograničava kolo, ne dioda. Bez otpornika dioda pregoreva.
  • Efektivna vrednost umesto vrha. „12 V” na transformatoru je efektivna vrednost, vrh je 17 V. Dioda, kondenzator i zasićenje se računaju prema vrhu.
  • Inverzni napon jednak Um. Sa kondenzatorom je inverzni napon do \( 2U_m \). Dioda 1N4148 (100 V) nije za mrežu.
  • Obrnut elektrolitski kondenzator. Ima plus i minus. Obrnut polaritet ga greje, naduva i može da ga raznese.
  • Premali radni napon kondenzatora. Kondenzator od 16 V na vrhu od 17 V ne sme da se koristi. Uzmi bar \( 1{,}5U_m \).
  • Talasanje zavisi od struje. Ako potrošač povuče više struje, \( \Delta u \) raste. Računaj uvek sa najvećom strujom.
  • Srednja i efektivna vrednost. DC voltmetar pokazuje \( U_{dc} \), a ne vrh i ne efektivnu vrednost. Uporedi sa vežbom 8.
  • Pogrešna sprega na osciloskopu. Talasanje od 1 V na 15 V jednosmernog napona se vidi tek sa AC spregom i malim V/div. Uporedi sa vežbom 9.
  • Mreža bez transformatora. Kolo nije galvanski odvojeno od mreže i opasno je po život. Eksperimentiši samo na malom naponu.
  • A reversed diode. The output is zero (or negative). The band on the case is the cathode and goes to the plus of the load.
  • Forgetting the 0.7 V drop. At small voltages (about 5 V and less) it is a large part of the voltage.
  • A diode or an LED without a resistor. The current is limited by the circuit, not by the diode. Without a resistor the diode burns out.
  • RMS instead of the peak. "12 V" on a transformer is the RMS value, the peak is 17 V. The diode, the capacitor and the saturation are calculated from the peak.
  • A reverse voltage equal to Um. With a capacitor the reverse voltage is up to \( 2U_m \). A 1N4148 diode (100 V) is not for the mains.
  • A reversed electrolytic capacitor. It has a plus and a minus. A wrong polarity heats it, bloats it, and it can burst.
  • Too low a capacitor voltage rating. A 16 V capacitor must not be used at a peak of 17 V. Take at least \( 1.5U_m \).
  • The ripple depends on the current. If the load draws more current, \( \Delta u \) grows. Always calculate with the largest current.
  • The average and the RMS value. A DC voltmeter shows \( U_{dc} \), not the peak and not the RMS value. Compare with exercise 8.
  • The wrong coupling on the oscilloscope. A ripple of 1 V on 15 V of direct voltage is only seen with AC coupling and a small V/div. Compare with exercise 9.
  • Mains without a transformer. The circuit is not galvanically isolated from the mains and it is dangerous to life. Experiment only at low voltage.

Deo 7Part 7

SažetakSummary

  • Dioda propušta struju od anode ka katodi kada je napon na njoj veći od praga \( U_\gamma \approx 0{,}7\ \mathrm{V} \) (Si), a u suprotnom smeru je zaustavlja. Razlog je osiromašeni sloj p-n spoja.
  • Dioda uvek mora imati otpornik (potrošač) na red, jer struju ne ograničava ona sama.
  • Poluperiodni usmerivač propušta jednu poluperiodu: \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \), \( U_{dc} = U_m/\pi \), \( U_{ef} = U_m/2 \), PIV = \( U_m \), učestanost talasanja 50 Hz, \( \gamma = 1{,}21 \), \( \eta = 40{,}5\ \% \).
  • Kondenzator paralelno sa opterećenjem izravnava napon: \( \Delta u \approx I_{dc}/(fC) \), \( U_{dc} \approx U_m - U_\gamma - \Delta u/2 \).
  • Sa filtrom dioda vodi kratkim jakim impulsima, a inverzni napon raste do \( 2U_m \).
  • Izbor: \( C \ge I_{dc}/(f\,\Delta u) \) (zaokruži nagore), \( U_C \ge 1{,}5U_m \), \( U_{RRM} \ge 2U_m \), \( I_{F(AV)} > I_{dc} \).
  • Merenje: DC voltmetar daje \( U_{dc} \), osciloskop sa DC spregom oblik, a sa AC spregom talasanje.
  • A diode passes current from the anode to the cathode when the voltage across it is above the threshold \( U_\gamma \approx 0.7\ \mathrm{V} \) (Si), and stops it in the opposite direction. The reason is the depletion layer of the p-n junction.
  • A diode must always have a resistor (a load) in series, because it does not limit the current itself.
  • A half-wave rectifier passes one half-cycle: \( U_m = U_2\sqrt{2} \), \( U_{dc} = U_m/\pi \), \( U_{rms} = U_m/2 \), PIV = \( U_m \), ripple frequency 50 Hz, \( \gamma = 1.21 \), \( \eta = 40.5\ \% \).
  • A capacitor in parallel with the load smooths the voltage: \( \Delta u \approx I_{dc}/(fC) \), \( U_{dc} \approx U_m - U_\gamma - \Delta u/2 \).
  • With a filter the diode conducts in short strong pulses, and the reverse voltage grows up to \( 2U_m \).
  • Selection: \( C \ge I_{dc}/(f\,\Delta u) \) (round up), \( U_C \ge 1.5U_m \), \( U_{RRM} \ge 2U_m \), \( I_{F(AV)} > I_{dc} \).
  • Measuring: a DC voltmeter gives \( U_{dc} \), an oscilloscope with DC coupling the shape, and with AC coupling the ripple.
