Šta zapravo pokazuje instrument za naizmenični naponWhat an instrument actually shows for an alternating voltage

Jednosmerni napon se ne menja, pa je jasno šta instrument pokazuje. Naizmenični napon se stalno menja, pa o njemu govorimo preko nekoliko različitih vrednosti. Za sinusni napon:

A direct voltage does not change, so it is clear what an instrument shows. An alternating voltage changes all the time, so we describe it with several different values. For a sine voltage:

\[ u(t) = U_m \sin(\omega t) = U_m \sin(2\pi f\,t) \]
  • Trenutna vrednost u(t) je vrednost napona u jednom trenutku.
  • Vršna vrednost (amplituda) Um je najveća vrednost. Razmak od najnižeg do najvišeg vrha je Upp = 2Um.
  • Perioda T je trajanje jednog punog ciklusa, a frekvencija f = 1/T je broj ciklusa u sekundi (Hz). Mreža u Srbiji ima 50 Hz, pa je T = 20 ms.
  • Efektivna vrednost U je ona vrednost jednosmernog napona koji bi na istom otporniku razvio istu snagu, to jest istu toplotu.
  • The instantaneous value u(t) is the voltage at one moment.
  • The peak value (amplitude) Um is the largest value. The distance from the lowest to the highest peak is Upp = 2Um.
  • The period T is the duration of one full cycle, and the frequency f = 1/T is the number of cycles per second (Hz). The mains in Serbia have 50 Hz, so T = 20 ms.
  • The RMS (effective) value U is the value of a direct voltage that would develop the same power, that is the same heat, in the same resistor.
\[ U = \frac{U_m}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0{,}707\,U_m \qquad I = \frac{I_m}{\sqrt{2}} \]

Snaga na otpornikuPower in a resistor

Ako koristiš efektivne vrednosti, sve formule su iste kao za jednosmernu struju: \( P = U\,I = I^2 R = U^2/R \) i \( I = U/R \).

If you use RMS values, all the formulas are the same as for direct current: \( P = U\,I = I^2 R = U^2/R \) and \( I = U/R \).

Za radoznale: odakle potiče √2?For the curious: where does √2 come from?

Efektivna vrednost je koren iz srednje vrednosti kvadrata, pa je za sinusoidu \( U = \sqrt{\dfrac{1}{T}\displaystyle\int_0^T U_m^2 \sin^2\omega t\,dt} = U_m\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2}} \), jer je srednja vrednost od \( \sin^2 \) tačno 1/2.

The RMS value is the square root of the mean of the square, so for a sine \( U = \sqrt{\dfrac{1}{T}\displaystyle\int_0^T U_m^2 \sin^2\omega t\,dt} = U_m\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2}} \), because the average of \( \sin^2 \) is exactly 1/2.

Deo 2Part 2 · šema vezethe circuit

Kako se vezuju ampermetar i voltmetarHow to connect an ammeter and a voltmeter

Ampermetar meri struju grane, pa kroz njega mora da prođe cela ta struja. Zato se vezuje redno: kolo se prekine na mestu gde merimo, a ampermetar se ubaci u prekid. Da ne bi menjao struju, treba da ima što manji unutrašnji otpor Ra (idealno nula).

An ammeter measures the current of a branch, so the whole current must pass through it. That is why it is connected in series: the circuit is broken where we measure, and the ammeter is inserted in the gap. To avoid changing the current, it should have as small an internal resistance Ra as possible (ideally zero).

Voltmetar meri razliku potencijala između dve tačke, pa se priključuje na njih, paralelno elementu, bez prekidanja kola. Da kroz njega ne bi odlazila struja, treba da ima što veći unutrašnji otpor Rv (idealno beskonačan).

A voltmeter measures the potential difference between two points, so it is connected across them, in parallel with the element, without breaking the circuit. To avoid drawing current away, it should have as large an internal resistance Rv as possible (ideally infinite).

Pravilo za pamćenjeRule to remember

Ampermetar u red, voltmetar uporedo. Kod naizmenične struje polaritet nije bitan, ali prekidač instrumenta mora biti na naizmeničnoj veličini (~).

Ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel. With alternating current the polarity does not matter, but the switch of the instrument must be set to the alternating quantity (~).

Nikad ne mešajNever mix them up

Ampermetar vezan paralelno sa izvorom ili otpornikom je kratak spoj, jer mu je otpor oko 1 Ω ili manje. Struja skače na desetine ampera i pregoreva instrument ili osigurač. Voltmetar vezan redno praktično prekida kolo, jer mu je otpor ogroman.

An ammeter connected in parallel with a source or a resistor is a short circuit, because its resistance is about 1 Ω or less. The current jumps to tens of amperes and burns out the instrument or a fuse. A voltmeter connected in series practically breaks the circuit, because its resistance is huge.

Izbor mernog opsegaChoosing the measuring range

  1. Ako ne znaš koju vrednost očekuješ, počni od najvećeg opsega. Kazaljka tako ne udari u ograničač.
  2. Smanjuj opseg dok kazaljka ne dođe u poslednju trećinu skale, a da ne pređe kraj.
  3. Kod digitalnog instrumenta uključi automatski opseg (AUTO) ili izaberi najmanji opseg koji ne pokazuje preopterećenje (OL).
  4. Proveri prekidač: ~ za naizmenične, ⎓ za jednosmerne veličine.
  1. If you do not know what value to expect, start from the largest range. That way the needle does not slam into the stop.
  2. Reduce the range until the needle is in the last third of the scale, without going past the end.
  3. On a digital instrument switch on the automatic range (AUTO) or choose the smallest range that does not show overload (OL).
  4. Check the switch: ~ for alternating, ⎓ for direct quantities.

Analogni instrumentThe analog instrument

Analogni instrument ima kazaljku koja se pomera preko skale. Dva najčešća tipa:

An analog instrument has a needle that moves over a scale. The two most common types:

MagnetoelektričniMoving-coil

Kalem u polju stalnog magneta. Otklon je srazmeran srednjoj vrednosti struje. Za naizmeničnu struju ispred kalema stoji ispravljač, a skala je baždarena da pokaže efektivnu vrednost samo za sinusoidu.

A coil in the field of a permanent magnet. The deflection is proportional to the average value of the current. For alternating current a rectifier stands in front of the coil, and the scale is calibrated to show the RMS value for a sine only.

ElektromagnetniMoving-iron

Komad mekog gvožđa u polju kalema. Otklon je srazmeran kvadratu struje, pa instrument direktno meri efektivnu vrednost. Skala nije ravnomerna. Koristi se za tablne instrumente na mreži.

A piece of soft iron in the field of a coil. The deflection is proportional to the square of the current, so the instrument measures the RMS value directly. The scale is not uniform. It is used for panel instruments on the mains.

Apsolutna greška Δmax je ista na celoj skali, pa je relativna greška najmanja pri kraju skale. Kazaljka na 10 % skale daje desetostruko veću relativnu grešku nego pri punom otklonu.

The scale carries other markings as well: the sign ~ for alternating, ⎓ for direct current, the accuracy class (for example 1.5) and the position in which the instrument should work (horizontal or upright). The correct position is a condition for the stated accuracy.

Deo 4Part 4 · cifre · tačnost · True RMSdigits · accuracy · True RMS

Digitalni multimetarThe digital multimeter

Digitalni instrument pomoću A/D konvertora pretvara napon u broj i prikazuje ga na displeju. Nema kazaljke ni paralakse, a čitanje je trenutno. Zato je u praksi veoma raširen, ali treba razumeti šta mu piše u podacima.

A digital instrument uses an A/D converter to turn the voltage into a number and shows it on a display. It has no needle and no parallax, and the reading is immediate. That is why it is so common in practice, but you need to understand what its specifications say.

  • 3½ cifre znači tri pune cifre (0 do 9) i jednu „polovinu” koja može biti samo 0 ili 1. Najveći prikaz je 1999.
  • Rezolucija r je vrednost poslednje cifre. Na opsegu 20 V displej prikazuje do 19,99 V, pa je r = 0,01 V. Na opsegu 200 V je r = 0,1 V.
  • Ulazni otpor je obično 10 MΩ na svim naponskim opsezima, pa digitalni voltmetar vrlo malo opterećuje kolo.
  • Automatski opseg (AUTO) sam bira najmanji opseg na kome se vrednost vidi.
  • 3½ digits means three full digits (0 to 9) and one "half" that can only be 0 or 1. The largest display is 1999.
  • The resolution r is the value of the last digit. On the 20 V range the display shows up to 19.99 V, so r = 0.01 V. On the 200 V range r = 0.1 V.
  • The input resistance is usually 10 MΩ on all voltage ranges, so a digital voltmeter loads the circuit very little.
  • The automatic range (AUTO) chooses the smallest range in which the value can be seen.

Naizmenični napon: srednja vrednost ili True RMSAlternating voltage: average-responding or True RMS

Većina jeftinih multimetara je „srednja vrednost × 1,11”: ispravi napon, izračuna srednju vrednost i pomnoži je faktorom oblika sinusoide 1,11. To je tačno samo za čistu sinusoidu. Instrument sa oznakom True RMS računa pravu efektivnu vrednost, pa je tačan i za trougaone, pravougaone i izobličene signale (struja iz napajanja računara, LED sijalica, regulatora).

Most cheap multimeters are "average × 1.11": they rectify the voltage, compute the average and multiply it by the form factor of a sine, 1.11. This is correct only for a pure sine. An instrument marked True RMS computes the real RMS value, so it is correct for triangle, square and distorted signals as well (the current of a computer power supply, an LED lamp, a dimmer).

Deo 5Part 5 · greška metodemethod error

Instrument menja ono što meriThe instrument changes what it measures

I instrument je otpornik. Kada ga priključimo, kolo koje merimo se menja, pa je rezultat malo drugačiji od vrednosti pre priključenja. To je greška metode, različita od greške instrumenta (klase tačnosti).

An instrument is a resistor too. When we connect it, the circuit we measure changes, so the result is slightly different from the value before connecting. This is the method error, different from the error of the instrument itself (the accuracy class).

Voltmetar je paralelan elementu, pa mu smanjuje otpor: umesto R deluje \( R' = \dfrac{R\,R_v}{R+R_v} \). Napon se zato smanji. Greška je mala samo ako je \( R_v \gg R \) (za 1 % treba \( R_v \ge 100\,R \)).

A voltmeter is parallel to an element, so it lowers its resistance: instead of R the element acts like \( R' = \dfrac{R\,R_v}{R+R_v} \). The voltage therefore drops. The error is small only if \( R_v \gg R \) (for 1 % you need \( R_v \ge 100\,R \)).

Unutrašnji otpor analognog voltmetra zavisi od opsega. Zadaje se kao osetljivost u kΩ/V: na opsegu 10 V instrument od 20 kΩ/V ima \( R_v = 20\ \mathrm{k\Omega/V}\cdot 10\ \mathrm{V} = 200\ \mathrm{k\Omega} \). Digitalni ima 10 MΩ na svakom opsegu.

The internal resistance of an analog voltmeter depends on the range. It is given as a sensitivity in kΩ/V: on the 10 V range an instrument of 20 kΩ/V has \( R_v = 20\ \mathrm{k\Omega/V}\cdot 10\ \mathrm{V} = 200\ \mathrm{k\Omega} \). A digital one has 10 MΩ on every range.

Ampermetar je redan, pa kolu dodaje svoj otpor Ra: \( I' = \dfrac{U}{R + R_a} \). Greška je mala samo ako je \( R_a \ll R \).

An ammeter is in series, so it adds its own resistance Ra to the circuit: \( I' = \dfrac{U}{R + R_a} \). The error is small only if \( R_a \ll R \).

Deo 6Part 6 · vežba korak po korakthe exercise step by step

Tok vežbeThe procedure

Cilj vežbe: izmeriti napon i struju na otporniku u kolu naizmenične struje analognim i digitalnim instrumentom, uporediti rezultate, izračunati otpor i snagu i proceniti greške merenja.

Aim of the exercise: to measure the voltage and current on a resistor in an AC circuit with an analog and a digital instrument, compare the results, calculate the resistance and the power, and estimate the measurement errors.

PriborEquipment

  • Izvor naizmeničnog napona 0 do 24 V (transformator sa regulacijom ili funkcijski generator), 50 Hz.
  • Otpornik oko 100 Ω, najmanje 5 W.
  • Analogni ampermetar i analogni voltmetar (~).
  • Digitalni multimetar.
  • Provodnici sa banana-utikačima.
  • An alternating voltage source 0 to 24 V (a regulating transformer or a function generator), 50 Hz.
  • A resistor of about 100 Ω, at least 5 W.
  • An analog ammeter and an analog voltmeter (~).
  • A digital multimeter.
  • Leads with banana plugs.

BezbednostSafety

Kolo sastavljaj samo kada je izvor isključen i podešen na nulu. Za vežbu koristi mali napon preko transformatora, nikad direktno mrežu od 230 V. Pre uključivanja neka nastavnik proveri vezu.

Build the circuit only when the source is switched off and set to zero. Use a low voltage through a transformer for the exercise, never the 230 V mains directly. Have the teacher check the connection before switching on.

KoraciSteps

  1. Nacrtaj šemu: izvor, ampermetar redno sa otpornikom, voltmetar paralelno otporniku.
  2. Izvor isključi i postavi na 0 V. Sastavi kolo prema šemi.
  3. Prekidače instrumenata postavi na naizmeničnu veličinu (~), a opsege na najveće.
  4. Uključi izvor i polako povećaj napon do oko 2 V. Smanji opsege tako da kazaljke budu u poslednjoj trećini skale.
  5. Pročitaj napon i struju na analognim i na digitalnom instrumentu, pa zapiši u tabelu opsege, broj podeoka i rezultate.
  6. Ponovi za još četiri napona (4, 6, 8 i 10 V). Promeni opsege kada je potrebno.
  7. Smanji napon na nulu, isključi izvor, pa tek onda rastavi kolo.
  8. Izračunaj \( R = U/I \) i \( P = U\,I \), a zatim greške (formule ispod).
  1. Draw the diagram: the source, the ammeter in series with the resistor, the voltmeter in parallel with the resistor.
  2. Switch the source off and set it to 0 V. Build the circuit according to the diagram.
  3. Set the switches of the instruments to the alternating quantity (~), and the ranges to the largest.
  4. Switch the source on and slowly raise the voltage to about 2 V. Reduce the ranges so the needles are in the last third of the scale.
  5. Read the voltage and the current on the analog and on the digital instrument, then write the ranges, the number of divisions and the results in the table.
  6. Repeat for four more voltages (4, 6, 8 and 10 V). Change the ranges when needed.
  7. Reduce the voltage to zero, switch the source off, and only then take the circuit apart.
  8. Calculate \( R = U/I \) and \( P = U\,I \), then the errors (formulas below).

Obrada rezultataProcessing the results

Digitalni instrument ima manju grešku, pa ga uzimamo kao referentnu vrednost. Za svaki red izračunaj:

The digital instrument has the smaller error, so we take it as the reference value. For every row calculate:

\[ \Delta U = U_a - U_d \qquad \delta U = \frac{\Delta U}{U_d}\cdot 100\,\% \qquad P = U_d\,I_d \]

Za red 3: \( \Delta U = 6{,}1 - 6{,}03 = 0{,}07\ \mathrm{V} \), \( \delta U = 0{,}07/6{,}03 \cdot 100\,\% = 1{,}2\,\% \), \( P = 6{,}03 \cdot 0{,}060 = 0{,}36\ \mathrm{W} \). Šta napisati u zaključkuWhat to write in the conclusion

Da li su se instrumenti slagali u okviru svojih klasa i kolika je bila najveća relativna greška. Zašto je greška veća pri malim naponima. Koliki je otpor otpornika i da li je približno stalan (Omov zakon važi za efektivne vrednosti). Šta bi se promenilo da su opsezi bili preveliki.

Whether the instruments agreed within their classes and how large the biggest relative error was. Why the error is larger at small voltages. What the resistance of the resistor is and whether it is roughly constant (Ohm's law holds for RMS values). What would have changed if the ranges had been too large.

Primer 2 · čitanje analognog instrumentaExample 2 · reading an analog instrument

Dato: voltmetar ima skalu od 50 podeoka. Kazaljka stoji na 36,5 podeoka. Koliki je napon na opsegu 50 V, a koliki na opsegu 10 V?

Given: a voltmeter has a scale of 50 divisions. The needle stands at 36.5 divisions. What is the voltage on the 50 V range, and on the 10 V range?

  1. Opseg 50 V: \( C = \dfrac{50}{50} = 1\ \mathrm{V/pod.} \), pa je \( U = 36{,}5 \cdot 1 = 36{,}5\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Opseg 10 V: \( C = \dfrac{10}{50} = 0{,}2\ \mathrm{V/pod.} \), pa je \( U = 36{,}5 \cdot 0{,}2 = 7{,}3\ \mathrm{V} \).
  1. Range 50 V: \( C = \dfrac{50}{50} = 1\ \mathrm{V/div.} \), so \( U = 36.5 \cdot 1 = 36.5\ \mathrm{V} \).
  2. Range 10 V: \( C = \dfrac{10}{50} = 0.2\ \mathrm{V/div.} \), so \( U = 36.5 \cdot 0.2 = 7.3\ \mathrm{V} \).

Deo 8Part 8 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Ampermetar paralelno. To je kratak spoj. Ampermetar je uvek redno, u prekidu kola.
  • Voltmetar redno. Praktično prekida kolo i ne pokazuje ono što misliš. Voltmetar ide paralelno elementu.
  • Jednosmerni opseg za naizmeničnu veličinu. Instrument će pokazati oko nule. Proveri prekidač ~ i ⎓.
  • Počinjanje od najmanjeg opsega. Kazaljka udari u ograničač, a kod digitalnog se može pregoreti osigurač. Počni od najvećeg.
  • Merenje na početku skale. Mali otklon znači veliku relativnu grešku. Smanji opseg dok ne uđeš u poslednju trećinu.
  • Zaboravljen opseg pri čitanju. Broj podeoka nije napon. Pomnoži sa konstantom opsega.
  • Paralaksa. Gledanje sa strane pomera kazaljku u odnosu na skalu. Pogledaj pravo, a kod ogledala poklopi kazaljku i njenu sliku.
  • Mešanje vršne i efektivne vrednosti. Instrument pokazuje efektivnu, a na osciloskopu čitaš vršnu. Pretvaraj ih sa √2.
  • Crvena sonda u priključku za struju dok meriš napon. To je ampermetar paralelno izvoru. Pre svakog merenja proveri gde su sonde.
  • Srednja vrednost × 1,11 kod nesinusnog signala. Greška može biti desetak procenata. Za takve signale koristi True RMS.
  • Zanemarivanje otpora instrumenta. U kolima sa velikim otporima analogni voltmetar „obara” napon.
  • An ammeter in parallel. That is a short circuit. An ammeter is always in series, in a gap of the circuit.
  • A voltmeter in series. It practically breaks the circuit and does not show what you think. A voltmeter goes in parallel with the element.
  • A DC range for an AC quantity. The instrument will show about zero. Check the ~ and ⎓ switch.
  • Starting with the smallest range. The needle slams into the stop, and on a digital one a fuse may blow. Start with the largest.
  • Measuring at the beginning of the scale. A small deflection means a large relative error. Reduce the range until you reach the last third.
  • Forgetting the range when reading. A number of divisions is not a voltage. Multiply by the constant of the range.
  • Parallax. Looking from the side moves the needle relative to the scale. Look straight on, and with a mirror cover the needle with its reflection.
  • Mixing peak and RMS values. The instrument shows RMS, and on an oscilloscope you read the peak. Convert with √2.
  • The red probe in the current jack while measuring voltage. That is an ammeter across the source. Check where the probes are before every measurement.
  • Average × 1.11 on a non-sine signal. The error can be around ten percent. Use True RMS for such signals.
  • Ignoring the resistance of the instrument. In circuits with large resistances an analog voltmeter "pulls down" the voltage.

Deo 9Part 9

SažetakSummary

  • Naizmenični napon ima trenutnu, vršnu, srednju i efektivnu vrednost. Instrumenti i mreža navode efektivnu: \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \). Za sinusoidu je \( U_{sr} = 2U_m/\pi \) i \( k_f = 1{,}11 \).
  • Ampermetar redno (mali otpor), voltmetar paralelno (veliki otpor). Obrnuto je kratak spoj ili prekid.
  • Opseg biraj od najvećeg ka manjem, do poslednje trećine skale. Prekidač mora biti na ~ za naizmenične veličine.
  • Analogno čitanje: \( X = \alpha \cdot C \), \( C = X_n/\alpha_{max} \). Klasa tačnosti \( k \): \( \Delta_{max} = k X_n/100 \), a relativna greška \( \delta = \Delta_{max}/X \) raste kada je kazaljka blizu početka skale.
  • Digitalno: tačnost ±(a % očitavanja + b cifara), rezolucija zavisi od opsega. Za nesinusne signale potreban je True RMS.
  • Instrument ima unutrašnji otpor i menja kolo. Voltmetar: \( R_v \gg R \), ampermetar: \( R_a \ll R \).
  • An alternating voltage has instantaneous, peak, average and RMS values. Instruments and the mains quote the RMS value: \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \). For a sine \( U_{sr} = 2U_m/\pi \) and \( k_f = 1.11 \).
  • The ammeter in series (small resistance), the voltmeter in parallel (large resistance). The other way round gives a short circuit or a break.
  • Choose the range from the largest down, to the last third of the scale. The switch must be on ~ for alternating quantities.
  • Analog reading: \( X = \alpha \cdot C \), \( C = X_n/\alpha_{max} \). Accuracy class \( k \): \( \Delta_{max} = k X_n/100 \), and the relative error \( \delta = \Delta_{max}/X \) grows when the needle is near the start of the scale.
  • Digital: the accuracy is ±(a % of reading + b digits), the resolution depends on the range. For non-sine signals True RMS is needed.
  • An instrument has an internal resistance and changes the circuit. Voltmeter: \( R_v \gg R \), ammeter: \( R_a \ll R \).
