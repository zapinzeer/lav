Deo 1Part 1 · mnogo zavojaka jedan do drugogmany turns side by side

Kalem (solenoid)The coil (solenoid)

Kada žicu namotamo u mnogo zavojaka, dobijamo kalem (solenoid). Polja svih zavojaka se sabiraju, pa je unutar kalema polje jako i skoro homogeno. Spolja, kalem sa strujom ponaša se kao šipkasti magnet sa severnim i južnim polom.

Wind a wire into many turns and you get a coil (solenoid). The fields of all the turns add up, so inside the coil the field is strong and almost uniform. From the outside, a coil with a current behaves like a bar magnet with a north and a south pole.

Odnos N/l je broj zavojaka po metru, koji često označavamo sa n. Tada je \( B=\mu_0 n I \). Polje zavisi od gustine zavojaka, a ne od prečnika kalema.

The ratio N/l is the number of turns per metre, often written n. Then \( B=\mu_0 n I \). The field depends on the density of the turns, not on the diameter of the coil.

Gde je severni pol?Where is the north pole?

Obuhvati kalem desnom šakom tako da savijeni prsti prate smer struje kroz zavojke. Ispruženi palac pokazuje severni pol N.

Grip the coil with your right hand so your curled fingers follow the current around the turns. Your outstretched thumb points to the north pole N.

Polje van kalemaThe field outside the coil

Formula B = μ0N I / l važi u sredini dugačkog kalema, kada je dužina mnogo veća od prečnika. Na krajevima kalema polje je otprilike upola slabije, a daleko od kalema linije polja se šire i polje brzo opada, baš kao kod šipkastog magneta.

The formula B = μ0N I / l holds in the middle of a long coil, when the length is much larger than the diameter. At the ends of the coil the field is about half as strong, and far from the coil the field lines spread out and the field falls quickly, just like for a bar magnet.

Pomisli prvo: kalem ima 200 zavojaka na 10 cm. Šta se desi sa poljem ako isti kalem razvučemo na 20 cm, a struja ostane ista?Think first: a coil has 200 turns over 10 cm. What happens to the field if we stretch the same coil to 20 cm with the same current?

Broj zavojaka po metru se prepolovi (n = N/l pada sa 2000 na 1000 po metru), pa se i polje prepolovi.

The number of turns per metre is halved (n = N/l falls from 2000 to 1000 per metre), so the field is halved too.

Deo 2Part 2 · kalem + gvožđecoil + iron

Gvozdeno jezgro i zasićenjeThe iron core and saturation

Ako u kalem stavimo gvozdeno jezgro, gvožđe se namagnetiše i polje postaje μr puta jače. Kalem sa gvozdenim jezgrom zovemo elektromagnet.

If we put an iron core into the coil, the iron becomes magnetised and the field gets μr times stronger. A coil with an iron core is called an electromagnet.

Ali gvožđe ne može da se namagnetiše neograničeno. Kada su svi njegovi mali magneti (domeni) poređani u istom smeru, dalje povećanje struje više ne pomaže. To se zove zasićenje. Kod gvožđa se dešava već oko 1,5 do 2 T.

But iron cannot be magnetised without limit. When all of its tiny magnets (domains) are lined up in the same direction, increasing the current further no longer helps. This is called saturation. For iron it happens at about 1.5 to 2 T.

Zašto elektromagnet?Why an electromagnet?

  • Može da se uključi i isključi.
  • Jačina se podešava strujom i brojem zavojaka.
  • Kada se smer struje obrne, polovi zamene mesta.
  • It can be switched on and off.
  • Its strength is set by the current and the number of turns.
  • When the current is reversed, the poles swap places.

Za radoznale: koliko jako držiFor the curious: how strongly it holds

Sila kojom elektromagnet privlači gvozdeni predmet na površini preseka S je približno \( F = \dfrac{B^2 S}{2\mu_0} \). Za B = 1 T i S = 10 cm² dobija se oko 400 N, što je težina tela od oko 40 kg. Sila raste sa kvadratom indukcije.

The force with which an electromagnet attracts an iron object across a cross-section S is approximately \( F = \dfrac{B^2 S}{2\mu_0} \). For B = 1 T and S = 10 cm² this gives about 400 N, the weight of a mass of about 40 kg. The force grows with the square of the induction.

Deo 3Part 3 · primenaapplication

Relej: mala struja upravlja velikomThe relay: a small current controls a large one

Relej je prekidač kojim upravlja elektromagnet. Slaba struja kroz kalem namagnetiše jezgro, jezgro privuče gvozdenu kotvu, a kotva zatvori kontakt u drugom, radnom kolu. Tako mala struja može da uključi motor, grejač ili auto-starter, a da se oni ne dodiruju sa osetljivom elektronikom.

A relay is a switch operated by an electromagnet. A weak current through the coil magnetises the core, the core pulls in an iron armature, and the armature closes a contact in a second, load circuit. In this way a small current can switch on a motor, a heater or a car starter, without those touching the sensitive electronics.

  1. Zatvori prekidač: struja teče kroz kalem.
  2. Jezgro postaje magnet i privlači kotvu.
  3. Kotva zatvara kontakt radnog kola, lampa svetli.
  4. Otvori prekidač: nema struje, opruga vraća kotvu i kontakt se otvara.
  1. Close the switch: a current flows through the coil.
  2. The core becomes a magnet and pulls in the armature.
  3. The armature closes the contact of the load circuit, the lamp lights.
  4. Open the switch: no current, the spring pulls the armature back and the contact opens.

Gde se još koriste elektromagnetiWhere else electromagnets are used

  • Dizalica za staro gvožđe: uključi se struja i teret se podigne, isključi se i teret pada.
  • Električno zvono: elektromagnet privlači čekić, ali tim pokretom prekida sopstveno kolo, pa se čekić vraća i ciklus se ponavlja.
  • Zvučnik: kalem u polju magneta se pomera i pokreće membranu.
  • MRI aparat u bolnici: jak superprovodni elektromagnet stvara polje od 1,5 do 3 T.
  • Voz na magnetnoj levitaciji: elektromagneti ga podižu iznad šine.
  • Scrapyard crane: switch the current on and the load lifts, switch it off and the load drops.
  • Electric bell: the electromagnet pulls the hammer, but that motion breaks its own circuit, so the hammer springs back and the cycle repeats.
  • Loudspeaker: a coil in a magnet's field moves and drives the diaphragm.
  • Hospital MRI scanner: a strong superconducting electromagnet creates a field of 1.5 to 3 T.
  • Magnetic levitation train: electromagnets lift it above the track.

Deo 4Part 4 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · kalem bez jezgraExample 1 · a coil without a core

Dato: kalem ima N = 500 zavojaka i dužinu l = 20 cm. Kroz njega teče struja I = 2 A. Traži se B u unutrašnjosti.

Given: a coil has N = 500 turns and length l = 20 cm. A current I = 2 A flows through it. Find B inside.

  1. Dužina u metrima: l = 0,2 m.
  2. Broj zavojaka po metru: \( n = N/l = 500/0{,}2 = 2500\ \mathrm{m^{-1}} \).
  3. \( B = \mu_0 n I = 4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 2500\cdot 2 = 6{,}28\cdot 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{T} \)
  1. Length in metres: l = 0.2 m.
  2. Turns per metre: \( n = N/l = 500/0.2 = 2500\ \mathrm{m^{-1}} \).
  3. \( B = \mu_0 n I = 4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 2500\cdot 2 = 6.28\cdot 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{T} \)

B ≈ 6,3 mTB ≈ 6.3 mT

Primer 2 · isti kalem sa gvozdenim jezgromExample 2 · the same coil with an iron core

U kalem iz Primera 1 stavimo jezgro relativne permeabilnosti μᵣ = 200. Traži se novo B.

An iron core with relative permeability μᵣ = 200 is put into the coil of Example 1. Find the new B.

  1. Jezgro množi polje sa μᵣ: \( B = \mu_r\,\mu_0 n I = 200\cdot 6{,}28\cdot 10^{-3} \).
  2. \( B = 1{,}26\ \mathrm{T} \)
  3. To je ispod zasićenja gvožđa (oko 1,6 T), pa je račun realan.
  1. The core multiplies the field by μᵣ: \( B = \mu_r\,\mu_0 n I = 200\cdot 6.28\cdot 10^{-3} \).
  2. \( B = 1.26\ \mathrm{T} \)
  3. This is below the saturation of iron (about 1.6 T), so the calculation is realistic.

B ≈ 1,26 T, dvesta puta jače nego bez jezgra.B ≈ 1.26 T, two hundred times stronger than without the core.

Primer 3 · koliko zavojakaExample 3 · how many turns

Dato: kalem bez jezgra dužine l = 10 cm treba da stvori B = 20 mT pri struji I = 1,5 A. Koliko zavojaka ima?

Given: a coil without a core, length l = 10 cm, must create B = 20 mT with a current I = 1.5 A. How many turns does it need?

  1. Iz formule izrazimo broj zavojaka: \( N = \dfrac{B\,l}{\mu_0 I} \).
  2. Jedinice: B = 0,02 T, l = 0,1 m.
  3. \( N = \dfrac{0{,}02\cdot 0{,}1}{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 1{,}5} = \dfrac{0{,}002}{1{,}885\cdot 10^{-6}} \approx 1061 \)
  1. Solve the formula for the number of turns: \( N = \dfrac{B\,l}{\mu_0 I} \).
  2. Units: B = 0.02 T, l = 0.1 m.
  3. \( N = \dfrac{0.02\cdot 0.1}{4\pi\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 1.5} = \dfrac{0.002}{1.885\cdot 10^{-6}} \approx 1061 \)

Primer 4 · koji je kraj severniExample 4 · which end is north

Gledamo kalem sa leve strane. Struja kroz zavojke koje vidimo ide odozgo nadole na prednjoj strani kalema. Koji kraj je severni pol, levi ili desni?

We look at a coil from the front. The current in the turns we can see goes from top to bottom on the front side of the coil. Which end is the north pole, the left or the right?

  1. Desnom šakom obuhvatimo kalem tako da prsti na prednjoj strani idu nadole.
  2. Ispruženi palac tada pokazuje udesno.
  3. Palac pokazuje severni pol.
  1. Wrap the right hand around the coil so the fingers on the front side point downwards.
  2. The outstretched thumb then points to the right.
  3. The thumb points to the north pole.

Desni kraj je severni pol (N), levi je južni (S). Ako se struja obrne, polovi zamene mesta.The right end is the north pole (N), the left end is the south (S). If the current is reversed, the poles swap.

Deo 5Part 5 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • l nije dužina žice. U formuli je l dužina kalema, a ne dužina žice kojom je namotan. Žica može biti dugačka desetine metara, a kalem samo nekoliko centimetara.
  • Centimetri. Dužinu kalema pretvori u metre: 20 cm = 0,2 m. Inače B ispadne sto puta pogrešno.
  • Zaboravljeno μᵣ. Kod elektromagneta formula ima faktor μᵣ. Bez njega dobijaš polje kalema u vazduhu.
  • Jezgro ne povećava struju. Struju određuje baterija i otpor žice. Jezgro samo pojačava polje za istu struju.
  • Pogrešan pol. Ne mešaj pravilo za pravi provodnik (palac je smer struje) sa pravilom za kalem (palac je smer polja, severni pol).
  • Zasićenje. Ako račun da B od 5 T za gvozdeno jezgro, rezultat nije realan. Gvožđe se zasiti oko 1,6 T.
  • l is not the length of the wire. In the formula l is the length of the coil, not the length of the wire it is wound from. The wire may be tens of metres long while the coil is only a few centimetres.
  • Centimetres. Convert the length of the coil to metres: 20 cm = 0.2 m. Otherwise B is wrong by a factor of a hundred.
  • Forgetting μᵣ. For an electromagnet the formula has the factor μᵣ. Without it you get the field of an air-core coil.
  • The core does not raise the current. The current is set by the battery and the resistance of the wire. The core only strengthens the field for the same current.
  • Wrong pole. Do not mix the rule for a straight wire (the thumb is the current) with the rule for a coil (the thumb is the field, the north pole).
  • Saturation. If your calculation gives a B of 5 T for an iron core, the result is not realistic. Iron saturates at about 1.6 T.
