  • calculate the force F = B I l sin α and check the units;
  • use the left-hand rule to find the direction of the force, also when the wire is shown end-on (⊙ and ⊗);
  • say when the force is largest and when it is zero;
  • explain why two parallel wires attract or repel each other and calculate the force per metre of length.

Deo 1Part 1 · oznaka F · jedinica njutn (N)symbol F · unit newton (N)

Sila na provodnik sa strujomThe force on a current-carrying wire

Struja je kretanje naelektrisanih čestica. Kada se one kreću u magnetnom polju, polje na svaku od njih deluje silom, a zbir tih sila deluje na ceo provodnik. To je Amperova sila. Provodnik u kome nema struje, ili koji miruje van polja, nema na šta da deluje.

A current is the motion of charged particles. When they move in a magnetic field, the field exerts a force on each of them, and the sum of those forces acts on the whole wire. This is the Ampère force. A wire with no current, or one outside the field, has nothing to act on.

Za provodnik koji je normalan na polje je α = 90°, sin α = 1, pa je sila najveća: F = B I l. Iz ove formule sledi da je tesla jedinica N/(A·m).

For a wire perpendicular to the field α = 90°, sin α = 1, so the force is largest: F = B I l. This formula shows that the tesla is the unit N/(A·m).

Pravilo leve rukeLeft-hand rule

Postavi levu ruku tako da linije polja ulaze u dlan, a ispruženi prsti pokazuju smer struje. Palac, ispružen u stranu, pokazuje smer sile.

Hold your left hand so the field lines enter your palm and your outstretched fingers point along the current. Your thumb, held out to the side, shows the direction of the force.

Na crtežima često gledamo provodnik „sa kraja”. Ako je polje u ravni crteža, a struja izlazi ili ulazi, sila je u ravni crteža i normalna na polje:

In drawings we often look at the wire "end-on". If the field is in the plane of the page and the current comes out of or goes into the page, the force is in the plane of the page and perpendicular to the field:

Ako obrneš struju ili polje, sila menja smer. Ako obrneš oba odjednom, smer sile ostaje isti.

If you reverse the current or the field, the force reverses. If you reverse both at once, the direction of the force stays the same.

Deo 2Part 2 · sin αsin α

Kako sila zavisi od uglaHow the force depends on the angle

Samo komponenta struje koja je normalna na polje daje silu. Zato se u formuli pojavljuje sin α, gde je α ugao između provodnika i linija polja.

Only the component of the current that is perpendicular to the field gives a force. That is why sin α appears in the formula, where α is the angle between the wire and the field lines.

α = 0°

F = 0

Provodnik je paralelan sa poljem. Nema sile.

The wire is parallel to the field. No force.

α = 30°

F = ½ B I l

sin 30° = 0,5, polovina najveće sile.

sin 30° = 0.5, half of the maximum force.

α = 90°

F = B I l

Provodnik je normalan na polje. Sila je najveća.

The wire is perpendicular to the field. The force is largest.

Smer sile je uvek normalan i na provodnik i na polje. Sila nikada nije paralelna sa poljem, niti sa strujom.

The force is always perpendicular to both the wire and the field. It is never parallel to the field or to the current.

Pomisli prvo: provodnik dužine 20 cm nalazi se u polju od 0,5 T, a kroz njega teče 5 A. Pod kojim uglom je sila jednaka nuli?Think first: a 20 cm wire sits in a 0.5 T field and carries 5 A. At which angle is the force zero?

Za α = 0° (i 180°), kada je provodnik paralelan sa linijama polja. Bez obzira na to koliko su veliki B i I, sin 0° = 0.

At α = 0° (and 180°), when the wire is parallel to the field lines. No matter how large B and I are, sin 0° = 0.

Deo 3Part 3 · struje deluju jedna na drugucurrents act on each other

Dva paralelna provodnikaTwo parallel wires

Prvi provodnik stvara magnetno polje B1 = μ0I1 / (2πd) na mestu drugog provodnika. To polje deluje Amperovom silom na struju I2. Isto važi i obrnuto, pa provodnici deluju jedan na drugi silama jednakog intenziteta i suprotnog smera (zakon akcije i reakcije).

The first wire creates a magnetic field B1 = μ0I1 / (2πd) at the position of the second wire. That field acts on the current I2 through the Ampère force. The same holds the other way round, so the wires act on each other with forces of equal magnitude and opposite direction (action and reaction).

Polje je normalno na provodnik, pa je α = 90° i sila na dužinu l je F = B1I2l. Sila po jedinici dužine je:

The field is perpendicular to the wire, so α = 90° and the force on a length l is F = B1I2l. The force per unit length is:

Isti smer privlačiSame direction attracts

Struje istog smera se privlače. Struje suprotnog smera se odbijaju. (Kod naelektrisanja je obrnuto: istoimena se odbijaju.)

Currents in the same direction attract. Currents in opposite directions repel. (For electric charges it is the other way round: like charges repel.)

Ova pojava je nekada služila za definiciju ampera: struja koja kroz dva beskonačno dugačka provodnika na rastojanju 1 m daje silu 2·10⁻⁷ N po metru dužine iznosi 1 A. Danas se amper definiše preko elementarnog naelektrisanja, ali ta vrednost sile i dalje važi.

This effect was once used to define the ampere: the current that gives a force of 2·10⁻⁷ N per metre of length in two infinitely long wires 1 m apart is 1 A. Today the ampere is defined through the elementary charge, but that value of the force still holds.

Deo 4Part 4 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · provodnik normalan na poljeExample 1 · a wire perpendicular to the field

Dato: B = 0,8 T, I = 6 A, l = 15 cm, provodnik je normalan na linije polja. Traži se F.

Given: B = 0.8 T, I = 6 A, l = 15 cm, the wire is perpendicular to the field lines. Find F.

  1. Dužina u metrima: l = 0,15 m.
  2. Normalan provodnik znači α = 90°, pa je sin α = 1.
  3. \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha = 0{,}8\cdot 6\cdot 0{,}15\cdot 1 = 0{,}72\ \mathrm{N} \)
  1. Length in metres: l = 0.15 m.
  2. A perpendicular wire means α = 90°, so sin α = 1.
  3. \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha = 0.8\cdot 6\cdot 0.15\cdot 1 = 0.72\ \mathrm{N} \)

F = 0,72 NF = 0.72 N

Primer 2 · nagnut provodnikExample 2 · a tilted wire

Dato: B = 0,5 T, I = 10 A, l = 40 cm, ugao između provodnika i polja je α = 30°. Traži se F.

Given: B = 0.5 T, I = 10 A, l = 40 cm, the angle between the wire and the field is α = 30°. Find F.

  1. l = 0,4 m, sin 30° = 0,5.
  2. \( F = 0{,}5\cdot 10\cdot 0{,}4\cdot 0{,}5 = 1\ \mathrm{N} \)
  1. l = 0.4 m, sin 30° = 0.5.
  2. \( F = 0.5\cdot 10\cdot 0.4\cdot 0.5 = 1\ \mathrm{N} \)

F = 1 N. Da je provodnik normalan na polje, sila bi bila 2 N.F = 1 N. If the wire were perpendicular to the field, the force would be 2 N.

Primer 3 · ubrzanje šipkeExample 3 · acceleration of a rod

Dato: šipka mase m = 40 g i dužine l = 20 cm leži normalno na polje B = 0,4 T. Struja je I = 5 A. Zanemari trenje. Traži se ubrzanje.

Given: a rod of mass m = 40 g and length l = 20 cm lies perpendicular to a field B = 0.4 T. The current is I = 5 A. Ignore friction. Find the acceleration.

  1. Jedinice: m = 0,04 kg, l = 0,2 m.
  2. \( F = B\,I\,l = 0{,}4\cdot 5\cdot 0{,}2 = 0{,}4\ \mathrm{N} \)
  3. Drugi Njutnov zakon: \( a = \dfrac{F}{m} = \dfrac{0{,}4}{0{,}04} = 10\ \mathrm{m/s^2} \)
  1. Units: m = 0.04 kg, l = 0.2 m.
  2. \( F = B\,I\,l = 0.4\cdot 5\cdot 0.2 = 0.4\ \mathrm{N} \)
  3. Newton's second law: \( a = \dfrac{F}{m} = \dfrac{0.4}{0.04} = 10\ \mathrm{m/s^2} \)

a = 10 m/s², približno kao slobodan pad.a = 10 m/s², about the same as free fall.

Primer 4 · dva provodnikaExample 4 · two wires

Dato: dva paralelna provodnika na rastojanju d = 2 cm, u oba teče I = 50 A u istom smeru. Traži se sila po metru dužine i da li se privlače ili odbijaju.

Given: two parallel wires d = 2 cm apart, both carrying I = 50 A in the same direction. Find the force per metre of length and whether they attract or repel.

  1. d = 0,02 m.
  2. \( \dfrac{F}{l} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d} = \dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 50\cdot 50}{0{,}02} = 0{,}025\ \mathrm{N/m} \)
  3. Struje su istog smera, pa se provodnici privlače.
  1. d = 0.02 m.
  2. \( \dfrac{F}{l} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d} = \dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-7}\cdot 50\cdot 50}{0.02} = 0.025\ \mathrm{N/m} \)
  3. The currents are in the same direction, so the wires attract.

F/l = 25 mN/m, privlačenje.F/l = 25 mN/m, attraction.

Deo 5Part 5 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Sila nije duž polja. Amperova sila je uvek normalna na linije polja. Ne očekuj da provodnik „klizi” duž linija polja.
  • Pogrešan ugao. U formuli je α ugao između provodnika i polja. Ako je provodnik normalan na polje, α = 90°, a ne 0°.
  • Centimetri. Dužinu provodnika pretvori u metre pre računanja.
  • Dužina u polju. Računa se samo deo provodnika koji je u polju, ne cela dužina žice.
  • Leva ili desna ruka. Za silu koristimo pravilo leve ruke. Pravilo desne ruke koristimo za smer polja oko provodnika.
  • Paralelne struje. Istosmerne struje se privlače, suprotnosmerne odbijaju. Kod naelektrisanja je obrnuto, pa lako dođe do zabune.
  • The force is not along the field. The Ampère force is always perpendicular to the field lines. Do not expect the wire to "slide" along the field lines.
  • Wrong angle. In the formula α is the angle between the wire and the field. If the wire is perpendicular to the field, α = 90°, not 0°.
  • Centimetres. Convert the length of the wire to metres before calculating.
  • Length in the field. Only the part of the wire that is in the field counts, not the whole length of the wire.
  • Left or right hand. For the force we use the left-hand rule. The right-hand rule is for the direction of the field around a wire.
  • Parallel currents. Currents in the same direction attract, in opposite directions repel. For electric charges it is the other way round, so it is easy to mix up.

SažetakSummary

  • Magnetno polje deluje na provodnik sa strujom Amperovom silom: \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha \). 1 T = 1 N/(A·m).
  • Sila je najveća za α = 90°, a nula za provodnik paralelan sa poljem (α = 0°).
  • Smer sile daje pravilo leve ruke. Sila je normalna i na provodnik i na polje.
  • Dva paralelna provodnika: \( \dfrac{F}{l} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d} \). Istosmerne struje se privlače, suprotnosmerne odbijaju.
  • A magnetic field exerts the Ampère force on a current-carrying wire: \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha \). 1 T = 1 N/(A·m).
  • The force is largest for α = 90° and zero for a wire parallel to the field (α = 0°).
  • The direction of the force is given by the left-hand rule. The force is perpendicular to both the wire and the field.
  • Two parallel wires: \( \dfrac{F}{l} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d} \). Currents in the same direction attract, in opposite directions repel.
