Deo 1Part 1 · od krive do kolafrom curves to a circuit

Zašto ekvivalentna šemaWhy an equivalent circuit

Tranzistor je nelinearna komponenta: njegove struje zavise od napona eksponencijalno, a karakteristike su krive. Računati direktno sa krivama je teško. Zato tranzistor zamenjujemo ekvivalentnom šemom: kolom od jednostavnih elemenata (izvora i otpornika) koje se, u određenim uslovima, spolja ponaša isto kao tranzistor. Posle toga kolo rešavamo Omovim i Kirhofovim zakonima, kao svako drugo.

A transistor is a nonlinear component: its currents depend on the voltages exponentially, and its characteristics are curves. Calculating directly with curves is hard. That is why we replace the transistor with an equivalent circuit: a circuit of simple elements (sources and resistors) that, under certain conditions, behaves from the outside the same as the transistor. After that we solve the circuit with Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws, like any other.

Ekvivalentna šema važi samo za jedan režim rada. U ovoj lekciji gledamo aktivni režim, u kom tranzistor pojačava. Pojačavač analiziramo u dva koraka:

An equivalent circuit holds only for one operating mode. In this lesson we look at the active mode, in which the transistor amplifies. We analyse an amplifier in two steps:

  1. Jednosmerna analiza: sa jednosmernim modelom nalazimo radnu tačku Q (IB, IC, UCE).
  2. Analiza za male signale: sa modelom za male signale, čiji parametri zavise od Q, računamo pojačanje i otpornosti.
  1. DC analysis: with the DC model we find the operating point Q (IB, IC, UCE).
  2. Small-signal analysis: with the small-signal model, whose parameters depend on Q, we calculate the gain and the resistances.

Deo 2Part 2 · 0,7 V i βIB0.7 V and βIB

Jednosmerni modelThe DC model

U aktivnom režimu spoj B–E je direktno polarisan i ponaša se kao dioda koja vodi: napon na njemu je skoro stalan, oko 0,7 V za silicijum. Zamenjujemo ga naponskim izvorom UBE ≈ 0,7 V. Kolektorska struja ne zavisi od UCE, već samo od bazne struje, pa izlaz zamenjujemo strujnim izvorom βIB koji je upravljan strujom baze.

In the active mode the B–E junction is forward biased and behaves like a conducting diode: the voltage across it is almost constant, about 0.7 V for silicon. We replace it with a voltage source UBE ≈ 0.7 V. The collector current does not depend on UCE, only on the base current, so we replace the output with a current source βIB controlled by the base current.

\[ U_{BE} = U_{BE0} \qquad I_C = \beta I_B \qquad I_B > 0 \qquad U_{CE} > U_{CEsat} \]

Posle računanja uvek proveri uslove. Ako je IB ≤ 0, tranzistor je zakočen; ako je UCE manji od oko 0,2 V (ili čak negativan), u zasićenju je, i treba koristiti model iz sledeće lekcije.

After the calculation always check the conditions. If IB ≤ 0, the transistor is cut off; if UCE is below about 0.2 V (or even negative), it is in saturation, and the model from the next lesson must be used.

Tačniji modelA more accurate model

Ponekad se izvoru od 0,7 V dodaje i mala otpornost baze rbb' (desetine do stotine oma), a strujnom izvoru paralelno izlazna otpornost ro. Za većinu školskih zadataka dovoljni su izvor 0,7 V i βIB.

Sometimes a small base resistance rbb' (tens to hundreds of ohms) is added to the 0.7 V source, and an output resistance ro in parallel with the current source. For most school problems the 0.7 V source and βIB are enough.

Deo 3Part 3 · hibridni π modelthe hybrid-π model

Model za male signaleThe small-signal model

Pojačavač pojačava male promene oko radne tačke. Kada se UBE promeni za mali iznos ube, bazna struja se promeni za ib, a kolektorska za ic. Za male promene kriva se može zameniti svojom tangentom u tački Q, pa veze postaju linearne. Tako nastaje hibridni π model:

An amplifier amplifies small changes around the operating point. When UBE changes by a small amount ube, the base current changes by ib, and the collector current by ic. For small changes a curve can be replaced by its tangent at the point Q, so the relations become linear. That gives the hybrid-π model:

\[ g_m = \frac{I_C}{U_T} \qquad r_\pi = \frac{\beta}{g_m} = \frac{\beta\,U_T}{I_C} \qquad r_o = \frac{U_A}{I_C} \qquad i_c = g_m u_{be} = \beta\,i_b \]

Važno je da parametri zavise od radne tačke: dvostruko veća struja IC daje dvostruko veću strminu i dvostruko manje rπ i ro. Zato se prvo uvek radi jednosmerna analiza.

It is important that the parameters depend on the operating point: a collector current IC twice as large gives twice the transconductance and half the rπ and ro. That is why the DC analysis is always done first.

Mala slova, velika slovaSmall letters, capital letters

Po dogovoru, velika slova sa velikim indeksima (IC, UBE) su jednosmerne vrednosti u radnoj tački, a mala slova sa malim indeksima (ic, ube) su male promene, signal. U modelu za male signale jednosmerni izvori se kratko spajaju (njihova promena je nula), pa UCC postaje masa.

By convention, capital letters with capital subscripts (IC, UBE) are DC values at the operating point, and small letters with small subscripts (ic, ube) are small changes, the signal. In the small-signal model the DC sources are shorted (their change is zero), so UCC becomes ground.

Deo 4Part 4 · h11e, h12e, h21e, h22eh11e, h12e, h21e, h22e

h-parametrih-parameters

U katalozima i mnogim udžbenicima tranzistor se opisuje hibridnim (h) parametrima, kao četvoropol sa ulazom B–E i izlazom C–E. Indeks „e” znači spoj sa zajedničkim emitorom. Jednačine su:

In datasheets and many textbooks a transistor is described by hybrid (h) parameters, as a two-port network with the input B–E and the output C–E. The subscript "e" means common emitter. The equations are:

\[ u_{be} = h_{11e}\,i_b + h_{12e}\,u_{ce} \qquad i_c = h_{21e}\,i_b + h_{22e}\,u_{ce} \]

Naponsko pojačanjeVoltage gain

Kada se na kolektor priključi otpornik RC, izlazni napon je \( u_{iz} = -g_m u_{be}(R_C \parallel r_o) \), pa je pojačanje stepena sa zajedničkim emitorom \( A_u = -g_m (R_C \parallel r_o) \approx -g_m R_C \). Minus znači da je izlaz u protivfazi sa ulazom.

When a resistor RC is connected to the collector, the output voltage is \( u_{out} = -g_m u_{be}(R_C \parallel r_o) \), so the gain of a common-emitter stage is \( A_u = -g_m (R_C \parallel r_o) \approx -g_m R_C \). The minus means the output is in antiphase with the input.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Model bez provere. Jednosmerni model aktivnog režima važi samo ako je IB > 0 i UCE > UCEsat. Proveri na kraju.
  • Mešanje modela. Izvor 0,7 V pripada jednosmernom modelu. U modelu za male signale ga nema: tu je rπ.
  • rπ kao stalna vrednost. rπ i gm zavise od IC; prvo nađi radnu tačku.
  • UT u voltima. UT = 25 mV = 0,025 V; gm = 1 mA/25 mV = 40 mS, ne 0,04 mS.
  • Zaboravljen minus. Stepen sa zajedničkim emitorom obrće fazu: Au je negativno.
  • Using the model without checking. The active-mode DC model holds only if IB > 0 and UCE > UCEsat. Check at the end.
  • Mixing the models. The 0.7 V source belongs to the DC model. It is not in the small-signal model: rπ is there instead.
  • rπ as a fixed value. rπ and gm depend on IC; find the operating point first.
  • UT in volts. UT = 25 mV = 0.025 V; gm = 1 mA/25 mV = 40 mS, not 0.04 mS.
  • Forgetting the minus. A common-emitter stage inverts the phase: Au is negative.

SažetakSummary

  • Ekvivalentna šema zamenjuje nelinearni tranzistor linearnim elementima i važi za jedan režim rada.
  • Jednosmerni model u aktivnom režimu: izvor UBE ≈ 0,7 V između B i E i strujni izvor βIB između C i E; uslovi IB > 0 i UCE > 0,2 V.
  • Model za male signale: \( g_m = I_C/U_T \), \( r_\pi = \beta/g_m \), \( r_o = U_A/I_C \); parametri zavise od radne tačke.
  • h11e ≈ rπ, h21e ≈ β, h22e ≈ 1/ro, h12e ≈ 0; pojačanje \( A_u \approx -g_mR_C \).
  • An equivalent circuit replaces the nonlinear transistor with linear elements and holds for one operating mode.
  • The DC model in the active mode: a source UBE ≈ 0.7 V between B and E and a current source βIB between C and E; conditions IB > 0 and UCE > 0.2 V.
  • The small-signal model: \( g_m = I_C/U_T \), \( r_\pi = \beta/g_m \), \( r_o = U_A/I_C \); the parameters depend on the operating point.
  • h11e ≈ rπ, h21e ≈ β, h22e ≈ 1/ro, h12e ≈ 0; the gain \( A_u \approx -g_mR_C \).
