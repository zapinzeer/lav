Deo 1Part 1 · ekran i komandethe screen and the controls

Kako se čita osciloskopHow to read an oscilloscope

Osciloskop crta grafik napona u vremenu. Horizontalna osa je vreme, a vertikalna napon. Ekran je podeljen mrežom na podeoke (engl. div): obično 10 horizontalnih i 8 vertikalnih. Dva dugmeta određuju šta jedan podeok znači:

An oscilloscope draws a graph of voltage against time. The horizontal axis is time and the vertical axis is voltage. The screen is divided by a grid into divisions (div): usually 10 horizontal and 8 vertical. Two knobs decide what one division means:

  • V/div (vertikalna osetljivost): koliko volti vredi jedan vertikalni podeok.
  • t/div (vremenska baza): koliko vremena vredi jedan horizontalni podeok.
  • V/div (vertical sensitivity): how many volts one vertical division is worth.
  • t/div (time base): how much time one horizontal division is worth.

Merenje je zapravo brojanje podeoka, pa množenje podešenom vrednošću:

Measuring is really counting divisions and multiplying by the set value:

\[ U_{pp} = n_y\cdot\mathrm{V/div} \qquad T = n_x\cdot\mathrm{t/div} \qquad f = \frac{1}{T} \]
\[ U_m = \frac{U_{pp}}{2} \qquad U = \frac{U_m}{\sqrt{2}}\ \ (\text{sinusoida / sine wave}) \]

Sprega ulaza: DC, AC, GNDInput coupling: DC, AC, GND

  • DC: prikazuje se ceo signal, i jednosmerna i naizmenična komponenta.
  • AC: kondenzator na ulazu zaustavlja jednosmernu komponentu. Koristi se kada je mali naizmenični signal „nasađen” na veliki jednosmerni napon (npr. talasanje napajanja).
  • GND: ulaz je uzemljen, vidiš liniju nula volti. Služi da nađeš gde je nula pre merenja.
  • DC: the whole signal is shown, both the direct and the alternating component.
  • AC: a capacitor at the input stops the direct component. It is used when a small alternating signal "rides" on a large direct voltage (e.g. supply ripple).
  • GND: the input is grounded, you see the line of zero volts. It is used to find where zero is before measuring.

Okidanje (trigger)The trigger

Okidanje određuje u kom trenutku počinje svako crtanje. Biraš nivo (napon) i nagib (signal raste ↗ ili opada ↘). Kada signal prođe kroz taj nivo u tom smeru, ekran počinje crtanje iz početka, pa se svaka perioda crta preko prethodne i slika stoji mirno. Ako je nivo van opsega signala, nema okidanja i slika „beži”.

The trigger decides at what moment each sweep starts. You choose a level (a voltage) and a slope (the signal rising ↗ or falling ↘). When the signal passes through that level in that direction, the screen starts drawing from the beginning, so every period is drawn on top of the previous one and the picture stands still. If the level is outside the range of the signal, there is no trigger and the picture "runs".

Podešavanje za dobro očitavanjeSetting up for a good reading

Izaberi V/div tako da signal zauzme 60 do 80 % visine ekrana. Izaberi t/div tako da se vide 2 do 3 periode. Očitavaj na središnjim osama, gde su sitne crtice na po 0,2 podeoka.

Choose V/div so that the signal fills 60 to 80 % of the screen height. Choose t/div so that 2 to 3 periods are visible. Read at the central axes, where the small ticks are at 0.2 division.

Sonda ×10The ×10 probe

Sonda ×10 slabi signal 10 puta (a ulazni otpor povećava na 10 MΩ). Ako kanal nije podešen na sondu ×10, očitanu vrednost množi sa 10. Najbolje je da se podešavanje kanala poklapa sa sondom.

A ×10 probe attenuates the signal 10 times (and raises the input resistance to 10 MΩ). If the channel is not set to a ×10 probe, multiply the reading by 10. It is best if the channel setting matches the probe.

Deo 2Part 2 · napon i struja na elementimavoltage and current on the elements

Otpornik, kalem i kondenzator na ekranuA resistor, a coil and a capacitor on the screen

Osciloskop meri samo napon, a mi želimo da vidimo i struju. Rešenje: u kolo se na red sa elementom veže mali otpornik Rs = 10 Ω. Napon na njemu je srazmeran struji, uRs = i · Rs, i u fazi je sa njom. Tako na istom ekranu vidiš napon (kanal 1) i struju (kanal 2).

An oscilloscope only measures voltage, but we also want to see the current. The solution: a small resistor Rs = 10 Ω is connected in series with the element. The voltage on it is proportional to the current, uRs = i · Rs, and is in phase with it. So on the same screen you see the voltage (channel 1) and the current (channel 2).

Kako zapamtitiHow to remember

Kalem: napon prednjači, struja kasni. Kondenzator: struja prednjači, napon kasni. Na ekranu ona kriva koja ranije prolazi kroz nulu naviše prednjači. Na engleskom se pamti „ELI the ICE man”: u L napon E prednjači struji I, u C struja I prednjači naponu E.

Coil: the voltage leads, the current lags. Capacitor: the current leads, the voltage lags. On the screen the trace that crosses zero upward earlier is the one that leads. Remember it as "ELI the ICE man": in L the voltage E leads the current I, in C the current I leads the voltage E.

Druga metoda je XY režim: kanal 1 ide na horizontalu, kanal 2 na vertikalu. Dobija se elipsa (Lisažuova slika), a fazni stav je \( \varphi = \arcsin(b/a) \), gde je b presek elipse sa vertikalnom osom, a a njena najveća visina. Ova metoda ne kaže da li struja kasni ili prednjači.

Another method is the XY mode: channel 1 goes to the horizontal, channel 2 to the vertical. The result is an ellipse (a Lissajous figure), and the phase is \( \varphi = \arcsin(b/a) \), where b is where the ellipse crosses the vertical axis and a is its greatest height. This method does not tell whether the current lags or leads.

Deo 3Part 3 · otpor naizmenične strujeresistance to alternating current

Reaktansa i frekvencijaReactance and frequency

Kalem i kondenzator se naizmeničnoj struji suprotstavljaju, ali ne kao otpornik. Njihov otpor zovemo reaktansa, a zavisi od frekvencije:

A coil and a capacitor oppose alternating current, but not like a resistor. We call their opposition reactance, and it depends on the frequency:

\[ X_L = \omega L = 2\pi f L \qquad X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C} = \frac{1}{2\pi f C} \]

  • Kalem propušta jednosmernu struju (XL = 0), a sa porastom frekvencije sve jače guši struju.
  • Kondenzator ne propušta jednosmernu struju (XC beskonačno), a sa porastom frekvencije sve bolje je propušta.
  • Omov zakon važi za amplitude i za efektivne vrednosti: I = U/X.
  • A coil passes direct current (XL = 0), and as the frequency rises it chokes the current more and more.
  • A capacitor does not pass direct current (XC is infinite), and as the frequency rises it passes it better and better.
  • Ohm's law holds for amplitudes and for RMS values: I = U/X.

Kada se otpornik veže na red sa kalemom ili kondenzatorom, otpori se ne sabiraju aritmetički. Rezultat je impedansa sa faznim stavom između 0° i 90°:

When a resistor is connected in series with a coil or a capacitor, the resistances do not add arithmetically. The result is an impedance with a phase between 0° and 90°:

\[ Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X^2} \qquad \varphi = \arctan\frac{X}{R} \qquad X = X_L\ \text{ili / or}\ -X_C \]

Reaktansa ne troši snaguReactance does not use up power

Idealni kalem i kondenzator ne razvijaju toplotu. Oni samo skladište energiju u polju i vraćaju je izvoru. Toplotu razvija samo otpornik, pa je snaga \( P = I^2 R \).

An ideal coil and capacitor produce no heat. They only store energy in a field and give it back to the source. Only the resistor produces heat, so the power is \( P = I^2 R \).

Deo 4Part 4 · naponi se sabiraju kao vektorivoltages add as vectors

Serijsko RLC koloThe series RLC circuit

Kada su R, L i C vezani na red, kroz sve teče ista struja. Zato je struja polazna (referentna) veličina, a naponi na elementima imaju različit fazni stav prema njoj:

When R, L and C are connected in series, the same current flows through all of them. So the current is the reference quantity, and the voltages on the elements have different phases relative to it:

  • UR je u fazi sa strujom.
  • UL prednjači struji za 90°.
  • UC kasni za strujom 90°, to jest suprotan je naponu UL.
  • UR is in phase with the current.
  • UL leads the current by 90°.
  • UC lags the current by 90°, that is it is opposite to UL.
\[ X = X_L - X_C \qquad Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X^2} \qquad I = \frac{U}{Z} \qquad \varphi = \arctan\frac{X_L - X_C}{R} \]
\[ U_R = I R \quad U_L = I X_L \quad U_C = I X_C \qquad U = \sqrt{U_R^2 + (U_L - U_C)^2} \]

Pošto naponi nisu u fazi, ne sabiraju se aritmetički: \( U \ne U_R + U_L + U_C \). Njihov zbir se crta kao fazorski dijagram: strelice čija je dužina amplituda, a smer fazni stav. Strelice rotiraju ugaonom brzinom ω, a njihova vertikalna projekcija je trenutna vrednost napona.

Because the voltages are not in phase, they do not add arithmetically: \( U \ne U_R + U_L + U_C \). Their sum is drawn as a phasor diagram: arrows whose length is the amplitude and whose direction is the phase. The arrows rotate with the angular velocity ω, and their vertical projection is the instantaneous voltage.

RezonancijaResonance

Kada je \( X_L = X_C \), reaktanse se poništavaju: \( Z = R \) je najmanja, struja najveća, a \( \varphi = 0 \). To se dešava na frekvenciji \( f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} \). Tada su \( U_L \) i \( U_C \) jednaki i mogu biti veći od napona izvora.

When \( X_L = X_C \), the reactances cancel: \( Z = R \) is smallest, the current is largest, and \( \varphi = 0 \). This happens at the frequency \( f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} \). Then \( U_L \) and \( U_C \) are equal and can be larger than the source voltage.

Tok vežbeThe procedure

Cilj vežbe: osciloskopom izmeriti napon na otporniku, kalemu i kondenzatoru, odrediti amplitudu, frekvenciju i fazni stav, i izračunati impedansu elemenata.

Aim of the exercise: to measure with an oscilloscope the voltage on a resistor, a coil and a capacitor, to determine the amplitude, the frequency and the phase, and to calculate the impedance of the elements.

PriborEquipment

  • Dvokanalni osciloskop i dve sonde (1:1 / 10:1).
  • Funkcijski generator, sinusni signal, 1 kHz.
  • Otpornik R = 100 Ω, kalem L = 20 mH, kondenzator C = 1 μF.
  • Merni otpornik Rs = 10 Ω.
  • Provodnici i protobord.
  • A two-channel oscilloscope and two probes (1:1 / 10:1).
  • A function generator, sine signal, 1 kHz.
  • A resistor R = 100 Ω, a coil L = 20 mH, a capacitor C = 1 μF.
  • A sense resistor Rs = 10 Ω.
  • Leads and a breadboard.

Masa osciloskopaThe oscilloscope ground

Mase obe sonde su međusobno spojene i povezane sa uzemljenjem. Ako masu sonde priključiš na tačku u kolu koja nije masa, napravićeš kratak spoj. Zato se mere naponi u odnosu na zajedničku masu, a napon između dve tačke, od kojih nijedna nije masa, dobija se razlikom kanala (CH1 − CH2).

The grounds of both probes are connected to each other and to the earth. If you connect a probe ground to a point in the circuit that is not ground, you will make a short circuit. That is why voltages are measured relative to the common ground, and the voltage between two points, neither of which is ground, is obtained as the difference of the channels (CH1 − CH2).

KoraciSteps

  1. Priključi sondu na izlaz „CAL” osciloskopa (pravougaoni signal) i podesi kompenzaciju sonde 10:1 da vrhovi budu ravni.
  2. Sastavi kolo sa slike sa otpornikom kao elementom X. Generator podesi na sinus, 1 kHz, oko 5 V amplitude.
  3. Obe sprege postavi na GND i pomeri linije na sredinu ekrana, pa vrati na DC. Okidanje: izvor CH1, nagib ↗, nivo 0.
  4. Podesi V/div i t/div da se vide 2 do 3 periode. Očitaj amplitudu napona Um sa kanala 1 i Um,Rs sa kanala 2. Izračunaj Im = Um,Rs/Rs i Z.
  5. Izmeri kašnjenje Δt između prolaza kroz nulu naviše i izračunaj φ. Zapiši da li struja kasni ili prednjači.
  6. Ponovi sa kalemom, pa sa kondenzatorom. Isključi generator pre svake promene kola.
  7. Opciono: izmeri na 100 Hz i 5 kHz i uporedi reaktanse.
  1. Connect a probe to the "CAL" output of the oscilloscope (a square signal) and adjust the compensation of the 10:1 probe so that the tops are flat.
  2. Build the circuit from the figure with the resistor as the element X. Set the generator to a sine, 1 kHz, about 5 V amplitude.
  3. Set both couplings to GND and move the lines to the middle of the screen, then return to DC. Trigger: source CH1, slope ↗, level 0.
  4. Set V/div and t/div so that 2 to 3 periods are visible. Read the voltage amplitude Um from channel 1 and Um,Rs from channel 2. Calculate Im = Um,Rs/Rs and Z.
  5. Measure the delay Δt between the upward zero crossings and calculate φ. Write down whether the current lags or leads.
  6. Repeat with the coil, then with the capacitor. Switch off the generator before every change of the circuit.
  7. Optional: measure at 100 Hz and 5 kHz and compare the reactances.

Primer 5 · AC sprega i talasanjeExample 5 · AC coupling and ripple

Dato: napajanje od 12 V ima talasanje oko 100 mV (vrh–vrh). Kako ga izmeriti osciloskopom?

Given: a 12 V supply has a ripple of about 100 mV (peak to peak). How can it be measured with an oscilloscope?

  1. Jednosmernu vrednost izmeri sa DC spregom na 2 V/div: kriva je 6 podeoka iznad nule, 12 V.

  2. For the ripple switch to AC coupling, which removes the 12 V, and reduce the scale to 20 mV/div. The ripple takes \( 100/20 = 5 \) divisions.

Deo 7Part 7 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Zaboravljen V/div ili t/div. Broj podeoka nije napon ni vreme. Uvek pomnoži sa podešenom vrednošću.
  • Upp uzet kao Um. Sa ekrana čitaš razmak od vrha do vrha. Amplituda je njegova polovina.
  • Zaboravljena sonda ×10. Očitanu vrednost množi sa 10 ako kanal nije podešen na sondu.
  • Efektivna i vršna vrednost. Osciloskop pokazuje vršnu, a instrumenti i utičnica efektivnu. \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \).
  • Masa sonde na pogrešnoj tački. To pravi kratak spoj. Mase obe sonde idu na zajedničku masu kola.
  • Slika „beži”. Nivo okidanja je van signala ili je izabran pogrešan izvor okidanja. Postavi nivo u opseg signala.
  • DC sprega za malo talasanje. Veliki jednosmerni napon nameće veliku skalu, pa se talasanje ne vidi. Pređi na AC spregu.
  • Pogrešan smer faze. Kriva koja ranije prolazi kroz nulu naviše prednjači. Kod kalema napon prednjači, kod kondenzatora struja.
  • Sabiranje napona aritmetički. Naponi na R, L i C se sabiraju vektorski: \( U \ne U_R + U_L + U_C \).
  • Mešanje reaktanse i otpora. Reaktansa zavisi od frekvencije i ne razvija toplotu. Impedansa je \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X^2} \), a ne \( R + X \).
  • Signal van ekrana. Ako vrhovi izlaze iz mreže, očitavanje nije moguće. Povećaj V/div.
  • Forgetting V/div or t/div. A number of divisions is neither voltage nor time. Always multiply by the set value.
  • Upp taken as Um. From the screen you read the distance from peak to peak. The amplitude is its half.
  • Forgetting the ×10 probe. Multiply the reading by 10 if the channel is not set for the probe.
  • RMS and peak value. The oscilloscope shows the peak, while instruments and the wall socket give RMS. \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \).
  • A probe ground on the wrong point. That makes a short circuit. The grounds of both probes go to the common ground of the circuit.
  • The picture "runs". The trigger level is outside the signal or the wrong trigger source is selected. Put the level inside the signal range.
  • DC coupling for a small ripple. A large direct voltage forces a large scale, so the ripple is not seen. Switch to AC coupling.
  • The wrong direction of the phase. The trace that crosses zero upward earlier leads. In a coil the voltage leads, in a capacitor the current.
  • Adding voltages arithmetically. The voltages on R, L and C add as vectors: \( U \ne U_R + U_L + U_C \).
  • Mixing reactance and resistance. Reactance depends on frequency and produces no heat. The impedance is \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X^2} \), not \( R + X \).
  • A signal off the screen. If the peaks leave the grid, reading is impossible. Increase V/div.

Deo 8Part 8

SažetakSummary

  • Osciloskop crta napon u vremenu. Očitavanje: \( U_{pp} = n_y\cdot\mathrm{V/div} \), \( T = n_x\cdot\mathrm{t/div} \), \( f = 1/T \), \( U_m = U_{pp}/2 \).
  • Sprega: DC (sve), AC (bez jednosmerne komponente), GND (nula). Okidanje određuje nivo i nagib na kojima počinje crtanje.
  • Struja se vidi kao napon na malom otporniku Rs. Fazni stav: \( \varphi = \Delta t/T\cdot 360^\circ \).
  • Otpornik: u fazi. Kalem: struja kasni 90°, \( X_L = 2\pi f L \). Kondenzator: struja prednjači 90°, \( X_C = 1/(2\pi f C) \).
  • Serijsko RLC: \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2} \), \( I = U/Z \), naponi se sabiraju kao vektori. Rezonancija: \( f_0 = 1/(2\pi\sqrt{LC}) \), \( Z = R \).
  • Mase sondi su zajedničke i povezane sa uzemljenjem, pa pazi gde ih priključuješ.
  • An oscilloscope draws voltage against time. Reading: \( U_{pp} = n_y\cdot\mathrm{V/div} \), \( T = n_x\cdot\mathrm{t/div} \), \( f = 1/T \), \( U_m = U_{pp}/2 \).
  • Coupling: DC (everything), AC (without the direct component), GND (zero). The trigger sets the level and the slope at which drawing starts.
  • The current is seen as the voltage on a small resistor Rs. The phase: \( \varphi = \Delta t/T\cdot 360^\circ \).
  • Resistor: in phase. Coil: the current lags 90°, \( X_L = 2\pi f L \). Capacitor: the current leads 90°, \( X_C = 1/(2\pi f C) \).
  • Series RLC: \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2} \), \( I = U/Z \), the voltages add as vectors. Resonance: \( f_0 = 1/(2\pi\sqrt{LC}) \), \( Z = R \).
  • The probe grounds are common and connected to the earth, so watch where you connect them.
