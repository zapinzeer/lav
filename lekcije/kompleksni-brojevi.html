Deo 1Part 1 · idejathe idea

Zašto kompleksni brojevi?Why complex numbers?

Fazor je strelica u ravni, a strelicu u ravni možemo da zapišemo jednim kompleksnim brojem. Tako se račun sa naizmeničnim veličinama (sabiranje, množenje, deljenje) svodi na običnu algebru, bez trigonometrije.

A phasor is an arrow in a plane, and an arrow in a plane can be written as one complex number. Calculations with alternating quantities (adding, multiplying, dividing) then reduce to ordinary algebra, without trigonometry.

U elektrotehnici imaginarna jedinica se označava slovom j, a ne i, jer je i već zauzeto: to je trenutna vrednost struje.

In electrical engineering the imaginary unit is written j, not i, because i is already taken: it is the instantaneous value of the current.

\[ j = \sqrt{-1} \qquad j^2 = -1 \]
Pomisli prvo: koliko je j³ i koliko je j⁴?Think first: what is j³ and what is j⁴?

\( j^3 = j^2\cdot j = -j \), a \( j^4 = (j^2)^2 = 1 \). Stepeni od j se ponavljaju u krugu od četiri: \( j,\ -1,\ -j,\ 1 \).

\( j^3 = j^2\cdot j = -j \), and \( j^4 = (j^2)^2 = 1 \). The powers of j repeat in a cycle of four: \( j,\ -1,\ -j,\ 1 \).

Deo 2Part 2 · oblik a + jbthe form a + jb

Algebarski oblik i kompleksna ravanRectangular form and the complex plane

Kompleksni broj ima realni deo a i imaginarni deo b:

A complex number has a real part a and an imaginary part b:

\[ \underline{Z} = a + jb \qquad a = \mathrm{Re}\,\underline{Z},\quad b = \mathrm{Im}\,\underline{Z} \]

Crta se u kompleksnoj ravni: realni deo na vodoravnoj, imaginarni na vertikalnoj osi. Broj \( \underline{Z} = 3 + j2 \) je tačka (3, 2), odnosno strelica od koordinatnog početka do te tačke. Kompleksne veličine se često podvlače da se razlikuju od običnih (efektivnih ili amplitudnih) brojeva.

It is drawn in the complex plane: the real part on the horizontal axis, the imaginary part on the vertical one. The number \( \underline{Z} = 3 + j2 \) is the point (3, 2), that is, the arrow from the origin to that point. Complex quantities are often underlined to tell them apart from ordinary (RMS or amplitude) numbers.

Sabiranje i oduzimanje je prosto: posebno realni, posebno imaginarni delovi.

Adding and subtracting is simple: real parts and imaginary parts separately.

\[ (a_1 + jb_1) \pm (a_2 + jb_2) = (a_1 \pm a_2) + j(b_1 \pm b_2) \]

Množenje se radi kao množenje zagrada, uz \( j^2 = -1 \):

Multiplication is done like multiplying brackets, with \( j^2 = -1 \):

\[ (a_1 + jb_1)(a_2 + jb_2) = (a_1a_2 - b_1b_2) + j(a_1b_2 + a_2b_1) \]

Množenje sa j ima jednostavno geometrijsko značenje: okreće strelicu za 90° suprotno kazaljci. Deljenje sa j okreće je za 90° u smeru kazaljke. Zato je j baš „operator kvadrature” koji nam treba za kalem i kondenzator.

Multiplying by j has a simple geometric meaning: it turns the arrow by 90° counter-clockwise. Dividing by j turns it by 90° clockwise. That is why j is exactly the "quadrature operator" we need for the coil and the capacitor.

\[ j\cdot(a + jb) = -b + ja \qquad \frac{1}{j} = -j \]

Konjugovano kompleksan broj ima suprotan znak imaginarnog dela: \( \underline{Z}^* = a - jb \). Proizvod \( \underline{Z}\,\underline{Z}^* = a^2 + b^2 \) je realan broj, pa konjugovanjem deljenje postaje lako:

The complex conjugate has the opposite sign of the imaginary part: \( \underline{Z}^* = a - jb \). The product \( \underline{Z}\,\underline{Z}^* = a^2 + b^2 \) is a real number, so conjugating makes division easy:

\[ \frac{a_1 + jb_1}{a_2 + jb_2} = \frac{(a_1 + jb_1)(a_2 - jb_2)}{a_2^2 + b_2^2} \]

Deo 3Part 3 · oblik |Z|∠φthe form |Z|∠φ

Polarni i eksponencijalni oblikPolar and exponential form

Istu strelicu možemo opisati dužinom i uglom. Dužina je modul |Z|, a ugao je argument φ:

The same arrow can be described by its length and its angle. The length is the modulus |Z|, and the angle is the argument φ:

\[ |\underline{Z}| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \qquad \varphi = \arg\underline{Z} = \arctan\frac{b}{a} \]
\[ a = |\underline{Z}|\cos\varphi \qquad b = |\underline{Z}|\sin\varphi \]

Pazi na kvadrantMind the quadrant

Kalkulator za \( \arctan(b/a) \) daje ugao samo između −90° i +90°. Ako je realni deo a negativan, dodaj ili oduzmi 180°. Zato uvek skiciraj broj u ravni.

A calculator gives \( \arctan(b/a) \) only between −90° and +90°. If the real part a is negative, add or subtract 180°. That is why you should always sketch the number in the plane.

Zamenom a i b dobija se trigonometrijski oblik, a uz Ojlerovu formulu eksponencijalni i polarni oblik:

Substituting a and b gives the trigonometric form, and with Euler's formula the exponential and polar forms:

\[ e^{j\varphi} = \cos\varphi + j\sin\varphi \]
\[ \underline{Z} = a + jb = |\underline{Z}|(\cos\varphi + j\sin\varphi) = |\underline{Z}|\,e^{j\varphi} = |\underline{Z}|\angle\varphi \]

Množenje i deljenje su najlakši u polarnom obliku, jer se eksponenti sabiraju i oduzimaju:

Multiplication and division are easiest in polar form, because exponents add and subtract:

\[ |Z_1|\angle\varphi_1 \cdot |Z_2|\angle\varphi_2 = |Z_1||Z_2|\angle(\varphi_1 + \varphi_2) \qquad \frac{|Z_1|\angle\varphi_1}{|Z_2|\angle\varphi_2} = \frac{|Z_1|}{|Z_2|}\angle(\varphi_1 - \varphi_2) \]

Deo 4Part 4 · od sinusa do brojafrom a sine to a number

Naizmenična veličina kao kompleksan brojAn alternating quantity as a complex number

Sinusni napon \( u(t) = U_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi) \) zamenjujemo kompleksnim brojem koji ima modul jednak amplitudi i argument jednak početnoj fazi. To je kompleksna amplituda:

We replace the sine voltage \( u(t) = U_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi) \) with a complex number whose modulus equals the amplitude and whose argument equals the initial phase. That is the complex amplitude:

\[ \underline{U}_m = U_m\,e^{j\varphi} = U_m\angle\varphi \qquad u(t) = \mathrm{Im}\left\{\underline{U}_m\,e^{j\omega t}\right\} \]

Faktor \( e^{j\omega t} \) je isti za sve veličine u kolu, pa ga ne pišemo. Ostaje samo broj \( \underline{U}_m \), a to je upravo naš zamrznuti fazor. U praksi se češće piše kompleksna efektivna vrednost \( \underline{U} = U\angle\varphi \), gde je \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \).

The factor \( e^{j\omega t} \) is the same for all quantities in the circuit, so we do not write it. Only the number \( \underline{U}_m \) remains, and that is exactly our frozen phasor. In practice the complex RMS value \( \underline{U} = U\angle\varphi \) is written more often, where \( U = U_m/\sqrt{2} \).

Lepa posledica: izvod po vremenu postaje množenje sa jω, a integral deljenje sa jω. Zato će kalem (u = L di/dt) i kondenzator (i = C du/dt) u narednim lekcijama imati vrlo jednostavne izraze:

A nice consequence: differentiating with respect to time becomes multiplication by jω, and integrating becomes division by jω. That is why the coil (u = L di/dt) and the capacitor (i = C du/dt) in the following lessons will have very simple expressions:

\[ \frac{d}{dt} \;\to\; j\omega \qquad \underline{Z}_R = R \qquad \underline{Z}_L = j\omega L \qquad \underline{Z}_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C} = -\,j\frac{1}{\omega C} \]

Količnik kompleksnog napona i kompleksne struje zove se kompleksna impedansa \( \underline{Z} = \underline{U}/\underline{I} \). Njen modul je odnos amplituda, a njen argument je fazna razlika napona i struje. Zato „Omov zakon” za naizmeničnu struju ima isti oblik kao za jednosmernu, samo sa kompleksnim brojevima.

The quotient of the complex voltage and the complex current is called the complex impedance \( \underline{Z} = \underline{U}/\underline{I} \). Its modulus is the ratio of the amplitudes and its argument is the phase difference of voltage and current. So "Ohm's law" for AC has the same form as for DC, just with complex numbers.

\[ \underline{Z} = \frac{\underline{U}}{\underline{I}} = \frac{U}{I}\angle(\varphi_u - \varphi_i) = |\underline{Z}|\angle\varphi \]

Primer 1 · iz algebarskog u polarni oblikExample 1 · rectangular to polar

Dato: \( \underline{Z} = -3 + j4 \). Traži se modul i argument.

Given: \( \underline{Z} = -3 + j4 \). Find the modulus and the argument.

  1. Modul: \( |\underline{Z}| = \sqrt{(-3)^2 + 4^2} = 5 \).
  2. Kalkulator daje \( \arctan\dfrac{4}{-3} = -53{,}13^\circ \). Ali je realni deo negativan, a imaginarni pozitivan: broj je u drugom kvadrantu.
  3. Zato dodajemo 180°: \( \varphi = -53{,}13^\circ + 180^\circ = 126{,}87^\circ \).
  1. Modulus: \( |\underline{Z}| = \sqrt{(-3)^2 + 4^2} = 5 \).
  2. The calculator gives \( \arctan\dfrac{4}{-3} = -53.13^\circ \). But the real part is negative and the imaginary part positive: the number is in the second quadrant.
  3. So we add 180°: \( \varphi = -53.13^\circ + 180^\circ = 126.87^\circ \).

Given: \( \underline{Z}_1 = 4\angle 30^\circ \) and \( \underline{Z}_2 = 2\angle 60^\circ \). Find \( \underline{Z}_1\underline{Z}_2 \) and \( \underline{Z}_1/\underline{Z}_2 \).

Primer 4 · impedansa iz napona i strujeExample 4 · impedance from voltage and current

Dato: \( \underline{U} = 230\angle 0^\circ \) V i \( \underline{I} = 5\angle -36{,}87^\circ \) A. Traži se impedansa \( \underline{Z} \).

Given: \( \underline{U} = 230\angle 0^\circ \) V and \( \underline{I} = 5\angle -36.87^\circ \) A. Find the impedance \( \underline{Z} \).

  1. Količnik u polarnom obliku: \( \underline{Z} = \dfrac{230}{5}\angle(0^\circ - (-36{,}87^\circ)) = 46\angle 36{,}87^\circ\ \Omega \).
  2. U algebarskom obliku: \( 46\cos 36{,}87^\circ = 36{,}8 \) i \( 46\sin 36{,}87^\circ = 27{,}6 \).
  3. Pozitivan imaginarni deo znači da kolo ima induktivni karakter: struja kasni za naponom.
  1. The quotient in polar form: \( \underline{Z} = \dfrac{230}{5}\angle(0^\circ - (-36.87^\circ)) = 46\angle 36.87^\circ\ \Omega \).
  2. In rectangular form: \( 46\cos 36.87^\circ = 36.8 \) and \( 46\sin 36.87^\circ = 27.6 \).
  3. A positive imaginary part means the circuit has an inductive character: the current lags the voltage.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Pogrešan kvadrant. Za negativno a kalkulator daje ugao koji treba korigovati za 180°. Uvek skiciraj broj u ravni.
  • Sabiranje u polarnom obliku. Moduli se ne sabiraju: \( 5\angle 0^\circ + 5\angle 90^\circ \) nije \( 10\angle 90^\circ \), nego \( 7{,}07\angle 45^\circ \). Za sabiranje pređi u algebarski oblik.
  • Zaboravljen j² = −1. Pri množenju zagrada član \( jb_1\cdot jb_2 = -b_1b_2 \) ima znak minus.
  • Mešanje amplitude i efektivne vrednosti. Ako pišeš \( \underline{U} = U\angle\varphi \), modul je efektivna vrednost; ako pišeš \( \underline{U}_m \), modul je amplituda. Ne mešaj ih u istom zadatku.
  • The wrong quadrant. For a negative a the calculator gives an angle that must be corrected by 180°. Always sketch the number in the plane.
  • Adding in polar form. Moduli do not add: \( 5\angle 0^\circ + 5\angle 90^\circ \) is not \( 10\angle 90^\circ \), but \( 7.07\angle 45^\circ \). To add, switch to rectangular form.
  • Forgetting j² = −1. When multiplying brackets the term \( jb_1\cdot jb_2 = -b_1b_2 \) has a minus sign.
  • Mixing amplitude and RMS. If you write \( \underline{U} = U\angle\varphi \), the modulus is the RMS value; if you write \( \underline{U}_m \), the modulus is the amplitude. Do not mix them in the same problem.

SažetakSummary

  • Fazor se piše kao kompleksan broj \( \underline{Z} = a + jb = |\underline{Z}|\angle\varphi \), gde je \( j^2 = -1 \).
  • Za sabiranje i oduzimanje koristi algebarski oblik, a za množenje i deljenje polarni.
  • Množenje sa j okreće fazor za +90°, deljenje sa j za −90°.
  • Sinus \( U_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi) \) odgovara broju \( \underline{U}_m = U_m\angle\varphi \), a izvod po vremenu množenju sa jω.
  • Impedansa \( \underline{Z} = \underline{U}/\underline{I} \): modul je odnos amplituda, a argument fazna razlika napona i struje.
  • A phasor is written as a complex number \( \underline{Z} = a + jb = |\underline{Z}|\angle\varphi \), where \( j^2 = -1 \).
  • Use rectangular form for adding and subtracting, and polar form for multiplying and dividing.
  • Multiplying by j turns a phasor by +90°, dividing by j by −90°.
  • A sine \( U_m\sin(\omega t + \varphi) \) corresponds to the number \( \underline{U}_m = U_m\angle\varphi \), and differentiating in time to multiplying by jω.
  • Impedance \( \underline{Z} = \underline{U}/\underline{I} \): the modulus is the ratio of amplitudes and the argument is the phase difference of voltage and current.
