Deo 1Part 1 · tri sloja, dva spojathree layers, two junctions

Građa i simboliStructure and symbols

Bipolarni tranzistor (BJT, od engl. bipolar junction transistor) je poluprovodnička komponenta sa tri sloja i tri izvoda. Kod NPN tranzistora tanak sloj P tipa nalazi se između dva sloja N tipa, a kod PNP tranzistora obrnuto. „Bipolarni” znači da u provođenju učestvuju obe vrste nosilaca, i elektroni i šupljine.

A bipolar transistor (BJT, bipolar junction transistor) is a semiconductor component with three layers and three leads. In an NPN transistor a thin P-type layer sits between two N-type layers, and in a PNP transistor it is the other way round. "Bipolar" means that both kinds of carriers, electrons and holes, take part in conduction.

Izvodi su emitor (E), baza (B) i kolektor (C). Slojevi nisu jednaki: emitor je jako dopiran (ima mnogo nosilaca koje „emituje”), baza je veoma tanka i slabo dopirana, a kolektor je najveći, jer „skuplja” nosioce i na njemu se razvija toplota. Zato se emitor i kolektor ne smeju zameniti iako su istog tipa.

The leads are the emitter (E), the base (B) and the collector (C). The layers are not equal: the emitter is heavily doped (it has many carriers to "emit"), the base is very thin and lightly doped, and the collector is the largest, because it "collects" the carriers and heat is produced there. That is why the emitter and the collector must not be swapped even though they are of the same type.

Tranzistor ima dva PN spoja: spoj emitor–baza i spoj kolektor–baza. Multimetrom na opsegu za diode tranzistor izgleda kao dve diode vezane u bazi, što se koristi za proveru ispravnosti i za određivanje tipa.

A transistor has two PN junctions: the emitter–base junction and the collector–base junction. On the diode range of a multimeter a transistor looks like two diodes joined at the base, which is used to check it and to find its type.

Deo 2Part 2 · IC = β IBIC = β IB

Kako tranzistor pojačava strujuHow a transistor amplifies current

Posmatrajmo NPN tranzistor kada je spoj B–E direktno polarisan (baza oko 0,7 V iznad emitora), a spoj C–B inverzno polarisan (kolektor na višem naponu od baze). Emitor tada ubacuje (injektuje) mnogo elektrona u bazu. Baza je tanka i ima malo šupljina, pa se samo mali deo elektrona tu rekombinuje i izađe kroz izvod baze. Ogromna većina prođe kroz bazu, a jako električno polje inverzno polarisanog spoja C–B ih „povuče” u kolektor.

Consider an NPN transistor when the B–E junction is forward biased (the base about 0.7 V above the emitter) and the C–B junction is reverse biased (the collector at a higher voltage than the base). The emitter then injects many electrons into the base. The base is thin and has few holes, so only a small part of the electrons recombine there and leave through the base lead. The vast majority pass through the base, and the strong electric field of the reverse-biased C–B junction "pulls" them into the collector.

The result: the collector current is much larger than the base current, but it is the base that controls it. If we double the base current, the collector current doubles too. By Kirchhoff's current law, everything that enters the transistor must also leave:

Pomisli prvo: ako je β = 100, koji deo struje emitora odlazi u bazu?Think first: if β = 100, what fraction of the emitter current goes to the base?

Na svakih 100 delova kolektorske struje dolazi 1 deo bazne, pa je IE = 101 deo. Baza dobija 1/101 ≈ 1 %, a α = 100/101 ≈ 0,99.

For every 100 parts of collector current there is 1 part of base current, so IE = 101 parts. The base gets 1/101 ≈ 1 %, and α = 100/101 ≈ 0.99.

β nije stalan brojβ is not a fixed number

Čak i tranzistori iste oznake imaju različito β (u katalogu za BC547 piše raspon, npr. 110–800, po grupama A, B, C), a β se menja i sa temperaturom i sa strujom. Zato se dobra kola projektuju tako da rade ispravno bez obzira na tačnu vrednost β.

Even transistors with the same marking have different β (the datasheet of a BC547 gives a range, e.g. 110–800, in groups A, B, C), and β also changes with temperature and current. That is why good circuits are designed to work correctly whatever the exact value of β.

PNP

Kod PNP tranzistora sve je isto, samo su naponi i struje suprotnih smerova: emitor je na najvišem potencijalu, baza je oko 0,7 V niža, a struje izlaze iz baze i kolektora.

In a PNP transistor everything is the same, only the voltages and currents have opposite directions: the emitter is at the highest potential, the base is about 0.7 V lower, and the currents flow out of the base and the collector.

Deo 3Part 3 · dva spoja, četiri kombinacijetwo junctions, four combinations

Režimi radaOperating modes

Svaki od dva spoja može biti direktno ili inverzno polarisan, pa tranzistor ima četiri režima rada. U praksi se koriste tri: aktivni režim za pojačavače, a zakočenje i zasićenje kada tranzistor radi kao prekidač.

Each of the two junctions can be forward or reverse biased, so a transistor has four operating modes. In practice three are used: the active mode for amplifiers, and cut-off and saturation when the transistor works as a switch.

Brza provera u koluA quick check in a circuit

Izmeri UBE i UCE. Ako je UBE manje od oko 0,6 V, tranzistor je zakočen. Ako je UBE ≈ 0,7 V, a UCE nekoliko volti, radi u aktivnom režimu. Ako je UCE samo 0,1–0,3 V, u zasićenju je.

Measure UBE and UCE. If UBE is less than about 0.6 V, the transistor is cut off. If UBE ≈ 0.7 V and UCE is a few volts, it works in the active mode. If UCE is only 0.1–0.3 V, it is in saturation.

Deo 4Part 4 · IC(UCE)IC(UCE)

Karakteristike i radna pravaCharacteristics and the load line

Ulazna karakteristika IB(UBE) izgleda kao karakteristika diode: dok je UBE ispod oko 0,6 V bazne struje skoro nema, a posle naglo raste. Izlazne karakteristike IC(UCE) crtaju se kao familija krivih, po jedna za svaku baznu struju. Posle kratkog strmog dela (zasićenje) svaka kriva postaje skoro vodoravna: kolektorska struja tada zavisi od IB, a skoro ne zavisi od UCE.

The input characteristic IB(UBE) looks like the characteristic of a diode: while UBE is below about 0.6 V there is almost no base current, and after that it rises sharply. The output characteristics IC(UCE) are drawn as a family of curves, one for each base current. After a short steep part (saturation) every curve becomes almost flat: the collector current then depends on IB and hardly depends on UCE.

U kolu na slici, po drugom Kirhofovom zakonu za izlazno kolo važi \( U_{CC} = R_CI_C + U_{CE} \). To je jednačina prave u ravni izlaznih karakteristika, radne prave. Ona seče osu UCE u tački UCC (tranzistor zakočen) i osu IC u tački UCC/RC (tranzistor kao kratak spoj). Stvarno stanje, radna tačka Q, je presek radne prave i karakteristike za zadatu baznu struju.

In the circuit in the figure, by Kirchhoff's voltage law for the output circuit, \( U_{CC} = R_CI_C + U_{CE} \). That is the equation of a line in the plane of the output characteristics, the load line. It crosses the UCE axis at UCC (transistor cut off) and the IC axis at UCC/RC (transistor as a short circuit). The real state, the operating point Q, is where the load line crosses the characteristic for the given base current.

\[ I_B = \frac{U_{BB} - U_{BE}}{R_B} \qquad I_C = \beta I_B \qquad U_{CE} = U_{CC} - R_CI_C \]

Gde postaviti radnu tačkuWhere to place the operating point

Za pojačavač Q se postavlja otprilike na sredinu radne prave (UCE ≈ UCC/2), da bi signal mogao da se ljulja i gore i dole bez sečenja. Za prekidač Q skače između dva kraja radne prave: zakočenja i zasićenja.

For an amplifier Q is placed roughly in the middle of the load line (UCE ≈ UCC/2), so the signal can swing both up and down without clipping. For a switch Q jumps between the two ends of the load line: cut-off and saturation.

Primer 3 · kada račun „ne izađe”Example 3 · when the calculation "does not work out"

Dato: isto kolo, ali RB = 22 kΩ.

Given: the same circuit, but RB = 22 kΩ.

  1. \( I_B = 4{,}3/22\ \mathrm{k\Omega} = 195\ \mu\mathrm{A} \), pa bi bilo \( \beta I_B = 19{,}5\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  2. Tada bi \( U_{CE} = 12 - 2\cdot 19{,}5 = -27\ \mathrm{V} \), što je nemoguće.
  3. Tranzistor je u zasićenju: \( U_{CE} \approx 0{,}2\ \mathrm{V} \), \( I_C = (12 - 0{,}2)/2\ \mathrm{k\Omega} = 5{,}9\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  1. \( I_B = 4.3/22\ \mathrm{k\Omega} = 195\ \mu\mathrm{A} \), so it would be \( \beta I_B = 19.5\ \mathrm{mA} \).

  3. The transistor is in saturation: \( U_{CE} \approx 0.2\ \mathrm{V} \), \( I_C = (12 - 0.2)/2\ \mathrm{k\Omega} = 5.9\ \mathrm{mA} \).

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • IC = β IB uvek. Važi samo u aktivnom režimu. Ako račun da negativan UCE, tranzistor je u zasićenju.
  • Zaboravljenih 0,7 V. Bazna struja je \( (U_{BB} - 0{,}7)/R_B \), a ne \( U_{BB}/R_B \).
  • Zamena emitora i kolektora. Slojevi nisu simetrični; obrnut tranzistor ima vrlo malo β.
  • Strelica na pogrešnom izvodu. Strelica je uvek na emitoru, a njen smer razlikuje NPN od PNP.
  • Tranzistor „pravi” struju. Energiju daje izvor UCC; tranzistor samo upravlja koliko struje propušta.
  • IC = β IB always. It holds only in the active mode. If the calculation gives a negative UCE, the transistor is in saturation.
  • Forgetting the 0.7 V. The base current is \( (U_{BB} - 0.7)/R_B \), not \( U_{BB}/R_B \).
  • Swapping the emitter and the collector. The layers are not symmetric; a reversed transistor has a very small β.
  • The arrow on the wrong lead. The arrow is always on the emitter, and its direction tells NPN from PNP.
  • The transistor "makes" current. The energy comes from the source UCC; the transistor only controls how much current it lets through.

SažetakSummary

  • Bipolarni tranzistor ima tri sloja (NPN ili PNP), tri izvoda (E, B, C) i dva PN spoja. Strelica u simbolu je na emitoru.
  • U aktivnom režimu: B–E direktno, C–B inverzno, \( I_C = \beta I_B \), \( I_E = I_B + I_C \), \( \alpha = \beta/(\beta + 1) \).
  • Zakočenje i zasićenje koriste se za prekidače, aktivni režim za pojačavače.
  • Radna prava \( U_{CE} = U_{CC} - R_CI_C \) i karakteristika za zadato IB daju radnu tačku Q.
  • A bipolar transistor has three layers (NPN or PNP), three leads (E, B, C) and two PN junctions. The arrow in the symbol is on the emitter.
  • In the active mode: B–E forward, C–B reverse, \( I_C = \beta I_B \), \( I_E = I_B + I_C \), \( \alpha = \beta/(\beta + 1) \).
  • Cut-off and saturation are used for switches, the active mode for amplifiers.
  • The load line \( U_{CE} = U_{CC} - R_CI_C \) and the characteristic for the given IB give the operating point Q.
