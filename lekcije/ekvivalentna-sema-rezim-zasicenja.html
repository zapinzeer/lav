Deo 1Part 1 · βIB > ICsatβIB > ICsat

Kada tranzistor ulazi u zasićenjeWhen a transistor enters saturation

U aktivnom režimu kolektorska struja raste sa baznom: \( I_C = \beta I_B \). Ali ona ne može da raste beskonačno. Najveća struja koju spoljašnje kolo može da propusti teče kada je napon na tranzistoru najmanji mogući. Taj najmanji napon je napon zasićenja UCEsat, oko 0,1–0,3 V. Tada je struja kolektora:

In the active mode the collector current grows with the base current: \( I_C = \beta I_B \). But it cannot grow without limit. The largest current the external circuit can pass flows when the voltage across the transistor is as small as possible. That smallest voltage is the saturation voltage UCEsat, about 0.1–0.3 V. The collector current is then:

\[ I_{Csat} = \frac{U_{CC} - U_{CEsat}}{R_C} \qquad I_{Bsat} = \frac{I_{Csat}}{\beta} \]

Ako je bazna struja veća od IBsat, tranzistor bi „hteo” da propusti βIB, ali kolo mu to ne dozvoljava. Kolektor se spušta skoro do napona emitora, pa i spoj C–B postaje direktno polarisan. To je zasićenje: oba spoja vode, a tranzistor se ponaša kao zatvoren prekidač.

If the base current is larger than IBsat, the transistor "wants" to pass βIB, but the circuit does not let it. The collector drops almost to the emitter voltage, so the C–B junction also becomes forward biased. That is saturation: both junctions conduct and the transistor behaves like a closed switch.

\[ \beta I_B > I_{Csat} \qquad k = \frac{I_B}{I_{Bsat}} = \frac{\beta I_B}{I_{Csat}} > 1 \]

Deo 2Part 2 · dva izvoratwo sources

Ekvivalentna šema u zasićenjuThe equivalent circuit in saturation

U zasićenju oba spoja vode i na oba je skoro stalan napon. Zato tranzistor zamenjujemo sa dva naponska izvora: između baze i emitora UBEsat ≈ 0,8 V, a između kolektora i emitora UCEsat ≈ 0,2 V. Strujnog izvora βIB više nema: obe struje sada određuje spoljašnje kolo.

In saturation both junctions conduct and both have an almost constant voltage. That is why we replace the transistor with two voltage sources: between the base and the emitter UBEsat ≈ 0.8 V, and between the collector and the emitter UCEsat ≈ 0.2 V. The current source βIB is gone: both currents are now set by the external circuit.

\[ I_B = \frac{U_{BB} - U_{BEsat}}{R_B} \qquad I_C = \frac{U_{CC} - U_{CEsat}}{R_C} \qquad I_C < \beta I_B \]

Poslednja nejednakost je uslov koji proveravamo na kraju: ako nije ispunjena, tranzistor nije u zasićenju već u aktivnom režimu, i treba koristiti model iz prethodne lekcije.

The last inequality is the condition we check at the end: if it does not hold, the transistor is not in saturation but in the active mode, and the model from the previous lesson must be used.

Idealni prekidačThe ideal switch

Kada je UCC mnogo veće od 0,2 V, napon zasićenja možemo zanemariti i tranzistor između C i E zameniti kratkim spojem. U zakočenju ga zamenjujemo prekidom. Tako tranzistor postaje elektronski prekidač kojim upravlja bazna struja.

When UCC is much larger than 0.2 V, we can neglect the saturation voltage and replace the transistor between C and E with a short circuit. In cut-off we replace it with an open circuit. So the transistor becomes an electronic switch controlled by the base current.

Deo 3Part 3 · uključeno, isključenoon, off

Tranzistor kao prekidačThe transistor as a switch

Mikrokontroler ili logičko kolo daje samo nekoliko miliampera, a sijalica, motor ili relej traže mnogo više. Tranzistor rešava problem: mala bazna struja ga uključuje, a kroz kolektor teče velika struja iz posebnog izvora. Dobar prekidač je ili potpuno zakočen ili duboko u zasićenju, nikad između.

A microcontroller or a logic circuit gives only a few milliamperes, while a lamp, a motor or a relay needs much more. The transistor solves the problem: a small base current turns it on, and a large current from a separate source flows through the collector. A good switch is either fully cut off or deep in saturation, never in between.

Razlog je snaga gubitaka na tranzistoru, \( P_T = U_{CE}I_C \). U zakočenju je IC ≈ 0, a u zasićenju je UCE ≈ 0,2 V, pa je u oba slučaja PT mala. U aktivnom režimu i napon i struja su veliki i tranzistor se greje. Zato se u praksi bira prezasićenje k ≈ 2–10: prekidač ostaje u zasićenju i kada je β manje od očekivanog.

The reason is the power loss in the transistor, \( P_T = U_{CE}I_C \). In cut-off IC ≈ 0, and in saturation UCE ≈ 0.2 V, so in both cases PT is small. In the active mode both the voltage and the current are large and the transistor heats up. That is why in practice an overdrive of k ≈ 2–10 is chosen: the switch stays in saturation even when β is smaller than expected.

Relej i zaštitna diodaA relay and the protection diode

Kalem releja ne trpi naglu promenu struje. Kada tranzistor naglo isključi struju, kalem indukuje veliki napon (\( u = L\,di/dt \)) koji može da probije tranzistor. Zato se paralelno kalemu, inverzno, vezuje zaštitna (zamajna) dioda kroz koju struja kalema može postepeno da opadne.

A relay coil does not tolerate a sudden change of current. When the transistor suddenly switches the current off, the coil induces a large voltage (\( u = L\,di/dt \)) that can break down the transistor. That is why a protection (flyback) diode is connected across the coil, reverse biased, so the coil current can decay gradually through it.

Cena dubokog zasićenjaThe price of deep saturation

U zasićenju se u bazi nagomila višak nosilaca. Kada isključimo baznu struju, treba vremena da se oni uklone, pa tranzistor kasni sa isključenjem (vreme nagomilavanja ts). Zato preveliko k usporava prekidač; za brze prekidače k se drži malim.

In saturation excess carriers pile up in the base. When the base current is switched off, it takes time to remove them, so the transistor switches off late (storage time ts). That is why too large a k slows the switch down; for fast switches k is kept small.

Primer 1 · da li je u zasićenju?Example 1 · is it in saturation?

Dato: UBB = 5 V, RB = 10 kΩ, UCC = 12 V, RC = 470 Ω, β = 100.

Given: UBB = 5 V, RB = 10 kΩ, UCC = 12 V, RC = 470 Ω, β = 100.

  1. Pretpostavimo zasićenje: \( I_B = (5 - 0{,}8)/10\ \mathrm{k\Omega} = 0{,}42\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  2. \( I_C = (12 - 0{,}2)/470 = 25{,}1\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  3. Provera: \( \beta I_B = 42\ \mathrm{mA} > 25{,}1\ \mathrm{mA} \). Pretpostavka je tačna, \( k = 42/25{,}1 = 1{,}67 \).
  1. Assume saturation: \( I_B = (5 - 0.8)/10\ \mathrm{k\Omega} = 0.42\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  2. \( I_C = (12 - 0.2)/470 = 25.1\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  3. Check: \( \beta I_B = 42\ \mathrm{mA} > 25.1\ \mathrm{mA} \). The assumption holds, \( k = 42/25.1 = 1.67 \).

Given: an LED (2 V, 20 mA) on UCC = 5 V, driven from 5 V, βmin = 100, we want k = 3. Find RC and RB.

  3. \( R_B = (5 - 0{,}8)/0{,}6\ \mathrm{mA} = 7\ \mathrm{k\Omega} \) (standardno 6,8 kΩ).

  2. \( I_{Bsat} = 20\ \mathrm{mA}/100 = 0.2\ \mathrm{mA} \), so \( I_B = 3\cdot 0.2 = 0.6\ \mathrm{mA} \).

Primer 3 · relej iz mikrokontroleraExample 3 · a relay from a microcontroller

Dato: relej 12 V sa otpornošću kalema 400 Ω, izlaz mikrokontrolera 3,3 V, βmin = 50, k = 2.

Given: a 12 V relay with a coil resistance of 400 Ω, a microcontroller output of 3.3 V, βmin = 50, k = 2.

  1. \( I_C = (12 - 0{,}2)/400 = 29{,}5\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  2. \( I_B = 2\cdot 29{,}5/50 = 1{,}18\ \mathrm{mA} \): to izlaz mikrokontrolera može da da.
  3. \( R_B = (3{,}3 - 0{,}8)/1{,}18\ \mathrm{mA} = 2{,}1\ \mathrm{k\Omega} \) (standardno 2,2 kΩ), i obavezno zaštitna dioda paralelno kalemu.
  1. \( I_C = (12 - 0.2)/400 = 29.5\ \mathrm{mA} \).
  2. \( I_B = 2\cdot 29.5/50 = 1.18\ \mathrm{mA} \): the microcontroller output can supply that.
  3. \( R_B = (3.3 - 0.8)/1.18\ \mathrm{mA} = 2.1\ \mathrm{k\Omega} \) (standard 2.2 kΩ), and always a protection diode across the coil.

Deo 5Part 5 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • IC = βIB u zasićenju. U zasićenju je IC manja od βIB i određuje je RC.
  • Bez provere uslova. Posle računa proveri βIB > IC; ako ne važi, kolo je u aktivnom režimu.
  • Računanje sa tipičnim β. Prekidač se projektuje sa najmanjim β iz kataloga i sa prezasićenjem k > 1.
  • Relej bez diode. Indukovani napon pri isključenju može da uništi tranzistor.
  • Baza bez otpornika. Spoj B–E je dioda; bez RB bazna struja nije ograničena.
  • IC = βIB in saturation. In saturation IC is smaller than βIB and is set by RC.
  • Not checking the condition. After the calculation check βIB > IC; if it does not hold, the circuit is in the active mode.
  • Calculating with a typical β. A switch is designed with the smallest β from the datasheet and with an overdrive k > 1.
  • A relay without a diode. The induced voltage at switch-off can destroy the transistor.
  • A base without a resistor. The B–E junction is a diode; without RB the base current is not limited.

SažetakSummary

  • U zasićenju oba spoja vode, UCE ≈ 0,2 V, a tranzistor je zatvoren prekidač.
  • Uslov: \( \beta I_B > I_{Csat} = (U_{CC} - U_{CEsat})/R_C \); faktor prezasićenja \( k = \beta I_B/I_{Csat} \).
  • Ekvivalentna šema: izvor UBEsat ≈ 0,8 V između B i E i izvor UCEsat ≈ 0,2 V između C i E.
  • Prekidač radi između zakočenja i zasićenja; tada su gubici \( P_T = U_{CE}I_C \) mali. Projektuje se sa βmin i k ≈ 2–10.
  • In saturation both junctions conduct, UCE ≈ 0.2 V, and the transistor is a closed switch.
  • Condition: \( \beta I_B > I_{Csat} = (U_{CC} - U_{CEsat})/R_C \); overdrive factor \( k = \beta I_B/I_{Csat} \).
  • Equivalent circuit: a source UBEsat ≈ 0.8 V between B and E and a source UCEsat ≈ 0.2 V between C and E.
  • A switch works between cut-off and saturation; the losses \( P_T = U_{CE}I_C \) are then small. It is designed with βmin and k ≈ 2–10.
