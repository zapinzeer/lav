Deo 1Part 1 · uvodintroduction

Šta je matična ploča?What is a motherboard?

Matična ploča (engl. motherboard) je najveća štampana ploča u računaru. Na njoj nema „pametnog” rada kao u procesoru, ali bez nje ništa ne bi radilo zajedno: ona fizički nosi sve komponente, električno ih povezuje bakarnim putanjama (trasama) i napaja ih. Možeš da je zamisliš kao grad: procesor je centar uprave, memorija su magacini, a trase na ploči su ulice i auto-putevi.

The motherboard is the largest printed circuit board in a computer. It does no "clever" work like the processor does, but nothing would work together without it: it physically carries all the components, electrically connects them with copper paths (traces) and powers them. Think of it as a city: the processor is the administrative centre, the memory modules are warehouses, and the traces on the board are streets and highways.

Moderna ploča je višeslojna (4 do 12 slojeva bakra spojenih u „sendvič”), a trase za brze signale (memorija, PCIe) crtaju se u parovima tačno određene dužine da bi signali stigli istovremeno. Na jednoj strani su svi utikači i slotovi, a na zadnjoj ivici ploče grupisani su spoljni portovi (I/O).

A modern board is multi-layered (4 to 12 layers of copper joined in a "sandwich"), and traces for fast signals (memory, PCIe) are drawn in pairs of precisely controlled length so the signals arrive at the same time. All the plugs and slots are on one side, and the external ports (I/O) are grouped on the rear edge of the board.

Pomisli prvo: koje tri stvari matična ploča radi za sve komponente?Think first: what three things does the motherboard do for all the components?

Nosi ih (mehanički), povezuje ih (signalne trase i magistrale) i napaja ih (razvodi i pretvara napone).

It carries them (mechanically), connects them (signal traces and buses) and powers them (distributes and converts the voltages).

Gde je šta?Where is what?

Zadnja ploča je uvek na istoj ivici (levo na slici) jer mora da izađe kroz otvor na zadnjoj strani kućišta. Slotovi za kartice su pri dnu, a napajanje (24-pin i 8-pin) pri ivicama bliže napajanju.

The rear panel is always on the same edge (left in the picture) because it has to come out through the opening at the back of the case. The slots for cards are at the bottom, and the power connectors (24-pin and 8-pin) are near the edges closest to the power supply.

Deo 3Part 3 · kako sarađujuhow they work together

Kako se delovi povezujuHow the parts are connected

Najbrži uređaji su vezani direktno na procesor: RAM (preko memorijskog kontrolera koji je u samom procesoru) i grafička kartica (PCIe x16). Sporiji uređaji (USB, SATA, mreža, dodatne PCIe trake) vezani su na čipset, a čipset je sa procesorom povezan jednom brzom vezom (DMI kod Intela, ekvivalent kod AMD-a). Stariji računari su imali dva čipa, „severni” i „južni most”; danas je severni most utopljen u procesor.

The fastest devices are connected directly to the processor: RAM (through the memory controller that sits inside the processor) and the graphics card (PCIe x16). Slower devices (USB, SATA, network, extra PCIe lanes) are connected to the chipset, and the chipset is linked to the processor with one fast link (DMI on Intel, the equivalent on AMD). Older computers had two chips, the "north" and the "south bridge"; today the north bridge is built into the processor.

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · propusnost memorijeExample 1 · memory bandwidth

Dato: DDR4-3200 modul ima magistralu širine 64 bita. Kolika je propusnost jednog kanala i dvokanalnog režima?

Given: a DDR4-3200 module has a 64-bit bus. What is the bandwidth of one channel and of dual-channel mode?

  1. \( B = \dfrac{3200\cdot 10^6\cdot 64}{8} = 25{,}6\ \mathrm{GB/s} \) po kanalu.
  2. Dva kanala rade paralelno: \( 2\cdot 25{,}6 = 51{,}2\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).
  3. Zato se dva modula stavljaju u slotove iste boje (2 i 4).
  1. \( B = \dfrac{3200\cdot 10^6\cdot 64}{8} = 25.6\ \mathrm{GB/s} \) per channel.
  2. Two channels work in parallel: \( 2\cdot 25.6 = 51.2\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).
  3. That is why two modules are put into the slots of the same colour (2 and 4).

B = 25,6 GB/s (1 kanal), 51,2 GB/s (2 kanala)B = 25.6 GB/s (1 channel), 51.2 GB/s (2 channels)

Primer 2 · PCIe vezaExample 2 · a PCIe link

Dato: PCIe 4.0 radi 16 GT/s po traci sa kodiranjem 128b/130b. Kolika je propusnost slota x16 i M.2 NVMe SSD-a sa x4?

Given: PCIe 4.0 runs at 16 GT/s per lane with 128b/130b encoding. What is the bandwidth of an x16 slot and of an M.2 NVMe SSD with x4?

  1. Po traci: \( 16\cdot\dfrac{128}{130}/8 = 1{,}97\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).
  2. x16: \( 16\cdot 1{,}97 = 31{,}5\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).
  3. x4 (M.2): \( 4\cdot 1{,}97 = 7{,}9\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).
  1. Per lane: \( 16\cdot\dfrac{128}{130}/8 = 1.97\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).
  2. x16: \( 16\cdot 1.97 = 31.5\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).
  3. x4 (M.2): \( 4\cdot 1.97 = 7.9\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).

x16 ≈ 31,5 GB/s, x4 ≈ 7,9 GB/sx16 ≈ 31.5 GB/s, x4 ≈ 7.9 GB/s

Primer 3 · struja VRM-aExample 3 · VRM current

Dato: procesor troši 125 W pri naponu jezgra 1,25 V, a VRM se napaja sa 12 V i ima efikasnost 90 %. Kolika je izlazna struja VRM-a i ulazna struja sa 12 V?

Given: a processor draws 125 W at a core voltage of 1.25 V, and the VRM is fed from 12 V with 90 % efficiency. What are the output current of the VRM and the input current from 12 V?

  1. Izlazna struja: \( I = P/U = 125/1{,}25 = 100\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. Ulazna snaga: \( 125/0{,}9 = 139\ \mathrm{W} \), pa je ulazna struja \( 139/12 = 11{,}6\ \mathrm{A} \).
  3. Gubici su 14 W i zato VRM treba hladnjake.
  1. Output current: \( I = P/U = 125/1.25 = 100\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. Input power: \( 125/0.9 = 139\ \mathrm{W} \), so the input current is \( 139/12 = 11.6\ \mathrm{A} \).
  3. The losses are 14 W, which is why the VRM needs heatsinks.

I(izlaz) = 100 A, I(ulaz) = 11,6 AI(out) = 100 A, I(in) = 11.6 A

Primer 4 · dijagnostikaExample 4 · troubleshooting

Računar se pali, ali pri svakom uključivanju pokazuje pogrešan datum i resetuje BIOS podešavanja. Koji deo je verovatno uzrok i kako se rešava?

A computer starts, but shows the wrong date and resets the BIOS settings every time it is switched on. Which part is the probable cause and how is it fixed?

  1. Podešavanja i sat čuva mali čip uz pomoć CMOS baterije dok je računar isključen.
  2. Ako je baterija ispražnjena, čip gubi podatke pri svakom isključivanju.
  3. Rešenje: zameniti CR2032 bateriju (3 V) novom i ponovo podesiti datum i BIOS.
  1. The settings and the clock are kept by a small chip with the help of the CMOS battery while the computer is off.
  2. If the battery is flat, the chip loses its data every time the power is removed.
  3. Fix: replace the CR2032 battery (3 V) with a new one and set the date and BIOS again.

Uzrok: CMOS baterija. Zameni CR2032.Cause: the CMOS battery. Replace the CR2032.

Deo 5Part 5 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Mešanje 8-pin EPS i PCIe konektora. Izgledaju skoro isto, ali imaju različit raspored kontakata. EPS ide samo u ploču, PCIe samo u grafičku karticu.
  • RAM u pogrešne slotove. Dva modula u susedne slotove rade samo u jednom kanalu. Prati uputstvo ploče (obično 2 i 4).
  • Zaboravljen I/O štitnik ili odstojnik. Odstojnik ispod ploče bez rupe ili nedostajući štitnik mogu da prouzrokuju kratak spoj ili oštećenje portova.
  • Pogrešan podnožje procesora. Procesor mora da odgovara podnožju (npr. LGA1700 ili AM5). Isti brend ne znači isto podnožje.
  • Mixing up the 8-pin EPS and PCIe connectors. They look almost the same but have a different pin arrangement. EPS goes only to the board, PCIe only to the graphics card.
  • RAM in the wrong slots. Two modules in adjacent slots work in single channel only. Follow the board manual (usually 2 and 4).
  • A forgotten I/O shield or standoff. A standoff under the board with no hole, or a missing shield, can cause a short circuit or damaged ports.
  • The wrong CPU socket. The processor must match the socket (e.g. LGA1700 or AM5). The same brand does not mean the same socket.

Deo 6Part 6

SažetakSummary

  • Matična ploča nosi, povezuje i napaja sve komponente računara.
  • Najbrži uređaji (RAM, grafička kartica) vezani su direktno na procesor, a ostali preko čipseta.
  • VRM pretvara 12 V u nizak napon i veliku struju za procesor; zato ima hladnjake.
  • Napajanje ide kroz 24-pinski ATX konektor (ploča) i 8-pinski EPS konektor (procesor).
  • CMOS baterija čuva sat i BIOS podešavanja; BIOS/UEFI pokreće računar i učitava operativni sistem.
  • The motherboard carries, connects and powers all the components of the computer.
  • The fastest devices (RAM, the graphics card) connect directly to the processor, the rest through the chipset.
  • The VRM converts 12 V into a low voltage and large current for the processor; that is why it has heatsinks.
  • Power comes through the 24-pin ATX connector (board) and the 8-pin EPS connector (processor).
  • The CMOS battery keeps the clock and the BIOS settings; the BIOS/UEFI starts the computer and loads the operating system.
