Deo 1Part 1 · građaconstruction

Šta je kondenzator?What is a capacitor?

Kondenzator čine dve provodne obloge razdvojene izolatorom (dielektrikom). Kada se priključi na izvor, na jednoj oblozi se nakupi pozitivno, a na drugoj negativno naelektrisanje. Kapacitivnost C govori koliko naelektrisanja kondenzator primi po voltu napona:

A capacitor consists of two conducting plates separated by an insulator (a dielectric). When it is connected to a source, positive charge accumulates on one plate and negative charge on the other. The capacitance C tells how much charge the capacitor takes in per volt of voltage:

\[ C = \frac{Q}{U} \qquad W = \tfrac{1}{2}CU^2 \]

Farad je ogromna jedinica, pa se koriste mikrofaradi (1 μF = 10−6 F), nanofaradi (10−9 F) i pikofaradi (10−12 F). Struja ne teče kroz dielektrik. Ona teče u kolu dok se obloge pune ili prazne, i tim kretanjem naelektrisanja dobijamo osećaj da struja „prolazi” kroz kondenzator.

The farad is a huge unit, so we use microfarads (1 μF = 10−6 F), nanofarads (10−9 F) and picofarads (10−12 F). Current does not flow through the dielectric. It flows in the circuit while the plates are charging or discharging, and that movement of charge gives the impression that current "passes" through the capacitor.

Deo 2Part 2 · jednosmerna strujadirect current

Kondenzator u kolu jednosmerne strujeA capacitor in a DC circuit

Priključimo kondenzator na bateriju E preko otpornika R. Na početku je kondenzator prazan, pa na njemu nema napona i cela baterija je na otporniku: struja je najveća, \( I_0 = E/R \). Kako se obloge pune, napon na kondenzatoru raste, napon na otporniku opada, pa opada i struja. Kada je \( u_C = E \), struje više nema i kondenzator je pun.

We connect a capacitor to a battery E through a resistor R. At the start the capacitor is empty, so there is no voltage across it and the whole battery is across the resistor: the current is largest, \( I_0 = E/R \). As the plates charge, the capacitor voltage rises, the resistor voltage falls, and so the current falls. When \( u_C = E \) there is no current any more and the capacitor is full.

\[ u_C(t) = E\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right) \qquad i(t) = \frac{E}{R}\,e^{-t/\tau} \qquad \tau = RC \]

Posle jedne vremenske konstante napon dostiže \( 1 - e^{-1} = 63\ \% \) od E, posle pet konstanti 99,3 %, pa se kondenzator smatra punim. Pražnjenje preko otpornika ide istim zakonom, ali opadajuće:

After one time constant the voltage reaches \( 1 - e^{-1} = 63\ \% \) of E, after five constants 99.3 %, and the capacitor is considered full. Discharging through a resistor follows the same law, but falling:

\[ u_C(t) = U_0\,e^{-t/\tau} \qquad i(t) = -\frac{U_0}{R}\,e^{-t/\tau} \]

Ustaljeno stanjeSteady state

Kada se sve smiri (posle ~5τ) struja kroz kondenzator je nula, pa se on za jednosmernu struju ponaša kao prekid kola. Kažemo da kondenzator „ne propušta” jednosmernu struju. Odatle dolazi njegova uloga da odvaja jednosmernu od naizmenične komponente.

When everything settles (after ~5τ) the current through the capacitor is zero, so for direct current it behaves like an open circuit. We say the capacitor "blocks" direct current. That is where its role of separating the direct and the alternating component comes from.

Deo 3Part 3 · modelmodel

Probaj punjenje i pražnjenjeTry charging and discharging

Menjaj R i C i posmatraj kako se menja brzina procesa. Veći R znači manja struja punjenja, pa je sporije punjenje. Veći C znači da treba doneti više naelektrisanja, pa je takođe sporije. Zato je \( \tau = RC \).

Change R and C and watch how the speed of the process changes. A larger R means a smaller charging current, so charging is slower. A larger C means more charge has to be delivered, so it is also slower. That is why \( \tau = RC \).

Pomisli prvo: R = 10 kΩ i C = 100 μF. Koliko traje potpuno punjenje (5τ)?Think first: R = 10 kΩ and C = 100 μF. How long does a full charge (5τ) take?

\( \tau = 10^4\cdot 10^{-4} = 1\ \mathrm{s} \), pa je potpuno punjenje oko 5 s.

\( \tau = 10^4\cdot 10^{-4} = 1\ \mathrm{s} \), so a full charge takes about 5 s.

Deo 4Part 4 · naizmenična strujaalternating current

Kondenzator u kolu naizmenične strujeA capacitor in an AC circuit

Sada na kondenzator dovedemo napon \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \). Struja kondenzatora je brzina promene naelektrisanja, \( i = dQ/dt = C\,du/dt \). Izvod sinusa je kosinus, pa je:

Now we apply a voltage \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \) to the capacitor. The capacitor current is the rate of change of charge, \( i = dQ/dt = C\,du/dt \). The derivative of a sine is a cosine, so:

\[ i = C\frac{du}{dt} = \omega C U_m\cos\omega t = \omega C U_m\sin(\omega t + 90^\circ) \]
\[ I_m = \omega C\,U_m \qquad X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C} = \frac{1}{2\pi fC} \qquad \underline{Z}_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C} = -jX_C = X_C\angle -90^\circ \]

Struja prednjači naponu za 90° (ICE). Reaktansa je obrnuto srazmerna učestanosti i kapacitivnosti: za jednosmernu struju (f = 0) je beskonačna (prekid kola), a sa porastom učestanosti opada, pa kondenzator sve bolje „propušta” naizmeničnu struju.

The current leads the voltage by 90° (ICE). The reactance is inversely proportional to frequency and capacitance: for direct current (f = 0) it is infinite (an open circuit), and as the frequency rises it falls, so the capacitor "passes" alternating current better and better.

Idealan kondenzator, kao i kalem, ne troši srednju snagu: \( p = UI\sin 2\omega t \) i \( P = 0 \). Reaktivna snaga \( Q_C = -UI \) ima suprotan znak u odnosu na kalem. To je razlog što se kondenzatori koriste za kompenzaciju induktivnog opterećenja (motori) u industriji.

An ideal capacitor, like a coil, uses no average power: \( p = UI\sin 2\omega t \) and \( P = 0 \). The reactive power \( Q_C = -UI \) has the opposite sign compared with the coil. That is why capacitors are used to compensate inductive loads (motors) in industry.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Obrnuta reaktansa. \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \): sa porastom učestanosti opada. Kod kalema je obrnuto.
  • Mikro u osnovne jedinice. 1 μF = 10−6 F. Greška od jedne stepenice daje reaktansu pogrešnu za 106.
  • Pogrešan smer kašnjenja. Kod kondenzatora struja prednjači (ICE). Kod kalema kasni (ELI).
  • Kondenzator „propušta” DC. Samo dok se puni ili prazni. U ustaljenom stanju kroz njega jednosmerna struja ne teče.
  • Pun posle jedne τ. Posle jedne τ je tek 63 %. Praktično pun kondenzator zahteva ~5τ.
  • An inverted reactance. \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \): it falls as frequency rises. For a coil it is the opposite.
  • Micro to base units. 1 μF = 10−6 F. A slip of one power gives a reactance wrong by a factor of 106.
  • The wrong direction of the lag. In a capacitor the current leads (ICE). In a coil it lags (ELI).
  • A capacitor "passes" DC. Only while it is charging or discharging. In the steady state no direct current flows through it.
  • Full after one τ. After one τ it is only 63 %. A practically full capacitor takes ~5τ.

Deo 7Part 7

SažetakSummary

  • Kapacitivnost \( C = Q/U \), energija \( W = CU^2/2 \). Jedinica je farad, praktično μF, nF i pF.
  • U kolu jednosmerne struje kondenzator se puni i prazni po eksponencijalnom zakonu sa vremenskom konstantom \( \tau = RC \); posle ~5τ je pun i struja je nula (prekid kola).
  • U kolu naizmenične struje \( i = C\,du/dt \): struja prednjači naponu za 90° (ICE).
  • Kapacitivna reaktansa \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \) opada sa učestanošću; impedansa je \( \underline{Z}_C = 1/(j\omega C) = -jX_C \).
  • Idealni kondenzator ne troši snagu (\( P = 0 \)), samo razmenjuje energiju sa izvorom.
  • Capacitance \( C = Q/U \), energy \( W = CU^2/2 \). The unit is the farad, in practice μF, nF and pF.
  • In a DC circuit a capacitor charges and discharges exponentially with the time constant \( \tau = RC \); after ~5τ it is full and the current is zero (an open circuit).
  • In an AC circuit \( i = C\,du/dt \): the current leads the voltage by 90° (ICE).
  • The capacitive reactance \( X_C = 1/(\omega C) \) falls with frequency; the impedance is \( \underline{Z}_C = 1/(j\omega C) = -jX_C \).
  • An ideal capacitor consumes no power (\( P = 0 \)), it only exchanges energy with the source.
