Deo 1Part 1 · središte računarathe centre of the computer

Zašto je matična ploča najvažnija?Why is the motherboard so important?

Procesor, memorija i grafička kartica određuju koliko je računar brz, ali matična ploča određuje šta uopšte može da se spoji: koji procesor, koja vrsta i količina memorije, koliko diskova, koliko monitora, da li ima Wi-Fi, koliko USB portova i koliko će kasnije moći da se nadogradi. Zato se računar obično gradi oko matične ploče.

The processor, memory and graphics card determine how fast a computer is, but the motherboard determines what can be connected at all: which processor, what type and amount of memory, how many drives, how many monitors, whether there is Wi-Fi, how many USB ports and how much it can be upgraded later. That is why a computer is usually built around the motherboard.

Prva PC ploča (IBM, 1981) imala je samo procesor, nekoliko čipova i slotove. Standard ATX iz 1995. godine (Intel) definisao je veličinu ploče, raspored rupa, konektore napajanja i položaj zadnjeg panela. Zahvaljujući njemu danas bilo koje ATX kućište i napajanje prima bilo koju ATX ploču. Razvoj ide ka tome da se sve više funkcija seli u procesor (memorijski kontroler, PCIe, grafika), pa ploča ostaje „razvodnik” i izvor napajanja.

The first PC board (IBM, 1981) had only a processor, a few chips and slots. The ATX standard from 1995 (Intel) defined the size of the board, the layout of the holes, the power connectors and the position of the rear panel. Thanks to it any ATX case and power supply today takes any ATX board. Development goes towards moving more and more functions into the processor (the memory controller, PCIe, graphics), so the board stays the "distributor" and the source of power.

Pitanje za razmišljanjeA question to think about

Zašto se memorija i grafička kartica vezuju na procesor, a ne na čipset? Zato što su najbrže i najpotrebnije: svaki dodatni čip na putu povećava kašnjenje (latenciju).

Why are the memory and the graphics card connected to the processor and not to the chipset? Because they are the fastest and most needed: every extra chip on the path increases the delay (latency).

Deo 3Part 3 · procesor i čipsetprocessor and chipset

Podnožje i čipsetThe socket and the chipset

Podnožje (socket) je fizički i električni interfejs za procesor. Kod LGA (land grid array) pinovi su na ploči, a procesor ima ravne kontakte, pa se kriva igla lako popravi, ali ploča se lako ošteti ako se pogrešno stavi procesor. Kod PGA pinovi su na procesoru. Procesori različitih generacija često koriste različita podnožja, a čak i isto podnožje ne znači da će stariji BIOS prepoznati novi procesor.

The socket is the physical and electrical interface for the processor. With LGA (land grid array) the pins are on the board and the processor has flat contacts, so a bent pin is easy to fix but the board is easily damaged if the processor is placed wrongly. With PGA the pins are on the processor. Processors of different generations often use different sockets, and even the same socket does not mean an older BIOS will recognise a new processor.

Čipset određuje koliko ima USB portova, SATA portova, PCIe traka, da li se procesor može „overklokovati” i koje su funkcije dostupne. Šifra obično kaže nivo: kod Intela Z (entuzijasti, overklok), B (srednja klasa), H (osnovna); kod AMD-a X (vrhunska), B (srednja), A (osnovna).

The chipset determines how many USB ports, SATA ports and PCIe lanes there are, whether the processor can be overclocked and which features are available. The code usually tells the level: for Intel Z (enthusiast, overclocking), B (mid-range), H (basic); for AMD X (high-end), B (mid-range), A (entry).

Deo 4Part 4 · radna memorijaworking memory

Memorijski slotoviThe memory slots

Moduli radne memorije (DIMM) idu u slotove sa urezom i dvema kopčama. Vrsta memorije mora da odgovara ploči: DDR4 i DDR5 su različiti i fizički se ne mogu zameniti (urez je na drugom mestu). Memorijski kontroler je u procesoru i podržava dva kanala: dva modula u pravim slotovima rade dvostruko brže od jednog.

Working-memory modules (DIMM) go into slots with a notch and two clips. The memory type must match the board: DDR4 and DDR5 are different and physically cannot be swapped (the notch is in a different place). The memory controller is in the processor and supports two channels: two modules in the right slots run twice as fast as one.

Deo 5Part 5 · magistralathe bus

PCIe slotovi i M.2PCIe slots and M.2

PCI Express je serijska magistrala sa „trakama” (lane). Svaka traka ima dva para žica, jedan za svaki smer. Uređaj koristi 1, 4, 8 ili 16 traka (x1, x4, x8, x16). Procesor daje obično 16 traka za grafičku i 4 za M.2 SSD, a čipset dodatne trake za ostalo. Broj traka je ograničen, pa ponekad uključivanje drugog M.2 slota smanjuje broj dostupnih SATA portova.

PCI Express is a serial bus made of "lanes". Each lane has two pairs of wires, one for each direction. A device uses 1, 4, 8 or 16 lanes (x1, x4, x8, x16). The processor usually provides 16 lanes for the graphics card and 4 for an M.2 SSD, and the chipset provides more lanes for the rest. The number of lanes is limited, so sometimes using a second M.2 slot reduces the number of available SATA ports.

Svaka nova generacija udvostručuje brzinu, a uređaji i slotovi su unazad kompatibilni: kartica PCIe 4.0 radi u slotu 3.0, ali brzinom 3.0. M.2 je oblik uređaja, ne protokola: M.2 SSD može da koristi PCIe (NVMe, brz) ili SATA (isti kao 2,5″ SATA SSD). Proveri oznaku „M-key, PCIe/NVMe” u specifikaciji ploče.

Each new generation doubles the speed, and devices and slots are backwards compatible: a PCIe 4.0 card works in a 3.0 slot, but at 3.0 speed. M.2 is a shape of device, not a protocol: an M.2 SSD can use PCIe (NVMe, fast) or SATA (the same as a 2.5″ SATA SSD). Check the "M-key, PCIe/NVMe" mark in the board specification.

Deo 6Part 6 · napajanje procesorapowering the processor

VRM i napajanje pločeThe VRM and board power

Procesor radi na oko 1 V, ali troši i do 250 A. Takvu struju ne možeš da vodiš sa 12 V kroz tanke trase, pa VRM (voltage regulator module) spušta napon na samoj ploči, blizu procesora. Svaka faza VRM-a ima dva MOSFET-a (jedan uključuje, jedan isključuje struju prema kalemu), prigušnicu (choke) i kondenzatore. Faze rade naizmenično (u „fazama”), pa je izlazni napon glatkiji. Kod DrMOS kola MOSFET-ovi i drajver su u jednom čipu.

The processor runs at about 1 V, but draws up to 250 A. You cannot carry such a current from 12 V through thin traces, so the VRM (voltage regulator module) steps the voltage down on the board itself, close to the processor. Each phase of the VRM has two MOSFETs (one switches the current on, one off, towards the coil), a choke and capacitors. The phases work alternately (in "phases"), so the output voltage is smoother. In a DrMOS circuit the MOSFETs and the driver are in one chip.

Ovo je isti princip „silazni pretvarač” (buck) koji smo videli u lekciji o napajanju: prekidač radi sa faktorom ispune \( D = U_{out}/U_{in} \). Za 12 V u i 1,2 V izlaz, \( D = 10\ \% \). Više faza znači manju struju po fazi, manje zagrevanje i stabilniji napon pri naglim promenama opterećenja.

This is the same "step-down" (buck) principle we saw in the lesson on power supplies: the switch works with the duty cycle \( D = U_{out}/U_{in} \). For 12 V in and 1.2 V out, \( D = 10\ \% \). More phases mean a smaller current per phase, less heating and a steadier voltage when the load changes suddenly.

Deo 7Part 7 · firmverfirmware

BIOS/UEFI i CMOSBIOS/UEFI and CMOS

Čim se računar uključi, procesor izvršava program iz BIOS/UEFI flash čipa. Taj program: (1) proverava hardver (POST, power-on self-test), (2) inicijalizuje memoriju i uređaje, (3) pronalazi disk sa operativnim sistemom po redosledu pokretanja (boot order) i (4) predaje mu kontrolu. Ako POST pronađe grešku, javlja je kodom na ploči (LED, 7-segmentni displej) ili zvučnim signalima.

As soon as the computer is switched on, the processor runs a program from the BIOS/UEFI flash chip. That program (1) checks the hardware (POST, power-on self-test), (2) initialises the memory and devices, (3) finds the disk with the operating system by the boot order and (4) hands control over to it. If POST finds an error, it reports it with a code on the board (LEDs, a 7-segment display) or with beeps.

  • UEFI je moderni naslednik BIOS-a: grafički meni, podrška za diskove preko 2 TB (GPT), brže pokretanje i Secure Boot.
  • TPM 2.0 je bezbednosni čip (ili funkcija procesora) koji traži Windows 11.
  • XMP/EXPO uključuje brzinu memorije, a krive ventilatora podešavaju buku i hlađenje.
  • CMOS baterija čuva sat i podešavanja; Clear CMOS (jumper ili dugme) vraća fabrička podešavanja.
  • BIOS flashback omogućava ažuriranje bez procesora, sa USB fleša.
  • UEFI is the modern successor of the BIOS: a graphical menu, support for drives over 2 TB (GPT), faster start-up and Secure Boot.
  • TPM 2.0 is a security chip (or processor feature) required by Windows 11.
  • XMP/EXPO switches on the memory speed, and fan curves set the noise and cooling.
  • The CMOS battery keeps the clock and the settings; Clear CMOS (a jumper or button) restores the factory settings.
  • BIOS flashback allows updating without a processor, from a USB stick.

Deo 9Part 9 · kako biratihow to choose

Kako se bira matična pločaHow to choose a motherboard

Počni od procesora, pa proveri redom: (1) podnožje odgovara procesoru; (2) čipset podržava potrebne funkcije i BIOS podržava taj model procesora; (3) vrsta memorije (DDR4 ili DDR5), broj slotova i maksimalna brzina; (4) form-faktor odgovara kućištu; (5) dovoljno M.2 i SATA za diskove i PCIe za kartice; (6) portovi (USB-C, 2,5 G mreža, Wi-Fi); (7) kvalitetan VRM ako je procesor jak ili se overklokuje; (8) cena i garancija.

Start from the processor, then check in turn: (1) the socket matches the processor; (2) the chipset supports the needed features and the BIOS supports that processor model; (3) the memory type (DDR4 or DDR5), the number of slots and the maximum speed; (4) the form factor fits the case; (5) enough M.2 and SATA for drives and PCIe for cards; (6) the ports (USB-C, 2.5 G network, Wi-Fi); (7) a good VRM if the processor is powerful or overclocked; (8) price and warranty.

Dato: procesor povlači 150 A, VRM ima 12 faza. Kolika je struja po fazi i koliki je faktor ispune za 12 V → 1,2 V?

  1. \( I_{phase} = 150/12 = 12.5\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. \( D = U_{out}/U_{in} = 1.2/12 = 0.1 = 10\ \% \).
  3. With 6 phases the current per phase would be 25 A, so the MOSFETs would be loaded twice as much.

Primer 4 · provera kompatibilnostiExample 4 · a compatibility check

Dato: želiš Ryzen sa podnožjem AM5 i DDR5 memoriju. Ponuđena je ploča sa podnožjem AM4 i DDR4 slotovima. Šta ne odgovara?

Given: you want a Ryzen with the AM5 socket and DDR5 memory. The offered board has an AM4 socket and DDR4 slots. What does not match?

  1. Procesor AM5 se ne može staviti u AM4 podnožje (drugačiji raspored i broj kontakata).
  2. DDR5 modul se ne može staviti u DDR4 slot (urez je na drugom mestu i naponi su različiti).
  3. Potrebna je ploča sa podnožjem AM5 i DDR5 slotovima (npr. sa čipsetom B650).
  1. An AM5 processor cannot be put into an AM4 socket (a different layout and number of contacts).
  2. A DDR5 module cannot be put into a DDR4 slot (the notch is in a different place and the voltages differ).
  3. A board with the AM5 socket and DDR5 slots is needed (e.g. with the B650 chipset).

Deo 11Part 11 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Ista marka, isto podnožje. Intel i AMD menjaju podnožja i po nekoliko puta u deceniji. Uvek proveri tačan naziv (LGA1700, AM5).
  • Ploča podržava procesor, ali BIOS ne. Starija verzija BIOS-a ne prepoznaje noviji procesor. Potrebno je ažuriranje (ponekad flashback).
  • Zanemarivanje traka. Previše M.2 i PCIe uređaja može deliti iste trake i usporiti jedan drugog ili isključiti SATA portove.
  • Slabo hlađenje VRM-a. Jak procesor na jeftinoj ploči pregreva VRM i procesor smanjuje brzinu (throttling).
  • Same brand, same socket. Intel and AMD change sockets several times a decade. Always check the exact name (LGA1700, AM5).
  • The board supports the processor, but the BIOS does not. An older BIOS version does not recognise a newer processor. An update is needed (sometimes flashback).
  • Ignoring lanes. Too many M.2 and PCIe devices may share the same lanes and slow each other down or disable SATA ports.
  • Weak VRM cooling. A powerful processor on a cheap board overheats the VRM and the processor slows down (throttling).

Deo 12Part 12

SažetakSummary

  • Matična ploča određuje šta se uopšte može spojiti: procesor (podnožje), memoriju, diskove, kartice i portove.
  • Najbrže veze (RAM, PCIe x16, M.2) idu direktno na procesor; ostalo ide preko čipseta.
  • Memorija je dvokanalna: ( B = fcdot 64/8 cdot N ). PCIe udvostručuje brzinu svakom generacijom.
  • VRM pretvara 12 V u ~1 V i veliku struju po fazama; više faza znači manje opterećenje po fazi.
  • BIOS/UEFI pokreće računar (POST, boot); CMOS baterija čuva podešavanja.
  • Biraj ploču redom: podnožje, čipset, memorija, form-faktor, M.2/SATA/PCIe, portovi, VRM.
  • The motherboard decides what can be connected at all: the processor (socket), memory, drives, cards and ports.
  • The fastest links (RAM, PCIe x16, M.2) go directly to the processor; the rest goes through the chipset.
  • Memory is dual-channel: ( B = fcdot 64/8 cdot N ). PCIe doubles its speed with every generation.
  • The VRM converts 12 V into ~1 V and a large current in phases; more phases mean less load per phase.
  • The BIOS/UEFI starts the computer (POST, boot); the CMOS battery keeps the settings.
  • Choose a board in order: socket, chipset, memory, form factor, M.2/SATA/PCIe, ports, VRM.
