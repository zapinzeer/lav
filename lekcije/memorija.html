Deo 1Part 1 · čemu služiwhat it is for

Šta je memorija i kako se deli?What is memory and how is it divided?

Računar mora negde da čuva programe i podatke. Ne postoji jedna memorija koja je istovremeno brza, velika, jeftina i trajna, pa se računar služi mešavinom. Dve osnovne grupe su unutrašnja (radna, glavna) i spoljna (masovna, trajna) memorija.

A computer has to keep programs and data somewhere. No single memory is at the same time fast, large, cheap and permanent, so a computer uses a mix. The two basic groups are internal (working, main) and external (mass, permanent) memory.

Deo 2Part 2 · hijerarhijahierarchy

Piramida memorijeThe memory pyramid

Memorije se slažu u hijerarhiju: što je bliže procesoru, to je brže, skuplje i manje. Procesor traži podatak prvo u najbržem nivou. Ako ga nađe (pogodak, hit), gotovo je. Ako ne (promašaj, miss), traži u sledećem, sporijem nivou.

Memories are stacked into a hierarchy: the closer to the processor, the faster, more expensive and smaller. The processor looks for data in the fastest level first. If it finds it (a hit), it is done. If not (a miss), it goes on to the next, slower level.

Pomisli prvo: zašto računar ne napravi svu memoriju od najbrže vrste?Think first: why does a computer not make all its memory from the fastest kind?

Cena. Keš (SRAM) košta stotine puta više po bajtu od RAM-a i zauzima mnogo prostora na čipu. Računar sa 16 GB keša bi bio nepraktičan.

Cost. Cache (SRAM) costs hundreds of times more per byte than RAM and takes a lot of room on the chip. A computer with 16 GB of cache would be impractical.

Deo 3Part 3 · radna memorijaworking memory

Unutrašnja memorija: registri, keš, RAM, ROMInternal memory: registers, cache, RAM, ROM

  • Registri: desetak do stotinu 64-bitnih ćelija u samom jezgru procesora. Najbrži, nulto kašnjenje.
  • Keš L1, L2, L3: SRAM unutar procesora. L1 je mali (32–64 KB po jezgru) i najbrži, L3 je zajednički i velik (do desetina MB).
  • RAM: DRAM u modulima (DIMM) u slotovima ploče. Svaki bit je kondenzator koji se mora osvežavati više puta u sekundi, inače gubi naboj. Radi kao radni sto programa.
  • ROM, flash BIOS/UEFI: neisparljiva memorija sa programom za pokretanje.
  • Registers: from about ten to a hundred 64-bit cells inside the processor core. The fastest, zero latency.
  • L1, L2, L3 cache: SRAM inside the processor. L1 is small (32–64 KB per core) and fastest, L3 is shared and large (up to tens of MB).
  • RAM: DRAM in modules (DIMMs) in the board slots. Each bit is a capacitor that must be refreshed many times a second, otherwise it loses its charge. It acts as the working desk of programs.
  • ROM, flash BIOS/UEFI: non-volatile memory holding the start-up program.

Brzina RAM-a se zapisuje kao DDR5-6000: „6000” je broj prenosa u sekundi (MT/s), a stvarna učestanost sata je upola manja jer se podaci prenose na uzlaznu i silaznu ivicu. Memorijski modul je 64-bitni, pa je propusnost jednog kanala \( B = 6000\cdot 10^6 \cdot 8\ \mathrm{B} = 48\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).

The speed of RAM is written as DDR5-6000: "6000" is the number of transfers per second (MT/s), and the real clock frequency is half of it because data moves on both the rising and falling edge. A memory module is 64-bit, so the bandwidth of one channel is \( B = 6000\cdot 10^6 \cdot 8\ \mathrm{B} = 48\ \mathrm{GB/s} \).

Deo 4Part 4 · trajna memorijapermanent storage

Spoljna memorija: HDD, SSD, flash i optikaExternal memory: HDD, SSD, flash and optical

HDD (hard disk) čuva podatke magnetno na rotirajućim pločama (5400 ili 7200 o/min), a glava ih čita pomerajući se po ploči. Mehanički je, pa je spor (10 ms za pristup) i osetljiv na udarce, ali je jeftin po gigabajtu.

An HDD (hard disk) stores data magnetically on spinning platters (5400 or 7200 rpm) and a head reads them by moving over the platter. It is mechanical, so it is slow (10 ms access) and sensitive to shocks, but cheap per gigabyte.

SSD nema pokretnih delova: podaci su u NAND flash ćelijama koje drže naboj i bez napona. Pristup je ~0,05 ms, a brzina zavisi od veze: SATA je ograničena na ~550 MB/s, a NVMe preko PCIe-a ide od 3500 MB/s (PCIe 3.0 x4) do preko 7000 MB/s (PCIe 4.0 x4). Ćelija NAND-a podnosi ograničen broj upisa, pa SSD ima wear leveling (ravnomerno trošenje).

An SSD has no moving parts: the data sit in NAND flash cells that hold charge without power. Access takes ~0.05 ms and the speed depends on the link: SATA is limited to ~550 MB/s while NVMe over PCIe goes from 3500 MB/s (PCIe 3.0 x4) to over 7000 MB/s (PCIe 4.0 x4). A NAND cell tolerates a limited number of writes, so an SSD uses wear levelling.

Deo 5Part 5 · jedinice i vremeunits and time

Jedinice i vreme prenosaUnits and transfer time

Osnovna jedinica je bit (0 ili 1); bajt ima 8 bitova. Pazi na razliku: proizvođači diskova računaju decimalno (1 GB = 109 B), a operativni sistem često binarno (1 GiB = 230 B ≈ 1,074 · 109 B). Zato disk „od 500 GB” u sistemu pokazuje oko 465 GiB.

The basic unit is the bit (0 or 1); a byte has 8 bits. Mind the difference: drive makers count in decimal (1 GB = 109 B), while the operating system often counts in binary (1 GiB = 230 B ≈ 1.074 · 109 B). That is why a "500 GB" drive shows about 465 GiB in the system.

Deo 6Part 6 · swap i keš diskaswap and disk cache

Kad RAM nije dovoljanWhen RAM is not enough

Ako programi traže više od RAM-a, operativni sistem prebacuje manje korišćene delove na disk: to je virtuelna memorija (swap, page file). Radi, ali je i do hiljadu puta sporija, pa računar „zastaje”. Dodavanje RAM-a je tada najbolje ulaganje.

If programs ask for more than the RAM, the operating system moves the less-used parts to the disk: this is virtual memory (swap, page file). It works, but is up to a thousand times slower, so the computer "stalls". Adding RAM is then the best investment.

Obrnuto, sistem koristi slobodan RAM kao keš diska: nedavno čitani fajlovi ostaju u RAM-u pa se drugi put otvaraju odmah.

Conversely, the system uses free RAM as a disk cache: recently read files stay in RAM so they open at once the second time.

Deo 8Part 8 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • RAM i disk su isto. RAM je radna memorija i gubi sadržaj bez struje. Disk je trajna memorija. „16 GB” RAM-a i „512 GB” diska su različite stvari.
  • Bit i bajt. Mb/s nije MB/s. Razlika je 8 puta.
  • SSD ne troši. NAND ima ograničen broj upisa. Za običnu upotrebu traje godinama, ali se ne sme ignorisati kod stalnih velikih upisa.
  • Mešanje modula. RAM moduli različitih brzina rade na brzini najsporijeg, a moduli u dvokanalnom režimu treba da budu isti i u pravim slotovima.
  • RAM and disk are the same. RAM is working memory and loses its contents without power. A disk is permanent storage. "16 GB" of RAM and a "512 GB" disk are different things.
  • Bit and byte. Mb/s is not MB/s. The difference is a factor of 8.
  • SSDs never wear. NAND has a limited number of writes. For normal use it lasts for years, but it must not be ignored under constant heavy writing.
  • Mixing modules. RAM modules of different speeds run at the speed of the slowest one, and modules in dual-channel mode should be identical and in the right slots.

Deo 9Part 9

SažetakSummary

  • Unutrašnja memorija (registri, keš, RAM) je brza i isparljiva; spoljna (SSD, HDD, USB) je sporija i trajna.
  • Hijerarhija: što je bliže procesoru, to je brže, skuplje i manje; keš skraćuje prosečno vreme pristupa.
  • DRAM mora da se osvežava; SRAM ne, ali je skuplji i koristi se za keš.
  • NVMe SSD je desetine puta brži od HDD-a, jer nema mehaniku i koristi PCIe.
  • Vreme prenosa ( t = S/v ); pazi na bite i bajtove, GB i GiB.
  • Kad RAM nije dovoljan, sistem koristi disk (swap) i sve usporava.
  • Internal memory (registers, cache, RAM) is fast and volatile; external memory (SSD, HDD, USB) is slower and permanent.
  • The hierarchy: the closer to the processor, the faster, more expensive and smaller; the cache shortens the average access time.
  • DRAM must be refreshed; SRAM does not, but costs more and is used for cache.
  • An NVMe SSD is tens of times faster than an HDD, since it has no mechanics and uses PCIe.
  • The transfer time is ( t = S/v ); watch bits and bytes, GB and GiB.
  • When RAM is not enough the system uses the disk (swap) and everything slows down.
