Deo 1Part 1 · vrste kvarovakinds of faults

Šta je kvar i kako se deleWhat a fault is and how faults are grouped

Kvar je stanje u kome računar ili neki njegov deo ne radi onako kako treba. Kvar se uvek nekako manifestuje (pokazuje): računar se ne pali, piska, gasi se, slika je izobličena, programi se ruše. Zadatak servisera je da od simptoma dođe do uzroka.

A fault is a state in which the computer or one of its parts does not work as it should. A fault always manifests (shows) itself somehow: the computer does not start, beeps, shuts down, the picture is distorted, programs crash. A technician's job is to get from the symptom to the cause.

With any disk fault, or even a suspicion of one, save the data (backup) first, and only then repair. Parts can be bought; photos and schoolwork cannot.

Deo 2Part 2 · pri paljenjuat power-on

POST: provera pri paljenjuPOST: the power-on self-test

Odmah po uključivanju BIOS/UEFI pokreće POST (Power-On Self-Test): redom proverava procesor, memoriju, grafičku karticu i uređaje za pokretanje sistema. Ako je sve u redu, čuje se jedan kratak pisak (ili nijedan) i počinje učitavanje operativnog sistema. Ako nešto ne valja, POST staje i javlja grešku:

Right after power-on the BIOS/UEFI runs POST (Power-On Self-Test): it checks the processor, memory, graphics card and boot devices in turn. If all is well, you hear one short beep (or none) and the operating system starts loading. If something is wrong, POST stops and reports an error:

  • zvučnim signalima (beep kodovima) preko malog zvučnika na ploči;
  • LED lampicama na ploči (CPU, DRAM, VGA, BOOT): ostane da svetli ona kod koje je stalo;
  • dvocifrenim kodom (Q-code, POST code) na malom displeju skupljih ploča;
  • porukom na ekranu, ako grafika radi.
  • beep codes through a small speaker on the board;
  • LEDs on the board (CPU, DRAM, VGA, BOOT): the one where it stopped stays lit;
  • a two-digit code (Q-code, POST code) on a small display on dearer boards;
  • a message on the screen, if graphics works.

Značenje zvučnih signala zavisi od proizvođača BIOS-a (AMI, Award, Phoenix) i ploče, pa se uvek proverava u priručniku. Često je: neprekidni ili ponavljani dugi pisak znači problem sa memorijom, a jedan dug i dva ili tri kratka problem sa grafikom.

The meaning of beeps depends on the BIOS maker (AMI, Award, Phoenix) and the board, so always check the manual. Commonly: a continuous or repeating long beep means a memory problem, and one long and two or three short a graphics problem.

Deo 4Part 4 · toplotaheat

Pregrevanje i prašinaOverheating and dust

Najčešći uzrok „čudnih” kvarova je pregrevanje. Prašina zatrpa rebra hladnjaka i filtere, ventilator se istroši, a termalna pasta između procesora i hladnjaka se posle nekoliko godina osuši. Kada temperatura pređe granicu, procesor i grafička kartica sami smanje brzinu (throttling), a ako ni to ne pomogne, računar se ugasi da bi se zaštitio.

The most common cause of "strange" faults is overheating. Dust clogs the cooler fins and filters, a fan wears out, and the thermal paste between the processor and the cooler dries out after a few years. When the temperature goes over the limit, the processor and graphics card slow themselves down (throttling), and if that does not help, the computer shuts down to protect itself.

Symptoms of overheating: the fans are always loud, the computer slows down after a few minutes of a game, it always shuts down under heavy work, and the case is hot. The fix: blow out the dust with compressed air (hold the fan so it does not spin), replace the thermal paste and make sure air can flow.

Deo 5Part 5 · hardver ili softverhardware or software

Hardverski ili softverski kvar?Hardware or software fault?

Isti simptom, na primer plavi ekran, može da izazove i neispravna memorija i loš drajver. Da bi ih razlikovao:

The same symptom, for example a blue screen, can be caused by both faulty memory and a bad driver. To tell them apart:

  • Da li se kvar javlja i pre učitavanja sistema (u BIOS-u, tokom POST-a)? Ako da, gotovo sigurno je hardver.
  • Pokreni računar sa USB-a sa drugim sistemom (live Linux, Windows instalacija). Ako tamo sve radi, problem je verovatno u instaliranom sistemu.
  • Probaj bezbedni režim (Safe Mode): sistem se učitava sa osnovnim drajverima.
  • Pogledaj Event Viewer (Pregledač događaja) i kod sa plavog ekrana (npr. MEMORY_MANAGEMENT ukazuje na RAM).
  • Seti se šta je poslednje promenjeno: novi program, drajver, ažuriranje ili deo.
  • Does the fault also appear before the system loads (in the BIOS, during POST)? If yes, it is almost certainly hardware.
  • Start the computer from USB with a different system (live Linux, Windows setup). If everything works there, the problem is probably in the installed system.
  • Try Safe Mode: the system loads with basic drivers.
  • Look at the Event Viewer and the code on the blue screen (e.g. MEMORY_MANAGEMENT points to RAM).
  • Remember what changed last: a new program, driver, update or part.

Deo 6Part 6 · metodamethod

Postupak traženja kvaraHow to look for a fault

Dobar serviser ne menja delove nasumice, nego radi po postupku. Najvažnije pravilo: menjaj jednu stvar u isto vreme, inače nećeš znati šta je pomoglo.

A good technician does not swap parts at random, but works by a procedure. The most important rule: change one thing at a time, otherwise you will not know what helped.

Jedna od najmoćnijih metoda je zamena ispravnim delom: sumnjivi deo zameniš delom za koji znaš da radi. Ako kvar nestane, našao si krivca. Druga je minimalna konfiguracija: ostaviš samo ploču, procesor, jedan modul RAM-a i napajanje, pa dodaješ delove jedan po jedan.

One of the most powerful methods is swapping with a known-good part: you replace the suspect part with one you know works. If the fault goes away, you have found the culprit. Another is the minimal configuration: leave only the board, processor, one RAM module and the power supply, then add parts one at a time.

Deo 7Part 7 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · pisak pri paljenjuExample 1 · beep at power-on

Dato: računar se pali, ventilatori se vrte, nema slike, a ploča ponavlja dug pisak. Lampica DRAM svetli.

Given: the computer starts, the fans spin, there is no picture, and the board repeats a long beep. The DRAM LED is lit.

  1. Ponavljani dug pisak i DRAM lampica ukazuju na memoriju.
  2. Isključi računar, izvadi RAM, proveri da nema prašine i vrati ga čvrsto do klika.
  3. Ako ne pomogne, probaj jedan po jedan modul i drugi slot.
  1. A repeating long beep and the DRAM LED point to memory.
  2. Switch off, take the RAM out, check for dust and put it back firmly until it clicks.
  3. If that does not help, try one module at a time and another slot.

kvar ili loše postavljen RAMfaulty or badly seated RAM

Primer 2 · gašenje u igriExample 2 · shutting down in a game

Dato: računar radi normalno u Wordu, ali se posle 10 minuta igre sam ugasi. Šta je verovatan uzrok?

Given: the computer works normally in Word, but after 10 minutes of a game it switches itself off. What is the likely cause?

  1. Kvar se javlja samo pod opterećenjem i posle nekog vremena.
  2. To ukazuje na pregrevanje (prašina, pasta) ili na slabo napajanje.
  3. Proveri temperature u HWMonitoru tokom igre; ako su ispod 85 °C, sumnjaj na napajanje.
  1. The fault appears only under load and after some time.
  2. That points to overheating (dust, paste) or a weak power supply.
  3. Check the temperatures in HWMonitor during the game; if they stay below 85 °C, suspect the power supply.

pregrevanje ili napajanjeoverheating or the power supply

Primer 3 · kliktanje diskaExample 3 · clicking drive

Dato: stari računar je postao vrlo spor, a iz kućišta se čuje ritmično kliktanje.

Given: an old computer has become very slow, and rhythmic clicking comes from the case.

  1. Kliktanje HDD-a je znak mehaničkog kvara glave.
  2. CrystalDiskInfo verovatno pokazuje „Caution” ili „Bad”.
  3. Odmah kopiraj važne podatke na drugi disk, pa zameni disk.
  1. Clicking from an HDD is a sign of a mechanical head fault.
  2. CrystalDiskInfo will probably show "Caution" or "Bad".
  3. Copy the important data to another drive at once, then replace the drive.

HDD otkazuje: prvo bekapthe HDD is failing: backup first

Primer 4 · sat kasniExample 4 · clock lags

Dato: posle svakog isključivanja iz struje računar pokazuje pogrešan datum i traži podešavanje BIOS-a.

Given: after every time it is unplugged, the computer shows the wrong date and asks for BIOS setup.

  1. BIOS podešavanja i sat čuva CMOS baterija.
  2. Kad se baterija isprazni, sve se vraća na početno.
  3. Zameni bateriju CR2032 i ponovo podesi datum.
  1. The BIOS settings and clock are kept by the CMOS battery.
  2. When the battery is flat, everything resets.
  3. Replace the CR2032 battery and set the date again.

Deo 8Part 8 · paziwatch out

Česte greške pri traženju kvaraCommon mistakes when looking for a fault

  • Više promena odjednom. Zamenio si RAM i drajver i proradilo je. Šta je pomoglo? Ne znaš.
  • Preskočene jednostavne stvari. Pola „kvarova” su izvučen kabl, isključen prekidač na napajanju ili pogrešan ulaz monitora.
  • Popravka pre bekapa. Pokušaji popravke diska koji otkazuje mogu da unište poslednju šansu da se spasu podaci.
  • Otvaranje napajanja. U napajanju ostaje opasan napon i kad je izvučeno iz struje. Neispravno napajanje se menja, ne popravlja.
  • Nezapisani postupak. Bez beleški isti kvar sledeći put tražiš ispočetka.
  • Several changes at once. You replaced the RAM and the driver and it worked. What helped? You do not know.
  • Skipped simple things. Half the "faults" are a loose cable, the PSU switch off or the wrong monitor input.
  • Repair before backup. Trying to fix a failing drive can destroy the last chance to save the data.
  • Opening the power supply. A dangerous voltage stays inside a power supply even when unplugged. A faulty PSU is replaced, not repaired.
  • An unrecorded procedure. Without notes you search for the same fault from scratch next time.

SažetakSummary

  • Kvarovi su hardverski ili softverski, trajni ili povremeni; svaki se manifestuje nekim simptomom.
  • POST proverava CPU, RAM, grafiku i disk; grešku javlja piskom, lampicom, kodom ili porukom.
  • Značenje piskova zavisi od BIOS-a i ploče: proveri priručnik.
  • Pregrevanje izaziva usporavanje i gašenje; rešenje je čišćenje i nova termalna pasta.
  • Hardver od softvera razlikuješ preko POST-a, pokretanja sa USB-a, bezbednog režima i dnevnika grešaka.
  • Postupak: podaci, ponavljanje, prvo jednostavno, jedna promena, zamena ispravnim delom, zapis.
  • Faults are hardware or software, permanent or intermittent; each shows itself through some symptom.
  • POST checks the CPU, RAM, graphics and drive; it reports an error with a beep, an LED, a code or a message.
  • The meaning of beeps depends on the BIOS and the board: check the manual.
  • Overheating causes slowing down and shutdowns; the fix is cleaning and new thermal paste.
  • You tell hardware from software through POST, booting from USB, Safe Mode and the error log.
  • Procedure: information, reproduce, simple first, one change, swap with a good part, record.
