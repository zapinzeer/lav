Deo 1Part 1 · čemu služiwhat it is for

Šta radi grafička karticaWhat a graphics card does

Grafička kartica (video kartica, GPU) pretvara podatke iz računara u sliku na monitoru. Svaka slika je mreža piksela, a kartica mora da izračuna boju svakog od njih i to da ponovi 60, 144 ili više puta u sekundi. Za Full HD to je preko dva miliona piksela po slici.

The graphics card (video card, GPU) turns data from the computer into a picture on the monitor. Every picture is a grid of pixels, and the card has to work out the colour of each of them and repeat it 60, 144 or more times a second. For Full HD that is over two million pixels per picture.

Grafika može biti:

Graphics can be:

  • integrisana (iGPU): deo procesora; koristi običan RAM, troši malo i dovoljna je za kancelariju, internet i filmove;
  • diskretna (posebna kartica): ima svoj procesor i svoju memoriju, mnogo je jača i potrebna je za igre, 3D modelovanje, montažu videa i veštačku inteligenciju.
  • integrated (iGPU): part of the processor; it uses ordinary RAM, uses little power and is enough for office work, the internet and films;
  • dedicated (a separate card): it has its own processor and its own memory, is much stronger and is needed for games, 3D modelling, video editing and artificial intelligence.

Deo 3Part 3 · zašto je brzawhy it is fast

Mnogo malih radnika: paralelni radMany small workers: parallel work

Procesor (CPU) ima mali broj vrlo pametnih jezgara, obično 4 do 16. Ona brzo rešavaju složene zadatke jedan za drugim. Grafički procesor ima hiljade jednostavnih jezgara. Svako je sporije i „gluplje”, ali kad treba obojiti dva miliona piksela po istom pravilu, hiljade radnika završe posao mnogo brže od nekoliko stručnjaka.

The processor (CPU) has a small number of very clever cores, usually 4 to 16. They solve complex tasks quickly one after another. The graphics processor has thousands of simple cores. Each is slower and "simpler", but when two million pixels have to be coloured by the same rule, thousands of workers finish much faster than a few experts.

Pomisli prvo: zašto onda ne bacimo CPU i sve radimo na GPU?Think first: so why not throw away the CPU and do everything on the GPU?

Većina programa ima korake koji zavise jedan od drugog: ne možeš da uradiš drugi dok ne završiš prvi. Tu hiljade radnika ne pomažu. GPU je odličan samo za poslove koji se dele na mnogo istih, nezavisnih delova (pikseli, video, neuronske mreže).

Most programs have steps that depend on each other: you cannot do the second until the first is done. Thousands of workers do not help there. A GPU is great only for jobs that split into many identical, independent pieces (pixels, video, neural networks).

Deo 4Part 4 · video memorijavideo memory

VRAM i rezolucijaVRAM and resolution

Gotova slika se čuva u delu video memorije koji se zove frejm bafer. Svaki piksel zauzima obično 4 bajta (crvena, zelena, plava i providnost). Zato je jedna slika u Full HD rezoluciji oko 8 MB, a u 4K oko 33 MB. To nije mnogo; veći deo VRAM-a zauzimaju teksture (slike koje se „lepe” na 3D modele) i sami modeli.

The finished picture is kept in a part of video memory called the frame buffer. Each pixel usually takes 4 bytes (red, green, blue and transparency). So one picture in Full HD is about 8 MB, and in 4K about 33 MB. That is not much; most of the VRAM is taken by textures (pictures "glued" onto 3D models) and by the models themselves.

Za kancelarijski rad dovoljna je integrisana grafika. Za igre u Full HD danas se preporučuje bar 8 GB VRAM-a, a za 4K, montažu i AI 12–16 GB i više. Kada VRAM ponestane, kartica pozajmljuje sistemski RAM preko PCIe veze, a to je mnogo sporije, pa slika počinje da zastajkuje.

For office work integrated graphics is enough. For games in Full HD at least 8 GB of VRAM is recommended today, and for 4K, editing and AI 12–16 GB or more. When the VRAM runs out, the card borrows system RAM over the PCIe link, which is much slower, so the picture starts to stutter.

Deo 5Part 5 · postupakprocedure

Ugradnja grafičke karticeInstalling a graphics card

  1. Isključi računar, izvuci kabl i dodirni kućište.
  2. Ukloni jedan ili dva metalna poklopca slotova na zadnjoj strani kućišta (koliko je kartica široka).
  3. Otvori bravicu na kraju gornjeg PCIe x16 slota (onaj najbliži procesoru, jer ima najviše traka).
  4. Spusti karticu ravno u slot i pritisni dok bravica ne klikne.
  5. Pričvrsti nosač kartice zavrtnjima za kućište.
  6. Priključi dodatno napajanje (6+2-pin ili 16-pin) ako ga kartica ima.
  7. Kabl monitora priključi u karticu, ne u ploču.
  8. Uključi računar i instaliraj najnoviji drajver proizvođača (NVIDIA, AMD ili Intel).
  1. Switch the computer off, pull out the cable and touch the case.
  2. Remove one or two metal slot covers at the back of the case (as wide as the card).
  3. Open the latch at the end of the top PCIe x16 slot (the one closest to the processor, as it has the most lanes).
  4. Lower the card straight into the slot and press until the latch clicks.
  5. Fix the card bracket to the case with screws.
  6. Connect the extra power (6+2-pin or 16-pin) if the card has it.
  7. Plug the monitor cable into the card, not into the board.
  8. Switch the computer on and install the latest driver from the maker (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel).

Teške karticeHeavy cards

Velike kartice teže i preko 1,5 kg i vremenom savijaju slot. Zato se uz njih isporučuje nosač (podupirač) koji drži kraj kartice. Pre kupovine proveri i dužinu kartice: mora da stane u kućište.

Large cards weigh over 1.5 kg and bend the slot over time. That is why they come with a support bracket that holds the end of the card. Before buying, also check the card length: it must fit in the case.

Deo 7Part 7 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · broj pikselaExample 1 · pixel count

Dato: monitor ima rezoluciju 1920 × 1080. Koliko piksela kartica mora da izračuna za jednu sliku, a koliko u sekundi pri 60 slika u sekundi (60 FPS)?

Given: a monitor has a resolution of 1920 × 1080. How many pixels must the card work out for one picture, and how many per second at 60 frames per second (60 FPS)?

  1. Jedna slika: 1920 · 1080 = 2 073 600 piksela.
  2. U sekundi: 2 073 600 · 60 ≈ 124 miliona piksela.
  1. One picture: 1920 · 1080 = 2,073,600 pixels.
  2. Per second: 2,073,600 · 60 ≈ 124 million pixels.

≈ 2,1 milion po slici, ≈ 124 miliona u sekundi≈ 2.1 million per picture, ≈ 124 million per second

Primer 2 · frejm baferExample 2 · frame buffer

Dato: koliko memorije zauzima jedna slika u 4K (3840 × 2160) ako piksel ima 4 bajta?

Given: how much memory does one picture in 4K (3840 × 2160) take if a pixel has 4 bytes?

  1. Broj piksela: 3840 · 2160 = 8 294 400.
  2. Bajtova: 8 294 400 · 4 = 33 177 600 B.
  3. To je oko 33 MB (milion bajtova).
  1. Pixel count: 3840 · 2160 = 8,294,400.
  2. Bytes: 8,294,400 · 4 = 33,177,600 B.
  3. That is about 33 MB (million bytes).

≈ 33 MB≈ 33 MB

Primer 3 · nema slikeExample 3 · no picture

Dato: ugradio si novu karticu. Računar radi, ventilatori se vrte, a monitor piše „No signal”.

Given: you installed a new card. The computer runs and the fans spin, but the monitor says "No signal".

  1. Proveri gde je kabl monitora: verovatno je ostao u izlazu na ploči.
  2. Prebaci ga u izlaz na kartici.
  3. Ako i dalje nema slike, proveri dodatno napajanje kartice i da li je bravica slota kliknula.
  1. Check where the monitor cable is: it is probably still in the output on the board.
  2. Move it to an output on the card.
  3. If there is still no picture, check the card's extra power and that the slot latch clicked.

kabl u karticu, pa napajanje i slotcable into the card, then power and slot

Primer 4 · izbor za učionicuExample 4 · choice for a classroom

Dato: škola kupuje 20 računara za rad u Wordu, Excelu i pretraživaču. Da li treba kupiti posebne grafičke kartice?

Given: a school is buying 20 computers for Word, Excel and a web browser. Should it buy dedicated graphics cards?

  1. Ti programi ne traže 3D grafiku.
  2. Integrisana grafika u procesoru je dovoljna, troši manje i nema dodatnu cenu.
  3. Novac je pametnije uložiti u SSD i dovoljno RAM-a.
  1. Those programs do not need 3D graphics.
  2. The integrated graphics in the processor is enough, uses less power and costs nothing extra.
  3. The money is better spent on an SSD and enough RAM.

ne, dovoljna je integrisana grafikano, integrated graphics is enough

Deo 8Part 8 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Kabl monitora u ploči. Kada postoji posebna kartica, izlazi na ploči obično ne rade. Monitor uvek u karticu.
  • Zaboravljeno dodatno napajanje. Kartica bez 8-pinskog ili 16-pinskog kabla ne daje sliku ili javlja grešku.
  • Pogrešan slot. Donji x16 slotovi često imaju samo 4 trake. Kartica radi, ali sporije.
  • Stari drajver. Bez pravog drajvera kartica radi u osnovnom režimu: niska rezolucija i bez 3D ubrzanja.
  • Kartica ne staje. Pre kupovine izmeri slobodno mesto u kućištu i uporedi ga sa dužinom kartice.
  • The monitor cable in the board. When there is a dedicated card, the outputs on the board usually do not work. The monitor always goes into the card.
  • Forgotten extra power. A card without its 8-pin or 16-pin cable gives no picture or reports an error.
  • The wrong slot. The lower x16 slots often have only 4 lanes. The card works, but slower.
  • An old driver. Without the right driver the card runs in basic mode: low resolution and no 3D acceleration.
  • The card does not fit. Before buying, measure the free room in the case and compare it with the length of the card.
