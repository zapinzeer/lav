Deo 1Part 1 · R i L na redR and L in series

Realni kalem kao RL koloA real coil as an RL circuit

Idealan kalem, kao što smo videli u lekciji o kalemu, ne troši snagu: energiju samo prima u magnetno polje i vraća izvoru. Pravi kalem je namotan od bakarne žice koja ima otpornost, pa se uvek malo i greje. Zato ga crtamo kao rednu vezu otpornika R i idealnog kalema L. Isti model važi i za mnoge potrošače u kući: motor, transformator i prigušnica fluorescentne cevi su „otpornik i kalem” istovremeno.

An ideal coil, as we saw in the lesson on the coil, consumes no power: it only takes energy into its magnetic field and returns it to the source. A real coil is wound from copper wire that has resistance, so it always heats up a little. That is why we draw it as a series connection of a resistor R and an ideal coil L. The same model fits many loads in the home too: a motor, a transformer and the choke of a fluorescent tube are a "resistor and a coil" at the same time.

Kroz oba elementa teče ista struja I. Napon na otporniku \( U_R = RI \) je u fazi sa strujom, a napon na kalemu \( U_L = X_L I \) prednjači struji za 90°. Ukupni napon je njihov fazorski (vektorski) zbir, ne običan zbir:

The same current I flows through both elements. The voltage across the resistor \( U_R = RI \) is in phase with the current, and the voltage across the coil \( U_L = X_L I \) leads the current by 90°. The total voltage is their phasor (vector) sum, not an ordinary sum:

\[ U = \sqrt{U_R^2 + U_L^2} \qquad Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} \qquad \operatorname{tg}\varphi = \frac{X_L}{R} \qquad I = \frac{U}{Z} \]

The phase shift is now between 0° and 90°: the "cleaner" the coil (less R compared to XL), the closer φ is to 90°. If the resistance dominates, φ is close to zero.

Deo 2Part 2 · p = u · ip = u · i

Trenutna snaga u RL koluInstantaneous power in an RL circuit

Neka je napon \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \), a struja kasni za uglom φ: \( i = I_m\sin(\omega t - \varphi) \). Trenutna snaga je proizvod trenutnog napona i trenutne struje. Posle malo trigonometrije (proizvod dva sinusa) dobijamo:

Let the voltage be \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \), and let the current lag by the angle φ: \( i = I_m\sin(\omega t - \varphi) \). The instantaneous power is the product of the instantaneous voltage and the instantaneous current. After a little trigonometry (the product of two sines) we get:

\[ p = u\,i = UI\cos\varphi - UI\cos(2\omega t - \varphi) \]

Prvi sabirak je stalan, a drugi je sinusoida dvostruke učestanosti čija je srednja vrednost nula. Zato je srednja snaga jednaka baš prvom sabirku, \( UI\cos\varphi \). Trenutna snaga je veći deo periode pozitivna (izvor daje energiju), a kratko negativna (kalem vraća deo energije iz magnetnog polja).

The first term is constant, and the second is a sine of double frequency whose average is zero. That is why the average power equals exactly the first term, \( UI\cos\varphi \). The instantaneous power is positive for most of the period (the source gives energy), and briefly negative (the coil returns part of the energy from its magnetic field).

Pomisli prvo: kada trenutna snaga postaje negativna?Think first: when does the instantaneous power become negative?

Kada napon i struja imaju suprotne znakove, to jest u intervalu dužine φ posle svakog prolaska napona kroz nulu. Za φ = 0 (čist otpornik) tog intervala nema, a za φ = 90° (idealni kalem) on traje pola vremena.

When the voltage and the current have opposite signs, that is in an interval of length φ after every zero crossing of the voltage. For φ = 0 (a pure resistor) there is no such interval, and for φ = 90° (an ideal coil) it lasts half the time.

Dva granična slučajaTwo limiting cases

Za φ = 0 je \( p = UI(1 - \cos 2\omega t) \ge 0 \) i srednja snaga je UI. Za φ = 90° je \( p = UI\sin 2\omega t \), a srednja snaga je nula. Realni kalem je uvek negde između.

For φ = 0, \( p = UI(1 - \cos 2\omega t) \ge 0 \) and the average power is UI. For φ = 90°, \( p = UI\sin 2\omega t \) and the average power is zero. A real coil is always somewhere in between.

Deo 3Part 3 · P, Q, SP, Q, S

Aktivna, reaktivna i prividna snagaActive, reactive and apparent power

U kolu naizmenične struje razlikujemo tri snage. Sve tri dobijamo iz trougla napona tako što svaku stranicu pomnožimo strujom I. Tako nastaje trougao snaga, sličan trouglu napona i trouglu impedanse, sa istim uglom φ.

In an AC circuit we distinguish three powers. We get all three from the voltage triangle by multiplying every side by the current I. That gives the power triangle, similar to the voltage triangle and the impedance triangle, with the same angle φ.

\[ P = UI\cos\varphi = RI^2 \qquad Q_L = UI\sin\varphi = X_L I^2 \qquad S = UI = ZI^2 \qquad S^2 = P^2 + Q_L^2 \]

Zašto tri različite jedinice?Why three different units?

Fizički su sve tri „volt puta amper”. Različita imena (W, var, VA) su dogovor da odmah vidimo o kojoj snazi je reč. Na pločici transformatora piše na primer 1 kVA, a na grejalici 2 kW.

Physically all three are "volts times amperes". The different names (W, var, VA) are a convention so we can see at once which power we mean. A transformer plate says for example 1 kVA, and a heater says 2 kW.

Znak reaktivne snageThe sign of reactive power

Po dogovoru, kalem uzima reaktivnu snagu, pa je QL pozitivna. U sledećoj lekciji videćemo da kondenzator reaktivnu snagu daje, pa je njegova Q negativna. Zato se kalem i kondenzator mogu međusobno „poništiti”.

By convention a coil draws reactive power, so QL is positive. In the next lesson we will see that a capacitor supplies reactive power, so its Q is negative. That is why a coil and a capacitor can "cancel" each other.

Deo 4Part 4 · cos φcos φ

Faktor snageThe power factor

Odnos aktivne i prividne snage zove se faktor snage. U kolu sa sinusnim naponom i strujom on je jednak kosinusu faznog stava:

The ratio of the active and the apparent power is called the power factor. In a circuit with a sinusoidal voltage and current it equals the cosine of the phase shift:

\[ \cos\varphi = \frac{P}{S} = \frac{R}{Z} = \frac{U_R}{U} \]

Faktor snage pokazuje koliki deo struje koja teče kroz vodove zaista „radi”. Ako motor treba da da 1 kW pri 230 V, uz cos φ = 1 mu treba 4,35 A, a uz cos φ = 0,5 čak 8,7 A. Dvostruka struja znači četiri puta veće gubitke u žicama (\( P_g = R_vI^2 \)), deblje provodnike i jači transformator. Zato elektrodistribucija naplaćuje industriji višak reaktivne energije, a veliki potrošači popravljaju cos φ kondenzatorima.

The power factor shows how much of the current flowing through the lines really "works". If a motor has to deliver 1 kW at 230 V, with cos φ = 1 it needs 4.35 A, and with cos φ = 0.5 as much as 8.7 A. A doubled current means four times larger losses in the wires (\( P_g = R_wI^2 \)), thicker conductors and a stronger transformer. That is why the power company charges industry for excess reactive energy, and big consumers improve cos φ with capacitors.

cos φ = 1

Čist otpornik: grejalica, sijalica sa vlaknom. Sva snaga je aktivna.

A pure resistor: a heater, a filament bulb. All the power is active.

cos φ ≈ 0,7–0,9

Asinhroni motor pod opterećenjem, transformator. Kolo je induktivno.

An induction motor under load, a transformer. The circuit is inductive.

cos φ ≈ 0,1–0,3

Motor u praznom hodu, prigušnica: skoro čista reaktivna snaga.

A motor running idle, a choke: almost purely reactive power.

Merenje u laboratorijiMeasuring in the lab

Ampermetar i voltmetar daju U i I, pa i prividnu snagu S = UI. Vatmetar meri direktno aktivnu snagu P. Faktor snage tada dobijamo kao cos φ = P/(UI), a reaktivnu snagu kao \( Q = \sqrt{S^2 - P^2} \).

An ammeter and a voltmeter give U and I, and so the apparent power S = UI. A wattmeter measures the active power P directly. The power factor is then cos φ = P/(UI), and the reactive power is \( Q = \sqrt{S^2 - P^2} \).

Dato: na pločici jednofaznog motora piše 230 V, 5 A, cos φ = 0,8. Kolike su P, Q i S?

  2. \( P = S\cos\varphi = 1150\cdot 0.8 = 920\ \mathrm{W} \).
  3. \( \sin\varphi = \sqrt{1 - 0.8^2} = 0.6 \), so \( Q = 1150\cdot 0.6 = 690\ \mathrm{var} \).

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Sabiranje napona kao brojeva. UR i UL su pomereni za 90°, pa je \( U = \sqrt{U_R^2 + U_L^2} \), a ne UR + UL.
  • P = UI. To je prividna snaga. Aktivna je P = UI cos φ.
  • Sabiranje P i Q. S nije P + Q, već \( \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2} \).
  • I²R sa pogrešnom otpornošću. Aktivna snaga je RI², a ne ZI². Toplota nastaje samo na otporniku.
  • Mešanje jedinica. P je u W, Q u var, S u VA. Odgovor „160 W reaktivne snage” nije tačan.
  • Adding voltages as numbers. UR and UL are 90° apart, so \( U = \sqrt{U_R^2 + U_L^2} \), not UR + UL.
  • P = UI. That is the apparent power. The active power is P = UI cos φ.
  • Adding P and Q. S is not P + Q but \( \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2} \).
  • I²R with the wrong resistance. The active power is RI², not ZI². Heat appears only in the resistor.
  • Mixing units. P is in W, Q in var, S in VA. The answer "160 W of reactive power" is not correct.

SažetakSummary

  • Realni kalem je R i L na red: \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} \), \( \operatorname{tg}\varphi = X_L/R \), struja kasni za uglom 0° < φ < 90°.
  • Trenutna snaga \( p = UI\cos\varphi - UI\cos(2\omega t - \varphi) \) je kratko negativna dok kalem vraća energiju.
  • \( P = UI\cos\varphi = RI^2 \) [W], \( Q_L = UI\sin\varphi = X_LI^2 \) [var], \( S = UI \) [VA], \( S^2 = P^2 + Q^2 \).
  • Faktor snage \( \cos\varphi = P/S \). Mali cos φ znači veću struju i veće gubitke za istu korisnu snagu.
  • A real coil is R and L in series: \( Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} \), \( \tan\varphi = X_L/R \), the current lags by an angle 0° < φ < 90°.
  • The instantaneous power \( p = UI\cos\varphi - UI\cos(2\omega t - \varphi) \) is briefly negative while the coil returns energy.
  • \( P = UI\cos\varphi = RI^2 \) [W], \( Q_L = UI\sin\varphi = X_LI^2 \) [var], \( S = UI \) [VA], \( S^2 = P^2 + Q^2 \).
  • The power factor \( \cos\varphi = P/S \). A small cos φ means a larger current and larger losses for the same useful power.
