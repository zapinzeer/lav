Deo 1Part 1 · pojam i podelaidea and kinds

Šta su izlazne jediniceWhat output devices are

Izlazne jedinice rade obrnuto od ulaznih: digitalne podatke iz računara pretvaraju u nešto što čovek može da vidi, čuje ili opipa. Podaci kroz njih idu od računara ka korisniku.

Output devices do the opposite of input devices: they turn digital data from the computer into something a person can see, hear or touch. Data goes through them from the computer to the user.

Deo 2Part 2 · slikapicture

Monitor i pikseliThe monitor and pixels

Slika na monitoru se sastoji od piksela, sitnih tačaka poređanih u redove i kolone. Svaki piksel ima tri podpiksela: crveni (R), zeleni (G) i plavi (B). Menjanjem jačine svakog od njih dobija se bilo koja boja. To je aditivno mešanje: što više svetla, to je boja svetlija, a sve tri na najjače daju belo.

The picture on a monitor is made of pixels, tiny dots arranged in rows and columns. Every pixel has three subpixels: red (R), green (G) and blue (B). By changing the brightness of each, any colour can be made. This is additive mixing: the more light, the brighter the colour, and all three at full give white.

Svaki podpiksel obično ima 256 nivoa (od 0 do 255), pa je ukupan broj boja 256 · 256 · 256 ≈ 16,7 miliona. Boja se često zapisuje heksadecimalno: #FF0000 je čisto crvena, #FFFFFF bela, a #000000 crna.

Each subpixel usually has 256 levels (from 0 to 255), so the total number of colours is 256 · 256 · 256 ≈ 16.7 million. A colour is often written in hexadecimal: #FF0000 is pure red, #FFFFFF white and #000000 black.

Ista rezolucija na većem ekranu daje veće piksele i manje oštru sliku. Zato se uvodi gustina piksela (PPI): broj piksela po inču. Dijagonala u pikselima računa se Pitagorinom teoremom, a zatim se podeli dijagonalom u inčima. Za monitor na stolu (oko 60 cm od očiju) oko 90–110 PPI izgleda dobro, a telefoni imaju 400 i više, jer se drže bliže očima.

The same resolution on a bigger screen gives bigger pixels and a less sharp picture. That is why we use pixel density (PPI): the number of pixels per inch. The diagonal in pixels comes from Pythagoras' theorem and is then divided by the diagonal in inches. For a desk monitor (about 60 cm from the eyes) about 90–110 PPI looks good, while phones have 400 or more, because they are held closer to the eyes.

Deo 4Part 4 · velika slikabig picture

ProjektorThe projector

Projektor baca uvećanu sliku na platno ili zid. Jaka lampa ili laser osvetljava mali čip (DLP sa milionima sićušnih ogledala ili LCD panel), a sočivo uvećava sliku. Najvažnije osobine su svetlina u lumenima (za učionicu sa svetlom bar 3000 lm), rezolucija i kontrast. U zamračenoj prostoriji slika je mnogo bolja, pa se zato zatvaraju zavese.

A projector throws an enlarged picture onto a screen or a wall. A strong lamp or laser lights up a small chip (DLP with millions of tiny mirrors, or an LCD panel), and a lens enlarges the image. The most important features are brightness in lumens (at least 3000 lm for a lit classroom), resolution and contrast. In a darkened room the picture is much better, which is why the curtains are closed.

Deo 5Part 5 · papirpaper

ŠtampačiPrinters

Štampač prenosi sliku na papir. Na papiru se boje mešaju suptraktivno: svaka boja mastila upija (oduzima) deo svetla. Zato štampači koriste cijan (C), magentu (M) i žutu (Y), a posebno i crnu (K), jer mešavina tri boje daje mutnu tamnobraon umesto čiste crne.

A printer transfers an image to paper. On paper colours mix subtractively: each ink colour absorbs (subtracts) part of the light. That is why printers use cyan (C), magenta (M) and yellow (Y), and also black (K) separately, because a mix of the three gives a muddy dark brown instead of pure black.

A printer is described by its resolution in DPI (600–1200 for text), its speed in pages per minute (ppm) and its cost per page (the price of the toner or ink divided by the number of pages).

Deo 6Part 6 · zvuksound

Zvučnici i slušaliceSpeakers and headphones

Zvuk je u računaru zapisan kao niz brojeva. Zvučna kartica (danas uglavnom ugrađena u ploču) ima DA konvertor (DAC) koji te brojeve pretvara u signal koji pokreće membranu zvučnika. Membrana gura vazduh i mi čujemo zvuk.

Inside the computer, sound is stored as a sequence of numbers. The sound card (today mostly built into the board) has a DA converter (DAC) that turns those numbers into a signal that moves the speaker cone. The cone pushes the air and we hear the sound.

  • 2.0: dva zvučnika, levi i desni (stereo);
  • 2.1: dva zvučnika i subwoofer za duboke tonove;
  • 5.1 i 7.1: prostorni zvuk sa zvučnicima ispred, sa strane i iza slušaoca, i subwooferom (to je „.1”).
  • 2.0: two speakers, left and right (stereo);
  • 2.1: two speakers and a subwoofer for deep tones;
  • 5.1 and 7.1: surround sound with speakers in front, at the sides and behind the listener, plus a subwoofer (that is the ".1").

Slušalice su mali zvučnici blizu uha; mogu biti žičane (3,5 mm ili USB) ili bežične (Bluetooth). Zvučnici za računar obično imaju svoje pojačalo i napajanje (aktivni zvučnici).

Headphones are small speakers close to the ear; they can be wired (3.5 mm or USB) or wireless (Bluetooth). Computer speakers usually have their own amplifier and power (active speakers).

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · gustina pikselaExample 1 · pixel density

Dato: monitor od 24″ ima rezoluciju 1920 × 1080. Kolika je gustina piksela?

Given: a 24″ monitor has a resolution of 1920 × 1080. What is its pixel density?

  1. Dijagonala u pikselima: √(1920² + 1080²) = √(3 686 400 + 1 166 400) = √4 852 800 ≈ 2203.
  2. Podelimo dijagonalom u inčima: 2203 / 24 ≈ 92.
  1. Diagonal in pixels: √(1920² + 1080²) = √(3,686,400 + 1,166,400) = √4,852,800 ≈ 2203.
  2. Divide by the diagonal in inches: 2203 / 24 ≈ 92.

Primer 2 · broj bojaExample 2 · number of colours

Dato: koliko boja može da prikaže piksel ako svaki podpiksel ima 256 nivoa?

Given: how many colours can a pixel show if each subpixel has 256 levels?

  1. Tri podpiksela, svaki sa 256 mogućnosti.
  2. Broj kombinacija: 256 · 256 · 256 = 16 777 216.
  1. Three subpixels, each with 256 options.
  2. Number of combinations: 256 · 256 · 256 = 16,777,216.

≈ 16,7 miliona boja≈ 16.7 million colours

Primer 3 · brzina štampeExample 3 · printing speed

Dato: laserski štampač štampa 24 strane u minuti. Koliko traje štampa testa od 6 strana za 30 učenika?

Given: a laser printer prints 24 pages per minute. How long does it take to print a 6-page test for 30 students?

  1. Ukupno strana: 6 · 30 = 180.
  2. Vreme: 180 / 24 = 7,5 minuta (plus nekoliko sekundi zagrevanja).
  1. Total pages: 6 · 30 = 180.
  2. Time: 180 / 24 = 7.5 minutes (plus a few seconds of warm-up).

Primer 4 · izbor štampačaExample 4 · choosing a printer

Dato: sekretarijat škole štampa 2000 crno-belih strana mesečno, a retko nešto u boji. Koji štampač da izabere?

Given: a school office prints 2000 black-and-white pages a month, and rarely anything in colour. Which printer should it choose?

  1. Mnogo crno-belih strana traži malu cenu po strani i brzinu.
  2. Laserski štampač ima jeftin toner po strani i ne suši mu se mastilo kada stoji.
  3. Za retku štampu u boji dovoljan je jedan jeftin inkdžet.
  1. Many black-and-white pages call for a low cost per page and speed.
  2. A laser printer has cheap toner per page and has no ink to dry out when idle.
  3. For rare colour printing one cheap inkjet is enough.

Deo 8Part 8 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Pogrešna rezolucija u sistemu. LCD izgleda oštro samo u svojoj izvornoj (native) rezoluciji. U drugoj slova postaju mutna.
  • Monitor od 144 Hz radi na 60 Hz. Osvežavanje se mora uključiti u podešavanjima ekrana, a kabl mora da ga podrži.
  • Mešanje RGB i CMYK. Slika pripremljena za ekran (RGB) na papiru izgleda tamnije i bleđe. Za štampu se priprema u CMYK.
  • Inkdžet koji dugo stoji. Mastilo se osuši u glavi. Štampaj bar jednu stranu nedeljno ili koristi laserski štampač.
  • The wrong resolution in the system. An LCD looks sharp only at its native resolution. At any other, text becomes blurry.
  • A 144 Hz monitor running at 60 Hz. The refresh rate must be switched on in the display settings, and the cable must support it.
  • Mixing up RGB and CMYK. A picture made for a screen (RGB) looks darker and duller on paper. For print it is prepared in CMYK.
  • An inkjet that sits unused. The ink dries in the head. Print at least one page a week or use a laser printer.

SažetakSummary

  • Izlazne jedinice pretvaraju podatke iz računara u sliku, štampu, zvuk ili dodir.
  • Piksel ima tri podpiksela (RGB); 256 nivoa po boji daje 16,7 miliona boja.
  • Monitor opisuju rezolucija, dijagonala, odnos stranica, osvežavanje, odziv i gustina (PPI).
  • LCD (TN, IPS, VA) koristi pozadinsko svetlo; OLED pikseli sami svetle.
  • Štampači mešaju boje suptraktivno (CMYK): laserski, inkdžet, matrični, termalni, 3D.
  • Zvučna kartica pretvara brojeve u zvuk; sistemi 2.0, 2.1, 5.1, 7.1.
  • Output devices turn data from the computer into pictures, print, sound or touch.
  • A pixel has three subpixels (RGB); 256 levels per colour give 16.7 million colours.
  • A monitor is described by resolution, diagonal, aspect ratio, refresh rate, response time and density (PPI).
  • LCD (TN, IPS, VA) uses a backlight; OLED pixels light themselves.
  • Printers mix colours subtractively (CMYK): laser, inkjet, dot-matrix, thermal, 3D.
  • The sound card turns numbers into sound; 2.0, 2.1, 5.1 and 7.1 systems.
