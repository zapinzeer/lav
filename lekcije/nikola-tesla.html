Život Nikole TesleThe life of Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla je rođen 10. jula 1856. u Smiljanu, u Lici (tada Vojna krajina u Austrijskom carstvu, danas Hrvatska), u porodici srpskog pravoslavnog sveštenika Milutina i majke Đuke. Majka je, kako je sam pisao, izrađivala korisne kućne sprave, i od nje je nasledio dar za pronalaženje. Studirao je tehniku u Gracu, a zatim slušao predavanja u Pragu.

Nikola Tesla was born on 10 July 1856 in Smiljan, in Lika (then the Military Frontier of the Austrian Empire, today Croatia), into the family of the Serbian Orthodox priest Milutin and his mother Đuka. His mother, as he wrote himself, made useful household devices, and he inherited his gift for invention from her. He studied engineering in Graz and then attended lectures in Prague.

Radio je u telefonskoj centrali u Budimpešti, pa u Edisonovoj kompaniji u Parizu, a 1884. je stigao u Njujork. Tamo je proveo ostatak života. Umro je 7. januara 1943. u hotelu „Njujorker”. Njegova zaostavština, urna i lične stvari danas su u Muzeju Nikole Tesle u Beogradu.

He worked at the telephone exchange in Budapest, then at Edison's company in Paris, and in 1884 he arrived in New York. He spent the rest of his life there. He died on 7 January 1943 in the Hotel New Yorker. His legacy, his urn and his personal belongings are today in the Nikola Tesla Museum in Belgrade.

Deo 2Part 2 · Budimpešta, 1882.Budapest, 1882

Obrtno magnetno poljeThe rotating magnetic field

Tadašnji motori su radili na jednosmernu struju i imali su komutator sa četkicama, koje varniče i troše se (vidi lekciju o elektromotoru). Tesla je hteo motor bez četkica, na naizmeničnu struju. Rešenje mu je, prema sopstvenom sećanju, sinulo 1882. tokom šetnje parkom u Budimpešti: ne treba okretati provodnike u polju, već okretati samo polje.

The motors of that time ran on direct current and had a commutator with brushes, which spark and wear out (see the lesson on the electric motor). Tesla wanted a brushless motor running on alternating current. By his own account, the solution came to him in 1882 during a walk in a park in Budapest: do not turn the conductors in the field, turn only the field.

Ako tri kalema postavimo pod uglom od 120° i kroz njih pustimo tri naizmenične struje koje kasne jedna za drugom za trećinu periode, njihova polja se sabiraju u jedno polje stalne jačine koje se obrće:

If we place three coils at 120° to each other and pass through them three alternating currents, each lagging the previous one by a third of a period, their fields add up to one field of constant strength that rotates:

\[ i_1 = I_m\cos\omega t \qquad i_2 = I_m\cos(\omega t - 120^\circ) \qquad i_3 = I_m\cos(\omega t - 240^\circ) \qquad |\vec B| = \tfrac{3}{2}B_m \]

Ništa se mehanički ne kreće, a polje se ipak okreće. Rotor u takvom polju (dovoljan je i običan metalni valjak) počinje da se okreće za njim, jer se u njemu indukuju struje.

Nothing moves mechanically, and yet the field turns. A rotor in such a field (even a plain metal cylinder will do) starts to turn after it, because currents are induced in it.

Asinhroni (indukcioni) motorThe asynchronous (induction) motor

Rotor uvek malo zaostaje za poljem. Da ga stigne, ne bi bilo relativnog kretanja, ne bi se indukovala struja i ne bi bilo sile. Zato se ovaj motor zove asinhroni. Jednostavan je, jeftin i skoro se ne kvari, pa i danas pokreće pumpe, ventilatore, liftove i fabričke mašine. Većina električne energije koju troše motori u svetu prolazi kroz potomke Teslinog motora.

The rotor always lags slightly behind the field. If it caught up, there would be no relative motion, no induced current and no force. That is why this motor is called asynchronous. It is simple, cheap and hardly ever breaks down, so it still drives pumps, fans, lifts and factory machines today. Most of the electrical energy used by motors in the world passes through descendants of Tesla's motor.

Deo 3Part 3 · jednosmerna protiv naizmeničnedirect against alternating

Višefazni sistem i rat strujaThe polyphase system and the war of the currents

Tesla nije izmislio naizmeničnu struju, ali je osmislio ceo višefazni sistem: generatore, transformatore, prenos i motore koji rade zajedno. Patente za taj sistem prijavio je 1887. i 1888, a 1888. održao je čuveno predavanje pred Američkim institutom elektroinženjera. Industrijalac Džordž Vestinghaus je otkupio patente i zaposlio ga.

Tesla did not invent alternating current, but he designed the whole polyphase system: generators, transformers, transmission and motors working together. He filed the patents for that system in 1887 and 1888, and in 1888 he gave a famous lecture to the American Institute of Electrical Engineers. The industrialist George Westinghouse bought the patents and hired him.

Edison je gradio mreže jednosmerne struje i žestoko se protivio naizmeničnoj, tvrdeći da je opasna. Taj sukob se zove rat struja. Presudila je fizika prenosa: gubici u vodovima zavise od kvadrata struje. Naizmenični napon se transformatorom lako podiže, pa se ista snaga prenosi pri visokom naponu i maloj struji, sa malim gubicima. Jednosmerni napon se tada nije mogao tako jednostavno menjati, pa su Edisonove centrale morale da budu na nekoliko kilometara od potrošača.

Edison built direct current networks and fiercely opposed alternating current, claiming it was dangerous. That conflict is called the war of the currents. The physics of transmission decided it: the losses in the lines depend on the square of the current. An alternating voltage is easily raised with a transformer, so the same power is transmitted at a high voltage and a small current, with small losses. At that time a direct voltage could not be changed so simply, so Edison's power stations had to be within a few kilometres of the consumers.

For the same power, a voltage 10 times higher means a current 10 times smaller and 100 times smaller losses. The victory was confirmed in 1893, when Westinghouse lit the World's Fair in Chicago, and in 1895–1896, when a hydroelectric plant based on Tesla's system started working at Niagara Falls and power was carried to the city of Buffalo, about 40 km away.

Deo 4Part 4 · rezonancija i visoke učestanostiresonance and high frequencies

Teslin transformator i bežični prenosThe Tesla coil and wireless transmission

Teslin transformator (Teslin kalem, patent iz 1891) je rezonantni transformator bez gvozdenog jezgra. Primarno kolo je LC kolo koje osciluje na visokoj učestanosti, a sekundar sa mnogo zavojaka je podešen na istu učestanost. Zahvaljujući rezonanciji energija se postepeno „prepumpava” u sekundar i na njemu nastaju naponi od stotina hiljada volti, uz duge varnice.

The Tesla coil (Tesla transformer, patented in 1891) is a resonant transformer without an iron core. The primary circuit is an LC circuit oscillating at a high frequency, and the secondary with many turns is tuned to the same frequency. Thanks to resonance the energy is gradually "pumped" into the secondary, where voltages of hundreds of thousands of volts appear, with long sparks.

Tesla je ovim uređajem palio sijalice i cevi sa gasom bez žica, pokazujući da polje visoke učestanosti može da prenese energiju kroz vazduh. U Kolorado Springsu je 1899. pravio veštačke munje duge desetine metara. U kuli Vordenklif na Long Ajlendu hteo je da prenosi poruke i energiju bežično preko cele planete, ali mu je ponestalo novca: kula nikada nije proradila i srušena je 1917.

With this device Tesla lit lamps and gas tubes without wires, showing that a high-frequency field can carry energy through the air. In Colorado Springs in 1899 he made artificial lightning tens of metres long. In the Wardenclyffe tower on Long Island he wanted to send messages and energy wirelessly across the whole planet, but he ran out of money: the tower never worked and was demolished in 1917.

NasleđeLegacy

Jedinica SI sistema za magnetnu indukciju nosi ime tesla (1 T = 1 Wb/m²), od 1960. Muzej Nikole Tesle u Beogradu osnovan je 1952. i čuva njegovu zaostavštinu. Njegovo ime nose beogradski aerodrom, Elektrotehnički institut „Nikola Tesla”, a lik mu je na novčanici od 100 dinara.

The SI unit of magnetic induction has been named the tesla (1 T = 1 Wb/m²) since 1960. The Nikola Tesla Museum in Belgrade was founded in 1952 and keeps his legacy. Belgrade airport and the Electrical Engineering Institute "Nikola Tesla" bear his name, and his portrait is on the 100 dinar banknote.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Činjenice i mitoviFacts and myths

  • „Tesla je izmislio naizmeničnu struju.” Ne. Naizmenične struje i transformatori postojali su i pre njega. Njegov doprinos je obrtno magnetno polje, višefazni sistem i asinhroni motor.
  • „Tesla je sam izmislio radio.” Radio je delo više ljudi (Maksvel, Herc, Tesla, Lodž, Markoni i drugi). Tesla je dao važne osnove: podešena rezonantna kola.
  • „Vordenklif je davao besplatnu struju.” Kula nikada nije proradila. Bežični prenos energije na velike daljine nije ostvaren ni do danas.
  • „Naizmenična struja je pobedila jer je bezbednija.” Obe su opasne. Pobedila je zato što se transformatorom napon lako menja, pa je prenos na daljinu jeftin.
  • "Tesla invented alternating current." No. Alternating currents and transformers existed before him. His contribution is the rotating magnetic field, the polyphase system and the induction motor.
  • "Tesla invented radio alone." Radio was the work of several people (Maxwell, Hertz, Tesla, Lodge, Marconi and others). Tesla laid important foundations: tuned resonant circuits.
  • "Wardenclyffe gave free electricity." The tower never worked. Wireless transmission of power over long distances has not been achieved even today.
  • "Alternating current won because it is safer." Both are dangerous. It won because a transformer changes the voltage easily, so long-distance transmission is cheap.

SažetakSummary

  • Nikola Tesla (1856, Smiljan – 1943, Njujork), srpski i američki pronalazač, od 1884. radio u SAD.
  • Tri struje pomerene za 120° u tri kalema pod 120° stvaraju obrtno magnetno polje stalne jačine \( \tfrac{3}{2}B_m \); na tome radi asinhroni motor.
  • Višefazni sistem sa transformatorima pobedio je u ratu struja jer visok napon znači malu struju i male gubitke \( R_vI^2 \).
  • Teslin transformator koristi rezonanciju \( f_0 = 1/(2\pi\sqrt{LC}) \); Tesla je dao važne doprinose radiju i daljinskom upravljanju, a jedinica T nosi njegovo ime.
  • Nikola Tesla (1856, Smiljan – 1943, New York), a Serbian-American inventor, worked in the USA from 1884.
  • Three currents shifted by 120° in three coils at 120° create a rotating magnetic field of constant strength \( \tfrac{3}{2}B_m \); the induction motor works on this.
  • The polyphase system with transformers won the war of the currents because a high voltage means a small current and small losses \( R_vI^2 \).
  • The Tesla coil uses resonance \( f_0 = 1/(2\pi\sqrt{LC}) \); Tesla made important contributions to radio and remote control, and the unit T bears his name.
