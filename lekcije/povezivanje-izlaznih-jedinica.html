Deo 1Part 1 · slikapicture

Video priključciVideo connectors

Monitor i projektor se povezuju na izlaz grafičke kartice (ili matične ploče, ako računar koristi integrisanu grafiku). Kroz godine su se priključci menjali, a danas se najviše koriste HDMI i DisplayPort.

A monitor and a projector connect to an output of the graphics card (or of the motherboard, if the computer uses integrated graphics). The connectors have changed over the years, and today HDMI and DisplayPort are used the most.

HDMI je standard za televizore, konzole i projektore, a podržava i CEC (jednim daljinskim upravljaš sa više uređaja). DisplayPort je čest na monitorima za računare i podržava povezivanje u lanac (MST): jedan izlaz, više monitora. Preko USB-C porta laptop može da šalje sliku (DisplayPort Alt Mode ili Thunderbolt), a često i da se puni istim kablom.

HDMI is the standard for TVs, consoles and projectors, and it also supports CEC (one remote controls several devices). DisplayPort is common on computer monitors and supports daisy-chaining (MST): one output, several monitors. Through a USB-C port a laptop can send a picture (DisplayPort Alt Mode or Thunderbolt), and often charge over the same cable.

Deo 2Part 2 · koliko podatakahow much data

Propusni opseg: zašto kabl nije svejednoBandwidth: why the cable matters

Kabl mora da prenese sve piksele svake slike, svaki put kad se slika osveži. Količina podataka u sekundi zavisi od tri stvari: rezolucije, osvežavanja i dubine boje (broja bitova po pikselu, obično 24, a za HDR 30). Kada je potrebno više podataka nego što priključak može da prenese, slika ne radi u toj kombinaciji, pa sistem ponudi nižu rezoluciju ili niže osvežavanje.

The cable has to carry every pixel of every picture, each time the picture refreshes. The amount of data per second depends on three things: resolution, refresh rate and colour depth (bits per pixel, usually 24, or 30 for HDR). When more data is needed than the connector can carry, the picture will not work in that combination, and the system offers a lower resolution or refresh rate.

Pomisli prvo: zašto monitor od 144 Hz preko starog HDMI kabla radi samo na 60 Hz?Think first: why does a 144 Hz monitor on an old HDMI cable run only at 60 Hz?

Za 144 Hz kabl mora da prenese 2,4 puta više slika u sekundi. Stari HDMI 1.4 nema toliki propusni opseg, pa sistem bira najveće osvežavanje koje kabl može da „progura”. Rešenje je DisplayPort ili HDMI 2.0/2.1 kabl i port.

For 144 Hz the cable has to carry 2.4 times more pictures per second. Old HDMI 1.4 does not have that bandwidth, so the system picks the highest refresh rate the cable can "push through". The fix is a DisplayPort or an HDMI 2.0/2.1 cable and port.

Deo 3Part 3 · u praksiin practice

Povezivanje monitora i projektoraConnecting monitors and projectors

  1. Ugasi ili ne moraš: HDMI, DP i USB-C podržavaju hot plug, ali VGA i DVI je bolje priključivati kad je monitor isključen.
  2. Ako računar ima posebnu grafičku karticu, kabl ide u karticu.
  3. Zavrni zavrtnje (VGA, DVI) ili sačekaj klik (DP).
  4. Na monitoru izaberi pravi ulaz (Input/Source: HDMI 1, DP...).
  5. U sistemu podesi rezoluciju na izvornu i najveće osvežavanje.
  1. Switch off or not: HDMI, DP and USB-C support hot plug, but VGA and DVI are better connected with the monitor off.
  2. If the computer has a dedicated graphics card, the cable goes into the card.
  3. Tighten the screws (VGA, DVI) or wait for the click (DP).
  4. On the monitor choose the right input (Input/Source: HDMI 1, DP...).
  5. In the system set the resolution to native and the highest refresh rate.

Više monitora: priključi svaki u svoj izlaz, pa u Windowsu pritisni Win + P. Možeš da biraš: samo ekran računara, Dupliraj (isto na oba, zgodno za projektor u učionici), Proširi (jedna velika radna površina preko oba) ili samo drugi ekran. U Podešavanja → Sistem → Ekran prevuci ekrane tako da odgovaraju tome kako stoje na stolu.

Several monitors: connect each to its own output, then press Win + P in Windows. You can choose: PC screen only, Duplicate (the same on both, handy for a classroom projector), Extend (one big desktop across both) or second screen only. In Settings → System → Display drag the screens to match how they stand on the desk.

Deo 4Part 4 · štampaprinting

Povezivanje štampačaConnecting a printer

Štampač se može povezati na tri načina:

A printer can be connected in three ways:

  • USB (USB-B na štampaču, USB-A na računaru): najjednostavnije, za jedan računar;
  • mreža: kablom (Ethernet, RJ-45) ili preko Wi-Fi-ja; štampač dobije svoju IP adresu i svi računari u mreži mogu da štampaju na njemu;
  • deljenje preko računara: štampač je USB-om vezan za jedan računar, koji ga „deli” ostalima (taj računar mora biti upaljen).
  • USB (USB-B on the printer, USB-A on the computer): the simplest, for one computer;
  • network: by cable (Ethernet, RJ-45) or over Wi-Fi; the printer gets its own IP address and every computer on the network can print to it;
  • sharing through a computer: the printer is connected by USB to one computer, which "shares" it with the others (that computer must be on).

Posle povezivanja instalira se drajver štampača. Windows mnoge štampače prepozna sam, a za ostale se drajver preuzima sa sajta proizvođača. Dokumenti za štampu čekaju u redu za štampu (print queue), koji vodi servis Print Spooler. Kad se štampa „zaglavi”, otvori red, otkaži zaglavljeni dokument i, ako treba, ponovo pokreni taj servis.

After connecting, the printer driver is installed. Windows recognises many printers by itself, and for the rest the driver is downloaded from the maker's site. Documents for printing wait in the print queue, which is run by the Print Spooler service. When printing gets "stuck", open the queue, cancel the stuck document and, if needed, restart that service.

Stari štampači koristili su široki paralelni (LPT) port sa 25 pinova, a danas ga skoro nijedan računar nema.

Old printers used the wide 25-pin parallel (LPT) port, which almost no computer has today.

Deo 6Part 6 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · količina podatakaExample 1 · amount of data

Dato: koliko bitova u sekundi treba za sliku 1920 × 1080, 60 Hz, 24 bita po pikselu (bez dodataka)?

Given: how many bits per second are needed for a 1920 × 1080, 60 Hz, 24 bits per pixel picture (without extras)?

  1. Piksela po slici: 1920 · 1080 = 2 073 600.
  2. Bitova po slici: 2 073 600 · 24 = 49 766 400.
  3. U sekundi: 49 766 400 · 60 ≈ 2,99 milijardi bita, odnosno oko 3 Gb/s.
  1. Pixels per picture: 1920 · 1080 = 2,073,600.
  2. Bits per picture: 2,073,600 · 24 = 49,766,400.
  3. Per second: 49,766,400 · 60 ≈ 2.99 billion bits, about 3 Gb/s.

Primer 2 · projektor sa VGAExample 2 · a VGA projector

Dato: stari projektor u učionici ima samo VGA ulaz, a novi laptop ima samo HDMI. Šta treba?

Given: an old classroom projector has only a VGA input, and a new laptop has only HDMI. What is needed?

  1. HDMI je digitalni, a VGA analogni signal.
  2. Pasivni kabl ne može da pretvori jedan u drugi.
  3. Treba aktivni konvertor HDMI → VGA, a zvuk ide posebno, preko 3,5 mm priključka ili zvučnika laptopa.
  1. HDMI is a digital and VGA an analogue signal.
  2. A passive cable cannot turn one into the other.
  3. An active HDMI → VGA converter is needed, and the sound goes separately, through the 3.5 mm jack or the laptop speakers.

aktivni HDMI → VGA konvertoran active HDMI → VGA converter

Primer 3 · projektor u učioniciExample 3 · classroom projector

Dato: nastavnik želi da na projektoru bude isto što i na laptopu. Koji režim bira?

Given: a teacher wants the projector to show the same as the laptop. Which mode do they choose?

  1. Pritisne Win + P.
  2. Izabere Dupliraj.
  3. Ako slika na projektoru nije oštra, rezoluciju podesi na onu koju projektor podržava.
  1. Press Win + P.
  2. Choose Duplicate.
  3. If the projector picture is not sharp, set the resolution to one the projector supports.

Primer 4 · štampač za učionicuExample 4 · printer for a classroom

Dato: u učionici je 15 računara i jedan štampač. Kako da svi štampaju, a da ne mora stalno da radi jedan računar?

Given: a classroom has 15 computers and one printer. How can everyone print without one computer having to stay on all the time?

  1. Deljenje preko računara traži da taj računar bude upaljen.
  2. Štampač se poveže kablom na mrežni svič (ili Wi-Fi) i dobije IP adresu.
  3. Na svakom računaru se doda mrežni štampač i drajver.
  1. Sharing through a computer needs that computer to be on.
  2. The printer is connected by cable to the network switch (or Wi-Fi) and gets an IP address.
  3. The network printer and driver are added on every computer.

Deo 7Part 7 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Pogrešan ulaz na monitoru. Kabl je u HDMI 2, a monitor gleda DP. Taster Input/Source na monitoru.
  • Pasivni adapter za VGA. Digitalni izlaz ne može pasivnim kablom u analogni ulaz. Treba aktivni konvertor.
  • Zvuk ide u monitor. Posle priključivanja HDMI-ja sistem često prebaci zvuk na monitor. Promeni izlazni uređaj.
  • Predugačak ili loš kabl. Za 4K i visoko osvežavanje treba sertifikovan kabl (npr. „Ultra High Speed HDMI”). Loš kabl daje treperenje i „sneg”.
  • The wrong input on the monitor. The cable is in HDMI 2, while the monitor is watching DP. Use the Input/Source button on the monitor.
  • A passive adapter for VGA. A digital output cannot go into an analogue input with a passive cable. An active converter is needed.
  • Sound goes to the monitor. After connecting HDMI the system often switches sound to the monitor. Change the output device.
  • A cable too long or poor. 4K and high refresh rates need a certified cable (e.g. "Ultra High Speed HDMI"). A poor cable gives flicker and "snow".

SažetakSummary

  • Video priključci: VGA (analogni), DVI, HDMI (slika i zvuk), DisplayPort i USB-C.
  • Potreban propusni opseg raste sa rezolucijom, osvežavanjem i dubinom boje.
  • Monitor ide u grafičku karticu; Win + P bira Dupliraj ili Proširi.
  • Digitalno → VGA traži aktivni konvertor; DP → HDMI može pasivno.
  • Štampač: USB, mreža (Ethernet/Wi-Fi) ili deljenje preko računara; red za štampu vodi Print Spooler.
  • Audio: zeleni za zvučnike, roze za mikrofon, S/PDIF i HDMI za digitalni zvuk.
  • Video connectors: VGA (analogue), DVI, HDMI (picture and sound), DisplayPort and USB-C.
  • The bandwidth needed grows with resolution, refresh rate and colour depth.
  • The monitor goes into the graphics card; Win + P picks Duplicate or Extend.
  • Digital → VGA needs an active converter; DP → HDMI can be passive.
  • Printer: USB, network (Ethernet/Wi-Fi) or sharing through a computer; the print queue is run by Print Spooler.
  • Audio: green for speakers, pink for the microphone, S/PDIF and HDMI for digital sound.
