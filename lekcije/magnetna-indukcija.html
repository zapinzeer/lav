Deo 1Part 1 · magnetno poljethe magnetic field

Magnetno polje i linije poljaThe magnetic field and field lines

Oko svakog magneta i oko svakog provodnika kroz koji teče struja postoji magnetno polje. Polje ne možemo da vidimo, ali vidimo šta radi: privlači gvožđe, okreće iglu kompasa, deluje silom na provodnik sa strujom. Koliko je to polje jako i u kom smeru deluje, opisuje vektor magnetne indukcije B.

Every magnet, and every wire carrying a current, is surrounded by a magnetic field. We cannot see the field, but we can see what it does: it attracts iron, turns a compass needle, pushes on a wire with current. How strong the field is, and which way it acts, is described by the magnetic induction vector B (also called magnetic flux density).

Polje prikazujemo linijama polja. Izvan magneta one izlaze iz severnog pola (N) i ulaze u južni pol (S), a unutar magneta zatvaraju se nazad ka N, pa su uvek zatvorene. Vektor B je tangenta na liniju polja, a gde su linije gušće, polje je jače.

We draw the field with field lines. Outside a magnet they leave the north pole (N) and enter the south pole (S), and inside the magnet they close back to N, so they always form closed loops. B is tangent to the field line, and where the lines are packed closer, the field is stronger.

Tri pravila za linije poljaThree rules for field lines

  • Linije polja su zatvorene i nikada se ne seku.
  • Smer linije je smer koji pokazuje severni kraj igle kompasa.
  • Gušće linije znače jače polje.
  • Field lines are closed and never cross.
  • The direction of a line is the direction the north end of a compass needle points.
  • Denser lines mean a stronger field.

Polovi i podela magnetaPoles and cutting a magnet

Istoimeni polovi se odbijaju, raznoimeni se privlače. Ako šipkasti magnet prelomimo na pola, ne dobijamo odvojen severni i odvojen južni pol, nego dva manja magneta, svaki sa svojim N i S. Izolovan magnetni pol nikada nije pronađen.

Like poles repel, unlike poles attract. If we break a bar magnet in half, we do not get a separate north and a separate south pole, but two smaller magnets, each with its own N and S. An isolated magnetic pole has never been found.

Pomisli prvo: kompas u Srbiji pokazuje na sever Zemlje. Šta to znači za magnetni pol Zemlje blizu Arktika?Think first: a compass points to Earth's north. What does that say about the magnetic pole of the Earth near the Arctic?

Severni kraj igle (N) privlači se sa južnim polom magneta. Dakle, blizu geografskog severa nalazi se južni magnetni pol Zemlje. Zato se geografski sever zove i „magnetni sever”, iako je tamo, fizički gledano, pol S.

The north end of the needle (N) is attracted to a magnet's south pole. So near geographic north lies the south magnetic pole of the Earth. It is only called the "magnetic north" because it is in the north, even though physically it is an S pole.

Deo 2Part 2 · oznaka B · jedinica tesla (T)symbol B · unit tesla (T)

Kako merimo B?How do we measure B?

Magnetno polje deluje silom na provodnik sa strujom. Ta sila se zove Amperova sila i zavisi od ugla između provodnika i linija polja:

A magnetic field pushes on a wire that carries a current. This push is called the Ampère force, and it depends on the angle between the wire and the field lines:

Sila je najveća kada je provodnik normalan na polje (α = 90°, sin 90° = 1), a jednaka nuli kada je paralelan sa poljem (sin 0° = 0). Iz najveće sile definišemo magnetnu indukciju:

The force is largest when the wire is perpendicular to the field (α = 90°, sin 90° = 1) and zero when the wire is parallel to it (sin 0° = 0). From the largest force we define magnetic induction:

\[ B = \frac{F}{I\,l} \qquad 1\,\mathrm{T} = 1\,\frac{\mathrm{N}}{\mathrm{A\cdot m}} \]

Polje ima indukciju od 1 tesle ako na provodnik dužine 1 m, kroz koji teče struja od 1 A, deluje silom od 1 N. Tesla je velika jedinica, pa se često koriste mT (mili) i μT (mikro).

A field has an induction of 1 tesla if it pushes on a 1 m wire carrying 1 A with a force of 1 N. The tesla is a large unit, so mT (milli) and μT (micro) are common.

Pravilo leve rukeLeft-hand rule

Postavi levu šaku tako da linije polja ulaze u dlan, a ispruženi prsti pokazuju smer struje. Odvojeni palac tada pokazuje smer sile.

Hold your left hand so the field lines enter your palm and your outstretched fingers point along the current. Your thumb, held out to the side, then shows the direction of the force.

Obrati pažnju: sila nije ni u smeru polja ni u smeru struje. Uvek je normalna na oba.

Notice: the force is neither along the field nor along the current. It is always perpendicular to both.

Magnetna indukcija je vektor: pored jačine ima i smer. Zato se u formuli za silu pojavljuje ugao, a u zadacima često crtamo i strelice. U ovoj lekciji računamo samo jačinu, a smer određujemo pravilom ruke.

Magnetic induction is a vector: besides strength it has a direction. That is why an angle appears in the force formula, and why we often draw arrows in problems. In this lesson we calculate only the magnitude and find the direction with a hand rule.

Deo 4Part 4 · vežbajpractice

Rešeni primeriWorked examples

Primer 1 · sila na provodnikExample 1 · force on a wire

Dato: l = 30 cm, I = 5 A, B = 0,2 T, provodnik je normalan na polje. Traži se sila F.

Given: l = 30 cm, I = 5 A, B = 0.2 T, the wire is perpendicular to the field. Find the force F.

  1. Dužinu pretvaramo u metre: 30 cm = 0,3 m.
  2. Normalan na polje znači α = 90°, pa je sin α = 1.
  3. \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha = 0{,}2\cdot 5\cdot 0{,}3\cdot 1 = 0{,}3\ \mathrm{N} \)
  1. Convert the length to metres: 30 cm = 0.3 m.
  2. Perpendicular to the field means α = 90°, so sin α = 1.
  3. \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha = 0.2\cdot 5\cdot 0.3\cdot 1 = 0.3\ \mathrm{N} \)

F = 0,3 NF = 0.3 N

Primer 2 · indukcija iz sileExample 2 · induction from a force

Dato: na provodnik dužine 20 cm kroz koji teče 3 A deluje sila 0,06 N, normalno na polje. Traži se B.

Given: a force of 0.06 N acts on a 20 cm wire carrying 3 A, perpendicular to the field. Find B.

  1. Pošto je α = 90°, koristimo B = F/(I l).
  2. \( B = \dfrac{F}{I\,l} = \dfrac{0{,}06}{3\cdot 0{,}2} = 0{,}1\ \mathrm{T} \)
  1. Since α = 90°, we use B = F/(I l).
  2. \( B = \dfrac{F}{I\,l} = \dfrac{0.06}{3\cdot 0.2} = 0.1\ \mathrm{T} \)

B = 0,1 T = 100 mTB = 0.1 T = 100 mT

Primer 3 · nagnut provodnikExample 3 · a tilted wire

Isti provodnik kao u primeru 1 (B = 0,2 T, I = 5 A, l = 0,3 m), ali zaklapa ugao od 30° sa linijama polja.

The same wire as in example 1 (B = 0.2 T, I = 5 A, l = 0.3 m), but it makes an angle of 30° with the field lines.

  1. sin 30° = 0,5.
  2. \( F = 0{,}2\cdot 5\cdot 0{,}3\cdot 0{,}5 = 0{,}15\ \mathrm{N} \)
  1. sin 30° = 0.5.
  2. \( F = 0.2\cdot 5\cdot 0.3\cdot 0.5 = 0.15\ \mathrm{N} \)

Deo 5Part 5 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Centimetri umesto metara. U formuli dužina mora biti u metrima. 20 cm je 0,2 m, ne 20.
  • Pogrešan ugao. U formuli za silu α je ugao između provodnika i linija polja, ne između provodnika i normale.
  • Sila duž polja. Sila nikada ne ide duž linija polja. Ona je normalna i na polje i na struju.
  • Linije polja koje „počinju” i „završavaju”. Linije su zatvorene. Izvan magneta idu od N ka S, a unutar magneta od S ka N.
  • Centimetres instead of metres. In the formula the length must be in metres. 20 cm is 0.2 m, not 20.
  • The wrong angle. In the force formula α is the angle between the wire and the field lines, not between the wire and the normal.
  • Force along the field. The force never points along the field lines. It is perpendicular to both the field and the current.
  • Field lines that "start" and "end". Lines are closed. Outside a magnet they run from N to S, and inside the magnet from S to N.

Deo 6Part 6

SažetakSummary

  • Magnetno polje postoji oko magneta i oko provodnika sa strujom. Crtamo ga zatvorenim linijama.
  • Magnetna indukcija B je vektor, a jedinica je tesla (1 T = 1 N/(A·m)).
  • Sila na provodnik: \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha \). Najveća je za α = 90°, a nula za α = 0°.
  • Smer sile određujemo pravilom leve ruke, a sila je normalna i na polje i na struju.
  • A magnetic field exists around magnets and around wires with current. We draw it with closed lines.
  • Magnetic induction B is a vector, and its unit is the tesla (1 T = 1 N/(A·m)).
  • Force on a wire: \( F = B\,I\,l\,\sin\alpha \). It is largest for α = 90° and zero for α = 0°.
  • We find the direction of the force with the left-hand rule, and the force is perpendicular to both field and current.
