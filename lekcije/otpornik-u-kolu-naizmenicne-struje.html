Otpornik i Omov zakonThe resistor and Ohm's law

Na idealan otpornik otpornosti R dovedemo napon \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \). U svakom trenutku važi Omov zakon, baš kao u kolu jednosmerne struje:

We apply a voltage \( u = U_m\sin\omega t \) to an ideal resistor of resistance R. At every moment Ohm's law holds, exactly as in a DC circuit:

\[ i = \frac{u}{R} = \frac{U_m}{R}\sin\omega t = I_m\sin\omega t \qquad I_m = \frac{U_m}{R},\quad I = \frac{U}{R} \]

Struja ima isti oblik (sinus), istu učestanost i istu fazu kao napon. Kažemo da su napon i struja u fazi, fazna razlika je \( \varphi = 0 \). Isto važi i za amplitude i za efektivne vrednosti, jer su obe veličine samo pomnožene istim brojem \( 1/R \).

The current has the same shape (a sine), the same frequency and the same phase as the voltage. We say that voltage and current are in phase, the phase difference is \( \varphi = 0 \). The same holds for amplitudes and for RMS values, because both quantities are simply multiplied by the same number \( 1/R \).

\[ \underline{Z}_R = \frac{\underline{U}}{\underline{I}} = R\angle 0^\circ = R \]

Dve strelice u istom pravcu i smeru. Razlika uglova je 0°, a odnos dužina je R (u odgovarajućim jedinicama).

Two arrows in the same direction. The angle difference is 0°, and the ratio of their lengths is R (in the appropriate units).

otpornost ne zavisi od f

Idealan otpornik se ponaša isto na 50 Hz, na 1 kHz i na jednosmernoj struji. Probaj da menjaš učestanost u modelu: struja se ne menja.

An ideal resistor behaves the same at 50 Hz, at 1 kHz and with direct current. Try changing the frequency in the model: the current does not change.

fazni stav 0°

Kada je napon maksimalan, i struja je maksimalna. Kada je napon nula, i struja je nula.

When the voltage is at its maximum, so is the current. When the voltage is zero, so is the current.

U faziIn phase

Otpornik je jedini element čija je impedansa realan broj. Kalem i kondenzator imaju imaginarni deo, pa će između napona i struje uneti fazni pomak od 90°.

The resistor is the only element whose impedance is a real number. The coil and the capacitor have an imaginary part, so they will introduce a 90° phase shift between voltage and current.

Snaga na otpornikuPower in a resistor

Trenutna snaga je proizvod trenutnog napona i trenutne struje. Pošto su u fazi, imaju uvek isti znak, pa je snaga uvek nenegativna. Otpornik samo troši energiju i pretvara je u toplotu:

The instantaneous power is the product of the instantaneous voltage and the instantaneous current. Since they are in phase, they always have the same sign, so the power is never negative. A resistor only consumes energy and turns it into heat:

\[ p = u\,i = U_mI_m\sin^2\omega t = U I\,(1 - \cos 2\omega t) \]

Snaga „pulsira” dvostrukom učestanošću \( 2\omega \) (na grafiku vidiš dva brega u jednoj periodi napona), između nule i \( U_mI_m = 2UI \). Njena srednja vrednost je tačno polovina maksimuma:

The power "pulses" at twice the frequency \( 2\omega \) (on the graph you see two humps in one voltage period), between zero and \( U_mI_m = 2UI \). Its average value is exactly half of the maximum:

\[ P = U\,I = I^2R = \frac{U^2}{R} = \frac{U_mI_m}{2} \]

Formule za snagu izgledaju isto kao u jednosmernoj struji, ali samo ako se koriste efektivne vrednosti. Ta snaga se zove aktivna snaga i meri u vatima.

The formulas for power look the same as in DC, but only if RMS values are used. This power is called the active power and is measured in watts.

Pravi otporniciReal resistors

Pravi otpornik ima malu induktivnost (posebno žičani) i kapacitivnost. Pri vrlo visokim učestanostima ponaša se kao kolo sa kalemom i kondenzatorom, a ne kao čist otpornik. U ovoj lekciji računamo sa idealnim.

A real resistor has a small inductance (especially a wire-wound one) and some capacitance. At very high frequencies it behaves like a circuit with a coil and a capacitor, not as a pure resistor. In this lesson we work with the ideal one.

Primer 2 · jednačina strujeExample 2 · the equation of the current

Dato: \( u = 50\sin 314t \) V na otporniku od 25 Ω. Napiši jednačinu struje i izračunaj snagu.

Given: \( u = 50\sin 314t \) V across a 25 Ω resistor. Write the equation of the current and calculate the power.

  1. \( I_m = U_m/R = 50/25 = 2\ \mathrm{A} \), a pošto su u fazi: \( i = 2\sin 314t \) A.
  2. Efektivne vrednosti: \( U = 50/\sqrt{2} = 35{,}4\ \mathrm{V} \), \( I = 2/\sqrt{2} = 1{,}41\ \mathrm{A} \).
  3. \( P = UI = 35{,}4\cdot 1{,}41 = 50\ \mathrm{W} \). Provera: \( I^2R = 2\cdot 25 = 50\ \mathrm{W} \).
  1. \( I_m = U_m/R = 50/25 = 2\ \mathrm{A} \), and since they are in phase: \( i = 2\sin 314t \) A.
  2. RMS values: \( U = 50/\sqrt{2} = 35.4\ \mathrm{V} \), \( I = 2/\sqrt{2} = 1.41\ \mathrm{A} \).
  3. \( P = UI = 35.4\cdot 1.41 = 50\ \mathrm{W} \). Check: \( I^2R = 2\cdot 25 = 50\ \mathrm{W} \).

Given: a voltmeter across a 6 Ω resistor reads 12 V (AC). Find the peak voltage, the current and the power.

Deo 4Part 4 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Snaga sa amplitudama. \( P = U_mI_m \) je dvaput previše. Tačno je \( P = U_mI_m/2 \), ili \( P = UI \) sa efektivnim vrednostima.
  • Fazni pomak na otporniku. Nema ga. Ako ti u zadatku sa otpornikom izađe ugao različit od nule, negde si pogrešio.
  • Negativna snaga. Snaga na otporniku nikad nije negativna, ni u jednom trenutku. Negativna snaga (vraćanje energije) postoji samo kod kalema i kondenzatora.
  • Zavisnost od učestanosti. Otpornost idealnog otpornika ne zavisi od učestanosti. To je razlika u odnosu na reaktanse kalema i kondenzatora.
  • Power from amplitudes. \( P = U_mI_m \) is twice too much. The correct one is \( P = U_mI_m/2 \), or \( P = UI \) with RMS values.
  • A phase shift in a resistor. There is none. If you get a non-zero angle in a resistor problem, you have made a mistake somewhere.
  • Negative power. Power in a resistor is never negative, at no moment. Negative power (returning energy) exists only in coils and capacitors.
  • Dependence on frequency. The resistance of an ideal resistor does not depend on frequency. That is the difference compared with the reactances of the coil and the capacitor.

Deo 5Part 5

SažetakSummary

  • Na otporniku važi Omov zakon za trenutne vrednosti: \( i = u/R \), pa i za amplitude i efektivne vrednosti.
  • Napon i struja su u fazi (φ = 0), a impedansa je realna: \( \underline{Z}_R = R \).
  • Trenutna snaga \( p = UI(1 - \cos 2\omega t) \) nikad nije negativna, pulsira sa 2ω.
  • Srednja (aktivna) snaga je \( P = UI = I^2R = U^2/R \), samo sa efektivnim vrednostima.
  • Ohm's law holds for a resistor for instantaneous values: \( i = u/R \), and so for amplitudes and RMS values too.
  • Voltage and current are in phase (φ = 0), and the impedance is real: \( \underline{Z}_R = R \).
  • The instantaneous power \( p = UI(1 - \cos 2\omega t) \) is never negative and pulses at 2ω.
  • The average (active) power is \( P = UI = I^2R = U^2/R \), with RMS values only.
