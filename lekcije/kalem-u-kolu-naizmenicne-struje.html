Deo 1Part 1 · zašto struja kasniwhy the current lags

Samoindukcija i napon na kalemuSelf-induction and the voltage across a coil

Kalem (zavojnica) ima induktivnost L, koja se meri u henrijima (H). Kada se struja kroz kalem menja, menja se i njegov magnetni fluks, pa se u samom kalemu indukuje napon koji se suprotstavlja promeni (Lencovo pravilo). Napon koji izvor mora da dovede na idealan kalem jednak je po veličini tom induktovanom naponu:

A coil has an inductance L, measured in henrys (H). When the current through the coil changes, its magnetic flux changes too, so a voltage is induced in the coil itself that opposes the change (Lenz's rule). The voltage the source must apply to an ideal coil equals that induced voltage in magnitude:

\[ u = L\,\frac{di}{dt} \]

Napon na kalemu je srazmeran brzini promene struje, a ne samoj struji. Zato jednosmerna struja (koja se ne menja, di/dt = 0) kroz idealan kalem ne daje nikakav napon: kalem se ponaša kao običan provodnik. Naizmenična struja se stalno menja, pa se kalem stalno „buni”.

The voltage across a coil is proportional to the rate of change of the current, not to the current itself. That is why a direct current (which does not change, di/dt = 0) through an ideal coil gives no voltage at all: the coil acts as an ordinary wire. An alternating current keeps changing, so the coil keeps "objecting".

Nula, jer je to vrh sinusoide i tu je kriva vodoravna. Zato je tada napon na kalemu nula. Najveći je napon kada struja najbrže raste, to jest kada prolazi kroz nulu.

Zero, because that is the top of the sine and the curve is flat there. So the voltage across the coil is zero then. The voltage is largest when the current rises fastest, that is when it crosses zero.

Induktivna reaktansa i fazni stavInductive reactance and the phase shift

Neka kroz kalem teče struja \( i = I_m\sin\omega t \). Napon je \( u = L\,di/dt \), a izvod sinusa je kosinus: \( u = \omega L I_m\cos\omega t \). Pošto je \( \cos x = \sin(x + 90^\circ) \), dobijamo:

Let a current \( i = I_m\sin\omega t \) flow through the coil. The voltage is \( u = L\,di/dt \), and the derivative of a sine is a cosine: \( u = \omega L I_m\cos\omega t \). Since \( \cos x = \sin(x + 90^\circ) \), we get:

\[ u = \omega L I_m\sin(\omega t + 90^\circ) \qquad U_m = \omega L\,I_m \qquad X_L = \omega L = 2\pi f L \]

Dva važna zaključka: (1) napon prednjači struji za 90°, odnosno struja kasni za naponom za 90°; (2) odnos amplituda napona i struje je induktivna reaktansa \( X_L = \omega L \), koja se meri u omima, ali nije otpornost: ona ne troši energiju.

Two important conclusions: (1) the voltage leads the current by 90°, that is the current lags the voltage by 90°; (2) the ratio of the voltage and current amplitudes is the inductive reactance \( X_L = \omega L \), measured in ohms, but it is not a resistance: it does not consume energy.

\[ I_m = \frac{U_m}{X_L} \qquad I = \frac{U}{X_L} \qquad \underline{Z}_L = j\omega L = jX_L = X_L\angle 90^\circ \]

XL → 0: the coil is a short circuit for direct current.

f → veliko

XL → velika: kalem „guši” visoke učestanosti. Zato se zovu prigušnice.

XL → large: the coil "chokes" high frequencies. That is why they are called chokes.

Pamćenje: ELIRemembering: ELI

U kalemu (L) napon (E) prednjači struji (I): ELI. Kod kondenzatora će biti obrnuto, pa će ti zapamćeno „ICE” pomoći: struja (I) prednjači naponu (E) u kondenzatoru (C).

In a coil (L) the voltage (E) leads the current (I): ELI. For a capacitor it will be the opposite, so "ICE" will help: the current (I) leads the voltage (E) in a capacitor (C).

Deo 3Part 3 · p = u · ip = u · i

Snaga i energija kalemaPower and energy of a coil

Trenutna snaga je \( p = ui = U_mI_m\sin\omega t\cos\omega t = UI\sin 2\omega t \). To je sinus dvostruke učestanosti koji je pola periode pozitivan, a pola negativan. Kada je pozitivan, kalem prima energiju i skladišti je u magnetnom polju; kada je negativan, vraća je izvoru:

The instantaneous power is \( p = ui = U_mI_m\sin\omega t\cos\omega t = UI\sin 2\omega t \). That is a sine of double frequency, positive for half of the time and negative for the other half. When it is positive the coil takes in energy and stores it in its magnetic field; when it is negative it returns it to the source:

\[ W = \tfrac{1}{2}Li^2 \qquad P = 0 \qquad Q_L = U I = X_L I^2 = \frac{U^2}{X_L} \]

Srednja snaga idealnog kalema je nula. Energija samo „ide i vraća se”, ali struja kroz izvor i provodnike teče i opterećuje ih. Ta veličina se zove reaktivna snaga Q i meri u varovima (var), da se ne meša sa vatima.

The average power of an ideal coil is zero. The energy just "goes and comes back", but the current through the source and the wires still flows and loads them. This quantity is called the reactive power Q and is measured in vars (var), so it is not confused with watts.

Pravi kalemA real coil

Pravi kalem ima i otpornost žice r. Model je red veza r i idealnog kalema, impedansa je \( \underline{Z} = r + j\omega L \), a fazni stav je manji od 90°. Čist 90° imaju samo idealni kalemovi.

A real coil also has the resistance of its wire r. The model is r in series with an ideal coil, the impedance is \( \underline{Z} = r + j\omega L \), and the phase shift is less than 90°. Only ideal coils have a clean 90°.

Dato: kalem \( L = 0{,}5 \) H, priključen na 230 V. Traži se \( X_L \) i struja pri 50 Hz, a zatim pri 500 Hz.

Given: a coil \( L = 0.5 \) H connected to 230 V. Find \( X_L \) and the current at 50 Hz, and then at 500 Hz.

  1. Pri 50 Hz: \( X_L = 2\pi\cdot 50\cdot 0{,}5 = 157\ \Omega \), \( I = 230/157 = 1{,}46\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. Pri 500 Hz: \( X_L = 2\pi\cdot 500\cdot 0{,}5 = 1571\ \Omega \), \( I = 230/1571 = 0{,}146\ \mathrm{A} \).
  3. Učestanost 10 puta veća daje reaktansu 10 puta veću i struju 10 puta manju.
  1. At 50 Hz: \( X_L = 2\pi\cdot 50\cdot 0.5 = 157\ \Omega \), \( I = 230/157 = 1.46\ \mathrm{A} \).

Deo 5Part 5 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Pogrešan smer fazne razlike. Kod kalema struja kasni, napon prednjači (ELI). Ne obrni to.
  • Reaktansa kao otpornost. Reaktansa ima jedinicu om, ali ne pretvara energiju u toplotu. Srednja snaga idealnog kalema je nula.
  • Zaboravljeno 2π. \( X_L = 2\pi f L \), ne \( fL \). I pretvori mH u H pre računanja.
  • Kalem na jednosmernoj struji. Idealan kalem na jednosmernoj struji ima XL = 0. Struju tada ograničava samo otpornost žice.
  • The wrong direction of the phase shift. In a coil the current lags, the voltage leads (ELI). Do not reverse it.
  • Reactance as resistance. Reactance has the unit ohm, but it does not turn energy into heat. The average power of an ideal coil is zero.
  • Forgetting 2π. \( X_L = 2\pi f L \), not \( fL \). And convert mH to H before calculating.
  • A coil on direct current. An ideal coil on direct current has XL = 0. The current is then limited only by the resistance of the wire.

Deo 6Part 6

SažetakSummary

  • Napon na kalemu je \( u = L\,di/dt \): srazmeran je brzini promene struje.
  • Napon prednjači struji za 90° (ELI). Impedansa je \( \underline{Z}_L = j\omega L \).
  • Induktivna reaktansa \( X_L = \omega L = 2\pi fL \) raste sa učestanošću i jednaka je nuli za jednosmernu struju.
  • Idealni kalem ne troši snagu (\( P = 0 \)), samo razmenjuje energiju \( W = Li^2/2 \) sa izvorom; reaktivna snaga je \( Q = UI \).
  • The voltage across a coil is \( u = L\,di/dt \): proportional to the rate of change of the current.
  • The voltage leads the current by 90° (ELI). The impedance is \( \underline{Z}_L = j\omega L \).
  • The inductive reactance \( X_L = \omega L = 2\pi fL \) grows with frequency and is zero for direct current.
  • An ideal coil consumes no power (\( P = 0 \)), it only exchanges the energy \( W = Li^2/2 \) with the source; the reactive power is \( Q = UI \).
