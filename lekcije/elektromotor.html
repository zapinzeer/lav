Deo 1Part 1 · od Amperove sile do rotacijefrom the Ampère force to rotation

Kako radi elektromotorHow an electric motor works

Elektromotor pretvara električnu energiju u mehaničku. Jednosmerni motor ima stalni magnet (stator) koji stvara polje, i zavojak na osovini (rotor) kroz koji teče struja.

An electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. A DC motor has a permanent magnet (the stator) that creates a field, and a loop on an axle (the rotor) carrying a current.

  1. Na dve naspramne strane zavojka struje idu u suprotnim smerovima.
  2. Polje magneta zato deluje na njih Amperovim silama suprotnog smera: jedna strana se podiže, druga spušta.
  3. Taj par sila je spreg koji zavrti zavojak oko osovine.
  4. Kada zavojak prođe uspravan položaj, komutator obrne smer struje, pa se zavrtanje nastavlja u istu stranu.
  1. On the two opposite sides of the loop the currents flow in opposite directions.
  2. The magnet's field therefore pushes on them with Ampère forces in opposite directions: one side goes up, the other down.
  3. That pair of forces is a couple that turns the loop about the axle.
  4. When the loop passes the upright position, the commutator reverses the current, so the turning continues in the same direction.

U modelu levo je severni (crveni), a desno južni (plavi) pol. Narandžaste tačke pokazuju smer struje u stranama zavojka, a žute strelice sile na njih. Probaj da isključiš komutator.

In the model the left pole is north (red) and the right pole is south (blue). The orange dots show the direction of the current in the sides of the loop, and the yellow arrows the forces on them. Try switching the commutator off.

Šta čini motorWhat makes a motor

Magnetno polje (stator) + provodnik sa strujom (rotor) + komutator sa četkicama koji obrće smer struje svakih pola obrtaja.

A magnetic field (stator) + a conductor with a current (rotor) + a commutator with brushes that reverses the current every half turn.

Isti uređaj radi i kao generator: kada rotor vrtimo spolja, u zavojku se indukuje napon. O tome govori lekcija „Pojava elektromagnetne indukcije”.

The same device also works as a generator: when we turn the rotor from outside, a voltage is induced in the loop. The lesson "Electromagnetic induction" covers that.

Deo 2Part 2 · oznaka M · jedinica N·msymbol M · unit N·m

Moment sile na zavojakThe torque on a loop

Na svaku od dve strane zavojka dužine l deluje sila F = B I l, jedna naviše, druga naniže. Zajedno čine spreg. Moment sprega je proizvod sile i kraka. Za pravougaoni zavojak širine b krak je b sin α, pa je:

On each of the two sides of the loop of length l acts a force F = B I l, one upwards and the other downwards. Together they form a couple. The torque of a couple is the force times the arm. For a rectangular loop of width b the arm is b sin α, so:

\[ M = F\,b\sin\alpha = B\,I\,l\,b\,\sin\alpha = B\,I\,S\sin\alpha \]

Ako zavojak ima N navoja, svaki doprinosi isto:

If the loop has N turns, each contributes equally:

\[ M = N\,B\,I\,S\,\sin\alpha \]

Moment je nula. Sile i dalje postoje, ali leže na istom pravcu (duž ravni zavojka), pa je njihov krak nula. Sila bez kraka ne vrti.

The torque is zero. The forces still exist, but they lie along the same line (along the plane of the loop), so their arm is zero. A force with no arm does not turn anything.

Deo 3Part 3 · zašto se motor ne zaustavljawhy the motor does not stop

Komutator i četkiceThe commutator and brushes

Pogledaj grafik iz Dela 2. Bez komutatora moment menja znak: u prvoj polovini obrtaja zavojak je gurnut napred, a posle neutralnog položaja bi bio vraćen nazad. Zavojak se zato njiše oko neutralnog položaja i staje.

Look at the graph in Part 2. Without a commutator the torque changes sign: in the first half turn the loop is pushed forward, but past the neutral position it would be pushed back. The loop therefore swings about the neutral position and stops.

Komutator je prsten podeljen na dve polovine (kolektorske lamele), od kojih je svaka vezana za jedan kraj zavojka. Dve nepokretne četkice naležu na prsten i dovode struju iz izvora. Kada zavojak prođe neutralni položaj, svaka polovina prelazi sa jedne četkice na drugu, pa se smer struje u zavojku obrne. Zahvaljujući tome moment uvek ima isti znak i rotor se vrti u istu stranu.

The commutator is a ring split into two halves (segments), each connected to one end of the loop. Two stationary brushes press against the ring and bring in current from the source. When the loop passes the neutral position, each half moves from one brush to the other, so the direction of the current in the loop reverses. Thanks to this the torque always has the same sign and the rotor turns the same way.

Kako se vidi u modeluHow to see it in the model

U Delu 2 uključi automatsko okretanje i gledaj tačku u kojoj se ⊙ i ⊗ zamene mesta: to je uspravni zavojak, upravo trenutak kada četkice prelaze preko procepa komutatora.

In Part 2 switch on auto-rotate and watch the point where ⊙ and ⊗ swap places: that is the upright loop, exactly the moment the brushes cross the gap of the commutator.

Stvarni motori imaju mnogo zavojaka pod različitim uglovima i mnogo lamela, pa je moment skoro konstantan. Postoje i motori naizmenične struje koji nemaju komutator jer se smer struje menja sam od sebe.

Real motors have many loops at different angles and many segments, so the torque is nearly constant. There are also AC motors that have no commutator because the current changes direction on its own.

Deo 4Part 4 · motor je i generatora motor is also a generator

Protivelektromotorna sila i polazna strujaBack-EMF and the starting current

Kada se zavojak vrti u magnetnom polju, njegov fluks se menja, pa se u njemu indukuje napon. Taj napon se suprotstavlja naponu izvora i zove se protivelektromotorna sila E. Proporcionalna je brzini obrtanja: E = k ω.

When the loop turns in a magnetic field, its flux changes, so a voltage is induced in it. This voltage opposes the source voltage and is called the back-EMF E. It is proportional to the speed: E = k ω.

Struja kroz rotor zato zavisi od razlike napona izvora i protivelektromotorne sile (R je otpor namotaja):

The current through the rotor therefore depends on the difference between the source voltage and the back-EMF (R is the resistance of the winding):

\[ I = \frac{U - E}{R} \]
  • Pri pokretanju rotor miruje, E = 0, pa je struja najveća: I = U/R.
  • Kako se motor ubrzava, E raste, a struja i moment opadaju.
  • U ustaljenom radu moment motora je jednak momentu opterećenja i trenja.
  • Kod zaglavljenog rotora je E = 0 i struja je velika, pa se namotaji pregreju.
  • When starting the rotor is at rest, E = 0, so the current is largest: I = U/R.
  • As the motor speeds up, E grows, and the current and torque fall.
  • In steady running the torque of the motor equals the torque of the load and friction.
  • With a jammed rotor E = 0 and the current is large, so the windings overheat.

Primer 1 · najveći momentExample 1 · maximum torque

Dato: N = 100 navoja, B = 0,2 T, I = 3 A, površina navoja S = 50 cm², ravan zavojka je paralelna polju. Traži se M.

Given: N = 100 turns, B = 0.2 T, I = 3 A, area of a turn S = 50 cm², the plane of the loop is parallel to the field. Find M.

  1. Površina u m²: S = 50·10−4 = 0,005 m².
  2. Ravan paralelna polju znači da je normala normalna na polje: α = 90°, sin α = 1.
  3. \( M = N\,B\,I\,S\,\sin\alpha = 100\cdot 0{,}2\cdot 3\cdot 0{,}005\cdot 1 = 0{,}3\ \mathrm{N\cdot m} \)
  1. Area in m²: S = 50·10−4 = 0.005 m².
  2. A plane parallel to the field means the normal is perpendicular to the field: α = 90°, sin α = 1.
  3. \( M = N\,B\,I\,S\,\sin\alpha = 100\cdot 0.2\cdot 3\cdot 0.005\cdot 1 = 0.3\ \mathrm{N\cdot m} \)

Primer 3 · polazna strujaExample 3 · the starting current

Dato: motor ima otpor namotaja R = 2 Ω i priključen je na U = 12 V. Pri punoj brzini protivelektromotorna sila je E = 9 V. Traži se struja pri pokretanju i u ustaljenom radu.

Given: a motor has a winding resistance R = 2 Ω and is connected to U = 12 V. At full speed the back-EMF is E = 9 V. Find the current at start and in steady running.

  1. Pri pokretanju je E = 0: \( I = \dfrac{U}{R} = \dfrac{12}{2} = 6\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. U ustaljenom radu: \( I = \dfrac{U-E}{R} = \dfrac{12-9}{2} = 1{,}5\ \mathrm{A} \).
  1. At start E = 0: \( I = \dfrac{U}{R} = \dfrac{12}{2} = 6\ \mathrm{A} \).
  2. In steady running: \( I = \dfrac{U-E}{R} = \dfrac{12-9}{2} = 1.5\ \mathrm{A} \).

6 A pri pokretanju, 1,5 A u radu: četiri puta manje.6 A at start, 1.5 A when running: four times smaller.

Primer 4 · mehanička snagaExample 4 · mechanical power

Dato: motor daje moment M = 0,1 N·m pri n = 1200 o/min. Traži se mehanička snaga.

Given: a motor delivers a torque M = 0.1 N·m at n = 1200 rpm. Find the mechanical power.

  1. Ugaona brzina: \( \omega = \dfrac{2\pi n}{60} = \dfrac{2\pi\cdot 1200}{60} = 125{,}7\ \mathrm{rad/s} \).
  2. \( P = M\,\omega = 0{,}1\cdot 125{,}7 = 12{,}6\ \mathrm{W} \)
  1. Angular velocity: \( \omega = \dfrac{2\pi n}{60} = \dfrac{2\pi\cdot 1200}{60} = 125.7\ \mathrm{rad/s} \).
  2. \( P = M\,\omega = 0.1\cdot 125.7 = 12.6\ \mathrm{W} \)

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Ugao α je sa normalom. U formuli M = N B I S sin α ugao se meri između normale zavojka i polja, a ne između ravni zavojka i polja. Ako je dat ugao ravni i polja, koristi kosinus.
  • Zaboravljeno N. Kod zavojka sa više navoja moment je N puta veći.
  • Površina u m². 50 cm² je 0,005 m², a ne 0,5 m².
  • Obrtaji i rad/s. Za snagu je potrebna ugaona brzina u rad/s: ω = 2πn/60. Broj 1200 o/min nije 1200 rad/s.
  • Struja nije stalna. Pri pokretanju struja je velika, a u radu mala. Nemoj računati struju kao U/R u ustaljenom radu.
  • Komutator ne menja polje. Komutator menja smer struje u zavojku, ne smer magnetnog polja.
  • The angle α is with the normal. In M = N B I S sin α the angle is measured between the normal of the loop and the field, not between the plane of the loop and the field. If the angle of the plane and the field is given, use the cosine.
  • Forgetting N. For a loop with several turns the torque is N times larger.
  • Area in m². 50 cm² is 0.005 m², not 0.5 m².
  • rpm and rad/s. For power you need the angular velocity in rad/s: ω = 2πn/60. A speed of 1200 rpm is not 1200 rad/s.
  • The current is not constant. It is large when starting and small when running. Do not calculate the running current as U/R.
  • The commutator does not change the field. It changes the direction of the current in the loop, not the direction of the magnetic field.

Deo 7Part 7

SažetakSummary

  • Na strane zavojka sa strujom u polju deluju Amperove sile suprotnog smera. Zajedno su spreg koji vrti rotor.
  • Obrtni moment: \( M = N\,B\,I\,S\,\sin\alpha \). Najveći je kada je ravan zavojka paralelna polju (α = 90°), a nula u neutralnom položaju (α = 0°).
  • Komutator obrće smer struje svakih pola obrtaja, pa moment ima stalan znak. Bez njega bi se rotor njihao i stao.
  • Rotor koji se vrti indukuje protivelektromotornu silu \( E = k\omega \), pa je \( I = (U-E)/R \). Pri pokretanju je struja najveća.
  • Mehanička snaga: \( P = M\omega = M\cdot 2\pi n/60 \).
  • Ampère forces of opposite direction act on the sides of a current-carrying loop in a field. Together they are a couple that turns the rotor.
  • Torque: \( M = N\,B\,I\,S\,\sin\alpha \). It is largest when the plane of the loop is parallel to the field (α = 90°) and zero in the neutral position (α = 0°).
  • The commutator reverses the current every half turn, so the torque has a constant sign. Without it the rotor would swing and stop.
  • A turning rotor induces a back-EMF \( E = k\omega \), so \( I = (U-E)/R \). The current is largest when starting.
  • Mechanical power: \( P = M\omega = M\cdot 2\pi n/60 \).
