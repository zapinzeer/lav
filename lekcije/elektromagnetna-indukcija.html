Deo 1Part 1 · Faradejev ogled, 1831.Faraday's experiment, 1831

Magnet i kalemA magnet and a coil

Struja stvara magnetno polje. Da li polje može da stvori struju? Majkl Faradej je 1831. povezao kalem sa osetljivim galvanometrom i počeo da pomera magnet. Struja se pojavila, ali samo dok se magnet kretao.

A current creates a magnetic field. Can a field create a current? In 1831 Michael Faraday connected a coil to a sensitive galvanometer and began moving a magnet. A current appeared, but only while the magnet was moving.

  • Magnet miruje u kalemu: nema struje, ma koliko jak bio.
  • Magnet se približava: struja ide u jednu stranu.
  • Magnet se udaljava: struja ide u suprotnu stranu.
  • Što se magnet kreće brže, struja je jača.
  • The magnet at rest in the coil: no current, however strong it is.
  • The magnet approaches: the current goes one way.
  • The magnet moves away: the current goes the opposite way.
  • The faster the magnet moves, the larger the current.

Zaključak: u kalemu nastaje napon, nazvan indukovani napon, kada se menja magnetni fluks kroz kalem. Ta pojava je elektromagnetna indukcija. Ako je kolo zatvoreno, kroz njega teče indukovana struja.

The conclusion: a voltage, called the induced voltage, appears in the coil when the magnetic flux through the coil changes. This effect is electromagnetic induction. If the circuit is closed, an induced current flows through it.

Pomisli prvo: šta bi pokazao galvanometar ako umesto magneta pomeramo kalem, a magnet miruje?Think first: what would the galvanometer show if we move the coil instead of the magnet, while the magnet stays still?

Isto što i pre. Bitno je samo relativno kretanje, jer se fluks kroz kalem menja u oba slučaja.

The same as before. Only the relative motion matters, because the flux through the coil changes in both cases.

Deo 2Part 2 · oznaka ε · jedinica volt (V)symbol ε · unit volt (V)

Faradejev zakonFaraday's law

Indukovani napon zavisi od toga koliko brzo se menja fluks. Što se fluks promeni više i za kraće vreme, napon je veći. Za kalem sa N zavojaka:

The induced voltage depends on how fast the flux changes. The more the flux changes, and the shorter the time, the larger the voltage. For a coil with N turns:

\[ \varepsilon = -N\,\frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} \]

Znak minus je Lencovo pravilo: smer indukovane struje je takav da se suprotstavlja promeni fluksa koja ju je izazvala. Ako samo tražiš jačinu napona, zadrži apsolutnu vrednost. Jedinica je 1 V = 1 Wb/s.

The minus sign is Lenz's rule: the direction of the induced current is such that it opposes the change of flux that caused it. If you only need the magnitude of the voltage, keep the absolute value. The unit is 1 V = 1 Wb/s.

Na grafiku fluksa u vremenu, napon je srazmeran nagibu krive: strma kriva daje veliki napon, ravna daje nulu.

On a graph of flux against time, the voltage is proportional to the slope of the curve: a steep curve gives a large voltage, a flat one gives zero.

Tri načina da se fluks promeniThree ways to change the flux

Fluks je \( \Phi = B\,S\cos\alpha \). Napon se indukuje kada se menja B (pomeranje magneta, promena struje), S (širenje ili sabijanje petlje) ili α (okretanje petlje, kao kod generatora).

The flux is \( \Phi = B\,S\cos\alpha \). A voltage is induced when B changes (moving a magnet, changing a current), S changes (stretching or squeezing the loop) or α changes (turning the loop, as in a generator).

Deo 3Part 3 · smer struje · provodnik u kretanjudirection of the current · a moving conductor

Lencovo pravilo: petlja kroz poljeLenz's rule: a loop through a field

Zamisli žičanu petlju koja klizi kroz oblast u kojoj postoji homogeno magnetno polje, usmereno u crtež (⊗). Pratimo šta se dešava u tri faze:

Imagine a wire loop sliding through a region with a uniform magnetic field directed into the page (⊗). We follow what happens in three phases:

  1. Ulazak. Fluks kroz petlju raste. Indukovana struja stvara polje iz crteža da se tome suprotstavi, pa teče suprotno od kazaljke na satu.
  2. Petlja potpuno u polju. Fluks je stalan, ΔΦ = 0, pa nema ni napona ni struje.
  3. Izlazak. Fluks opada. Struja stvara polje u crtež da nadoknadi gubitak, pa teče u smeru kazaljke na satu.
  1. Entering. The flux through the loop grows. The induced current creates a field out of the page to oppose this, so it flows anticlockwise.
  2. Loop fully inside. The flux is constant, ΔΦ = 0, so there is no voltage and no current.
  3. Leaving. The flux falls. The current creates a field into the page to make up the loss, so it flows clockwise.

Isti rezultat dobijamo i preko sile na naelektrisanja u strani koja se kreće. Za provodnik dužine l koji se kreće brzinom v normalno na polje, fluks se menja za ΔΦ = B l v Δt, pa je:

We get the same result from the force on the charges in the moving side. For a conductor of length l moving at speed v perpendicular to the field, the flux changes by ΔΦ = B l v Δt, so:

\[ \varepsilon = B\,l\,v\,\sin\alpha \]

Pravilo desne ruke za indukovanu strujuRight-hand rule for the induced current

Postavi desnu ruku tako da linije polja ulaze u dlan, a palac pokazuje smer kretanja provodnika. Ispruženi prsti tada pokazuju smer indukovane struje.

Hold your right hand so the field lines enter your palm and your thumb points along the motion of the conductor. Your outstretched fingers then show the direction of the induced current.

Zašto minus? Zakon održanja energijeWhy the minus? Conservation of energy

Struja u strani petlje koja je u polju trpi Amperovu silu F = B I l, i ta sila uvek koči kretanje. Mehanički rad koji utrošimo da bismo gurali petlju pretvara se u električnu energiju, i na kraju u toplotu u petlji. Kada bi struja imala suprotan smer, petlja bi se sama ubrzavala i dobijali bismo energiju iz ničega.

The current in the side of the loop that is in the field feels an Ampère force F = B I l, and this force always brakes the motion. The mechanical work we do to push the loop is turned into electrical energy, and finally into heat in the loop. If the current had the opposite direction, the loop would speed up by itself and we would get energy from nothing.

Deo 4Part 4 · okretanje zavojkaturning a loop

Generator naizmenične strujeThe alternating-current generator

Ako zavojak okrećemo u homogenom magnetnom polju, ugao α se stalno menja, pa se menja i fluks \( \Phi = B S \cos\omega t \). Prema Faradejevom zakonu indukuje se napon koji je sinusoida:

If we turn a loop in a uniform magnetic field, the angle α keeps changing, and so does the flux \( \Phi = B S \cos\omega t \). By Faraday's law a voltage is induced that is a sine wave:

\[ \varepsilon = N\,B\,S\,\omega\,\sin\omega t \qquad \varepsilon_{max}=N\,B\,S\,\omega \]

  • Napon je najveći kada je fluks nula (ravan zavojka je paralelna polju) jer se tada fluks menja najbrže.
  • Napon je nula kada je fluks najveći (zavojak normalan na polje), jer fluks tada za trenutak miruje.
  • Svakih pola obrtaja napon menja znak: dobija se naizmenična struja.
  • Veći N, B, S ili brže okretanje daju veći napon.
  • The voltage is largest when the flux is zero (the plane of the loop is parallel to the field) because the flux then changes fastest.
  • The voltage is zero when the flux is largest (the loop perpendicular to the field), because the flux then momentarily stands still.
  • Every half turn the voltage changes sign: this gives alternating current.
  • Larger N, B, S or faster turning give a larger voltage.

Struju iz zavojka odvode klizni prstenovi (celi prstenovi, a ne podeljeni kao komutator) i četkice, pa na izlazu dobijamo naizmenični napon. Mreža u Srbiji ima frekvenciju 50 Hz i efektivni napon 230 V. Efektivni napon je \( U = \varepsilon_{max}/\sqrt{2} \).

The current is taken from the loop by slip rings (whole rings, not split like a commutator) and brushes, so the output is an alternating voltage. The mains in Serbia have a frequency of 50 Hz and an RMS voltage of 230 V. The RMS voltage is \( U = \varepsilon_{max}/\sqrt{2} \).

Primer 1 · Faradejev zakonExample 1 · Faraday's law

Dato: kalem ima N = 200 zavojaka. Fluks kroz njega se za Δt = 0,1 s promeni za ΔΦ = 0,5 mWb. Traži se indukovani napon.

Given: a coil has N = 200 turns. The flux through it changes by ΔΦ = 0.5 mWb in Δt = 0.1 s. Find the induced voltage.

  1. Jedinice: ΔΦ = 0,5·10−3 Wb.
  2. \( |\varepsilon| = N\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} = 200\cdot\dfrac{0{,}5\cdot 10^{-3}}{0{,}1} = 1\ \mathrm{V} \)
  1. Units: ΔΦ = 0.5·10−3 Wb.
  2. \( |\varepsilon| = N\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} = 200\cdot\dfrac{0.5\cdot 10^{-3}}{0.1} = 1\ \mathrm{V} \)

Primer 3 · generatorExample 3 · a generator

Dato: zavojak ima N = 100 navoja, površinu S = 0,02 m², okreće se u polju B = 0,5 T brzinom n = 3000 o/min. Traži se maksimalni i efektivni napon i frekvencija.

Given: a loop has N = 100 turns, an area S = 0.02 m², and turns in a field B = 0.5 T at n = 3000 rpm. Find the maximum and RMS voltage and the frequency.

  1. Frekvencija: \( f = n/60 = 3000/60 = 50\ \mathrm{Hz} \).
  2. Ugaona brzina: \( \omega = 2\pi f = 314\ \mathrm{rad/s} \).

  4. Efektivni napon: \( U = \varepsilon_{max}/\sqrt{2} = 314/1{,}414 = 222\ \mathrm{V} \).
  1. Frequency: \( f = n/60 = 3000/60 = 50\ \mathrm{Hz} \).
  2. Angular velocity: \( \omega = 2\pi f = 314\ \mathrm{rad/s} \).
  3. \( \varepsilon_{max} = N\,B\,S\,\omega = 100\cdot 0.5\cdot 0.02\cdot 314 = 314\ \mathrm{V} \)
  4. RMS voltage: \( U = \varepsilon_{max}/\sqrt{2} = 314/1.414 = 222\ \mathrm{V} \).

Primer 4 · magnet se spušta u kalemExample 4 · a magnet is lowered into a coil

Severni pol magneta se spušta u kalem od N = 500 zavojaka. Fluks kroz kalem poraste od 0 do 2 mWb za 0,05 s. Traži se napon i smer polja koje stvara kalem.

The north pole of a magnet is lowered into a coil of N = 500 turns. The flux through the coil rises from 0 to 2 mWb in 0.05 s. Find the voltage and the direction of the field created by the coil.

  1. \( |\varepsilon| = N\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} = 500\cdot\dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-3}}{0{,}05} = 20\ \mathrm{V} \)
  2. Fluks raste, pa se kalem suprotstavlja: stvara polje usmereno od magneta. Gornji kraj kalema postaje severni pol i odbija magnet.
  1. \( |\varepsilon| = N\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} = 500\cdot\dfrac{2\cdot 10^{-3}}{0.05} = 20\ \mathrm{V} \)
  2. The flux grows, so the coil opposes: it creates a field directed away from the magnet. The upper end of the coil becomes a north pole and repels the magnet.

Deo 6Part 6 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Veliki fluks ne znači veliki napon. Napon zavisi od brzine promene fluksa, ne od njegove vrednosti. Konstantan fluks, ma kako veliki, ne daje napon.
  • Zaboravljeno N. Kod kalema se napon množi brojem zavojaka.
  • Znak minus. Minus pokazuje smer (Lencovo pravilo), a ne da je napon „negativan”. Jačina napona je uvek pozitivna.
  • Indukovano polje ne poništava polje. Indukovana struja samo usporava promenu fluksa, ne poništava ga u potpunosti.
  • Leva i desna ruka. Za silu na struju koristimo levu ruku, a za indukovanu struju u provodniku koji se kreće desnu ruku.
  • Efektivni i maksimalni napon. U utičnici je 230 V efektivno. Maksimalni napon je 230·√2 ≈ 325 V.
  • A large flux does not mean a large voltage. The voltage depends on the rate of change of the flux, not on its value. A constant flux, however large, gives no voltage.
  • Forgetting N. For a coil the voltage is multiplied by the number of turns.
  • The minus sign. The minus shows direction (Lenz's rule), not that the voltage is "negative". The magnitude of the voltage is always positive.
  • The induced field does not cancel the field. The induced current only slows the change of the flux, it does not cancel it completely.
  • Left and right hand. For the force on a current we use the left hand, and for the induced current in a moving conductor the right hand.
  • RMS and maximum voltage. The socket gives 230 V RMS. The maximum voltage is 230·√2 ≈ 325 V.

Deo 7Part 7

SažetakSummary

  • Kada se magnetni fluks kroz kalem menja, indukuje se napon (elektromagnetna indukcija). Kada fluks miruje, napona nema.
  • Faradejev zakon: \( \varepsilon = -N\,\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} \). Napon je srazmeran brzini promene fluksa i broju zavojaka.
  • Lencovo pravilo: indukovana struja se suprotstavlja promeni koja ju je izazvala. To je zakon održanja energije.
  • Provodnik dužine l koji se kreće brzinom v normalno na polje: \( \varepsilon = B\,l\,v \).
  • Generator: \( \varepsilon = N B S \omega \sin\omega t \), naizmenični (sinusni) napon sa \( \varepsilon_{max} = NBS\omega \) i \( U = \varepsilon_{max}/\sqrt{2} \).
  • When the magnetic flux through a coil changes, a voltage is induced (electromagnetic induction). When the flux is steady, there is no voltage.
  • Faraday's law: \( \varepsilon = -N\,\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} \). The voltage is proportional to the rate of change of the flux and to the number of turns.
  • Lenz's rule: the induced current opposes the change that caused it. This is conservation of energy.
  • A conductor of length l moving at speed v perpendicular to the field: \( \varepsilon = B\,l\,v \).
  • Generator: \( \varepsilon = N B S \omega \sin\omega t \), an alternating (sine) voltage with \( \varepsilon_{max} = NBS\omega \) and \( U = \varepsilon_{max}/\sqrt{2} \).
