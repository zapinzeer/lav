Deo 1Part 1 · pre početkabefore you start

Priprema i zaštita od statičkog elektricitetaPreparation and protection against static electricity

Ugradnja matične ploče traje desetak minuta, a greška može da je uništi. Zato prvo pripremi radno mesto: čist, suv sto, dobra svetlost, mali krstasti odvijač (Phillips #2) i kutijica za zavrtnje. Ploču vadi iz kutije tek kad si spreman i nikada je ne stavljaj na metalnu površinu.

Installing a motherboard takes about ten minutes, but a mistake can destroy it. So first prepare the workplace: a clean, dry table, good light, a small Phillips #2 screwdriver and a small box for screws. Take the board out of its box only when you are ready and never put it on a metal surface.

Glavni neprijatelj je elektrostatičko pražnjenje (ESD): statički „šok” koji ti često ni ne osetiš, a koji može da ošteti osetljive čipove. Zato ploču drži za ivice, ne dodiruj kontakte i pre rada dodirni metalno kućište.

The main enemy is electrostatic discharge (ESD): a static "shock" you often cannot even feel, but which can damage sensitive chips. So hold the board by its edges, do not touch the contacts, and touch the metal case before you start.

  • nosi antistatičku narukvicu spojenu na kućište, ili češće dodiruj metalni deo kućišta dok radiš;
  • ploču drži za ivice, ne dodiruj kontakte, čipove i lemne tačke;
  • radi na drvenom ili plastičnom stolu, ne na tepihu, i izbegavaj vunene i sintetičke džempere;
  • ploču odlaži samo na kutiju u kojoj je došla ili na antistatičku kesu.
  • wear an antistatic wrist strap clipped to the case, or touch a metal part of the case often while you work;
  • hold the board by its edges and do not touch contacts, chips or solder points;
  • work on a wooden or plastic table, not on a carpet, and avoid wool and synthetic sweaters;
  • put the board only on its own box or on an antistatic bag.
Pomisli prvo: zašto nije dovoljno samo da isključiš računar iz struje?Think first: why is it not enough just to switch the computer off?

Ugašen računar ne štiti od ESD (taj problem je u tebi, a ne u računaru). Napajanje ipak treba da bude iskopčano: prekidač na 0 i kabl izvučen, jer ploča ostaje „budna” i kada je računar ugašen.

A switched-off computer gives no ESD protection (the problem is in you, not in the computer). The power supply should still be unplugged: switch at 0 and the cable out, because the board stays "awake" even when the computer is off.

Deo 2Part 2 · korak po korakstep by step

Ugradnja u 3D: šest korakaInstallation in 3D: six steps

Redosled je važan. Mnogo je lakše da procesor, hladnjak i memoriju stavljaš na ploču dok je još van kućišta, na kutiji, jer ćeš tako imati puno mesta za prste. Zato se u praksi ploča prvo opremi, pa tek onda ugrađuje. Šest koraka koje vidiš u modelu:

The order matters. It is much easier to fit the processor, cooler and memory on the board while it is still outside the case, on its box, because then you have plenty of room for your fingers. So in practice the board is equipped first and installed afterwards. The six steps you see in the model:

  1. pripremi kućište i postavi distancere;
  2. ugradi I/O štitnik;
  3. spusti ploču;
  4. zavrni zavrtnje;
  5. priključi napajanje (24-pin i EPS);
  6. priključi prednje priključke (dugmad, USB, audio).
  1. prepare the case and fit the standoffs;
  2. fit the I/O shield;
  3. lower the board;
  4. drive the screws;
  5. connect the power (24-pin and EPS);
  6. connect the front-panel connectors (buttons, USB, audio).

Pravilo ukrštanjaThe crossing rule

Zavrtnje zatežeš kao točak na automobilu: prvo jedan ugao, pa suprotni, pa ostala dva, pa sredinu. Tako se ploča ne iskrivi. Prvo svi zavrtnji lagano, pa tek onda svi čvrsto.

You tighten the screws like a car wheel: first one corner, then the opposite one, then the other two, then the middle. That way the board does not warp. First all the screws lightly, only then all of them firmly.

Deo 3Part 3 · podloga pločewhat the board sits on

Distanceri i form-faktoriStandoffs and form factors

Ploča nikada ne sme da dodiruje metalnu ploču kućišta. Između stoje distanceri (engl. standoffs): mesingani valjci visine oko 6 mm sa navojem. Kućište je bušeno za sve form-faktore, ali ploča ima rupe samo na nekim mestima, pa distancere stavljaš tačno na ta mesta. Svaka rupa na ploči mora da ima distancer, a distancer bez rupe iznad sebe može da kratko spoji tragove ploče.

The board must never touch the metal tray of the case. Between them sit the standoffs: brass cylinders about 6 mm high with a thread. The case is drilled for all form factors, but a board has holes only in some places, so you fit the standoffs exactly at those places. Every hole in the board needs a standoff, and a standoff with no hole above it can short the tracks of the board.

Zavrtnji su najčešće M3 × 5 mm (mali, sa širokom glavom) ili #6-32; distanceri imaju unutrašnji navoj iste vrste. Kućište obično dolazi sa kesicom pravih zavrtnjeva i distancera. Ne mešaj ih sa zavrtnjima za disk i kućište: imaju drugačiji navoj.

The screws are most often M3 × 5 mm (small, with a wide head) or #6-32; the standoffs have an internal thread of the same kind. A case usually comes with a bag of the right screws and standoffs. Do not mix them with the screws for drives and the side panel: they have a different thread.

Deo 4Part 4 · napajanjepower

Kablovi napajanjaPower cables

Ploča dobija napajanje preko dva velika konektora. 24-pin ATX napaja ceo sistem, a 8-pin EPS (ili 4+4 pin) je namenjen isključivo procesoru. Grafička kartica ima svoj kabl (PCIe 6+2 pin) koji ide iz napajanja, a ne sa ploče.

The board gets its power through two large connectors. The 24-pin ATX feeds the whole system, while the 8-pin EPS (or 4+4 pin) is meant only for the processor. The graphics card has its own cable (PCIe 6+2 pin) that comes from the power supply, not from the board.

The connectors have a key and a latch, so they only go in one way. If it goes in hard, something is wrong: never force it. Always listen for the "click" of the latch.

Deo 5Part 5 · prednja pločafront panel

Prednji priključci (front panel)Front-panel connectors

Najsitniji i najzahtevniji deo posla su prednji kablovi iz kućišta. Svaki je mali konektor koji ide na red pinova uz donju ivicu ploče. Skoro uvek je to jedan red od 2 × 5 pinova (grupa „F_PANEL” ili „JFP1”), a USB i audio imaju svoje, veće konektore.

The smallest and most demanding part of the job is the front cables from the case. Each is a small connector that goes onto a row of pins along the bottom edge of the board. It is almost always one block of 2 × 5 pins (the "F_PANEL" or "JFP1" group), while USB and audio have their own, larger connectors.

Deo 6Part 6 · prvo pokretanjefirst power-on

Prvo paljenje i šta ako ne radiFirst power-on and what to do if it does not work

Pre nego što zatvoriš kućište, uključi računar sa otvorenim bokom. Redosled provere: 1. da li su oba konektora napajanja ukopčana do kraja; 2. da li je prekidač napajanja na „I”; 3. da li su memorija i grafička kartica zaglavljeni; 4. da li je kabl monitora u grafičkoj kartici (a ne u ploči).

Before closing the case, switch the computer on with the side open. Order of checks: 1. both power connectors are fully plugged in; 2. the power switch is at "I"; 3. the memory and graphics card are fully seated; 4. the monitor cable is in the graphics card (not in the board).

Dato: ugrađuješ ploču. Koje kablove iz napajanja moraš da priključiš da bi se računar pokrenuo?

Given: you are installing a board. Which cables from the power supply must you connect for the computer to start?

  1. 24-pin ATX kabl ide u veliki konektor na ploči.

  2. The 8-pin EPS cable goes into the connector next to the processor.

Primer 4 · zavrtnjiExample 4 · screws

Dato: ATX ploča se pričvršćuje sa 9 zavrtnjeva M3. Nacrtaj redosled: koje zavrtnje zavrćeš prvo?

Given: an ATX board is fixed with 9 M3 screws. Describe the order: which screws do you drive first?

  1. Prvo jedan ugao (npr. gore levo), pa dijagonalno suprotni (dole desno).
  2. Zatim druga dva ugla i sredinu, sve lagano.
  3. Na kraju svi čvrsto, istim ukrštenim redom.
  1. First one corner (e.g. top left), then the diagonally opposite one (bottom right).
  2. Then the other two corners and the centre, all lightly.
  3. Finally all of them firmly, in the same crossing order.

Deo 8Part 8 · paziwatch out

Česte greškeCommon mistakes

  • Višak distancera. Distancer bez rupe iznad sebe može da kratko spoji ploču. Prebroj rupe na ploči pre nego što ih postaviš.
  • Zaboravljen I/O štitnik. Kad je ploča već zavrnuta, štitnik se ne može lako staviti; moraš da je vadiš.
  • Zaboravljen EPS kabl. Računar se ne pali, a sve ostalo izgleda ispravno. EPS ide u gornji levi ugao.
  • Prejako zatezanje. Ploča puca oko rupe i tragovi se prekidaju. Zateži dok ne osetiš otpor, ne više.
  • Izvrnute diode. Prednja dioda ne svetli. Okreni konektor, ne menjaj kabl.
  • A spare standoff. A standoff with no hole above it can short the board. Count the holes in the board before you place them.
  • A forgotten I/O shield. Once the board is screwed in, the shield cannot be fitted easily; you have to take the board out.
  • A forgotten EPS cable. The computer does not start while everything else looks fine. The EPS goes into the upper left corner.
  • Over-tightening. The board cracks around the hole and the tracks break. Tighten until you feel resistance, not more.
  • Reversed LEDs. The front LED does not light. Turn the connector round, do not swap the cable.

Deo 9Part 9

SažetakSummary

  • Ploča se ugrađuje tek kad je kućište spremno; prvo se stavi procesor, hladnjak i memorija van kućišta.
  • Zaštiti ploču od ESD: dodiruj kućište, drži ploču za ivice, izvuci kabl napajanja.
  • Distanceri idu tačno ispod rupa ploče; I/O štitnik se stavlja pre ploče.
  • Zavrtnje zatežeš ukršteno, prvo lagano pa čvrsto.
  • 24-pin napaja ploču, 8-pin EPS procesor; prednji priključci imaju polaritet kod dioda.
  • Prvo paljenje radi sa otvorenim kućištem; kod problema proveri napajanje, memoriju i kabl monitora.
  • The board is installed only when the case is ready; the processor, cooler and memory go on first, outside the case.
  • Protect the board from ESD: touch the case, hold the board by its edges, unplug the power cable.
  • Standoffs go exactly under the holes of the board; the I/O shield goes in before the board.
  • You tighten the screws crosswise, lightly first and then firmly.
  • The 24-pin powers the board, the 8-pin EPS the processor; LEDs on the front panel have polarity.
  • The first power-on is done with the case open; if it fails, check the power, the memory and the monitor cable.
