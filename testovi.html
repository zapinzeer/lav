Izaberi gotov test, pogledaj sva pitanja i tačne odgovore, pa napravi sobu. Učesnici upišu kod i čekaju da ih pokreneš. Kad završe, vidiš koliko je ko pogodio, a koliko pogrešio, i koja su pitanja bila najteža. Prijava nije potrebna, ni za domaćina ni za učesnike.

Pick a ready-made test, look through all the questions and correct answers, then create a room. Participants type the code and wait for you to start. When they finish you see how many each of them got right and wrong, and which questions were the hardest. No login is needed, neither for the host nor for participants.
